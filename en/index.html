A High-Return Life Guide

Every item answers two questions: what it costs, and what it buys back.

Who benefits is graded too: you first, then your spouse and immediate family, then friends and colleagues, strangers last. Last does not mean zero — only that the return is unlikely, and that you have to weigh the risks along with it.

You do not have to do all of it. This is a shortlist ordered by value for money, not a task list. Taking one or two entries with you counts; come back for the rest when you need them. For the cheapest ones, set "Money spent" to "No money" and "Willpower needed" to "No willpower" on the left. The author has not done most of them either. Dotted "item X" references in the text open in place, so you can see what that item says without hunting for it.
