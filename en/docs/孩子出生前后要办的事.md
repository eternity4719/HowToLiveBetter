# What to do before and after a child is born: a checklist in order of time

The book is arranged by subject, so everything about pregnancy, childbirth and looking after a baby is spread across several sections. This checklist puts it in order of time, from preparing to conceive through to the child's first birthday. Each line only says what to do. Why it is worth doing and how strong the evidence is, look at the item in the brackets. Start from the heading for the stage you have reached.

## One: preparing to conceive

1. Take one 0.4 mg folic acid tablet every day, starting 3 months before you conceive. See item 1 in section 27 (start 0.4 mg of folic acid every day before conception).
2. Telephone the social insurance or medical insurance office once and ask whether your months of continuous maternity insurance contributions are enough. See item 11 in section 27 (check your maternity insurance status before conception).
3. Tell the people you live with that nobody is going to smoke indoors from now on. See item 2 in section 2 (do not smoke at home or in the car).
4. If someone at home lives on your income, insure the earner first with term life insurance. See item 40 in section 5 (insure the earner first with term life insurance).

## Two: after conception, before the birth

1. Before 13 weeks of pregnancy, open a Mother and Child Health Handbook (《母子健康手册》) at the community health centre. That is where you pick up the free prenatal checks. See item 2 in section 27 (before 13 weeks of pregnancy open a Mother and Child Health Handbook).
2. At the first prenatal check, test for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B. You do not pay for the tests yourself. See item 3 in section 27 (test for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B at the first prenatal check).
3. Not one cigarette and not one drink for the whole of pregnancy. See item 4 in section 27 (not one cigarette and not one drink during pregnancy).
4. Give your full medical history at the prenatal check. If you have risk factors for pre-eclampsia, the doctor decides whether to start low-dose aspirin after 12 weeks of pregnancy. See item 5 in section 27 (if you have risk factors for pre-eclampsia, start one low-dose aspirin tablet a day).
5. At 24 weeks of pregnancy or later, have one screening for gestational diabetes. See item 6 in section 27 (have one screening for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later).
6. After 20 weeks of pregnancy, do not take a painkiller such as ibuprofen on your own. See item 5 in section 34 (do not take a painkiller such as ibuprofen on your own).
7. Learn the list of 「立刻去医院」 signs, and make sure the people you live with learn it too. See item 7 in section 27 (memorise this list of 「立刻去医院」 signs).
8. At a prenatal check, ask whether the hospital can give pain relief in labour. See item 9 in section 27 (if you want pain relief in labour, say so directly).
9. Without a medical indication, do not ask for a caesarean section. See item 10 in section 27 (without a medical indication do not ask for a caesarean section).
10. If you are not giving birth in the place where you are insured, ask in advance whether you need an out-of-area maternity filing. See item 11 in section 27 (check your maternity insurance status before conception).
11. Maternity leave is at least 98 days, and 15 days of it can be taken before the birth. See item 2 in section 18 (maternity leave is 98 days).
12. If your employer cuts your pay or moves you to another post because of the pregnancy, keep the written notice and the chat records. See item 3 in section 18 (your pay may not be cut and you may not be dismissed because of pregnancy).
13. Borrow the cot and the pram first, and buy second-hand if you cannot borrow them. Do not buy a car seat or a mattress second-hand. See item 11 in section 20 (for big items, work through borrow, then second-hand, then new).
14. Buy nappies in a small pack first and try them for a week. Only then buy in bulk. See item 10 in section 20 (judge nappies on three things rather than the brand).
15. Decide the name in advance and check that the household registration system can produce every character in it. See item 12 in section 27 (decide the name in advance and do not get a character wrong).

## Three: during the hospital birth

1. If your waters break, lie down flat where you are, raise your hips and call 120. Do not walk about. See item 8 in section 27 (if your waters break, lie down flat where you are, raise your hips and call 120).
2. Start feeding the baby within the first hour after the birth. See item 4 in section 20 (breastfeed only for the first 6 months, not even water).
3. Give the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of the birth. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the child also gets the hepatitis B immune globulin. See item 2 in section 20 (give the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of birth).
4. The vitamin K shot has to be given. If you gave birth at home or at a place that is not a proper institution, ask about it yourself. See item 7 in section 20 (the vitamin K shot at birth is not optional).
5. Do not refuse either the heel-prick screening or the hearing screening. Before you sign the consent form, check which items you have to pay for yourself. See item 13 in section 27 (do not refuse either the newborn heel-prick screening or the hearing screening).

## Four: before you leave the hospital

1. Get the Medical Certificate of Birth (《出生医学证明》) done, and check the parents' and the child's names and identity numbers character by character. See item 12 in section 27 (get the Medical Certificate of Birth done before you leave the hospital).
2. Use the Medical Certificate of Birth to enrol the child in resident medical insurance online. You do not have to wait for the household registration. If you are not sure, telephone 12393 and ask. See item 14 in section 27 (enrol the child in resident medical insurance as soon as you have the Medical Certificate of Birth).
3. Use the car seat from the moment you start the journey home. Under 1 year old it goes in facing backwards, and the child is not held in your arms. See item 10 in section 1 (use a child car seat for children under 4).

## Five: the first month after the birth

1. Within one month, go to the police station to register the birth. See item 15 in section 27 (go to the police station to register the birth within one month of the child being born).
2. After the household registration is done, apply for the child-rearing subsidy where the child's hukou is. It is 3600 yuan per child per year. See item 1 in section 18 (the national child-rearing subsidy is 3600 yuan per child per year).
3. Put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with nothing soft on the bed. See item 1 in section 20 (put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface).
4. If you are formula-feeding, use water above 70 ℃. Throw away what is left. See item 5 in section 20 (make up formula with water above 70 ℃).
5. A baby under 3 months old at 38 ℃ goes straight to hospital. See item 8 in section 20 (a baby under 3 months old at 38 ℃ goes straight to hospital).
6. When you are about to lose your temper, put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes. See item 9 in section 20 (never shake a baby, however tired and angry you are).
7. The list of 「立刻去医院」 signs for the mother counts for the whole of the first year after the birth. See item 7 in section 27 (memorise this list of 「立刻去医院」 signs).

## Six: from the end of the first month to one year old

1. Go to the 42-day postpartum check. It includes a screening for postpartum depression. See item 16 in section 27 (do not skip the 42-day postpartum check).
2. Go to the vaccination clinic for the national immunisation programme vaccines according to the child's age in months. The DTaP starts at 2 months. See item 3 in section 20 (complete every vaccine on the national immunisation programme).
3. Breastfeed only for the first 6 months. From 6 months add solid food, and every day include an iron-rich animal food. See item 4 in section 20 (from 6 months add solid food and keep breastfeeding).
4. No honey before 1 year old. See item 6 in section 20 (no honey before 1 year old).
5. If the baby has severe eczema or an egg allergy, see the doctor first and then decide whether to add peanuts early. See item 12 in section 20 (severe eczema or an egg allergy is not a reason to avoid peanuts).
6. When a child under 2 has a cold, do not give compound cold medicines on your own. See item 4 in section 34 (do not give a child under 2 compound cold medicines or cough medicines).
7. Fit limiters on the windows and the balcony. Window screens do not count as protection. See item 11 in section 1 (if there are children at home, fit window and balcony limiters).
8. No phones, tablets or other screens from 0 to 3 years old. See item 5 in section 30 (no screens from 0 to 3 years old).
9. After you go back to work, if the child is not yet one year old, you get 1 hour a day for breastfeeding. See item 3 in section 18 (your pay may not be cut and you may not be dismissed because of pregnancy).
10. Bind the employee medical insurance personal account to the family, and the child's treatment and medicine can be paid from it. See item 13 in section 5 (bind your family once in the medical insurance app).
11. At the annual individual income tax settlement, fill in the deduction for care of infants and toddlers under 3. See item 2 in section 5 (claim every special additional deduction).
