# 17. When there are elderly at home

This section measures money and personal freedom. With elderly people at home, most of the trouble is not a shortage of money; it is not having said the words clearly and signed the paper in advance. The last two items cover pressure ulcers in someone bedridden long-term and cataract surgery. Both are measured by mortality rather than compared with the other items here.

### 1. While the older person is still clear-headed, name in writing who will be their guardian
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan to have one notarised. One conversation with the older person settles it. The hard part is finding the words: you have to say out loud the day when he can no longer manage his own affairs
- In plain language: While the older person is still clear-headed, spend a few hundred yuan on a written document at a notary office saying who will be their guardian in future. Without that document, once the older person can no longer make their own decisions, who signs for them and who handles their money has to be appointed by the court. If the family disagrees, it takes even longer.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that an adult who is clear-headed and able to decide for himself, which the law calls 「an adult with full capacity for civil conduct」, may agree in advance with somebody over who will be their guardian. The person agreed with may be a close relative, or another individual or organisation willing to act as guardian. Once agreed, it has to be settled 「以书面形式确定自己的监护人」, in writing that names the guardian. When he can no longer make decisions, or can only make some of them, which the law calls 「丧失或者部分丧失民事行为能力」, the person named in the document takes over. Without that document, who signs for him and who handles his money has to go through an appointment by the court
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This kind of document is called voluntary guardianship, 意定监护, which means a guardian you name yourself in advance. That person comes before the default order the law sets out. An older person living alone, children living in another city, or a complicated family situation all make it more necessary to do this in advance. Have it notarised at a notary office
### 2. Make a will, and remember that a later one overturns an earlier one, and that a notarised will no longer comes first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Writing a will by hand costs nothing. Having it notarised costs from several hundred to over a thousand yuan. The hard part is finding the words: you have to talk to the family about what comes after you die
- In plain language: The law recognises six forms of will, and if the formalities are wrong the whole thing is void, which is the commonest way this goes wrong. A handwritten will has to be written out by the testator from beginning to end, signed, and dated. Where several wills contradict each other, the last one made governs. From 2021 a notarised will no longer ranks first automatically.
- Benefit: The Civil Code recognises six forms of will: handwritten by the testator, written by somebody at his direction, printed, recorded on audio or video, oral, and notarised. Each has its own formal requirements, which the law calls 「形式要件」, the elements of form. If several wills contradict each other, the last one made governs. From 2021 the notarised one no longer ranks first automatically
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第一千一百三十四条至第一千一百四十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: A will handwritten by the testator has to be written out by them from beginning to end, signed, and dated. A printed will needs more than two witnesses. The testator and the witnesses have to sign every page and give the date. Heirs, people named in the will to receive something, and their close relatives may not act as witnesses. Get the formalities wrong and the whole will is void, which is the commonest way this goes wrong
### 3. Keep the older person's money in a separate account, and set a rule that two people confirm any large payment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One conversation with the family and you have agreed a figure. The hard part is how to raise it, so that the older person does not think you are watching them
- In plain language: The con that pushes older people into buying health products, collectibles or investment products always works the same way: spend a few months getting close to him, then take everything at once. Agree a rule in advance that any payment over a certain amount has to be mentioned to a certain person first. That pushes back the moment of paying, until it is past the most impulsive stretch.
- Benefit: The health product, collectible and investment scams aimed at older people all start by spending a few months building up trust, then take everything at once. Agree in advance that 「any payment over a certain amount has to be mentioned to a certain person first」. That pushes back the decision to pay, until it is past the most impulsive stretch
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature ; for the general rules on fraud see section 8 and section 13
- Notes: Do not put it as 「I am afraid you will be cheated」. Put it as 「the family's money goes into one set of accounts」 and the older person takes it far more easily. Do not hand all of the older person's money to the children to keep either, because that produces a different kind of dispute
### 4. Give the older person one sentence they can say at any time to get out of the situation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One conversation with the older person, a few minutes is enough
- In plain language: Selling and fraud both work by pressure in the moment, which makes it hard to refuse on the spot. Agree one sentence with the older person in advance: 「I need to go back and talk it over with my family.」 He can say it at any time and get himself a way out. That works better than explaining the reasons afterwards.
- Benefit: Selling and fraud both rely on pressure in the moment. Agreeing the sentence 「I need to go back and talk it over with my family」 in advance gives the older person a way out. It works better than explaining the reasons afterwards
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature
- Notes: Use it together with item 3 in this section (keep the older person's money in a separate account). Do the fall-proofing changes round the house while you are at it: see section 1. The sentence gives the older person a way out so that he does not have to stand his ground on the spot; it is not a way of watching him. When he is surrounded by sellers, the hardest part is saying no in front of a roomful of people, and that is harder than telling whether they are genuine
### 5. Stay away from every 「invest in your retirement」 scheme that asks the older person to pay first: cards, bed reservations, retirement flats, living-out schemes and products for older people are one kind of illegal fundraising
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Read the list of names below to the older person once, and it takes a few minutes. After that, refuse them straight away: do not go to the lectures, do not go to the visits, do not leave your phone number. The hard part is that once the older person has been a few times and knows the people, it is not easy to talk them round
- In plain language: Retirement schemes that ask the older person to pay first and promise a return are all the same con: membership cards, bed reservations, retirement flats, living-out schemes, and selling products for older people at a meeting. Under the rules, money lost this way has to be borne by the person who lost it. One man took 136 million yuan by taking reservations for beds in a retirement home and harmed more than 2,100 people. The only step you can stop is the moment of paying.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) lists five forms this takes, and recognising the name is more reliable than recognising the face. The first is borrowing the name of a retirement service institution: no such body exists, a venue is rented for the occasion, and it claims to have signed a cooperation agreement with a retirement home in order to 「碰瓷」, to attach itself to a real one. The second is doing business as 「elderly care services」: selling 「VIP cards」, 「membership cards」 and 「prepaid cards」, taking payment in advance for 「elderly care service fees」, and promising services well beyond its own capacity to provide beds. The third is doing business as investment in 「retirement projects」. Common names include taking a share in a health and retirement base and selling a retirement flat that does not exist. Others are letting a bed out for a long term and running 「time bank」 mutual eldercare. The signs waved are refund of the principal on sale, subletting after sale, and a promised buy-back. The fourth is doing business as selling 「products for older people」: buying goods back, holding goods to sell for you, cash back on spending, free health checks, gifts, selling by lecture, health talks and free clinics by experts. The fifth is doing business as 「living out in retirement」: cheap or free travel, a rebate on money stored with them, and earning retirement by points. That the money cannot be recovered is written into the regulations. Article twenty-five of the Regulations on the Prevention and Control of Illegal Fundraising says 「因参与非法集资受到的损失，由集资参与人自行承担」, the loss from taking part in illegal fundraising is borne by those who took part, so the money lost is yours alone. In the cases official bodies have announced, Dai Mouping and others raised 136 million yuan by illegal fundraising under the name of reserving beds in a retirement home and harmed more than 2,100 people, and Dai Mouping was sentenced to 15 years for fundraising fraud with a fine of 400,000 yuan. Cao Miaoming was sentenced to life imprisonment for fundraising fraud of 13,207 million yuan in the name of investing in the retirement industry, affecting more than 110,000 people, with political rights deprived for life and all personal property confiscated. Selling health products at a meeting is fraud. Ni Mourong used free banquets, gifts and a coach service to bring older people along to his lectures. The lecturers invented cancer-preventing and anti-cancer effects with 「a false persona, false cases and an inflated price cut」. Older people were induced to buy 「dihydroquercetin」 at dozens of times the cost price. The sales came to more than 620,000 yuan. Ni Mourong was sentenced to 10 years and 3 months, with a fine of 120,000 yuan (nationwide, the regulations in force from 2021-5-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) (2023). Risk Notice on Illegal Fundraising in the Field of Elderly Care Services, 关于养老服务领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm> (published on the Chinese government website) ; Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) and China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (中国银保监会) (2021). Risk Notice on Illegal Fundraising in the Field of Elderly Care, 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html> ; State Council (国务院) (2021). Regulations on the Prevention and Control of Illegal Fundraising (State Council Order No. 737, articles two, twenty-five and thirty), 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm> ; Supreme People's Procuratorate (最高人民检察院) (2022). Typical Cases of Procuratorates Cracking Down on and Rectifying Fraud against the Elderly, 检察机关打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml> ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (2025). Typical Cases of Punishing Fraud Against People's Livelihoods According to Law (case five, the Ni Mourong fraud case), 依法惩治涉民生领域诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例五 倪某荣诈骗案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html>
- Notes: You do not judge by 「this one looks official」, you judge by 「does it ask you to pay first, and does it promise a return」. A retirement home is in the business of selling services, so once the talk turns to investment, rebates or getting your money back, do not believe it. Being taken to lectures, on trips or to free clinics is not itself illegal and is very hard to stop. What you can stop is the moment of paying, and the two-person confirmation rule in item 3 in this section works with it. If the money has already been paid, call 110 or 96110 at once to ask for the payment to be stopped, following item 2 in section 8, and report it at the same time to the local lead department in charge of dealing with illegal fundraising. Raising money against your home is a different route: see item 6 in this section (reverse-mortgage insurance for the elderly).
### 6. Apart from the insurance companies' reverse-mortgage insurance for the elderly, stay away from every other 「raise money against your home」 scheme, and never mortgage the flat to buy investment products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Do not hand the property certificate at home to anybody who comes to the door in the name of 「raising money against your home」. For mortgages, guarantees and loans, show the agreement to your children before you sign. The hard part is that the people who come are often warm and attentive and have made many trips, and the older person finds it hard to say no
- In plain language: There is only one legitimate scheme that raises money against a home, the reverse-mortgage insurance for the elderly sold by insurance companies, and it is still in the pilot stage. The kind sold by people who come to your door has nothing to do with it: it tricks the older person into mortgaging the flat and then uses the borrowed money to buy the investment products it recommends. Some people do not even know the flat has been mortgaged, and in the end they lose the flat and still owe the loan.
- Benefit: The consumer protection bureau of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission set out both the genuine version and the fake one. There is only one legitimate scheme that raises money against a home, and it is called 「老年人住房反向抵押养老保险」. An older person with full legal ownership of a flat mortgages the property to an insurance company. The flat is still theirs to live in, to use and to collect rent from. To sell it or dispose of it they need the consent of the mortgagee, which is the insurance company. The older person draws a pension on the agreed terms until death. This business is still at the pilot stage and few people use it. The 「以房养老」 sold by criminals 「完全无关」, has nothing to do at all with the reverse-mortgage insurance for the elderly that the state is piloting, and is 「假借国家政策、为非法集资活动造势宣传的手段」, a means of borrowing the name of state policy to advertise and build up support for illegal fundraising. The method is to trick the older person into mortgaging the property and then use the borrowed money to buy the investment products it recommends, and this 「往往是『以新还旧』的庞氏骗局」, is often a Ponzi scheme that pays the old claims with new money. The consequences are given in the original wording: 「有的参与人甚至都不知道自己的房产被抵押，最终失去了房子，还背负贷款」, some participants do not even know their property has been mortgaged, and in the end they lose the flat and carry the loan. The notice also gives a line for reading a promised return. The words are: 「理财产品如果承诺收益率超过 6% 就要打问号，超过 8% 就很危险，10% 以上就要准备损失全部本金，『保本高收益』就是金融诈骗」, if an investment product promises a return above 6% you should be suspicious, above 8% it is very dangerous, and above 10% you should be ready to lose the whole principal, and 「保本高收益」, capital-protected with a high return, is financial fraud. The same notice also requires that 「慎重对待合同签署环节，不在空白合同签字」, the signing of the contract be handled carefully and no signature be put on a blank contract (nationwide, 2021-6)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Consumer Protection Bureau of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (中国银保监会消费者权益保护局) (2021). Risk Notice on Being Alert to Financial Fraud under the Names of 「investing in your retirement」 and 「raising money against your home」, 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html> (reprinted by the Hunan Provincial Local Financial Administration Bureau) ; Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) and China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (中国银保监会) (2021). Risk Notice on Illegal Fundraising in the Field of Elderly Care, 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示（第四种表现形式：以宣称「以房养老」为名非法集资）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>
- Notes: It is graded B because these statements come from regulators' risk notices and from judgement, not from research data. The three lines of 6%, 8% and 10% are reference lines the regulator gives consumers, not standards set by law. The China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission has now been merged into the National Financial Regulatory Administration, and the original page will not open, so the reprint on a provincial financial regulator's site is cited here. Losing money on an investment product is the smaller problem: once the flat has been mortgaged out, the older person still owes a loan, and that loss is far larger than the principal. For the general rules on signing and blank contracts, see item 17 in section 8; for stopping a payment after you have been cheated, see item 2 in section 8

### 7. If an older person at home is bedridden long-term or severely disabled, apply to the medical insurance department in your home area for long-term care insurance; it is not only for old people
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Submit one application at the medical insurance department in your home area. Then have a disability level assessment. If you pass at the first assessment, the fund pays the assessment fee and you pay nothing
- In plain language: Long-term care insurance pays for the care you buy. If someone at home is severely disabled and it has lasted more than half a year, apply at the medical insurance department in your home area. You do not pay the care fees out of pocket first; around 70% is reimbursed for people insured as employees and around 50% for people insured as residents, and bed fees and meal costs are not reimbursed. There is no age threshold, so a young person who is severely disabled can apply as well.
- Benefit: Qualifying long-term care service costs carry no deductible. The deductible is the amount you pay yourself before the cover starts, and here you pay nothing up front. For people insured under the employed-worker scheme, the fund reimburses around 70%. For people insured under the scheme for residents who are not in employment, around 50%. The most that can be paid in one year, the annual maximum payment limit, is not more than 50% of the previous year's per-capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the local pooling region
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National Healthcare Security Administration and seven other departments (国家医保局等八部门) (2026). Notice on Issuing the Implementation Plan for Accelerating the Establishment of the Long-Term Care Insurance System, 关于印发《加快建立长期护理保险制度实施方案》的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm> : the people covered are 「按规定参保缴费且失能状态长期持续（一般为6个月以上），经申请通过评估认定的失能人员」, those who have paid contributions as required and whose disability has continued for a long time, generally more than 6 months, and who are recognised as disabled after applying and passing an assessment, and 「制度起步阶段保障重度失能人员」, the scheme covers severely disabled people at the stage where it starts ; 「待遇享受不设起付标准。符合规定的长期护理服务费用，按未就业城乡居民参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为50%左右；按单位职工参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为70%左右，退休人员享受单位职工参保待遇」, benefits carry no deductible threshold; for qualifying long-term care service costs the fund pays around 50% for people insured under the scheme for residents who are not in employment, and around 70% for people insured under the employed-worker scheme, and retired people get the employed-worker treatment ; 「参保人员基金年度最高支付限额不超过统筹地区上年度城乡居民人均可支配收入的50%」, the annual maximum the fund pays for one person is not more than 50% of the previous year's per-capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the pooling region ; 「待遇享受固定等待期原则上按照6个月设置」, the fixed waiting period for benefits is in principle set at 6 months ; also see the Opinions of the General Offices of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council on Accelerating the Establishment of the Long-Term Care Insurance System, 中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅《关于加快建立长期护理保险制度的意见》. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Notes: The disability has to 「长期持续（一般为6个月以上）」, to have continued for a long time, generally more than 6 months; a few weeks in hospital does not reach the condition for applying. There is also a 6-month waiting period before you can draw the benefit. Costs that are not care, such as bed fees and meal costs, are not paid by long-term care insurance. Medical costs that medical insurance should pay are not paid by it either. If you are already receiving work-injury living and care payments, you do not receive this on top. This money and the care allowance inside the two disability allowances are two sets of formalities and two separate sums, and they do not clash: see item 8 in section 7 (the two allowances). Care at home and in the community is reimbursed at a slightly better rate, so you do not have to go into an institution. The scheme is being put in place region by region, and the assessment standards differ. Ring 12393 or the medical insurance agency in your home area first, and ask whether it has started locally and how to book an assessment. If the assessment is not passed, do not give up: you can apply again once the disability has become worse.
### 8. If someone at home is bedridden long-term, treat pressure ulcers as the main enemy: get an electric pressure-relief mattress, turn them on a schedule, and look at the bony parts every day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A home electric alternating-pressure air mattress starts at a few hundred yuan. Each turn takes a few minutes. The hard part is that you have to do it every two or three hours, through the night as well, and one person cannot manage it alone
- In plain language: Among older patients who already had pressure ulcers before hip fracture surgery, the chance of dying within 30 days is about 20% higher. Switching to an electric pressure-relief mattress cuts new pressure ulcers by about 60%. Turning the person on schedule, done properly, cuts them by about 70%. Do not leave anyone lying face down for long. Look at the base of the spine, the heels, the hips and the shoulder blades every day, and if you find a red mark that does not fade under pressure, find a nurse or a doctor the same day.
- Benefit: 19520 hip fracture surgery patients (the US NSQIP registry, 2016 to 2019). People who already had pressure ulcers before the operation had a higher proportion of deaths within 30 days. Once the other factors are taken into account, the association still holds (OR 1.2, odds up 21%, P=0.004). These people were also more likely to have other problems: deep vein thrombosis (OR 1.59), pneumonia (OR 1.39) and unplanned readmission (OR 1.43), and their average length of stay was 0.4 days longer. In a follow-up of 324 hospitalised older people (average age 86) the median follow-up was 18 months. Pressure ulcers predicted long-term mortality on their own (HR 1.500, 95% CI 1.059 to 2.126, P=0.020). Predicting on their own means that once the other factors are set aside, they predict by themselves. Pooling 65 randomised trials (network meta-analysis). What is compared is an electric active-air support surface against a standard hospital mattress, in other words a standard support surface. An electric active-air support surface is the kind of electric pressure-relief mattress. People on it had a lower incidence of pressure ulcers (RR 0.42, 95% CI 0.29 to 0.63, moderate certainty). For an electric mixed-air support surface it is RR 0.22 (0.07 to 0.66). A Cochrane review of 11 trials and 4462 people (updated in 2026). Turning every 2 hours against turning every 4 hours makes no difference that can be pinned down (RR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79 to 1.39, very low certainty). Comparisons between the other intervals are just as uncertain. In a trial of 1226 ICU patients, wearable sensors gave real-time reminders and visual alerts, and this actually got the 2-hourly turning done. The incidence of pressure ulcers was lower than with routine care (RR 0.28, 95% CI 0.10 to 0.75, moderate certainty). In a trial of 116 people, the group lying face down had a higher incidence of pressure ulcers than the group lying on the back (RR 4.55, 95% CI 2.31 to 8.98)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Porter SB, Pla R, Chow JH, et al. (2022). Preoperative Pressure Ulcers, Mortality, and Complications in Older Hip Fracture Surgery Patients. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews, 6(11). <https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-22-00117> ; Ottaviani S, Rondanina E, Longo E, et al. (2026). Impact of pressure ulcers and frailty on long-term mortality: a prospective cohort study of hospitalized older adults. European Geriatric Medicine, 17(2), 665-672. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-025-01331-8> ; Shi C, Dumville JC, Cullum N (2018). Support surfaces for pressure ulcer prevention: A network meta-analysis. PLoS ONE, 13(2), e0192707. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0192707> ; Latimer SL, Chaboyer WP, Probst S, et al. (2026). Repositioning for pressure injury prevention in adults. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD009958. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009958.pub4>
- Notes: There are three reasons for the B. First, the two mortality studies only show that 「people with pressure ulcers did worse afterwards」, which is not the same as 「preventing pressure ulcers brings the death rate down」. Second, the randomised trials on the turning interval rest on very shaky conclusions. Third, the evidence on the mattress is harder, but what the studies compare are hospital mattresses, and applying it to a home mattress is an extrapolation. Check the base of the spine, the heels, the hips and the shoulder blades every day for a red mark that does not fade under pressure; that is routine nursing practice and has no separate literature. For the care services that long-term care insurance pays for, see item 7 in this section. If one leg suddenly swells, treat it as a deep vein thrombosis: see item 11 in section 13. For the period bedridden after a fall or a serious injury, see item 34 in section 1

### 9. Where an older person's cataract is already affecting how they see the road, go to the eye department for an assessment and an operation; where both eyes have it, do not leave the second one too long
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A fair amount of money. First take the older person to the eye department for one examination and let the doctor decide whether it should be done. One eye is one operation, and many hospitals do it as day surgery, so the person can go home the same day. Where both eyes have a cataract, it has to be done in two operations. How much you pay yourself depends on which intraocular lens, that is, the artificial lens, is put in and how the local medical insurance reimburses it, so ask before the operation.
- In plain language: Women over 70 who have the first eye's cataract done as soon as possible fall about 34% less often over a year, and fewer of them have a fracture. But the number of people who fall is not lower. Whether the second eye is done makes no clear difference to falls. Older people who have the operation are about 16% lower in hip fracture within a year.
- Benefit: A randomised trial took 306 women over 70 with a cataract. Half had the first eye operated on within about 4 weeks and the other half waited the usual 12 months. Over a year, the people operated on as soon as possible fell about 34% less often. The original figure is a fall-rate ratio of 0.66, credible range 95% CI 0.45 to 0.96. The people who fell at least once were 49% in the surgery group and 45% in the waiting group, which is no lower. The people who fell more than twice were 18% in the surgery group and 25% in the waiting group. In the surgery group 4 people had a fracture (3%), in the waiting group 12 people (8%). The Cochrane review included this trial and concluded that surgery on the first eye in women reduced the number of falls, and surgery on the second eye did not. The randomised trial of the second eye took 239 women over 70. The fall rate in the surgery group was 32% lower, but the difference may be chance (fall-rate ratio 0.68, 95% CI 0.39 to 1.19). In a 5% sample of the US Medicare system there were 1113640 cataract patients over 65 (2002 to 2009). This study only followed records and had no control group. The people who had the operation were about 16% lower in the chance of a hip fracture within a year (OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.81 to 0.87). The absolute difference is 0.20 percentage points, about 2 fewer per 1000 people

- Notes: Disputed. Pooling the two randomised trials, the number of people who fell is not clearly lower (OR 0.81, 95% CI 0.55 to 1.17). In the whole-population hospital records for Western Australia, in the year after the first eye was done, hospital admissions for injuries from falls went up by 27%. In the stretch between the two operations, admissions of this kind were 2.14 times what they had been before the operation. A comparison like this before and after cannot separate the effect of the operation from the effect of getting older. In another Australian follow-up study, the number of falls fell most after the second eye was done. So where both eyes have a cataract, do not leave the second one too long. In the stretch between the two operations, be especially careful when walking and on stairs. Cochrane also included a trial that treated vision problems in older people, and the number of falls went up by about 57% instead. The operation is about whether you can see clearly; to fall less you also need item 13 in section 1 (balance and leg strength). Whether the bones can take a fall, see item 39 in section 1 (a bone density check) and item 40 in section 1 (treating osteoporosis once it is found). The national eye health plan states that cataract surgery to restore sight is already carried out in every county, so you do not have to go to a hospital in a big city.
