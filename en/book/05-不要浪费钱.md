# 5. 不要浪费钱

这一节只算钱：能省下多少，每年收益差多少，一笔钱长期平均下来能拿回多少。这些不换算成寿命或时间。涉及投资的条目只讲怎么算账，只用核实过的历史数据，都不构成投资建议。

# 5. Do not waste money

This section counts money only: how much you can save, how much the yearly return differs, how much a sum of money gives back on average over the long run. Nothing here is converted into years of life or into time. The entries that touch investing only explain how the arithmetic works. They use checked historical data and none of them is investment advice.

### 1. Turn off automatic renewal and renew by hand each time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Work once through the automatic-deduction lists in Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android. It takes 10 to 20 minutes. After that you tap once more yourself every time something renews.
- In plain language: What you save is the subscriptions you stopped using long ago and still pay for. Going through the automatic-deduction lists in Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android takes under 20 minutes. By law a merchant must warn you clearly before taking the money, and must give you a way to cancel at any time that is easy to find and easy to click.
- Benefit: The money you save equals the total of the subscriptions you no longer use but are still billed for. It differs for everyone, so there is no single figure. The regulator requires merchants to notify you clearly before renewal, and to give you a simple way to cancel at any time, with no separate charge for cancelling (China, in force since 2021)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2021, amended 2025). Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Transactions（网络交易监督管理办法）(SAMR Order No. 37, Article eighteen). <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- Notes: For a service you really use often, paying yearly is usually cheaper than paying monthly, so leaving renewal on can be the better choice. The ones to turn off are the ones you forgot about months ago and are still paying for.
### 2. Settle your annual individual income tax from March to June, and claim every special additional deduction
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You do it in the Individual Income Tax app, and it takes a bit over ten minutes.
- In plain language: These groups of people usually get money back: anyone who worked only a few months of the year, anyone who changed jobs during the year, and anyone who left out deductions for children's education, a home loan, rent, or supporting an elderly relative. It takes a bit over ten minutes on your phone. If your annual comprehensive income is under 120,000 yuan, or the tax you owe is under 400 yuan, you can skip it.
- Benefit: One settlement puts four kinds of income together: salary and wages, remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration, and royalties. What you overpaid comes back, and what you underpaid you have to pay. Two situations let you skip it: your annual comprehensive income does not exceed 120,000 yuan, or the tax you owe does not exceed 400 yuan. Both conditions come from a 2023 State Taxation Administration announcement, in force until 2027-12-31. In the other direction, the following people usually get money back: people with income in only a few months of the year, people who changed jobs during the year, and people who left out deductions for children's education, a home loan, rent, or supporting an elderly relative
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局) (2025). Measures for the Settlement of Comprehensive Individual Income Tax (个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法) (Article six) and the official explanation. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm> ; Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration (2023). Announcement on Continuing the Settlement and Payment of Comprehensive Individual Income Tax. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm>
- Notes: People who owe tax still have to file, and not filing is illegal. There are seven special additional deductions: children's education, continuing education, serious medical treatment, housing loan interest, housing rent, supporting an elderly relative, and care of children under 3. A housing loan and rent cannot both be claimed; pick one.
### 3. The housing provident fund is not only for buying a home: rent, renovation and building fees can all be withdrawn, under the new rules from 20 September 2026
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You go through the withdrawal procedure once.
- In plain language: The provident fund is not frozen unless you buy a home. The new rules list nine situations you can withdraw for, and paying rent, renovating the home you live in, and paying building management fees for the home you live in are all among them. Once you file, the provident fund centre must tell you within 3 days whether it approves the withdrawal.
- Benefit: The amended regulations list nine situations in which you can withdraw. They include paying rent, renovating the home you live in, and paying the building management fees for the home you live in. The housing provident fund management centre must decide within 3 days of accepting your application whether to approve the withdrawal or refuse it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Decision of the State Council on Amending the Regulations on the Administration of Housing Provident Funds（国务院关于修改《住房公积金管理条例》的决定）(State Council Order No. 844, adopted at the 93rd executive meeting of the State Council on 2026-7-31, 「自2026年9月20日起施行」): Article twenty-four as amended「职工有下列情形之一的，可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额：（一）支付房租的；（二）购买、建造、翻建、大修自住住房的；（三）偿还购房贷款本息的；（四）装修自住住房的；（五）支付自住住房物业费的；（六）离休、退休的；（七）完全丧失劳动能力，并与单位终止劳动关系的；（八）出境定居的；（九）国务院批准的其他住房消费情形。」(the nine situations in which a worker may withdraw the balance in the account); Article twenty-five「住房公积金管理中心应当自受理申请之日起3日内作出准予提取或者不准提取的决定」(the centre decides within 3 days of accepting the application); the contribution rate in Article eighteen「均不得低于职工上一年度月平均工资的5％」(no lower than 5% of the worker's average monthly wage the previous year). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>
- Notes: The money in the account is your own salary plus the part your employer adds. Left alone it earns interest at a very low rate. The regulations also say that when a worker dies, or is declared dead, the heirs and beneficiaries may withdraw the balance. Which documents to file and how the procedure runs are set by the local housing provident fund management centre. Look up your local centre's guide before you go.
### 4. Recalculate your phone and broadband plan once a year, downgrade what you do not use, and complain if the carrier refuses to switch you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One look at the bill takes a bit over ten minutes. The hard part is holding your ground when the service agent refuses to downgrade you.
- In plain language: The plan you are on is often tens of yuan a month dearer than the plans on sale now. Tens of yuan a month is several hundred yuan a year thrown away. By law a carrier with no valid reason may not refuse to let you port your number, may not drag its feet and leave it undone, and may not set a separate tariff just for people who port. If they stall, complain.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology rules state that a carrier may not「无正当理由拒绝、阻止、拖延向用户提供携号转网服务」. It may not「采取拦截、限制等技术手段影响携号转网用户的通信服务质量」. It may not「为携号转网用户设置专项资费方案和营销方案」. An old plan is often tens of yuan a month dearer than the plans on sale now. Staying on it throws away several hundred yuan a year
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (工业和信息化部) (2019). Provisions on the Administration of Number Porting Services（携号转网服务管理规定）(MIIT 信管〔2019〕242). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>
- Notes: First check in the carrier's app how much data and how many minutes you actually used in the last six months, then pick a plan from that. If the agent says the downgrade has to be done at a shop, or that it cannot change during the contract period, ask for the written basis. If they cannot produce one, complain on 12300.
### 5. Do not buy lottery tickets
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Not buying is the saving.
- In plain language: By law, the share of sales that lotto and number games set aside as prizes is at least 50%, and for Double Colour Ball it is 51%. That means that for every 1 yuan you put in, you get 0.51 yuan back on average over the long run, a steady loss of 49%. Nationwide lottery sales in 2024 were 623.486 billion yuan, of which 161.031 billion yuan went straight into the public welfare fund and never counted as prizes.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Finance sets the minimum share of sales that goes to prizes: 50% for lotto and number games, 65% for scratch cards, 73% for sports betting. Double Colour Ball pays out 51% of sales, so out of every 100 yuan in sales only 51 yuan becomes prizes. Put in terms of one person, every 1 yuan put in returns 0.51 yuan on average over the long run, a loss of 49%. Lottery sales nationwide in 2024 were 623.486 billion yuan, of which 161.031 billion yuan went into the public welfare fund and did not come back to the people who bought the tickets (China, 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Finance (财政部) (2015). Notice on Regulating and Strengthening the Management of the Composition Ratios of Lottery Funds（关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知）(财综〔2015〕94). <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm> ; Ministry of Finance (2025). Ministry of Finance Announcement No. 8 of 2025 (2024 Lottery Public Welfare Fund Raising and Allocation). <http://zhs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202508/t20250828_3970977.htm> ; Rules of the China Welfare Lottery Double Colour Ball Game（第十四条）. <https://mzj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/flcp/202111/5dccc1a5da89434e94a62f8ea0e5887e.shtml>
- Notes: Over the long run you lose, and the system fixes that. It has nothing to do with luck. Buying is fine too, but treat it as 2 yuan of entertainment each time, not as saving.
### 6. Walk away from "high returns", "principal guaranteed" and "steady profit"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. All it takes is to stop when you hear those words.
- In plain language: The regulator's own words: a return above 6% should make you ask questions, above 8% is dangerous, and above 10% you should expect to lose your whole principal. Regulated investment products are no longer allowed to promise that the principal is safe or that you will earn. Money lost in illegal fundraising is borne by you alone under the rules, and nobody covers you if it goes wrong.
- Benefit: In 2018 the former chairman of the banking and insurance regulator said something at the Lujiazui forum. His words were:「收益率超过 6% 的就要打问号，超过 8% 的就很危险，10% 以上就要准备损失全部本金」. The new asset management rules require financial institutions to「不得承诺保本保收益」and to「出现兑付困难时，金融机构不得以任何形式垫资兑付」. So regulated products no longer have a guaranteed principal. Losses from taking part in illegal fundraising are「由集资参与人自行承担」, and nobody pays them back when it goes wrong (China, 2018 and 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Guo Shouqing (2018). Preventing and Resolving Financial Risks, Striding Over the Major Checkpoints（防范化解金融风险 奋力跨越重大关口）(speech at the 10th Lujiazui Forum, on the National Financial Regulatory Administration website):「在打击非法集资过程中，努力通过多种方式让人民群众认识到，高收益意味着高风险，收益率超过6%的就要打问号，超过8%的就很危险，10%以上就要准备损失全部本金」. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=182418> ; reprinted by the Tianjin Municipal Local Financial Administration Bureau. <https://jrgz.tj.gov.cn/ztlm/ztzl/jrjfwphjr/202006/t20200625_2744709.html> ; People's Bank of China and others (2018). Guiding Opinions on Regulating the Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions（关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见）(银发〔2018〕106, Article two and Article six). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5323101.htm> ; State Council (2021). Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Illegal Fundraising（防范和处置非法集资条例）(State Council Order No. 737, Article twenty-five). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5588815.htm>
- Notes: The 6%, 8% and 10% lines are where a regulator drew the boundary from experience. They are not provisions of law. The words were said about cracking down on illegal fundraising, and they target investment products and investment companies that promise high returns. None of this is investment advice.
### 7. Do not use the minimum payment on a credit card, and do not open instalments or consumer loans to buy things
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You have to keep enough cash to pay the statement in full before the due date. The hard part is resisting instalments when a large bill arrives.
- In plain language: The minimum payment is not a discount. It is a loan that charges interest every day, which works out at around 18% a year. The moment you use it the interest-free period ends, and interest runs on the unpaid principal from that day to the last. When you look at an interest rate, only the annualised figure counts. The People's Bank of China already requires card issuers to display the annualised rate prominently and forbids showing only a daily rate.
- Benefit: Before 2020 credit card overdraft rates had a ceiling and a floor: the ceiling was「日利率万分之五」and the floor was 0.7 times the ceiling. Five ten-thousandths a day works out at 18.25% a year, and that is simple interest: at 18.25% a year the interest does not compound into the principal. From 2021 the card issuer and the cardholder set the price themselves. Once you use the minimum payment, interest runs every day on the full unpaid principal and the interest-free period ends at the same time. The People's Bank of China already requires card issuers to display the annualised rate(the rate converted to a whole year)prominently and states that they「不得仅展示日利率、日还款额」. Read the annualised figure before you decide (China, notice of 2020)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: People's Bank of China (中国人民银行) (2020). Notice on Promoting the Market-Based Reform of Credit Card Overdraft Interest Rates（关于推进信用卡透支利率市场化改革的通知）(银发〔2020〕327). <https://xining.pbc.gov.cn/zhengwugongkai/4081330/4406346/4693549/4159909/index.html>
- Notes: The 18.25% is five ten-thousandths multiplied straight by 365, with no compounding. Compounded monthly it is higher. Some banks give a discount, so go by the annualised rate printed on your own statement. When you really cannot get the cash together, look for a proper channel with a lower rate first, and do not let a minimum payment roll and gather interest.
### 8. Do not tip streamers, do not top up games, do not buy on impulse
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend a few minutes on three things: turn off password-free payment, set a limit per payment and per day, and delete the payment methods bound to the app. The hard part is resisting the tap in the moment.
- In plain language: Players in China spend about 480 yuan a year each on games, and almost none of it comes back. The law only lets a parent get a refund for money a minor spent without the parent's consent; adults have no such rule. The cheapest defence is to set it up in advance: turn off password-free payment, set a per-payment and daily limit, and unbind the payment method.
- Benefit: This is a large item of spending. Actual sales in China's game market in 2024 were 325.783 billion yuan, with 674 million users, or about 480 yuan per person (worked out by dividing the first figure by the second). Money spent is almost never recovered. There is one exception: when a person with limited capacity for civil conduct (here, a minor) tips or tops up without the guardian's consent, and the guardian asks for the money back,「人民法院应予支持」. A tip or a top-up an adult makes for themselves has no such rule. Separately, minors may play online games only on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, and only in the 1 hour from 20 to 21 (China, 2020 and 2021)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: National Press and Publication Administration (国家新闻出版署) (2024). China's game market revenue exceeded 325,700 million yuan this year, a new high (中国音数协游戏工委《2024 年中国游戏产业报告》). <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/ywdt/202412/t20241216_877445.html> ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (2020). Guiding Opinions on the Proper Handling of Civil Cases Involving the Novel Coronavirus Epidemic, Part Two（关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二））(法发〔2020〕17 号, item 9). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/230181.html> ; National Press and Publication Administration (2021). Notice on Further Strict Management and the Effective Prevention of Minors Becoming Addicted to Online Games（关于进一步严格管理 切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知）(国新出发〔2021〕14 号). <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202108/t20210830_666285.html>
- Notes: It is graded B because nobody has measured directly how much you save by not tipping. All that is available is an aggregate figure for the games industry. Turning off password-free payment and setting limits are rules of thumb, and on their own they would be graded C. The wording of Article nineteen and Article one hundred and forty-five of the Civil Code is already quoted word for word in item 9 in this section (a child tops up and tips with a phone). None of this is investment advice.
### 9. A child who tops up and tips with a phone: spending above the age of eight that the parent has not approved can be reclaimed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You have to spend time collecting evidence and negotiating with the platform. The platform usually refuses first and refunds later. If you cannot settle it you can only sue, and that takes months to start and the legal fees are yours to pay. So prepare the documents first; going through the platform and 12315 is cheaper. The hard part is that the child will not say anything, and you have to stay patient and get the details.
- In plain language: Money a child over eight spends, if the amount is plainly out of proportion to the child's age and ability to understand, and the parent neither agrees to it nor accepts the bill afterwards, is an invalid transaction, and you can demand the money back. A child under eight cannot in any case decide to spend money alone. If the parent says nothing at all, the law treats that as refusing to approve the transaction.
- Benefit: A minor over eight is a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, which means they may only do things suited to their age and ability to understand. A civil legal act not suited to their age and ability to understand is valid only if the legal representative agrees or approves it afterwards: the parent has to say yes in advance, or accept the bill afterwards. A child under eight has no capacity for civil conduct and cannot decide alone; a legal representative acts for them. If the legal representative expresses no view, that counts as a refusal to approve: a parent who stays silent is refusing the bill
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code（民法典）. Full text as reprinted by the Supreme People's Procuratorate. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> Article nineteen：「八周岁以上的未成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力相适应的民事法律行为。」Article twenty：「不满八周岁的未成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」Article one hundred and forty-five：「限制民事行为能力人实施的纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为有效；实施的其他民事法律行为经法定代理人同意或者追认后有效。」「法定代理人未作表示的，视为拒绝追认。」
- Notes: The whole case rests on evidence. You have to show that the child, not you, was operating the phone. Useful material includes: top-ups clustered in school hours or while a parent was at work, the device's usage records, the child's chat and game logs, and a statement from the child. There is no fixed sum that counts as "suited to the age and ability", and a game skin costing tens of yuan is not the same as a tip costing tens of thousands. Stopping it early is cheaper: set a ceiling on password-free payments, turn on the youth mode, and do not tell the child your payment password. For the case where you yourself tip or top up on impulse, see item 8 in this section (do not tip streamers, do not top up games, do not buy on impulse).
### 10. Teach your child one sentence: anyone who tells you to use your parent's phone or to read out a verification code is a fraudster
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Go through the six kinds below with your child, and agree one sentence with them: "if you get scammed, tell me first and I will not shout at you". Also set a ceiling on password-free payments, turn on the youth mode, and do not tell the child your payment password. The hard part is keeping your temper once it really has happened.
- In plain language: The six kinds of scam work differently, but the last step is always the same: get the child to use a parent's phone to transfer money or to read out a verification code. So one sentence is enough: anyone who tells you to touch your parent's phone or to read out a code is a fraudster. Add one more: "if you get scammed, tell me first and I will not shout at you". Money can only come back if the child is willing to say so that day.
- Benefit: The Quanzhou anti-fraud centre worked with the Quanzhou education bureau to break down six kinds of fraud that target teenagers. The methods differ, but the last step is the same: get the child to transfer money from a parent's phone. First, trading game accounts and equipment privately at a low price. Xiao Wang transferred 260 yuan privately, and the other party said「未成年人转账违规，账号要冻结」. He tapped a "verification link" on his father's phone, and more than 30,000 yuan left his account. Second, "lifting the anti-addiction limit". The other party threatened「不转账取消，你家长要坐牢」, and the child transferred 7000 yuan from the mother's phone. The text states that the anti-addiction limit is set by the platform and that there is「根本没有『私下解锁』渠道」. Third, refunds for tips. The fraudster posed as an "official customer service" line, said that a minor's tip could be refunded, and asked for「用家长支付宝给指定直播间刷礼物才能退款」. 73,000 yuan was tipped out of the mother's account and she was blocked. The text says「有人加你让你『刷礼物退款』，100% 是诈骗」. Fourth, posing as a classmate or a teacher. The fraudster stole an account and sent a voice message naming the person to borrow money. In the school term they also joined class groups as a fake teacher and sent「交学杂费 500 元」, with the same avatar and nickname as the real teacher, and arranged for a plant in the group to say「已交」. Fifth, fandom. The fraudster pulled the child into a "celebrity fan group, claim an 888 yuan red packet". First the child transferred 48.88 yuan as a "verification". Then they frightened the child with「未成年人参与违规，平台要冻结 8 万，家长要坐牢」. In total they took 16,000 yuan. The text says「真警察、律师绝不会在网上联系未成年人调查，更不会要家长手机操作」. Sixth, using cheap goods on a second-hand platform to lure people off the platform and into a private transfer. The same material also lists three lines a child should not cross. First, do not lend, sell or rent out a bank card or a phone card; if the card is used to move stolen money, that can amount to the crime of assisting the operation of a criminal network, and a record of it can affect entry to postgraduate study and the civil service. Second, "phone bank" part-time work is illegal. Third, in "high-paid jobs" abroad, a person can be controlled the moment they arrive (nationwide, 2025-10)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Quanzhou Anti-Fraud Centre (泉州市反诈骗中心) (2025). First Lesson of the School Term: Anti-Fraud Is a Required Course for Teenagers, and These 6 Traps Must Be Avoided! <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202510/t20251028_7026836.htm> (reprinted by the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department)
- Notes: It is graded B because what the authorities publish is a breakdown of fraudsters' scripts and a few cases, with no case rate and no statistics on how effective the precautions are. Keep this apart from item 9 (a child tops up and tips with a phone): money a child spends on tipping and top-ups can be reclaimed under the Civil Code. Money taken by a fraudster does not fall under that rule and can only be reported and stopped through item 2 in section 8. Whether it comes back depends on whether the money is still on the account. The core script fraudsters use with children is "you have broken the rules, your parents will go to prison". Say that plainly to your child before it happens; the sentence itself is the signal that this is a scam, and it works better than asking questions afterwards. You must actually keep your promise about "tell me first, I will not shout". Children hide it for fear of being scolded, and that is the common reason the loss grew in these cases. ### 19. Do not put all your money into one share, one platform or one property
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is accepting one thing: you will not happen to pick exactly the one that does best.
- In plain language: If several things do not rise and fall in perfect lockstep, spreading your money leaves the average return unchanged and makes the account swing less. Put the other way round, if everything is in one share, one platform or one property, then when it goes wrong everything about you goes wrong.
- Benefit: As long as several assets do not rise and fall in perfect lockstep, spreading your money leaves the expected return (what you can expect to earn) unchanged and makes the volatility (how far the account moves up and down) smaller. This is the basic result on portfolio choice put forward by Markowitz in 1952, and it has been used ever since. The other way round, if everything is in one share or one platform, its variance is your entire variance, and it moves as much as you do. When it goes wrong there is no hedge, and no other asset can make good the loss
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Markowitz, H. (1952). Portfolio Selection. The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77–91. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x>
- Notes: It is graded B because that classic paper is pure mathematical derivation and does not work out how much spreading saves you in money. Spreading only makes the account move less; it does not promise that you will not lose. Buying a broad market index fund (see item 17 in this section) is itself the cheapest way to spread. None of this is investment advice.

### 28. Do the comparison before repaying a mortgage early; do not go on feel
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. It takes half an hour to do the sums. You have to estimate honestly the steady after-tax return you can actually get.
- In plain language: Repaying a mortgage early is a certain gain of your mortgage rate. So there is only one question to ask: is the after-tax return you can get steadily over the long run higher than your mortgage rate? With equal principal and interest, interest takes the larger share in the early years, so the earlier you repay the more you save. - Benefit: The arithmetic works like this. First set r as the annual interest rate on your mortgage, as your contract states it. On 2026-8-20 the 5-year LPR above is 3.5%, and the rate actually applied is that LPR plus or minus a spread. Then set g as the annualised after-tax return on the other investment you could choose: the proportion of this money that you can expect to earn in a year on average by putting it somewhere else. The certain return on repaying early is r, and each payment you make is an r earned for certain. If g is reliably greater than r, keeping the money invested does better. If g is below r, or if you cannot pin g down at all, repaying early locks in a risk-free r. With equal principal and interest, interest takes the larger share of your early payments, so the earlier you repay the more interest you save. You can read the figure straight off the bank's app under "early repayment calculation" (China, 2026 rates)

### 37. Sell shares that are down according to a rule you set in advance; do not try to get even by adding to the position
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is admitting this one has already gone down, and then selling it.
- In plain language: People's instinct is to sell what has gone up and to hold what has gone down. In seven years of records from 10,000 American accounts, shares that had risen were sold at a rate more than half as high again as those that had fallen. The same people were also more eager to add to shares that were down. Yet the losing shares they held on to did 3.4 percentage points worse over the following year, on average, than the ones they sold.
- Benefit: The study looked at every trade in 10,000 accounts at one US discount brokerage from 1987 to 1993. The proportion of profitable shares sold (PGR) was 0.148. The proportion of losing shares sold (PLR) was 0.098. The two differ by 0.050, with a t value above 35. By day, a share that has risen is more than 50% more likely to be sold than one that has fallen. The same accounts were also more eager to add to losing shares. The proportion of losing shares bought again was 0.135, against 0.094 for winning shares, with a t value of 19. The study also tested the idea that what you hold on to will come back. The result runs the other way: the sold winners produced an excess return over the following year on average 3.4 percentage points higher than the held losers. Excess return means the part above a market index. The author also worked out an example at the average. Selling 1000 dollars of shares, selling the losing one gave a return over the following year about 4.4% higher than selling the winning one. That 4.4% includes the tax saved by realising the loss sooner. The tax calculation follows the US system (United States, 1987 to 1993)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Odean T (1998). Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses? The Journal of Finance 53(5):1775-1798. <https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00072>
- Notes: This item is about whether to sell; item 15 (do not trade shares often) is about not acting all the time. The two do not conflict. If you set a rule in advance and follow it, you end up acting fewer times. Adding to the position to bring the average cost down is not wrong as arithmetic: buy a little more and the average cost is a little lower. ### 40. Insure only the losses you could not carry yourself; cover the ones you can carry with the emergency fund
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. What you have to do is list the large losses your household could run into and see which ones savings could not absorb. It takes half an hour.
- In plain language: Not all of the premium you pay comes back as claims; some covers the insurer's costs and profit. So what is worth buying is a loss that rarely happens but that the household could not pay for if it did: a serious illness, injuring someone while driving, the loss of the person the household depends on for income. Small losses that savings can absorb go to the emergency fund.
- Benefit: Of the premiums an insurer collects, only some is paid out as claims. The rest covers selling and running the business, and the company's profit. The regulator requires a company selling short-term personal health insurance to publish an all-in loss ratio on its website every six months. It is roughly the share of premiums paid out in claims, and if you want to know how much a company pays out you can look it up on its website. So for any one loss, insurance costs you a little money on average over the long run. What you get back is turning one large expense that could crush a household into a small amount a year. The smaller the loss and the more comfortably you can afford it, the worse the trade. By that standard, the few things this book has already written about are worth buying. One-year medical insurance: see item 20 in section 7 (one-year medical insurance). If you have a car, see item 26 in this section (third-party liability cover). If you travel abroad, see item 4 in section 21 (medical care abroad). If someone at home lives on your income, see item 41 in this section (term life insurance) (nationwide, since 2021)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: General Office of the former China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (中国银保监会办公厅) (2021). - Notes: It is graded C because no official figure exists for working out which insurance is good value, so what is given here is a way to decide. ### 41. If someone at home lives on your income, insure the earner first with term life insurance; do not insure the child first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Several hundred to several thousand yuan a year, depending on age, cover and the term (the author's rough estimate, no official figure). You have to disclose your health condition truthfully before taking out the policy, which takes half an hour.
- In plain language: Term life insurance covers a fixed number of years, and if the person dies in that time the household receives a sum of money. It is meant for the family that lives on your income, such as when a mortgage is still outstanding or the children are still young. If nobody depends on you, you do not need it. A child earns nothing, and the law also limits how much can be paid out on a death policy for a child.
- Benefit: The regulator defines life insurance as personal insurance with a person's lifespan as the subject, divided into term life, whole life, endowment and the like. Term life insurance makes payment of the sum insured conditional on the death of the insured, and the insurance period is a fixed number of years. Whole life insurance covers to the end of life. Accident insurance pays out only when an accident causes death or disability, does not pay on death from illness, and cannot replace life insurance. A contract whose payment condition is death requires the consent of the insured person and their agreement to the sum insured, and is otherwise void. A parent taking out death insurance on a minor child is not subject to that restriction, but the total sums paid out on the death of the child may not exceed the limit set by the regulator. The sum insured on life insurance is usually in the hundreds of thousands of yuan, so the benefit is graded large. There is no official figure for how much cover to buy. The author's method is: the unpaid mortgage, plus what the child costs until they reach adulthood, plus a few years of household expenses (nationwide, 2015 amendment)
- Evidence grade: C

- Notes: It is graded C because officially there are only definitions and limits, and "insure the adult first" is worked out from the question of whose income, if it stopped, the household could not survive without. When a child is hurt, the household does not lose an income. When the earner dies, the mortgage and the cost of the children are still there. ### 42. Regretting personal insurance of more than one year: cancel inside the cooling-off period and almost the whole premium comes back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Call the insurer's customer service line or go to the counter. It takes a bit over ten minutes. The hard part is finding a quiet half hour within 15 days of signing to read the contract.
- In plain language: Every personal insurance policy of more than one year has a cooling-off period. For a policy bought at a bank it is 15 days from the day you receive the policy, and cancelling in that period costs at most 10 yuan in handling charges. Cancel after the cooling-off period and you only get back what the policy says is refundable, which is usually less than you paid. When the insurer rings to ask whether you understood, do not say you did if you did not.
- Benefit: For personal insurance with an insurance period of more than one year, the regulator requires the policy to state the day the cooling-off period starts from and how many days it runs for. It also requires the policy to state what the policyholder may do during that period. The cooling-off period for long-term health insurance may not be shorter than 15 days. Insurance bought through a bank with an insurance period of more than one year always carries a 15-day cooling-off period in the contract. It runs from the day you receive the policy and sign for it in writing, counted in calendar days. The cover of such a policy has to carry this notice:「您在收到保险合同后15日内有全额退保（扣除不超过10元的工本费）的权利。超过15日退保有损失。」After the cooling-off period you can still cancel the contract at any time. The insurer must, within 30 days, refund the cash value of the policy (the part of it that can be refunded) as the contract provides. The financial regulator's risk warning says that when a long-term personal insurance policy is cancelled after the cooling-off period, the cash value is often lower than the total premium paid. For a new personal insurance policy of more than one year, the insurer must call you during the cooling-off period. The call has to confirm that you know about the loss on cancellation and about your rights during the cooling-off period (nationwide, since 2010 and 2019)

### 43. Before you sign at a bank counter, check whether what you are holding is a deposit or an insurance policy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend two more minutes on the front of the contract before signing.
- In plain language: A bank may sell insurance on its behalf, but it may not describe it as a deposit. The cover of an insurance contract must print "insurance contract" and the name of the insurance company in large type, and may not contain the words "deposit" or "savings". If the cover names an insurance company, the money is not a deposit, and cancelling after the cooling-off period loses you money. For someone over 65 buying at a bank, in principle only products with a certain return may be sold.
- Benefit: The rules state that a bank may not sell insurance mixed in with savings deposits, funds or bank wealth management products. It may not draw a simple analogy between the return on insurance and the return on a deposit, and may not describe an uncertain return as a guaranteed return. Insurance policy documents and promotional materials may not use the bank's name or logo, and may not contain wording such as "deposit", "savings" or "jointly launched with the bank". The cover of an insurance policy must print "insurance contract" in type of no less than 72 point, and the name of the insurance company in type of no less than second size. Three kinds of buyer may in principle be sold only products whose policy benefits are certain: a policyholder over 65 years old; a buyer of a regular-premium product who is over 60 years old; and someone whose declared annual income is below the local per-capita disposable income of residents. Policies for these three kinds of buyer must also go to the insurer for manual underwriting, and may not be issued automatically at the branch. An insurer's staff may not sell insurance inside a bank branch. Insurance sold by a bank with a term of more than one year has a 15-day cooling-off period, and cancelling after it has passed means losing money (nationwide, from 2019-10)

### 44. If you want to cancel a policy, go to the insurer yourself, not to a cancellation agent, and if you were misled complain on 12378
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Calling the insurer's customer service line or going to the counter yourself takes one or two more trips than handing it to an intermediary.
- In plain language: Most of the people advertising "full refund, no fee unless we succeed" online are illegal cancellation agents. They take three to five tenths of the money that comes back, and they want your identity card, your bank card and a verification code. Once the policy is cancelled, the cover is gone as well. If you think you were misled, start with the insurer's customer service line, and if you cannot settle it call 12378. The whole process is free.
- Benefit: In 2026-2 the National Financial Regulatory Administration, the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Public Security, the People's Bank of China and the China Securities Regulatory Commission jointly issued a risk warning. People offering "rights protection on your behalf" charge high service fees, and if you want to drop out halfway you could end up in court for a "breach". ### 45. Name a specific person as the beneficiary on the policy, and claim the money when something happens: within 5 years for life insurance and 2 years for other insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Fill it in once when you take out the policy; to change the beneficiary later you must notify the insurer in writing.
- In plain language: If no beneficiary is named, or the beneficiary has died first, the sum insured counts as the estate and has to be used to pay the dead person's debts first. If the beneficiary is written as "wife, [name]" and you later divorce, at the time it counts as though nothing was named. The people in your family need to know the policy exists: for life insurance you have 5 years from learning of the death to claim, and 2 years for other insurance.
- Benefit: The Insurance Law provides that the beneficiary is designated by the insured person or the policyholder. Where the policyholder designates, the insured person's consent is required. There are three situations in which the sum insured counts as the estate of the insured person and is distributed under the rules of inheritance. First, no beneficiary was designated, or the designation is unclear. Second, the beneficiary died before the insured person and there is no other beneficiary. Third, the beneficiary has lawfully lost or given up the right to the benefit and there is no other beneficiary. Where the beneficiary and the insured person died in the same event and the order cannot be established, the beneficiary is presumed to have died first. Money counted as the estate must first clear the taxes and debts of the dead person, and only what is left is divided. The Supreme People's Court's interpretation states that where the beneficiary is written as "name plus relationship" and the relationship has changed by the time it is claimed, that counts as no beneficiary having been designated. Changing the beneficiary without notifying the insurance company does not take effect against the insurer. The statute of limitations for claiming the money is 5 years for life insurance and 2 years for other insurance, both counted from the day you knew or should have known of the event. The sum insured on life insurance is usually in the hundreds of thousands of yuan, so the benefit is graded large (nationwide, 2015 amendment, in force since 2021)
