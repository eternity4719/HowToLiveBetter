# 19. Working, leaving, and work injuries

This section is about money: how the amounts are worked out, how the documents are signed, and how the deadlines run. The first three items cover the money you should be getting while you are still in the job. The next six cover leaving, and the seven after that cover occupational disease and work injuries. The money on a work injury is an order of magnitude larger than on a dismissal, and the deadlines are harder. Damage caused by an occupational disease cannot be put back. Three more items sit at the end of the section: what to do if you are treated badly at work, how to get a certificate of leaving, and how the income tax on severance works.

### 1. Overtime pay runs in three bands at 1.5, 2 and 3 times, and if it is not paid you complain to the labour inspectorate, with a further 50% to 100% added if the deadline passes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. As a matter of course, keep a copy of your clock-in records, the shift roster and any overtime approval form, and screenshot the messages where your manager assigns work in the work chat. The hard part is remembering to save them every month.
- In plain language: Ordinary overtime is paid at 1.5 times your wage. Overtime on a rest day, where you were not given time off in lieu, is paid at 2 times. Working on a statutory holiday such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labour Day or National Day is paid at 3 times, and time off in lieu cannot be used instead. If the company does not pay, complain to the labour inspectorate. If it still does not pay after being ordered to pay within a deadline, the employer has to pay an additional half to one times the arrears.
- Benefit: The normal working time set by the law is no more than 8 hours a day and, on average, no more than 44 hours a week. Ordinary overtime is generally no more than 1 hour a day. Where there is a special reason to work longer, it is no more than 3 hours a day, and no more than 36 hours in a month added up. Overtime pay falls into three bands. When ordinary working hours are extended, it is not less than 150% of your wage. When you work on a rest day and time off in lieu cannot be arranged, it is not less than 200%. When you work on a statutory holiday, it is not less than 300%. In the statutory-holiday band the article offers no option of substituting time off in lieu. Where the employer arranges overtime and does not pay for it, the labour administration orders it to pay within a deadline. If it still does not pay after the deadline, the labour administration orders it to pay the worker a further compensation of not less than 50% and not more than 100% of the amount payable, on top of the arrears (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2018). Labour Law, 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html> (published by the State Administration for Market Regulation) ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law, 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: Rest days and statutory holidays are counted separately. The statutory holidays are only the few fixed days in a year, such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labour Day and National Day. A long weekend created by shifting a day off is counted as a rest day, and is paid at 200%. 「The company requires approval for overtime」 and 「I stayed of my own accord」 do not mean the company can pay nothing, but they make your evidence harder to produce, so keep the assignment messages and the approval records. If the company wants to use the two special working-time systems, the comprehensive working-hours system and the irregular working-hours system, it needs the labour administration's approval first; it is not the company's own decision. For the time limit on arbitration of a dispute, see item 19 in section 8
### 2. Annual leave is 5, 10 or 15 days according to total length of service, and days you cannot take are paid at 300% of your daily wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Add up the years you have worked for all employers, and your social insurance contribution record is the proof. The hard part is refusing to sign the form 「voluntarily giving up annual leave」 when the company hands it to you.
- In plain language: When the years you have worked for all employers add up to at least 1 year and less than 10, you get 5 days of annual leave a year. At 10 years it is 10 days, and at 20 years it is 15 days. Changing jobs does not stop the total from carrying over. Where the employer cannot arrange the leave and you agree to do without it, those days are paid at 3 times your daily wage. Once the form 「voluntarily giving up annual leave」 is signed, only your ordinary wage is left.
- Benefit: You get annual leave after 12 months of continuous work. The number of days is set by your **cumulative** working time, adding up the years you have worked for all employers. At least 1 year and less than 10 gives 5 days, at least 10 years and less than 20 gives 10 days, and 20 years gives 15 days. Statutory holidays and rest days do not count towards annual leave. Where the employer genuinely cannot arrange the leave because of work and has your own agreement not to take it, the days you did not get are paid 「按照该职工日工资收入的 300% 支付年休假工资报酬」, as annual leave pay at 300% of that employee's daily wage. That 300% already includes the one share you would have got for a normal working day, so what you actually receive extra is 200%. Daily wage = your own monthly wage ÷ 21.75. The monthly wage is the average for the 12 months before this money is paid, with overtime pay taken out. If you joined the company during the year, it is pro-rated by the calendar days left in that company. On leaving, it is pro-rated and settled by how much of the year you have worked, and leave already taken in excess is not clawed back. Where the employer neither arranges the leave nor pays, an order to correct it within a deadline is issued. If it is not corrected after the deadline, a further compensation of the same amount is added to the annual leave pay (nationwide, in force from 2008-1-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2007). Regulations on Paid Annual Leave for Employees, 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm> ; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (人力资源和社会保障部) (2008). Measures for the Implementation of Paid Annual Leave for Enterprise Employees, 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Notes: Cumulative working time has to be added up across employers, so if you have changed jobs do not use only the length of service with your current employer. **Only where the employee 「因本人原因且书面提出不休」, does not take the leave for their own reasons and asks in writing, may the employer pay only the ordinary wage**, so do not sign the form 「voluntarily giving up annual leave」. If you take personal leave totalling more than 20 days and the employer does not dock your pay, you get no annual leave that year. A sick leave longer than the corresponding number of months also disqualifies you: less than 10 years of service means 2 months, 10 to 20 years means 3 months, and more than 20 years means 4 months. Home-visit leave, marriage and bereavement leave, maternity leave and the work-injury period of paid recovery do not count towards annual leave.
### 3. The probation period has a legal ceiling, the wage may not be below 80%, and it can only be agreed once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you sign, spend a few minutes matching the term written in the contract against the length of the probation period.
- In plain language: For a contract of 1 year or less, the probation period is at most 1 month. For 1 to 3 years it is at most 2 months. For 3 years or more it is at most 6 months. The same company may only put you on probation once, and a second probation after you have passed and been moved to another post is unlawful. The probation wage may not be below the lowest grade for the same post or 80% of the wage agreed in the contract, and it may not be below the local minimum wage.
- Benefit: For a contract of at least 3 months and less than 1 year, the probation period may not exceed 1 month. For at least 1 year and less than 3 years it may not exceed 2 months. For 3 years or more, and for an open-ended contract with no end date, it may not exceed 6 months. A contract that ends when one job is finished, or that runs for less than 3 months in total, may not have a probation period agreed at all. 「同一用人单位与同一劳动者只能约定一次试用期」, the same employer and the same employee may agree a probation period only once, so one company puts one person on probation one time only. Where the contract mentions only a probation period, that probation period does not stand, and the period stated is the term of the employment contract. The probation wage 「不得低于本单位相同岗位最低档工资或者劳动合同约定工资的百分之八十，并不得低于用人单位所在地的最低工资标准」, may not be below the lowest grade wage for the same post in the employer or eighty per cent of the wage agreed in the employment contract, and may not be below the minimum wage standard where the employer is located. Where the probation period agreed goes beyond the legal length and the person in fact worked, the employer has to pay a separate compensation, at the monthly wage standard applying after the probation period, for the excess period. An employer that wants to terminate the contract during probation may only do so in the cases the law lists, and it has to give the employee its reasons (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law, 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: The three commonest unlawful practices are these. First, a contract of only 1 year with a 3-month probation period agreed. Second, moving the person to another post after they have passed and running a second probation period. Third, 「no social insurance during probation」, because the obligation to enrol starts on the first day of work and has nothing to do with probation; for the employer's side of the obligation see item 16 in section 12. Being dismissed during probation does not mean getting nothing at all; it depends on whether the reason the employer gives is one of those the law allows. Where the termination is unlawful, the 2N in item 6 applies.
### 4. Work out N before a dismissal: one month's wage for each full year, and half a month for under six months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One calculation with a calculator, ten minutes.
- In plain language: The economic compensation due when you are dismissed is worked out like this: a full year of service gives one month's wage, six months or more but under a year counts as a full year, and under six months gives half a month. The monthly wage here is the average over the 12 months before you leave, and bonuses, allowances and subsidies all count in. Where the wage is above 3 times the local average monthly wage for the previous year, it is capped at 3 times, and the years of service count for at most 12 years.
- Benefit: The economic compensation set by the Labour Contract Law is the money the employer owes you when it dismisses you, and it is commonly called N. The method is 「每满一年支付一个月工资……六个月以上不满一年的，按一年计算；不满六个月的，向劳动者支付半个月工资」, that is, one month's wage for each full year of service, a full year counted where the service is six months or more but under a year, and half a month's wage where it is under six months. Where the monthly wage is above three times the local previous-year average monthly employee wage, the calculation is capped at three times, and the length of service counts for at most twelve years
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (in force from 2008-1-1), 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: 「月工资」 means the average wage over the twelve months before the employment contract is terminated. Bonuses, allowances and subsidies all count in, and it is not only the basic wage. This is the part a company most easily pushes down. Whether this money has to pay income tax, see item 18 in this section (the tax on severance)

### 5. If the company dismisses you without giving 30 days' notice, it has to pay one month's wage on top
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Where the company dismisses you because you can no longer work after an illness, because you cannot do the work, or because the circumstances have changed substantially, and does not give you 30 days' written notice, it has to pay one month's wage on top of N. That is what is usually called N+1. When you negotiate, put that one month down as a separate line, and do not let the company fold it into N.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law gives an employer terminating an employment contract under this article two ways of doing it. One is 「提前三十日以书面形式通知劳动者本人」, to notify the worker in person in writing thirty days in advance. The other is 「额外支付劳动者一个月工资后，可以解除劳动合同」, to pay the worker one month's wage and then terminate the contract. If they want you gone at once, they have to find that extra month's wage. That extra month is usually called severance in lieu of notice, and it is a separate amount counted outside N. The one month is worked out on your wage in the last month, while N is worked out on the average wage over the 12 months before you leave, so the two use different bases
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (in force from 2008-1-1), 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十六、三十九、四十、四十一、四十六、四十七、八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm> ; State Council (国务院) (2008). Regulations on the Implementation of the Labour Contract Law, 劳动合同法实施条例（国务院令第 535 号）第二十条：「用人单位依照劳动合同法第四十条的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_1107604.htm>
- Notes: That extra month only appears in the three situations in Article forty of the Labour Contract Law: after an illness or an injury not caused by work, where the medical period has ended and you can do neither your old job nor the one arranged for you; where you cannot do the work and still cannot after training or a transfer to another post; and where the objective circumstances at the time the contract was signed have changed substantially and negotiation fails. No other situation carries that month. An economic layoff (Article forty-one) only requires the company to explain the situation 30 days ahead to the trade union or to all employees. Where the company proposes termination and the two sides agree, it is N. Where you are dismissed for a fault of your own such as serious disciplinary breach, there is no compensation. Where the company terminates the contract unlawfully it is 2N. Where one of the three situations in Article forty applies, work out N and the 1 separately when you negotiate, and do not let the company count the extra month inside N

### 6. Where the company terminates the contract unlawfully, the compensation is twice the economic compensation standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Labour arbitration takes several months of waiting. The hard part is that you have to keep it moving for those months and cannot drop it halfway.
- In plain language: Where the company terminates the contract unlawfully, the compensation is the economic compensation standard doubled, which is what is usually called 2N. Whether the termination is unlawful depends on the particular circumstances. Ask a legal aid service or a lawyer first, and only then decide whether to negotiate with the company or go to arbitration.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law provides that where an employer terminates or ends an employment contract in breach of the Law, it 「应当依照本法第四十七条规定的经济补偿标准的二倍向劳动者支付赔偿金」, shall pay the employee compensation at twice the economic compensation standard set by Article forty-seven of the Law. The method is to work out one share on the economic compensation scale and then double it for you
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (in force from 2008-1-1), 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: This is what is usually called 2N. Whether a termination is unlawful depends on the particular circumstances. Consult a legal aid service or a lawyer first, then decide whether to negotiate or go to arbitration; the route is in section 7
### 7. Do not sign 「resignation for personal reasons」, because the moment you sign there is no N
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is standing up to the pressure to sign on the spot.
- In plain language: If you sign a statement that you are resigning for personal reasons, the economic compensation and the unemployment insurance benefit usually both fall away. Resigning of your own accord is not one of the cases in which compensation is payable, except where the employer is at fault in a way the law lists, such as unpaid wages or failure to enrol in social insurance. You do not have to sign on the day; sleep on it and decide in the morning.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law limits the cases in which economic compensation is payable to the few categories the law lists. An employee who leaves of their own accord is not among them, unless it is because of a legal fault by the employer such as unpaid wages or failure to enrol in social insurance. Once you have signed 「voluntary resignation」, the economic compensation and the unemployment insurance benefit usually both fall away
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007). Labour Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (in force from 2008-1-1), 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: The line companies use is 「sign first so the process can move on, the compensation comes separately」. If you want it, have it written into the agreement on the spot; if it is not written down, do not sign. Not signing on the day has no legal consequence, so sleep on it and decide in the morning
### 8. Before you leave, save your payslips, attendance records, employment contract, social insurance record and chat logs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Half an hour to save the material. The hard part is remembering to do it before you go.
- In plain language: Once the computer and the accounts are handed back, the payslips, attendance records, contract, social insurance record and chat logs are gone for good. At arbitration, what you earned and whether you worked overtime still rest on the material in your own hands. What you are saving is your own employment records; do not take away the company's source code or customer list.
- Benefit: Once the computer and the accounts are handed back, this material cannot be obtained any more. In arbitration the burden of proof leans towards the employer, but what you earned and whether you worked overtime still rest on the evidence in your own hands
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature ; for the route to enforce your rights see section 7
- Notes: What you save is your own employment records. Do not take the company's source code, customer list or technical documents while you are at it; for the risk of taking them see section 11. When you leave, ask the employer for a certificate of leaving as well, see item 17 in this section (certificate of leaving)


### 9. Before you take a post with dust, noise or chemicals, check whether the contract states the hazards; the three occupational health examinations are arranged and paid for by the employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. When you sign, read through the part of the contract on 「notification of occupational disease hazards」 clause by clause. Keep your own copy of every examination report. The hard part is asking in person and getting the documents into your hands in person.
- In plain language: When you sign, the employer must state truthfully in the contract what occupational disease hazards the post carries, how to guard against them, and what benefits attach. If it hides them from you, you can refuse to do the work, and the employer may not dismiss you for that. The three occupational health examinations, before you start, while you are in the post, and when you leave, are arranged and paid for by the employer. Without the examination on leaving, the employer may not terminate the contract.
- Benefit: The Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases provides that when the employment contract is signed the employer has to make four things clear: what occupational disease hazards this work may produce, what consequences follow, how to guard against them, and what benefits attach. The words of the article are 「如实告知劳动者，并在劳动合同中写明，不得隐瞒或者欺骗」, tell the worker truthfully, write it into the employment contract, and do not conceal or deceive. If after the contract is signed you are moved to harmful work the contract did not describe, the employer has to tell you truthfully again and agree the contract terms with you. Where the employer fails to do this, 「劳动者有权拒绝从事存在职业病危害的作业，用人单位不得因此解除与劳动者所订立的劳动合同」, the worker has the right to refuse work that carries occupational disease hazards, and the employer may not terminate the contract on that ground. There are three examinations in all: before starting, while in the post, and on leaving. 「职业健康检查费用由用人单位承担」, the cost of the occupational health examination is borne by the employer, so the employer pays and the results are given to you in writing. A person who has not had the examination before starting may not be put on work involving exposure to occupational disease hazards. A person whose physical condition is unsuited to this work, and who therefore has a contraindication, may not be put on the contraindicated work either. Where health harm related to the job is found, the person has to be moved off the post and properly placed elsewhere. **「对未进行离岗前职业健康检查的劳动者不得解除或者终止与其订立的劳动合同」, an employment contract may not be terminated or ended with a worker who has not had the occupational health examination before leaving.** On leaving you 「有权索取本人职业健康监护档案复印件，用人单位应当如实、无偿提供，并在所提供的复印件上签章」, have the right to ask for a copy of your own occupational health monitoring file, and the employer must supply it truthfully and free of charge and put its seal on the copy. The employer has to make a true photocopy of that occupational health monitoring file, give it to you free of charge, and seal the copy (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2018). Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases, 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (reprinted by the Beijing Municipal People's Government)
- Notes: Three questions at the interview tell you roughly whether the company is legitimate: does this post carry occupational disease hazards, does the contract say so, and who pays for the examination before you start. If they cannot answer, or say 「just start and we will see」, weigh the risk yourself. The examination on leaving is the one most often forgotten: without it, the employer may not terminate or end the employment contract. That examination is also the key document for applying for an occupational disease diagnosis later, and the same goes for the copy of the file, so get it into your hands before you go. An occupational disease is handled as a work injury, and the benefits are covered from item 11 onwards.

### 10. Damage from dust, noise and toxic chemicals is irreversible: the employer must supply protective equipment, and you may refuse work with no protection
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is wearing a particle mask, earplugs and goggles all the time, and refusing unprotected work, and the price of that is being called 「fussy」 by colleagues.
- In plain language: Pneumoconiosis, noise-induced deafness and chemical eye burns are all occupational diseases in law, and once the lungs or the hearing are damaged there is no way back. You have the right to know what hazards the post carries and the right to demand protective facilities and protective equipment from the employer. You do not have to do unprotected work. If the employer cuts your pay or dismisses you for that, it does not count.
- Benefit: From 2025-8-1 the occupational diseases in law are 12 major classes and 135 kinds, including 4 open clauses. The first class is occupational pneumoconiosis. It includes silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis and talc pneumoconiosis. It also includes cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, potter's pneumoconiosis, aluminium pneumoconiosis, welder's pneumoconiosis and foundry worker's pneumoconiosis, plus one open clause. Occupational diseases of the ear, nose, throat and mouth include noise-induced deafness and blast-induced deafness. The occupational eye diseases include chemical eye burns and photochemical keratitis. All of this damage is irreversible, and no treatment can repair the lungs or the hearing. The law gives you four rights you can use on the spot. First, to know what occupational disease hazards are present in this workplace, what consequences they cause, and what protective measures should be taken. Second, to require the employer to provide qualified protective facilities and personal protective equipment and to improve the working conditions. Third, to criticise, report and accuse practices that break the laws on the prevention and control of occupational diseases, and practices that endanger life and health. Fourth, to 「拒绝违章指挥和强令进行没有职业病防护措施的作业」, refuse unlawful instructions and orders to carry out work with no occupational disease protection; you do not have to take an instruction that breaks the rules or take unprotected work. And 「因劳动者依法行使正当权利而降低其工资、福利等待遇或者解除、终止与其订立的劳动合同的，其行为无效」, where an employer reduces a worker's wages or benefits, or terminates or ends the employment contract, because the worker lawfully exercised a legitimate right, that act is void. If it cuts your pay, cuts your treatment or dismisses you because you used these rights, it does not count (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委), Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (人力资源社会保障部), National Disease Control and Prevention Administration (国家疾控局) and All-China Federation of Trade Unions (中华全国总工会) (2024). Classification and Catalogue of Occupational Diseases, 职业病分类和目录（2025 年 8 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992843.htm> ; for the count of 12 major classes and 135 kinds in the new catalogue see National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) (2024). The new catalogue adjusts occupational diseases to 12 major classes and 135 kinds, 新版目录将职业病调整为 12 大类 135 种. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202412/content_6992838.htm> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2018). Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases, 职业病防治法（第三十九条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (reprinted by the Beijing Municipal People's Government)
- Notes: You have to recognise the right mask: only a particle respirator stops dust, and an ordinary gauze mask or a disposable surgical mask does not. If the employer does not supply earplugs for a noisy post, buy your own; a pair costs a few yuan, and lost hearing does not come back. 「Work two years, save up enough and leave」 does not hold with pneumoconiosis. Silicosis can appear many years after you stop working with dust, and it keeps getting worse. That is why the examination on leaving matters so much (see item 9 in this section, the three occupational health examinations arranged and paid for by the employer). The new catalogue also adds two classes. One is occupational musculoskeletal disorders, where the entry is carpal tunnel syndrome, limited to manufacturing workers who use the wrist repeatedly for long hours or do heavy work. The other is occupational mental and behavioural disorders, where the entry is post-traumatic stress disorder, limited to police, health workers, fire and rescue and other emergency response workers who take part in handling sudden incidents. For what to do when a chemical splashes on the skin, see item 21 in section 13.

### 11. If you are hurt at work or hit on the way to or from work, the first step is to get it recognised as a work injury, and if the employer does not report it you do
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, and one trip to the social insurance administration. You have to bring three documents: the application for recognition as a work injury, proof of the employment relationship, and a medical diagnosis certificate.
- In plain language: An injury at work counts as a work injury. Being hit on the way to or from work, where the main responsibility is not yours, also counts. But without recognition as a work injury, the medical costs, the wages paid during recovery and the disability allowance all fall away. The employer has to report within 30 days. If the employer does not, you, or your family, or the trade union can report it yourself within one year to the social insurance administration.
- Benefit: Without recognition as a work injury, the medical costs, the wages paid during recovery, which are called 停工留薪期工资, and the disability allowance all fall away. Where the employer does not report within 30 days, it pays the work-injury benefits due for that period out of its own pocket
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2010 revision). Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance (State Council Order No. 586), 工伤保险条例（国务院令第 586 号）. Article fourteen lists seven cases that 「应当认定为工伤」, must be recognised as a work injury, including 「（六）在上下班途中，受到非本人主要责任的交通事故或者城市轨道交通、客运轮渡、火车事故伤害的」, on the way to or from work, suffering harm in a traffic accident, urban rail accident, passenger ferry accident or train accident where the main responsibility is not the person's own. - Notes: The 1 year has to be taken seriously, so do not let it run out. So where the employer sits on the report until the period has passed, apply anyway and bring the evidence of the employer's delay. If the social insurance department does not accept the application, or you disagree with the conclusion, you can apply for an administrative reconsideration and you can also sue directly. Where the employer says it is not a work injury, the employer has to prove it. But you also have to keep material: photographs from the day, colleagues' contact details, the medical records and the attendance record. Article sixteen lists three cases that are not recognised: intentional crime, intoxication or drug use, and self-injury or suicide. ### 12. Do not believe 「getting to my desk counts as a work injury」: if you feel suddenly unwell, call 120 first, and do not hurry to clock in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Hurrying to the company when you feel suddenly unwell will not get you a single extra yuan. Being treated as a work injury requires the illness to begin during working hours and at the workplace, so falling ill on the way without arriving is not covered in the first place. Nor is it covered if rescue goes beyond 48 hours. The few dozen minutes you lose trying to meet the conditions are paid for out of your own chance of survival.
- Benefit: Being treated as a work injury requires that the person 「在工作时间和工作岗位」, during working hours and at the workplace, dies of sudden illness, or dies despite rescue within 48 hours. Delaying medical care in order to meet those two conditions costs you your own chance of being alive, and the compensation you buy with it may not even reach you
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance, article fifteen, paragraph one, item (one): 「在工作时间和工作岗位，突发疾病死亡或者在48小时之内经抢救无效死亡的」 is treated as a work injury ; article fourteen, item (six) only lists as a work injury the case of suffering harm in a traffic accident, urban rail accident, passenger ferry accident or train accident on the way to or from work where the main responsibility is not the person's own, and sudden illness on the way is not in it. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: Both conditions have to be met together: during working hours, and at the workplace. Falling ill on the way without arriving does not apply. If someone falls ill at the post and is taken home or to hospital and dies despite rescue within 48 hours, it can still apply. What counts is where the person was when the illness began, not where they finally died. Short videos put this the wrong way round when they say 「get to the company and there is money」. What changes the outcome is whether those few dozen minutes after the illness began were spent in hospital. And 48 hours is a hard line: rescue going beyond 48 hours does not count instead.
### 13. If the employer has not enrolled you in work-injury insurance, the benefits still exist, and the employer pays the same amounts in full
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. If the employer does not accept it, you have to go to arbitration or even to court, which runs from several months. The hard part is that you have to keep it moving for those months.
- In plain language: If the employer has not enrolled you in work-injury insurance, the benefits still exist. They are simply paid in full by the employer on the same items and the same standards, with nothing missing. Not enrolling adds a penalty on the employer: an order to make up the contributions, a late payment charge of five hundredths of one per cent per day, and if it is still not paid, a fine of 1 to 3 times the amount outstanding. 「The company bought no work-injury insurance, so it cannot pay」 is untrue.
- Benefit: The regulations say plainly that where an employer has not enrolled its employees in work-injury insurance and an employee suffers a work injury, that employer pays the money on the items and the standards the regulations set out. Which payments are due and how much each is exactly the same as when insurance is in force, and only the payer changes to the employer itself
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance, article sixty-two, paragraph two: 「依照本条例规定应当参加工伤保险而未参加工伤保险的用人单位职工发生工伤的，由该用人单位按照本条例规定的工伤保险待遇项目和标准支付费用。」 The first paragraph of the same article orders the employer to enrol and make up the contributions within a deadline, and says 「自欠缴之日起，按日加收万分之五的滞纳金；逾期仍不缴纳的，处欠缴数额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: 「The company bought no work-injury insurance for you, so it cannot pay」 is the commonest untrue sentence in this kind of case. Not enrolling only adds a penalty to the employer; your benefits do not shrink.
### 14. Once the injury is stable, get a labour capacity assessment; the disability grade converts straight into money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Going through the labour capacity assessment takes a stretch of waiting before and after.
- In plain language: Once the injury is stable, have the labour capacity assessed, and the grade converts straight into money. The one-off disability payment is worked out on your own wage: grade five 18 months, grade six 16 months, grade seven 13 months, grade eight 11 months, grade nine 9 months, grade ten 7 months. Before the assessment result comes out, do not sign a 「lump-sum settlement」 agreement.
- Benefit: The one-off disability payment converts the disability grade into a number of months of wage and is paid in a single sum: five 18 months, six 16 months and seven 13 months of the person's own wage, and eight 11 months, nine 9 months and ten 7 months of the person's own wage
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance, article twenty-one: 「经治疗伤情相对稳定后存在残疾、影响劳动能力的，应当进行劳动能力鉴定」, where after treatment the injury is relatively stable and there is a disability affecting the ability to work, a labour capacity assessment shall be carried out ; article twenty-two: 「劳动功能障碍分为十个伤残等级，最重的为一级，最轻的为十级」, loss of labour function is divided into ten disability grades, the most serious being grade one and the least grade ten ; article thirty-six, the benefits for grades five and six, being a one-off disability payment of 18/16 months of the person's own wage and a monthly disability allowance of 70%/60% of the person's own wage ; article thirty-seven, the benefits for grades seven to ten, being 13/11/9/7 months of the person's own wage. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Notes: At grades five and six the employment relationship is kept, the person is still an employee of that employer, and the disability allowance is paid monthly. For grades seven to ten, when the contract ends or the person proposes to terminate it, there are two other payments: the one-off work-injury medical subsidy, paid by the fund, and the one-off employment subsidy, paid by the employer, with the amount set by the province. Do not sign a 「lump-sum settlement」 agreement before the assessment result comes out.

### 15. There are three payments on a death at work, and you have to tell them apart: the funeral subsidy, the pension for dependants, and the one-off death benefit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, and one set of formalities.
- In plain language: A death at work brings three payments, and do not negotiate only one of them. The funeral subsidy is 6 months of the local previous-year average monthly employee wage. The pension for dependants is paid monthly on the deceased's own wage, at 40% a month for a spouse and 30% a month for each other dependant. The one-off death benefit is 20 times the previous year's national per-capita disposable income of urban residents, and that single figure applies nationwide.
- Benefit: The money on a death at work is in three parts. The first is the funeral subsidy, 6 months of the previous-year average monthly employee wage in the area where the person is insured, which is called the pooling region. The second is the pension for dependants, paid monthly on the deceased's own wage, at 40% a month for a spouse and 30% a month for each other dependant. The third is the one-off death benefit, 20 times the previous year's national per-capita disposable income of urban residents, and that single figure is the only one there is, with no regional difference. For a death at work in 2026, this payment is about 1,130,000 yuan (exact figure 1130040 yuan). The calculation is the previous year's national per-capita disposable income of urban residents, 56502 yuan, multiplied by 20.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance, article thirty-nine: 「（一）丧葬补助金为6个月的统筹地区上年度职工月平均工资；（二）供养亲属抚恤金按照职工本人工资的一定比例发给由因工死亡职工生前提供主要生活来源、无劳动能力的亲属。标准为：配偶每月40%，其他亲属每人每月30%，孤寡老人或者孤儿每人每月在上述标准的基础上增加10%……（三）一次性工亡补助金标准为上一年度全国城镇居民人均可支配收入的20倍。」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm> ; National Bureau of Statistics (国家统计局) (2026). - Notes: The one-off death benefit is the same nationwide, and the amount is updated each year with the figures the National Bureau of Statistics publishes. Do not accept the argument 「the local standard here is lower」 when you negotiate the compensation.
### 16. If you are bullied, insulted or obstructed at work for a long time, do not just endure it: write it down and keep the evidence, then take a complaint, a police report or arbitration according to what it was
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->

- In plain language: People who are bullied at work have about 60% more risk of a heart attack or stroke later, and the more they are bullied the higher the risk. People who are bullied are about twice as likely as others to have thoughts of suicide a few years on. Being sworn at and put upon is not a small thing that passes once you have endured it.
- Benefit: On health, three cohorts in Sweden and Denmark put together cover 79201 people in work aged 18 to 65. None of them had cardiovascular disease at the start, and the average follow-up was 12.4 years. 9% said they had been bullied at work in the past year. People who had been bullied had about 59% more risk of cardiovascular disease, meaning coronary heart disease and cerebrovascular disease, (HR 1.59, 95% CI 1.28 to 1.98). Those who had experienced violence at work had about 25% more (HR 1.25, 1.12 to 1.40). In both cases the more often it happened, the higher the risk. People who had been bullied were about 2 times as likely as others to have thoughts of suicide later (OR 2.05, 95% CI 1.08 to 3.89). In the other direction, people who already had thoughts of suicide were not more likely to be bullied later. Where an employer uses violence or threats to force you to work, you may terminate the contract at once without prior notice and still claim economic compensation. The employer has to take measures to prevent and stop anyone using their position or a superior-subordinate relationship to carry out sexual harassment. Victims may require the harasser to bear civil liability. Labour arbitration is to close the case within 45 days of accepting it, and a complicated case may be extended by at most a further 15 days.

### 17. When you leave, ask the employer for a certificate of leaving that states the term of the contract, the date you leave, your post and your years of service
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask for it in person before you go, or send one written message asking for it, and keep the record that you asked. The hard part is having to open your mouth about it when the relationship has already broken down. Where the employer puts it off and does not give it to you, you complain or apply for arbitration, which takes from a few tens of days to several months.
- In plain language: Whether you were dismissed, whether the contract simply ran out, or whether you resigned, the employer has to give you a certificate of leaving when you leave, and has to move your file and your social insurance over within 15 days. The certificate has to state the term of the contract, the date you leave, your post and how many years you worked there. You need it to register as unemployed. Where the employer does not give it to you, the labour department orders it corrected, and if you suffer loss because of it the employer has to pay it.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law provides that the employer must issue a certificate when the employment contract is terminated or ended. The article does not distinguish who proposed it: a contract that runs out and is not renewed, a resignation of your own accord, and a dismissal are all covered. The employer also has to complete the transfer formalities for your file and your social insurance relationship within 15 days. The implementing regulations set out four things the certificate must state: the term of the employment contract, the date of termination or ending, the post, and the years of service with that employer. The Regulations on Unemployment Insurance provide that an employee who becomes unemployed is to take the certificate issued by the employer and register as unemployed with the social insurance agency without delay. The unemployment insurance benefit runs from the day the registration is made. Where the employer does not issue a written certificate, the labour administrative department orders it corrected, and where it causes you loss the employer has to pay it. The law separately provides that where a new employer takes on somebody who has not yet terminated the contract with the old employer and causes the old employer loss, it bears joint and several liability. The certificate of leaving is your written evidence that you have already terminated the contract with the former employer (nationwide)

- Notes: The article does not tie issuing the certificate to handing over the work; what is paid only after the handover is the economic compensation. Where the employer puts it off, send one written message asking again. ### 18. When you get severance on leaving, look at the income tax first: the part within three times the local previous-year average employee wage is exempt, the excess is worked out separately, and it is not added to that year's wages
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Look up the local previous-year average employee wage, then spend a few minutes checking how the employer has deducted the tax. The individual income tax app shows the filed detail of every item of income.
- In plain language: The one-off payment you get for terminating the employment contract is not taxed on the part within three times the local previous-year average employee wage. The excess is taxed on its own, and is not added to that year's wages. Where the employer has folded the whole amount into that month's wages and taxed it, ask the employer first, then ring 12366 and ask the tax authority.
- Benefit: A document of the Ministry of Finance and the State Taxation Administration provides that one-off compensation income received where an individual terminates the employment relationship with an employer, which includes the economic compensation, living allowances and other allowances. The part within three times the local previous-year average employee wage is exempt from individual income tax. The part over three times is not added to that year's comprehensive income and is taxed separately on the comprehensive income tax rate table. On that table the part of the year's income up to 36000 yuan is taxed at 3%, the part from 36000 yuan to 144000 yuan at 10%, and the bands above that rise to 45%. As an example, if the local previous-year average employee wage is 100,000 yuan, three times that is 300,000 yuan. If you get 360,000 yuan of compensation, only the 60,000 yuan above that figure is taxed, which comes to 3480 yuan. The same 360,000 yuan worked out entirely on this table would come to 58080 yuan. This has applied since 2019-1-1, and no end date is written (nationwide)

- Notes: In the same document, the item on the single annual bonus carries an end date, which was later extended to 2027-12-31. The item on compensation for terminating the employment relationship carries no end date, and does not follow the bonus item. The document names only the economic compensation, living allowances and other allowances; it does not name severance in lieu of notice or the compensation for an unlawful termination. Whether those two count is not clear, so ring 12366 and ask the local tax authority. Which figure to use for the 「local previous-year average employee wage」 is also settled by the local tax authority. For how the compensation itself is worked out, see item 4 in this section (work out N before a dismissal), item 5 in this section (no 30 days' notice given) and item 6 in this section (the compensation for an unlawful termination).
