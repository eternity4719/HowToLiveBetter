# 31. What are the paths after eighteen

This section measures money and time, and refusing military service also bears on personal freedom. It is a map of the paths, not a manual for any one of them. It first sets the legal thresholds for the various paths side by side, and then goes through one by one the things you only need once you have chosen: what serving gives you, what openings there are besides the civil service examination for getting into an establishment, which two majors on the national entrance examination fill posts with a staffing quota attached, whether a qualification can still be completed later, what kind of company to look for if you want to work abroad, what to do about social insurance if you are not in any establishment, and whether there is money to borrow for starting out with part of the interest paid by the state.

There are far more paths open after eighteen than are written here. Only those with public documents at the national level, whose thresholds and benefits can be checked line by line, are collected here. Going into a state-owned enterprise, going into a factory, becoming a seafarer, working as a freelancer, going on to senior high school or university, these are all paths too. They simply have no single document to look up, and this book does not write conditions for them out of impression.

Which path you take is your own business. This section offers two things only: which document the threshold is written in, and which deadlines and qualifications on the way up expire and are then void. How to work out the question of studying or working, and how to choose a skill, see section 23. Overtime pay, work injuries and severance once you are in an establishment, see section 19. The capital for opening a shop or a company and how to get out, see section 12. Studying abroad, see section 32. Protection after losing your job, see section 7. None of these is repeated here.

### 1. Check the thresholds first: the age and education requirements for twelve paths are all fixed in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Ten minutes to go through them and see whether you can reach them.
- In plain language: The age and education thresholds for these twelve paths are all written into documents. Going out to work requires being at least 16 years old. Military service requires a man to be at least 18 years old, and this is relaxed to 24 for university graduates. Sitting the civil service examination requires being 18 to 35 years old with a college education or above. The self-study examination for a qualification and registering as an individual business are the two paths that look at neither your education nor your age.
- Benefit: For military service, look at the Military Service Law（兵役法）: 「年满十八周岁的男性公民，应当被征集服现役；当年未被征集的，在二十二周岁以前仍可以被征集服现役。普通高等学校毕业生的征集年龄可以放宽至二十四周岁，研究生的征集年龄可以放宽至二十六周岁」, that is, male citizens who have reached eighteen years old shall be called up for active service; those not called up in that year may still be called up before the age of twenty-two; for graduates of ordinary higher education institutions the call-up age may be relaxed to twenty-four, and for postgraduates to twenty-six. For the civil service examination, look at article eighteen of the Provisions on the Recruitment of Civil Servants《公务员录用规定》: the conditions for application include 「年龄为十八周岁以上，三十五周岁以下」, aged eighteen or above and under thirty-five, and 「具有大学专科以上文化程度」, having an educational level of college or above. These two may be adjusted appropriately with the approval of the civil service department at provincial level or above. Article nineteen of the same Provisions lists five kinds of person who may not apply. For example those who have been criminally punished for a crime, those dismissed from public office, and those listed as subjects of joint punishment for dishonesty. For firefighters, look at the Measures for the Recruitment of Firefighters into the National Comprehensive Fire and Rescue Force《国家综合性消防救援队伍消防员招录办法》. Article five: 「年龄为 18 周岁以上、22 周岁以下」, aged eighteen or above and under twenty-two, and 「具有高中以上文化程度」, having an educational level of senior high school or above. Article six: 「大学专科以上学历人员、解放军和武警部队退役士兵……年龄可以放宽至 24 周岁」, for people with a college education or above, and for soldiers discharged from the People's Liberation Army and the Armed Police Force, the age may be relaxed to twenty-four. For civilian posts in the armed forces, look at article nine of the Regulations on Civilian Personnel of the People's Liberation Army of China《中国人民解放军文职人员条例》, whose basic conditions include 「年满 18 周岁」, being at least 18 years old. Article nineteen of the same Regulations states that the highest age for first recruitment and appointment to posts at a lower grade is 35 years old. Posts at a lower grade are the entry-level posts. For public institutions, look at article eight of the Regulations on the Administration of Personnel of Public Institutions《事业单位人事管理条例》: 「事业单位新聘用工作人员，应当面向社会公开招聘」, a public institution recruiting new staff shall recruit openly from the public. For an individual business, look at the Regulations on the Promotion of the Development of Individual Businesses《促进个体工商户发展条例》: 「有经营能力的公民……依法登记为个体工商户的，适用本条例」, citizens with the capacity to run a business who register as an individual business in accordance with the law are governed by these Regulations. For the self-study examination, look at the Interim Regulations on Higher Education Self-Study Examinations《高等教育自学考试暂行条例》. Article three: 「中华人民共和国公民，不受性别、年龄、民族、种族和已受教育程度的限制，均可依照本条例的规定参加高等教育自学考试」, citizens of the People's Republic of China, without restriction as to sex, age, ethnicity, race or level of education already received, may all take part in higher education self-study examinations in accordance with these Regulations. Article twenty: 「各级各类全日制学校的在校生不得报考」, students enrolled on full-time courses at schools of every level and type may not apply. For publicly funded teacher training students, look at article two of the Implementation Measures for Publicly Funded Education of Students on the Bridging Programme for Undergraduates and Postgraduates at Normal Universities Directly under the Ministry of Education《教育部直属师范大学本研衔接师范生公费教育实施办法》. It recruits only at six normal universities directly under the Ministry. They are Beijing Normal University（北京师范大学）, East China Normal University（华东师范大学）, Northeast Normal University（东北师范大学）, Central China Normal University（华中师范大学）, Shaanxi Normal University（陕西师范大学）and Southwest University（西南大学）. The undergraduate and postgraduate stages are designed as one and assessed in stages: after finishing the undergraduate degree you go on to the postgraduate degree. Tuition and accommodation while on campus are borne by the central finance, and a living allowance is given as well. Article five of the same Measures: 「招收公费师范生实行提前批次录取」, publicly funded teacher training students are admitted in an advance batch, that is, they are admitted before the ordinary batch. For contract medical students, look at the Notice on Employment Placement and Performance Management of Rural Order-Directed Free-Trained Medical Students《关于做好农村订单定向免费培养医学生就业安置和履约管理工作的通知》. It states: 「在录取后、获得入学通知书前，须与培养学校和定向就业所在地的县级卫生健康行政部门……人力资源社会保障部门签署协议」, that is, after admission and before receiving the admission notice, the student must sign an agreement with the training school and with the county-level health administration department and the human resources and social security department of the place where the employment is directed. For working abroad, look at the Regulations on the Administration of Overseas Labour Cooperation《对外劳务合作管理条例》. Article five: engaging in overseas labour cooperation requires the approval of the commerce department in charge and the acquisition of a qualification to operate. Article seven, paragraph four: 「未依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格证书并办理登记，不得从事对外劳务合作」, no one may engage in overseas labour cooperation without having obtained the certificate of qualification for overseas labour cooperation in accordance with the law and completed registration. A company that has no such certificate but organises people to go abroad to work is itself breaking the law. Article eight, paragraph two: 「任何单位和个人不得以商务、旅游、留学等名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作」, no unit or individual may organise workers to go abroad to work in the name of business, tourism, study abroad or anything of the kind. Platform work and flexible employment have no statutory education threshold; for enrolling in insurance yourself and for the work-injury cover, see items 11 and 12 in this section. The Compulsory Education Law（义务教育法）「禁止用人单位招用应当接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年」, that is, prohibits employers from employing children and adolescents of school age who should be receiving compulsory education (nationwide)

### 2. Register for military service before 31 October in the year you turn eighteen; a conscript serves two years of active duty
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Registration is filled in once online and takes a few minutes. If you really do serve, what it costs is two years of full-time work, and you cannot do anything else at the same time in those two years.
- In plain language: Registering for military service is a legal duty. It is not signing up to join the army, and being registered does not mean you are going. A man who reaches eighteen in that year has to complete his registration online before 31 October. If you do not register, the county will order you to correct it within a set time, and if you still do not, it will compel you to perform military service and fine you. If you really do serve, a conscript serves two years of active duty.
- Benefit: For registration, look at the Provisions on Military Service Registration《兵役登记工作规定》. It states: 「县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关应当于每年 10 月 31 日前，组织本行政区域内当年 12 月 31 日以前年满 18 周岁的男性公民进行初次兵役登记」, that is, the military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities without districts and municipal districts shall, before 31 October each year, organise the initial registration for military service of the male citizens in their administrative area who will have reached 18 years old before 31 December of that year. The same Provisions also state: 「初次兵役登记主要采取网络登记的方式进行，由公民通过兵役登记网络平台自主完成」, the initial registration for military service is carried out mainly online, completed by the citizen independently through the military service registration online platform. For what happens if you do not register, look at article fifty-seven, paragraph one of the Military Service Law. That paragraph covers 「拒绝、逃避兵役登记的」, those who refuse or evade registration for military service. The penalty is 「由县级人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由县级人民政府强制其履行兵役义务，并处以罚款」, the county-level people's government orders correction within a set time; if the correction is not made by the deadline, the county-level people's government compels the person to perform military service and imposes a fine. The Military Service Law also states that 「义务兵服现役的期限为二年」, the period of active service for a conscript is two years, and that 「根据军队需要和本人自愿，可以征集年满十七周岁未满十八周岁的公民服现役」, depending on the needs of the armed forces and on the person's own wish, citizens who have reached seventeen but not eighteen may be called up for active service. For non-commissioned officers, 「服现役满十二年或者符合国家规定的其他条件的，由安置地的县级以上地方人民政府安排工作」, that is, where they have completed twelve years of active service or meet other conditions laid down by the state, the local people's government at county level or above in the place of resettlement will arrange work. And 「服现役满三十年或者年满五十五周岁或者符合国家规定的其他条件的，作退休安置」, where they have completed thirty years of active service, or have reached fifty-five years of age, or meet other conditions laid down by the state, they are retired under the resettlement arrangements. Arranging work means the government gives you a job. Retiring under the resettlement arrangements means being retired under the retirement rules. The Regulations on Military Conscription《征兵工作条例》state that 「优先保证普通高等学校毕业生和对政治、身体条件或者专业技能有特别要求的兵员征集」, priority is given to recruiting graduates of ordinary higher education institutions and to personnel with special requirements as to political, physical or professional skills. The same Regulations also state that 「应征公民为普通高等学校的全日制在校生、应届毕业生的，可以在入学前户籍所在地或者学校所在地应征」, a citizen called up who is a full-time student or a graduating student at an ordinary higher education institution may be called up before enrolment in the place of household registration or the place where the school is (nationwide)

- Notes: Registration and being called up are two different things, and the cost differs by a long way. The set of penalties that stops you leaving the country within two years only applies to people who back out after being called up, see item 3 in this section (refusing military service). Women are not required to register initially, and are called up according to the needs of the armed forces. Soldiers moving on to a military academy, and good soldiers being promoted to officers, have their conditions written into the internal rules of the armed forces, which cannot be looked up from outside. The Military Service Law says only two things: active-duty officers are selected and recruited from among 「表现优秀的现役士兵」, serving soldiers with outstanding performance, and the military academies are not subject to the call-up age limit when they recruit students. This book does not give the specific score lines and the quotas.
### 3. If you refuse to serve after being called up, you cannot leave the country or go back to school within two years, and you cannot join the civil service or a state-owned enterprise
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The cost is that before you sign up you have to settle in your own mind whether you are going.
- In plain language: If you sign up and then refuse to go and serve, there is a list of consequences. You cannot be recruited as a civil servant, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions cannot hire you either. You cannot leave the country for two years, you cannot go up a level of education, and you cannot go back to school to continue your studies. You also get put on the list of subjects in serious breach of national defence obligations. So "sign up first and come back if it does not work" is an expensive idea.
- Benefit: Item two of paragraph one of article fifty-seven of the Military Service Law is 「应征公民拒绝、逃避征集服现役的」, a citizen called up who refuses or evades being called up for active service. The penalty is likewise 「由县级人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由县级人民政府强制其履行兵役义务，并处以罚款」, the county-level people's government orders correction within a set time; if the correction is not made by the deadline, it compels the person to perform military service and imposes a fine. Paragraph two of the same article: 「有前款第二项行为，拒不改正的，不得录用为公务员或者参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的工作人员，不得招录、聘用为国有企业和事业单位工作人员，两年内不准出境或者升学复学，纳入履行国防义务严重失信主体名单实施联合惩戒。」, that is, a person who does the second item of the preceding paragraph and refuses to correct it may not be recruited as a civil servant or as a worker managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国公务员法）, may not be recruited or appointed as a worker of a state-owned enterprise or a public institution, may not leave the country or go back to school to continue their education within two years, and is put on the list of subjects in serious breach of national defence obligations for joint punishment. Joint punishment means several departments work from the same list and enforce the restrictions above together. Article fifty-eight applies the same set of sanctions to people who have already enlisted. Where a serviceman refuses to perform his duties or leaves the unit with the purpose of evading military service, the consequences depend on how it is dealt with. Where a person is struck off the rolls of the army, has military rank stripped, or is held criminally responsible in accordance with law, the sanctions in paragraph two of article fifty-seven apply. Among these, being struck off the rolls also carries a fine (nationwide, in force from 2021-10-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Military Service Law（兵役法）（2021 revision, articles fifty-seven and fifty-eight）. <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm>（published by the Ministry of National Defense）
- Notes: What is counted here is personal freedom and the right to be employed, not money. This list of sanctions applies to only two kinds of person. One is "a citizen called up who refuses or evades being called up for active service". The other is someone who, after enlisting, is struck off the rolls, has military rank stripped, or is held criminally responsible. Merely failing to register for military service is not in it; military service registration is item 2 in this section. The fine presupposes that the person "refuses to correct it", and it is the county-level people's government that determines it and imposes it. How much the fine is varies from place to place and this book does not give it. Whether you go is yours to decide, but decide it before you sign up.

### 4. The two most solid benefits for university students who enlist: tuition reimbursed up to 20000 yuan a year, and the place kept for two years with a special plan for the postgraduate entrance examination
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. After being discharged you hand in the papers the school asks for and apply, which means one or two trips.
- In plain language: If students still at university, recent graduates and new entrants enlist, the state pays the tuition for those years of study, reimbursed against what you actually paid: up to 20000 yuan a year for undergraduate and junior-college students, and up to 25000 yuan a year for postgraduate students. Where the tuition was already low, you only get back what you actually paid. Your place is kept for two years. For the postgraduate entrance examination there is a separate special plan that only takes retired university-student soldiers.
- Benefit: A 2024 notice from the Ministry of Finance and three other departments says that college students going into military service get three things: a tuition reimbursement, repayment of the national student loan used for tuition, and a tuition reduction. Repayment of the loan means the state pays that loan off for you. The standards for all three are 「adjusted accordingly to a maximum of no more than 20000 yuan per undergraduate or junior-college student per year, and no more than 25000 yuan per postgraduate student per year」. The new standards apply from the autumn term of 2024. How much you actually get depends on this sentence: 「the higher of the tuition the student actually pays and the national student loan received (including the principal and all the interest accrued before it is repaid in full)」. The sentence after it is 「any part above the standard shall not be reimbursed, repaid or reduced」. So what you get each year is the smaller of 「the higher of the actual tuition and the student loan」 and the cap. If your tuition is 5000 yuan a year, you get 5000 yuan back. How many years you get is counted by the years of study, not by the years of service. The 2019 measures on the administration of student aid funds say: 「the years of study already completed before enlistment are the years for tuition reimbursement or repayment of the national student loan; the remaining required years of study completed after being discharged and returning are the years for tuition reduction」. Graduates who enlist get the tuition for the whole length of their course. On keeping your place, see the Law on the Protection of Veterans《退役军人保障法》. It says: 「students already admitted to an ordinary higher education institution before enlistment, or already studying in one, keep their admission qualification or their student status during their service, and are allowed to enrol or return within two years of discharge, and may transfer to another subject in the same institution according to the state's rules」. The same law also says: 「higher education institutions may, in line with the state's overall arrangements, recruit veterans by separate plans and separate admissions」. For the postgraduate entrance examination, see the 2026 notice from the General Office of the Ministry of Education. The plan is called the 「special plan for retired university-student soldiers」. The notice says: 「issued separately and used exclusively within the total scale of the national postgraduate admissions plan; it shall not be turned into the ordinary plan or any other special plan. The admitting institution decides for itself … its requirements for the initial examination results (generally no higher than those for candidates under the ordinary plan for the corresponding subject) … candidates with longer service are admitted first where the conditions are equal」. Issued separately and used exclusively means this slice of the places is set aside on its own and can only be used to recruit retired university-student soldiers. In 2026 the plan is run by 532 ordinary higher education institutions (nationwide)

### 5. After being discharged the government arranges work in only six situations; those who take up self-employment must report within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Take your certificate of discharge and go to the veterans affairs department in the place of resettlement once.
- In plain language: "Serve and you get a post assigned" stopped being the general case long ago. Now there are only six situations in which the government arranges work, and a conscript who serves two years and is discharged is in most cases none of them. The category where the government does not arrange work is called self-employment, and it has a hard deadline: you must report within 30 days of leaving active service. If you go more than 30 days without a legitimate reason, you are treated as having given up the resettlement treatment.
- Benefit: Article twenty-five of the Regulations on the Resettlement of Veterans《退役军人安置条例》lists six situations in which the people's government of the place of resettlement arranges work. The wording is: 「（一）军士服现役满 12 年的；（二）服现役期间个人获得勋章、荣誉称号的；（三）服现役期间个人荣获三等战功、二等功以上奖励的；（四）服现役期间个人获得一级表彰的；（五）因战致残被评定为 5 级至 8 级残疾等级的；（六）是烈士子女的」, that is: (one) non-commissioned officers who have completed 12 years of active service; (two) those who personally received a medal or an honorary title during their active service; (three) those who personally won third-class battle merit or a second-class merit or above during their active service; (four) those who personally received a first-level commendation during their active service; (five) those whose disability from injury in battle was assessed at level 5 to level 8; (six) the children of martyrs. The same Regulations also state: 「安置地人民政府应当在接收退役军士和义务兵的 6 个月内完成安排退役军士和义务兵工作的任务」, that is, the people's government of the place of resettlement shall finish the task of arranging work for discharged non-commissioned officers and conscripts within 6 months of taking them over. An employer 「应当按照规定与其签订不少于 3 年的中长期聘用合同或者劳动合同。其中，企业接收军龄 10 年以上的退役军士的，应当与其签订无固定期限劳动合同」, that is, shall sign with them a medium or long term employment contract or labour contract of not less than 3 years as required. Among these, where an enterprise takes on a discharged non-commissioned officer with 10 or more years of service, it shall sign an open-ended labour contract with them. An open-ended contract is one with no end date on it. While waiting to be given work, 「按照当地月最低工资标准逐月发放生活补助」, that is, a living allowance is paid month by month at the local monthly minimum wage standard. Where a person has not been placed in a post as required, and it is not for reasons of their own, 「按照不低于本单位同等条件人员平均工资 80% 的标准，逐月发放生活费直至上岗为止」, that is, a living allowance is paid month by month at a standard of not less than 80% of the average wage of people on equivalent terms in the same unit, until they take the post. The situations in which work may not be arranged as resettlement include being expelled from the Communist Party of China and having been criminally punished. For the deadline for self-employment, look at article fifty-one: 「自主就业的退役军士和义务兵，应当自被批准退出现役之日起 30 日内，到安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门报到。无正当理由不按照规定时间报到超过 30 日的，视为放弃安置待遇。」, that is, discharged non-commissioned officers and conscripts taking up self-employment shall, within 30 days of the date they are approved for discharge from active service, report to the veterans affairs department of the people's government of the place of resettlement. Where they do not report by the prescribed time, without legitimate reason, for more than 30 days, this is treated as having given up the resettlement treatment (nationwide, in force from 2024-9-1)

### 6. Three accounts after being discharged: the one-off discharge pay, service years counted as years of employment, and 20000 yuan off your tax a year if you go into business yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When you are discharged the armed forces transfer your social insurance relationship and your money out to you. You then go to the social insurance agency in the place of resettlement (the counter that handles social insurance) to continue it.
- In plain language: After being discharged there are three accounts. The first is the one-off discharge pay for those taking up self-employment, paid according to the years of active service, and increased where the person was decorated. The second is that the years you served do not count as a gap in social insurance; service counts as years of employment, and the contribution years are added together with those before you enlisted and after you were discharged. The third is tax: if you open a shop or run a business yourself, for 3 years you can have up to 20000 yuan less tax a year.
- Benefit: For the first, look at the Regulations on the Resettlement of Veterans《退役军人安置条例》. It states: 「对自主就业的退役军士和义务兵，根据其服现役年限发放一次性退役金」, that is, a one-off discharge pay is paid to discharged non-commissioned officers and conscripts taking up self-employment on the basis of their years of active service. Who pays it: 「由中央财政专项安排，具体标准由国务院退役军人工作主管部门、中央军事委员会政治工作部门会同国务院财政部门……确定，并适时调整」, that is, it is set aside by the central finance, and the specific standard is determined and adjusted from time to time by the veterans work department of the State Council together with the political work department of the Central Military Commission and the financial department of the State Council. The increases for decoration come in five bands. A medal or an honorary title adds 25%; first-class battle merit or a first-level commendation adds 20%. Second-class battle merit, first-class merit or a second-level commendation adds 15%. Third-class battle merit or second-class merit adds 10%, and fourth-class battle merit or third-class merit adds 5%. The same Regulations also state: 「退役军人未及时就业的，可以依法向户口所在地人力资源社会保障部门申领失业保险待遇，服现役年限视同参保缴费年限，但是以退休、供养方式安置的退役军人除外」, that is, a veteran who has not found work in good time may apply in accordance with the law to the human resources and social security department where their household registration is for unemployment insurance benefits, and years of active service count as years of contribution, except for veterans resettled under retirement or dependants' support. Counting years as contribution years means the years in which you paid nothing are counted as though you had. For the second, look at the Law on the Protection of Veterans《退役军人保障法》: 「退役军人服现役年限计算为工龄，退役后与所在单位工作年限累计计算」, that is, years of active service are counted as years of employment and, after discharge, are added to the years worked at the unit. Years of employment means the years spent working, so the years of service and the years after discharge carry on adding up. The same law also states that years of active service and the years of contribution to pension, medical and unemployment insurance before enlistment and after discharge are 「依法合并计算」, that is, lawfully combined, and that 「退役的军士和义务兵服现役经历视为基层工作经历」, that is, the active service of discharged non-commissioned officers and conscripts counts as work experience at the grassroots level. The Law on Social Insurance for Servicemen《军人保险法》: 「军人服现役年限视同职工基本医疗保险缴费年限，与入伍前和退出现役后参加职工基本医疗保险的缴费年限合并计算」, that is, years of active service count as years of contribution to the basic medical insurance for employees, and are combined with the years contributed before enlistment and after discharge. For the third, look at Announcement No. 14 of 2023 from the Ministry of Finance, the State Taxation Administration and the Ministry of Veterans Affairs. The announcement deals with discharged soldiers taking up self-employment who run individual businesses: 「在 3 年（36 个月，下同）内按每户每年 20000 元为限额依次扣减其当年实际应缴纳的增值税、城市维护建设税、教育费附加、地方教育附加和个人所得税。限额标准最高可上浮 20%」, that is, within 3 years (36 months, the same applies below) a limit of 20000 yuan per household per year is deducted in order from the value-added tax, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharge, local education surcharge and individual income tax they should actually have paid that year. The limit may be raised by up to 20%. The deduction works by coming down the list of those taxes in order for that year until the limit is used up; it is not money paid to you. Where an enterprise hires them, 「定额标准为每人每年 6000 元，最高可上浮 50%」, that is, the fixed amount is 6000 yuan per person per year, and may be raised by up to 50%. The period of operation runs from 2023-1-1 to 2027-12-31. Where 3 years have not run out at the end, it can continue to be enjoyed until they are up (nationwide)

### 7. Getting into an establishment is not only the civil service examination: two years of grassroots service let you apply for the targeted recruitment quota of about 10% a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The cost is two years of grassroots service in a township. What you get for those two years is a fiscal subsidy, not a market wage, and the hard part is lasting the two years.
- In plain language: If you want into an establishment, the civil service examination is not the only route. Serve the full two years on a grassroots service programme of the kind called "Supporting and Assisting Agriculture, Supporting and Assisting Rural Revitalisation, Supporting and Assisting Border Areas and Supporting and Assisting Poverty Alleviation"「三支一扶」, pass the assessment, and you can apply for a targeted recruitment quota that is open only to people who have done that. Each province has to set aside around 10% of its civil service recruitment plan for these people each year. Sit the postgraduate entrance examination within three years and 10 points are added to your first-round total. The cost is that for those two years you are on a fiscal subsidy.
- Benefit: The notice for the fourth round of the "Three Supports and One Assistance" plan「三支一扶」says "the period of service is two years"「服务期为两年」. Targeted recruitment and special recruitment mean this part of the quota is examined only among people who have done grassroots service; nobody else can apply. Being admitted without examination means sitting no further exam and going straight into the bachelor's degree course. The notice also says: 「参加『三支一扶』计划前无工作经历的人员期满且考核合格的，两年内在参加机关和企事业单位考录（招聘）、自主创业、落户、升学等方面可同等享受应届毕业生相关政策。『三支一扶』人员在基层服务年限计算为工龄，其参加工作时间按其到基层报到之日起算」, that is, a person with no work experience before joining the "Three Supports and One Assistance" plan who completes the period and passes the assessment may, for two years, enjoy on the same terms the policies for fresh graduates as regards applying to enter an organ or a public or commercial enterprise, starting a business of their own, settling a household registration, and going up a level of education. The years of grassroots service count as years of employment, and the time counted as their working life runs from the date they reported to the grassroots post. The central finance subsidy is 30,000 yuan each per year in the western region, 24,000 yuan in the central region and 12,000 yuan in the eastern region. The opinion of the General Offices of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council states: 「参加基层服务项目前无工作经历的人员服务期满且考核合格后 2 年内，在参加机关事业单位考录（招聘）、各类企业吸纳就业、自主创业、落户、升学等方面可同等享受应届高校毕业生的相关政策」, that is, a person with no work experience before joining a grassroots service programme who completes the period and passes the assessment may, for 2 years, enjoy on the same terms the policies for fresh university graduates as regards applying to enter an organ or public institution, taking up employment with any kind of enterprise, starting a business of their own, settling a household registration, and going up a level of education. ### 8. Special-post teachers who finish the three-year service period, pass the assessment and want to stay should be put on the establishment in good time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The cost is three years teaching in a rural compulsory education school, and before you start you have to pass the teacher qualification examination. The hard part is lasting those three years.
- In plain language: The special-post plan is one of the few routes that writes "admitted to the establishment when the period ends" into the rules. The conditions are an undergraduate degree or above, mainly in teacher-training subjects, an age of not more than 30, and the matching teacher qualification certificate. The period of service is three years, and for those three years the pay and the treatment are the same as those of established teachers in local public schools. Where someone passes the assessment at the end of the three years and wants to stay, the document requires them to be put on the establishment in good time.
- Benefit: The 2024 special-post plan notice gives the recruitment conditions as: 「要求本科及以上学历，以师范类专业为主，小学阶段可适当招聘师范高等专科学校毕业生」, that is, an undergraduate degree or above is required, mainly in teacher-training subjects, and at primary stage graduates of higher vocational teacher-training colleges may be recruited as appropriate; 「年龄不超过 30 周岁」, aged not more than 30; and the matching teacher qualification certificate must be held. And: 「支持鼓励符合条件的退役军人到中小学任教，可综合考虑服役年限等因素对退役军人相应放宽年龄限制」, that is, veterans who meet the conditions are supported and encouraged to teach in primary and secondary schools, and the age limit may be relaxed for them by taking factors such as length of service into account. ### 9. Firefighters and civilian posts in the armed forces are two more openly advertised routes, and the thresholds are lower than for the civil service
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. From applying to being appointed you go through a medical examination, a political assessment, a physical fitness test, a psychological test and an interview, over several months. The hard part is that each of these stages washes people out.
- In plain language: Neither route has the "college education or above" threshold. Firefighters are recruited from people aged 18 to 22 with an educational level of senior high school or above, and people with a college education or above and discharged soldiers may have the age relaxed to 24. Civilian posts in the armed forces require only that you be at least 18 years old, and the highest age for first recruitment to entry-level posts is 35. What both routes share is that the political assessment and the medical examination wash people out.
- Benefit: For firefighters, look at the Measures for the Recruitment of Firefighters into the National Comprehensive Fire and Rescue Force《国家综合性消防救援队伍消防员招录办法》. The basic conditions in article five include 「年龄为 18 周岁以上、22 周岁以下」, aged 18 or above and under 22, and 「具有高中以上文化程度」, having an educational level of senior high school or above. Article six: 「大学专科以上学历人员、解放军和武警部队退役士兵、具有 2 年以上灭火救援实战经验的政府专职消防队员和政府专职林业扑火队员，年龄可以放宽至 24 周岁；对消防救援工作急需的特殊专业人才，经应急管理部批准年龄可以进一步放宽，原则上不超过 28 周岁」, that is, for people with a college education or above, for soldiers discharged from the People's Liberation Army and the Armed Police Force, and for government full-time firefighters and government full-time forest fire-fighting teams with more than 2 years of practical experience in fire fighting and rescue, the age may be relaxed to 24; for special professional talent urgently needed for fire and rescue work, with the approval of the Ministry of Emergency Management the age may be relaxed further, in principle to no more than 28. Article seven: 「消防员面向社会公开招录，主要从本省级行政区域常住人口中招录，根据需要也可以面向其他省份招录」, that is, firefighters are recruited openly from the public, mainly from among the resident population of the province-level administrative region, and according to need also from other provinces. Article nine sets out the recruitment procedure as 「宣传动员、组织报名、资格审查、体格检查、政治考核、体能测试和岗位适应性测试、心理测试和面试、公示、录用」, that is, publicity and mobilisation, organising applications, qualification review, physical examination, political assessment, physical fitness test and job suitability test, psychological test and interview, publication of the results, and appointment. For civilian posts in the armed forces, look at article eighteen of the Regulations on Civilian Personnel of the People's Liberation Army of China《中国人民解放军文职人员条例》: 「军队实行公开招考、直接引进、专项招录相结合的文职人员招录聘用制度」, that is, the armed forces operate a system for the recruitment and appointment of civilian personnel that combines open competitive recruitment, direct introduction and special recruitment. That is three lines: recruiting people through an open examination, bringing in talent who already exists directly, and recruiting for a specific group on purpose. The basic conditions in article nine include 「年满 18 周岁」, being at least 18 years old; 「符合军队招录聘用文职人员的政治条件」, meeting the political conditions for the armed forces to recruit and appoint civilian personnel; and 「符合岗位要求的文化程度、专业水平和工作能力」, having the educational level, professional standard and working ability the post requires. Article nineteen states that the highest age for first recruitment and appointment to posts at a lower grade is 35. The same Regulations also state that 「文职人员岗位应当优先招录聘用符合条件的退役军人」, that is, civilian posts should give priority in recruitment and appointment to veterans who meet the conditions (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and Ministry of Emergency Management (2021). - Notes: The Regulations on Promoting the Employment and Entrepreneurship of Veterans《退役军人就业创业促进条例》, in force from 2026-8-1, set out two more things. First, a certain proportion of firefighter recruitment has to be set aside for discharged non-commissioned officers and conscripts. Second, when recruiting police auxiliary staff, veterans are to be given priority under equal conditions. Firefighting is a post in a disciplined service, so weigh up the occupational risk before you go in.
### 10. A qualification is not a one-off purchase: the self-study examination has no limit on age or on the education you already have, and the adult entrance examination and the open university can both complete it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Several hundred yuan a year in registration and course materials. The cost is long-term self-study, or going to classes in your spare time; the shortest part-time route to a bachelor's degree, studied while working, takes two and a half years. The hard part is keeping it up over a long period.
- In plain language: If you did not get into education at eighteen, there are still three proper channels: the self-study examination, the adult entrance examination and the open university. The self-study examination has no limit on age and does not look at how far you got before, but students on full-time courses may not apply. The adult entrance examination requires you already to hold a certificate from senior high school or from a junior college. All three routes are slow, but none of them looks at how you did at eighteen.
- Benefit: For the self-study examination, look at the Interim Regulations on Higher Education Self-Study Examinations《高等教育自学考试暂行条例》. Article three: 「中华人民共和国公民，不受性别、年龄、民族、种族和已受教育程度的限制，均可依照本条例的规定参加高等教育自学考试」, that is, citizens of the People's Republic of China, without restriction as to sex, age, ethnicity, race or level of education already received, may all take part in higher education self-study examinations in accordance with these Regulations. Article twenty: 「各级各类全日制学校的在校生不得报考」, students enrolled on full-time courses at schools of every level and type may not apply. Article twenty-five sets out the three conditions for getting the certificate: passing and passing all the courses, completing the graduation thesis or the practical assignment, and passing the assessment of moral character. Do all of these and you may obtain the certificate. This article also states that 「获得专科（基础科）或本科毕业证书者，国家承认其学历」, that is, the state recognises the qualification of those who obtain a junior college (foundation) or undergraduate certificate. Article thirty-two sets out the pay and treatment of those who obtain the certificate: 「非在职人员录用后，与普通高等学校同类毕业生相同」, that is, on appointment, those who are not in work are treated the same as comparable graduates of ordinary higher education institutions. Article twenty-one of the Law on Higher Education《高等教育法》: 「国家实行高等教育自学考试制度，经考试合格的，发给相应的学历证书或者其他学业证书」, that is, the state operates a system of higher education self-study examinations, and those who pass are issued with the corresponding certificate of qualification or other certificate of attainment. For the adult entrance examination, look at the 2025 Measures for the Enrolment of Adult Higher Education Institutions nationwide《2025 年全国成人高校招生办法》. Those eligible to apply are 「国家承认学历的各类高、中等学校在校生以外的从业人员和社会其他人员」, that is, people who work and other people in society, other than students enrolled at recognised higher and secondary schools. 「报考高起本或高起专的考生应高级中等教育学校毕业或者具有同等学力。报考专升本的考生必须是已取得……专科毕业证书、本科结业证书或以上证书的人员」, that is, candidates applying to start from the first year of the undergraduate or the first year of the junior college course should have graduated from a senior secondary education school or have the equivalent academic ability; candidates applying to upgrade to a bachelor's degree must already hold a junior college certificate, an undergraduate completion certificate or a higher certificate. 「脱产最短学习时间为：高起本四年、高起专和专升本两年，非脱产最短学习时间为：高起本五年、高起专和专升本两年半」, that is, the shortest period of study on a full-time basis is four years for starting the undergraduate course, and two years for the junior college course and for upgrading to a bachelor's degree; the shortest period of study while working is five years for starting the undergraduate course, and two and a half years for the junior college course and for upgrading to a bachelor's degree. Studying full-time means leaving work and studying all day; studying while working means not leaving work. For the open university, look at the Opinion on Running Open Universities Well《教育部关于办好开放大学的意见》. It states: 「开放大学实行注册入学，学生修完课程并获得相应学分，即可获得课程证书，修满规定的学分并达到相关要求，即可获得相应的学历与非学历证书，符合学位授予条件的应授予相应学位」, that is, open universities enrol by registration; once a student has completed the courses and earned the corresponding credits they receive a certificate for the courses, and once they have earned the prescribed credits and met the relevant requirements they receive the corresponding certificate of qualification and non-qualification certificate, and where they meet the conditions for a degree the corresponding degree is awarded (nationwide)

### 11. Not being in an establishment means flexible employment: you enrol for pension and medical insurance yourself where you work, and the household registration restriction has already been lifted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Pension and medical insurance for you to pay in full, calculated on the local contribution base, thousands of yuan a month. The contribution base is the reference figure used to work out the premium. This is the fixed cost that flexible employment most easily leaves out, and the hard part is paying it on time every month.
- In plain language: Not being in an establishment does not mean social insurance does not exist; it only means nobody pays your half. The documents require each place to lift the household registration restriction on flexible workers enrolling in the place where they work, with the very largest cities allowed to lift it step by step. So do not read "freedom" as meaning you need not think about social insurance. If you stop paying, your pension and your medical insurance reimbursement are both worked out under the rules for a break in contributions.
- Benefit: For enrolment, look at the Guiding Opinion on Safeguarding the Labour Rights and Interests of Workers in New Forms of Employment《关于维护新就业形态劳动者劳动保障权益的指导意见》. It states: 「各地要放开灵活就业人员在就业地参加基本养老、基本医疗保险的户籍限制，个别超大型城市难以一步实现的，要结合本地实际，积极创造条件逐步放开。组织未参加职工基本养老、职工基本医疗保险的灵活就业人员，按规定参加城乡居民基本养老、城乡居民基本医疗保险，做到应保尽保。督促企业依法参加社会保险。企业要引导和支持不完全符合确立劳动关系情形的新就业形态劳动者根据自身情况参加相应的社会保险」, that is, each place is to lift the household registration restriction on flexible workers taking part in basic pension insurance and basic medical insurance in the place where they work; where the very largest cities find it hard to do this in one step, they are to work with local conditions and actively create the conditions to lift it progressively. Flexible workers who have not joined the basic pension insurance for employees or the basic medical insurance for employees are to be organised, as required, into the basic pension insurance for urban and rural residents and the basic medical insurance for urban and rural residents, so that everyone who should be covered is covered. Enterprises are to be urged to take part in social insurance in accordance with the law. Enterprises are to guide and support workers in new forms of employment who do not fully meet the conditions for establishing a labour relationship into taking out the corresponding social insurance according to their own circumstances. The same document divides the employment relationship into three kinds: 「符合确立劳动关系情形的，企业应当依法与劳动者订立劳动合同。不完全符合确立劳动关系情形但企业对劳动者进行劳动管理……指导企业与劳动者订立书面协议……个人依托平台自主开展经营活动、从事自由职业等，按照民事法律调整双方的权利义务」, that is, where the conditions for establishing a labour relationship are met, the enterprise shall conclude an employment contract with the worker in accordance with the law; where they are not fully met but the enterprise does exercise labour management over the worker, it is to guide the enterprise and the worker to conclude a written agreement; and where the individual runs a business on their own account through a platform, works as a freelancer and so on, the rights and obligations of the two sides are governed by civil law (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and eight other departments (2021). - Notes: The original says the household registration restriction is to be "lifted"（放开）, and it leaves an exception for the very largest cities; it does not say the restriction has been abolished nationwide. ### 12. Delivering food, driving a ride-hailing car, carrying freight in the same city: the platform pays the work-injury cover premium job by job and you pay nothing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money; the platform pays this. What you have to do is check whether the platform you take jobs from is inside the pilot. There are two ways: look for "work injury cover" on the protection or benefits page in the app, or telephone the human resources and social security bureau of your province and ask whether that platform has reported in.
- In plain language: For people who deliver food, drive a ride-hailing car or carry freight in the same city, the platform has to pay the work-injury cover premium for you job by job and you pay not one cent; every job you take is covered. This is still a pilot, so before you take jobs check whether your province and your platform are in scope. ### 14. If you want to work abroad, first check whether the company has the qualification for overseas labour cooperation: it may not take a deposit from you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you leave, spend a quarter of an hour checking the company's qualification, then read both contracts to the end.
- In plain language: As far as the law is concerned, working abroad is not regulated for you but for the company that organises you to go out. Only an enterprise that has obtained the certificate of qualification for overseas labour cooperation and completed registration may do this. When you hear "get a tourist visa over there first and switch to a work visa later", that sentence in itself shows that the other side is breaking the law. The company also may not take a deposit from you on any pretext, or ask you to put up property as security.
- Benefit: Regulations on the Administration of Overseas Labour Cooperation《对外劳务合作管理条例》, article five: engaging in overseas labour cooperation requires the approval of the commerce department of the province, or of a city with subdistricts, and the acquisition of a qualification to operate. That qualification is the certificate of qualification for overseas labour cooperation; without it nobody may organise people to go abroad to work. Article seven: the list of certificates is reported to the commerce department of the State Council and circulated to Chinese embassies and consulates abroad. This article also states 「未依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格证书并办理登记，不得从事对外劳务合作」, that is, no one may engage in overseas labour cooperation without having obtained the certificate of qualification in accordance with the law and completed registration. Article eight: 「任何单位和个人不得以商务、旅游、留学等名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作」, that is, no unit or individual may organise workers to go abroad to work in the name of business, tourism, study abroad or anything of the kind. Article nine: the enterprise shall, within 5 working days of the date of registration, deposit at a designated bank 「不低于 300 万元人民币的对外劳务合作风险处置备用金」, that is, a reserve fund for handling the risks of overseas labour cooperation of not less than 3 million yuan, and the list of those who have deposited it is published to society. Article ten: the reserve fund is used to pay the service fees that should be refunded, the remuneration and the losses which the enterprise is liable for by law and refuses to bear or cannot bear. It is also used for 「因发生突发事件，劳务人员回国或者接受紧急救助所需费用」, that is, the cost of workers returning home or receiving emergency assistance when an emergency occurs. Article eleven: workers may not be organised to go abroad to work in anything connected with gambling or with sexual activity. Article twelve: nobody may be organised to go abroad without training being arranged. Article thirteen: accident and injury insurance covering the person during the period of working abroad must be bought for the workers. Articles twenty-one and twenty-three: written contracts must be concluded separately with the foreign employer and with the workers, and nobody may be organised to go abroad without them. Article twenty-five: 「不得以任何名目向劳务人员收取押金或者要求劳务人员提供财产担保」, that is, no deposit may be collected from workers on any pretext, and workers may not be required to provide property as security. Article twenty-six: filing with the commerce department in charge has to be done within 10 working days of the date the contract is concluded. Article twenty-nine: where a worker's actual rights do not match the contract, the enterprise shall assist with the claim; where the worker has not received the compensation due, the worker has the right to require the enterprise to bear the compensation liability. Article thirty-one: where personal safety cannot be secured because of the security situation of the country or region concerned, the enterprise may not organise workers to go there. Article thirty-seven: the commerce department in charge establishes a system of bad credit records and public notices. The penalties come later. Article thirty-nine: where there is no qualification to engage in overseas labour cooperation, the business is investigated and shut down, and where a crime is constituted, criminal liability is pursued. Article forty: where a business organises people to go abroad, or lends out its qualification, or organises work connected with gambling or pornography, in the name of business, tourism or study abroad, the certificate of qualification is revoked. Article forty-three: where no contract is signed, or where people are organised to go abroad by concealing information or providing false information, the fine is not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 200,000 yuan; a separate fine of not less than 20,000 yuan and not more than 50,000 yuan is imposed on the person principally responsible (nationwide)

Within 10 working days of signing, the company has to file with the commerce department in charge. ### 15. Working remotely for a foreign company from home: under the tax law this money is domestic income, you file the personal income tax yourself, and the remittance goes through as "remuneration for employment"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One settlement and payment a year. The window runs from 3-1 to 6-30 of the following year. Where the remittance exceeds the annual total, you also have to have an employment contract and proof of income ready. The hard part is remembering to file this tax yourself.
- In plain language: Whether this money is domestic or foreign depends on where you do the work, not on where the money is paid in from. A person in China working remotely for a company abroad earns domestic income and has to pay tax in China. The foreign company has no establishment in China, so nobody withholds the tax for you and you have to file it yourself. The money comes in under the heading "remuneration for employment"「职工报酬」.
- Benefit: Whether this money counts as domestic income is set by article three of the Implementation Regulations of the Individual Income Tax Law（国务院令第 707 号, in force from 2019-1-1）《个人所得税法实施条例》. It states: 「除国务院财政、税务主管部门另有规定外，下列所得，不论支付地点是否在中国境内，均为来源于中国境内的所得：（一）因任职、受雇、履约等在中国境内提供劳务取得的所得」, that is, unless otherwise provided by the finance and taxation departments of the State Council, the following income is income sourced within China regardless of whether the place of payment is within China: (one) income obtained from providing labour within China through taking a post, being employed or performing a contract. Article six of the same Regulations defines two of the categories of income. One is wages and salaries: 「工资、薪金所得，是指个人因任职或者受雇取得的工资、薪金、奖金、年终加薪、劳动分红、津贴、补贴以及与任职或者受雇有关的其他所得」, that is, income from wages, salaries, bonuses, year-end increases, profit shares, allowances, subsidies and other income connected with taking a post or being employed. The other is remuneration for personal services: 「劳务报酬所得，是指个人从事劳务取得的所得，包括从事设计、装潢、安装、制图、化验、测试、医疗、法律、会计、咨询、讲学、翻译、审稿、书画、雕刻、影视、录音、录像、演出、表演、广告、展览、技术服务、介绍服务、经纪服务、代办服务以及其他劳务取得的所得」, that is, income from carrying out personal services, including income from design, decoration, installation, drawing, chemical testing, testing, medical work, legal work, accounting, consultancy, lecturing, translation, reviewing, painting and calligraphy, carving, film and television, sound recording, video recording, performance, acting, advertising, exhibitions, technical services, referral services, agency services, agency work and other personal services. Article six of the Individual Income Tax Law《个人所得税法》sets out how the taxable income is worked out: 「居民个人的综合所得，以每一纳税年度的收入额减除费用六万元以及专项扣除、专项附加扣除和依法确定的其他扣除后的余额，为应纳税所得额」, that is, for the comprehensive income of a resident individual, the taxable income is the balance of the income for each tax year after deducting the basic deduction of sixty thousand yuan and the special deductions, the special additional deductions and the other deductions determined in accordance with law. The same article also states that 「劳务报酬所得、稿酬所得、特许权使用费所得以收入减除百分之二十的费用后的余额为收入额」, that is, for remuneration for personal services, royalties for written work and royalties for the use of rights, the income amount is the income less a deduction of 20%. The 20% deduction covers only these three; wages and salaries are not among them. So wages and salaries go into the income amount at full value, and the two categories of income come out with a different tax. Article nine: 「个人所得税以所得人为纳税人，以支付所得的单位或者个人为扣缴义务人」, that is, for individual income tax the taxpayer is the person receiving the income and the withholding agent is the unit or person paying it. Article ten: 「有下列情形之一的，纳税人应当依法办理纳税申报：……（二）取得应税所得没有扣缴义务人；……（四）取得境外所得」, that is, in any of the following cases the taxpayer shall file a tax return in accordance with the law: where taxable income is obtained with no withholding agent; where income from abroad is obtained. Article eleven: 「居民个人取得综合所得，按年计算个人所得税……需要办理汇算清缴的，应当在取得所得的次年三月一日至六月三十日内办理汇算清缴」, that is, where a resident individual obtains comprehensive income, individual income tax is worked out by the year, and where a settlement and payment is required it is to be carried out within the period from the first day of the third month to the thirtieth day of the sixth month of the year after the income was obtained. For bringing the money in, look at the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange（中国人民银行令〔2006〕第 3 号, in force from 2007-2-1）《个人外汇管理办法》. Article nine: 「对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额内的，凭本人有效身份证件在银行办理；超过年度总额的，经常项目项下凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的相关证明等材料在银行办理」, that is, settlement of foreign exchange by individuals and purchases of foreign exchange by individuals on the domestic side are managed by an annual total. Article two of the Detailed Rules for Implementation of the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange（汇发〔2007〕1 号）: 「对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额分别为每人每年等值 5 万美元」, that is, settlement by individuals and purchases of foreign exchange by individuals on the domestic side are managed by an annual total, and the annual total is the equivalent of 50,000 US dollars per person per year. ### 16. Not being in an establishment and going into business yourself: look at the guaranteed entrepreneurship loan for the starting money, up to 300,000 yuan for an individual, with the state paying half the interest
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money to ask about it. Go once to the human resources and social security department for the eligibility check, and then go once each to the guarantee institution and to the bank that handles the loan (the bank that makes the loan). The hard part is that borrowed money still has to be repaid.
- In plain language: If you do have to borrow in order to start, look at this one first, because the state pays part of the interest. An individual can borrow up to 300,000 yuan, for up to 3 years, and the state pays half the interest. There is one hard condition that is often overlooked: when you apply, neither you nor your spouse may have any other loan, except that student loans, small loans for people who have escaped poverty, housing loans, car loans and small consumer loans below 50,000 yuan do not count.
- Benefit: The conditions for an individual applicant are in article six of the Measures for the Administration of Special Funds for the Development of Inclusive Finance（财金〔2023〕75 号）《普惠金融发展专项资金管理办法》. The first condition is that you belong to a key group for employment. The document lists the registered unemployed in towns and cities, people with employment difficulties (including people with disabilities), veterans, and people released from prison. It also lists university graduates (including former village college students and students returning from study abroad), and workers and unemployed people in enterprises cutting excess capacity. And returning migrant workers starting a business, online shopkeepers, people who have escaped poverty, and farmers starting their own businesses in the countryside. The second condition is that there may be no other loan in the name: 「除助学贷款、脱贫人口小额信贷、住房贷款、购车贷款、5 万元以下小额消费贷款（含信用卡消费）以外，申请人提交创业担保贷款申请时，本人及其配偶无其他贷款」, that is, apart from student loans, small loans for people who have escaped poverty, housing loans, car loans and small consumer loans below 50,000 yuan (including credit card spending), when an applicant submits the application for an entrepreneurship guaranteed loan, neither the applicant nor their spouse may have any other loan. Article nine: 「对符合条件的创业担保贷款，财政部门给予贷款实际利率 50% 的财政贴息。对展期、逾期的创业担保贷款，财政部门原则上不予贴息」, that is, for a qualifying entrepreneurship guaranteed loan, the finance department gives a fiscal interest subsidy of 50% of the actual interest rate of the loan. For an entrepreneurship guaranteed loan whose term is extended or that is overdue, the finance department in principle gives no interest subsidy. An interest subsidy means the state pays half of the interest. Extending the term means pushing the repayment date further out; being overdue means the due date has passed and nothing has been paid. In neither case does the state pay any more. Article ten: in principle the requirement for a counter-guarantee is removed for an entrepreneurship guaranteed loan of 100,000 yuan or less applied for by an individual, so there is no need to find somebody else or to put up property as security for the guarantee institution. Article eleven: individual entrepreneurship guaranteed loans inside the scope of grant and subsidy support are 「额度不超过 30 万元，贷款期限不超过 3 年」, that is, the amount does not exceed 300,000 yuan and the term of the loan does not exceed 3 years. Where people go into business together, the figure may be raised appropriately, up to 110% of the sum of the individual ceilings. The interest rate ceiling for grant and subsidy support comes in three bands: LPR+50BPs in the eastern region, LPR+150BPs in the central and western regions, and LPR+250BPs in areas that have escaped poverty. LPR is the loan prime rate quoted by banks, and BPs are the unit in which an interest rate is measured; the more is added, the higher the interest. Those who 「还款积极、带动就业能力强、创业项目好」, that is, repay actively, have a strong capacity to bring employment and have a good business project, may apply again after the term ends, up to 3 times in total. Article eight: the applicant first applies to the local human resources and social security department for the eligibility check, and then submits the application to the guarantee institution and to the bank handling the loan. Article twelve: with the agreement of the provincial people's government, localities may widen the scope of support, raise the ceiling and raise the proportion of the interest subsidy (nationwide)
