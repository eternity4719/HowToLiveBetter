# 7. How to get by with no money

This section counts money and protection. How much you can receive, how much you can be spared, what services you can get and what conditions you have to meet are all written down here, and nothing is converted into years of life. The policies change every year and differ from place to place, so go by the amounts and the thresholds published for your own area in that year. The items are ordered like this: those that cost no money, need little paperwork and work fast come first. Those that take years of qualifying, and those that differ a lot between places, come later. Those resting on experience alone, with no official document, are put last.

### 1. If you have lost your job, apply online first for unemployment insurance benefit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You can submit the application on your phone, and there are three ways in: the 「掌上 12333」 app, the electronic social insurance card mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, and the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. A social insurance card or an identity card is enough to do it, and in most areas you do not have to go in person. The condition is that you have completed unemployment registration first.
- In plain language: If your employer and you have paid in for a full year, and you did not resign of your own accord, you can get the money once you have completed unemployment registration. 1 year to under 5 years, at most 12 months. 5 to under 10 years, at most 18 months. 10 years or more, at most 24 months. While you are receiving it, unemployment insurance pays your medical insurance for employees.
- Benefit: Three conditions have to be met at the same time. First, your employer and you have paid unemployment insurance for a cumulative 1 year or more. Second, you did not resign of your own accord (the law calls this "employment interrupted not by the person's own wishes"). Third, you have completed unemployment registration and you are willing to look for work. How long you can receive it is calculated by the years you have paid in. A cumulative 1 year to under 5 years, at most 12 months. 5 to under 10 years, at most 18 months. 10 years or more, at most 24 months. How much is paid each month is set by the provincial government, and every province differs. The amount sits below the local minimum wage and above means-tested income support in cities. The national target is to raise it gradually to 90% of the minimum wage standard. While you are receiving unemployment insurance benefit you still take part in basic medical insurance for employees and get the benefits under it. The basic medical insurance contribution that would be due is paid out of the unemployment insurance fund, and you pay none of it yourself (nationwide, the regulations in force since 1999)

- Notes: If you resigned of your own accord, that is not "employment interrupted not by the person's own wishes" and you cannot receive anything. So do not sign "voluntary resignation for personal reasons" when you leave, see item 7 in section 19. Unemployment insurance benefit is counted from the day you complete unemployment registration, so registering a month late means losing a month of money. The first thing to do after you leave is to go and register. Registration needs a certificate of leaving issued by the employer, see item 17 in section 19 (certificate of leaving). Whether a non-compete agreement has to be honoured, and what to do when the company will not pay compensation, see item 12 in section 11 (signed a non-compete agreement). While you are receiving it, unemployment insurance pays your medical insurance for employees. The amounts differ a great deal from province to province, so go by what the place you are insured in has published. The "minimum wage 90%" comes from the 2017 guiding opinions of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Ministry of Finance (人社部发〔2017〕71 号), which say in so many words 「逐步将失业保险金标准提高到最低工资标准的90%」. That is a target, not what is paid at 90% now. ### 2. If your wages are owed to you, complain to labour inspection first and then apply for labour arbitration; neither route costs anything, and most cases finish within a few months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money; neither the complaint nor the arbitration costs anything. You need your employment contract, payslips, attendance records, transfer records and chat records, and 1 to 3 trips to the human resources and social security bureau or the arbitration commission. Applying for arbitration has a time limit of 1 year (the law calls it the arbitration limitation period). Where you are still working for this employer and your wages are being withheld, that 1-year limit does not apply. If you have already left, you must apply within 1 year of the day you left. The hard part is making the trips one after another and assembling the evidence document by document, and losing heart in between.
- In plain language: Neither route costs money. First call 12333 or go to the labour inspection office at the human resources and social security bureau and make a complaint. It has to finish the investigation within 60 working days of opening the case, and it can order the company to pay by a deadline; if the company still does not pay, it has to pay 50% to 100% on top. If that does not work, apply for labour arbitration, which finishes within 45 days. But "case closed" does not mean "the money has arrived": if the company has no assets and the owner has run off, you may win and still get nothing.
- Benefit: The first step is a complaint. Call 12333, or go to the labour inspection office of the local human resources and social security bureau and make a complaint. The law requires the investigation to be finished within 60 working days of opening the case, and where the circumstances are complicated it may be extended by a further 30 working days. If the facts are confirmed, the office can order the company to pay by a deadline. If it still does not pay by the deadline, it has to pay an extra compensation of 50% to 100% of the amount due. In 2024 inspection agencies nationwide handled 137,000 cases and recovered 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people. If you have an IOU or a wage sheet and the unpaid wages are clear, you can also apply straight to a basic-level court for a payment order. A payment order is the court's command to the company to pay, and the court issues it within 15 days of accepting the application. The second step is to apply for arbitration. The law requires the case to be closed within 45 days of acceptance, with a possible further extension of 15 days. Somebody claiming wages can apply for advance enforcement: part of the award is enforced without waiting for the whole case to be decided. Where the amount owed is no more than 12 times the local monthly minimum wage, the award is final on arbitration and the company cannot sue. In 2024, 4,257,000 cases were handled nationwide and 4,156,000 were closed. The amount closed was 93.47 billion yuan. The mediation success rate was 79.6%, the arbitration closure rate 98.2% and the arbitration finality rate 73.7%. When people say this drags on for "half a year to a year", they mean the cases that go on to court after the arbitration: 6 months at first instance (3 months under the summary procedure), then another 3 months at second instance. In 2024 courts closed 88,000 cases of claims for labour remuneration and recovered 28.54 billion yuan, and 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labour remuneration (nationwide)

### 3. If you cannot afford to sue, apply for legal aid; wage claims, support payments and work injury cases are already within scope
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Legal aid charges the person receiving it not one yuan.
- In plain language: Claiming wages, claiming support payments for parents, claiming social insurance or means-tested income support, and losses from having done a brave and selfless act can all get free legal aid. Call 12348, or go to the local legal aid centre. You no longer have to go back to your home town to get a certificate of economic hardship; stating your situation truthfully yourself is enough. Where the death penalty may be imposed, and defendants who are blind, deaf, mute or minors, are assigned a lawyer as of right.
- Benefit: The State Council's regulations set out six kinds of civil case covered by legal aid. The text of the regulations is:「依法请求国家赔偿的；请求给予社会保险待遇或者最低生活保障待遇的；请求发给抚恤金、救济金的；请求给付赡养费、抚养费、扶养费的；请求支付劳动报酬的；主张因见义勇为行为产生的民事权益的」. Put plainly: claiming state compensation, claiming social insurance benefits or means-tested income support, claiming a pension or relief payment, claiming support payments for parents, children or dependants, claiming wages, and claiming civil rights arising from having done a brave and selfless act. On the criminal side, defendants who may be sentenced to death, and defendants who are blind, deaf, mute or minors, are assigned a lawyer regardless of their financial situation
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2003). Regulations on Legal Aid（法律援助条例）(State Council Order No. 385, Articles ten, eleven and twelve). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm> ; Ministry of Justice official (司法部有关负责人) (2021). Answers to Reporters' Questions on Implementing the Legal Aid Law（就贯彻实施法律援助法答记者问）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: The Legal Aid Law has applied since 2022-1-1, and one thing changed. You used to have to「提交经济困难证明」. Now you only have to state your financial situation truthfully yourself, and no longer have to go back to your home town for a certificate. The way in is the legal aid centre or the public legal service centre in your area, and you can also call 12348.
### 4. If you have nowhere to turn, go to a rescue station; it provides food, a bed and a ticket home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You can go to the local rescue management station yourself, or tell a police officer or the civil affairs department and ask them to take you there. On arrival you have to register your name and belongings truthfully. The hard part is having to tell a stranger that you have no money and nowhere to go.
- In plain language: A rescue station provides food and a bed, takes you to hospital if you are ill, and helps you contact your family. If you cannot afford to get home it issues a ticket. The stay is generally no more than 10 days. Coming and going are both voluntary; it is not a detention centre and it pays out no cash. The ticket is issued to your registered place of residence or your place of residence, so work out where you are going back to before you set off.
- Benefit: A rescue station provides food and shelter that meet hygiene requirements. Anyone who falls ill there suddenly is taken to hospital for treatment. It also helps you contact relatives and your employer. If you cannot afford the ticket back to your registered place of residence or your place of residence, it issues you a travel voucher, which is the train or boat ticket. Your registered place of residence is your home town; your place of residence is where you live now. The period of rescue is generally no more than 10 days. It can be extended while you wait for family to collect you, for treatment, or for placement. When police and other administrative bodies meet a person who is homeless and begging while carrying out their duties, they should tell that person where to seek help. For people with disabilities, minors, the elderly and those who have difficulty moving, they should guide and accompany them there (nationwide, since 2003)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2003). Measures for the Assistance and Management of Homeless and Begging Persons with Nowhere to Live in Cities（城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法）(State Council Order No. 381, Articles five, six and seven). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm> ; Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) (2003). Detailed Implementation Rules for the Measures for the Assistance and Management of Homeless and Begging Persons with Nowhere to Live in Cities（城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法实施细则）(Ministry of Civil Affairs Order No. 24, Articles eleven and twelve). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: Seeking help and leaving are both voluntary; it is not a detention centre. What it provides is basic food and a bed, and no cash. The travel voucher is issued to your registered place of residence or your place of residence, so work out where you are going before you set off.

### 5. To find work, start with free public employment services and the day labour market, not with an agency that charges
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Register with a public employment service body at county level or above, such as an employment service centre or a human resources market. The local day labour market will also take registrations. If you do not know the address, call 12333 and ask.
- In plain language: Government bodies such as employment service centres and human resources markets introduce jobs, give vocational guidance, answer policy questions and register you for employment and unemployment, and none of it costs anything. The law also states in writing that they may not make money out of these services. Registration and publication of information at a day labour market are free as well. Do unemployment registration while you are there, because you need it both to receive unemployment insurance benefits and to be recognised as a person with difficulty finding work.
- Benefit: The law requires all of the following to be free: consultation on employment policies; information on where jobs are going and where workers are needed; vocational guidance and job placement; registering for employment and for unemployment; and employment assistance for people with difficulty finding work. These bodies may not carry out profit-making activities and may not make money out of these services. Registration and publication of job information for day labourers is also free to the public, and has been brought within the scope of public employment information services. Day labourers who have been out of work a long time (that is, who go a long time without finding work) and other day labourers in difficulty, such as those from low-income households, are to be given more help (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2007, amended 2015). Employment Promotion Law（就业促进法）(Article thirty-five). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm> ; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and four other departments (2022). Opinions on Strengthening the Construction of Day Labour Markets and Improving Job Search and Recruitment Services（关于加强零工市场建设 完善求职招聘服务的意见）(人社部发〔2022〕38). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: Unemployment registration is the preliminary step both for receiving unemployment insurance benefits and for being recognised as a person with difficulty finding work, so do it while you are there. For day labour paid off by the day, check the money on the spot, keep the other party's contact details and a record of the work done.
### 6. If something sudden happens, apply for temporary assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Apply to the township government or the sub-district office and set out clearly what has happened at home, such as a fire, a traffic accident, a family member falling suddenly seriously ill, or an unavoidable cost that has suddenly gone up. After review and public notice, the county-level civil affairs department approves it. Prepare the documents from the list your local office gives you; it usually takes 1 to 2 trips.
- In plain language: If something sudden happens at home, such as a fire, a car crash or a family member falling suddenly seriously ill, and basic living costs are crushed for a time, you can apply to the township government or the sub-district office for temporary assistance. You do not have to be on means-tested income support already. More often than not it is a single payment, and the amount differs a great deal from place to place.
- Benefit: Two kinds of household can receive temporary assistance. One is households which, because of an accident or a family member falling suddenly seriously ill, cannot hold together even the most basic costs of food, shelter and medical care for a time. The other is households on means-tested income support whose unavoidable costs have suddenly risen beyond what they can bear. Which events are covered and how much money is given is set and published by the local government at county level or above (national system, amounts set locally, since 2014)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures on Social Assistance（社会救助暂行办法）(State Council Order No. 649, Articles forty-seven, forty-eight and forty-nine). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Notes: You do not have to be on means-tested income support already. More often than not it is a single payment, and the amount differs a great deal from place to place, so go by what your area published that year.
### 7. If your income is below the local means-tested income support line, apply for means-tested income support
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. A family member who lives with you submits one written application to the township government or the sub-district office where your household registration is. If it is awkward for you to do it yourself, you can ask the village committee or the residents' committee to do it for you. You have to accept a check of the family's income and property. It usually takes 2 to 3 trips, with your identity card, household register and proof of income and property. The hard part is laying your finances open to be checked, and then waiting through the public notice.
- In plain language: Divide the whole family's income by the number of people; if the result is below the local means-tested income support line and the family's property is also within the rules, you can apply. At the end of 2024, the urban line averaged 798.1 yuan per person per month nationwide and the rural line 593.9 yuan. What is paid is the difference between the line and what each member of the household has per month. If you own a house, a car and savings, or your children can support you, the application may be turned down.
- Benefit: Divide the whole family's income by the number of people; if the result is below the local means-tested income support standard and the family's property is also within the range set, you can receive means-tested income support. At the end of 2024, the average urban standard was 798.1 yuan per person per month and the rural standard 593.9 yuan. Urban means-tested income support covered 6,250,000 people and rural support covered 33,615,000. The payment is made monthly and is the difference: the local standard minus the household's income per head. If the local standard is 798.1 yuan and everyone in the household has 500 yuan a month, each person receives 298.1 yuan a month, not 798.1 yuan. The standard is set this way: take the local per-capita consumption expenditure of the previous year, multiply it by a set ratio, and adjust it again from time to time. Put plainly, it is what the average person locally spent the year before, worked out with a discount (national average, different in every place)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2014, revised 2019). Interim Measures on Social Assistance（社会救助暂行办法）(State Council Order No. 649, Articles nine, ten and eleven; Article twelve:「县级人民政府民政部门按照共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地最低生活保障标准的差额，按月发给最低生活保障金」). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm> ; National Bureau of Statistics (2025). Social services developing well and public wellbeing rising — a statistical report on social livelihood since the 「十四五」. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202509/content_7042627.htm> ; Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) and four other departments (2024). Guiding Opinions on Further Improving the Determination and Adjustment of the Minimum Living Guarantee Standard（关于进一步做好最低生活保障标准确定调整工作的指导意见）(民发〔2024〕16 号). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_7007237.htm>
- Notes: Means-tested income support is the last line of defence, and you have to apply at your registered place of residence. If the house, car or savings at home go over the local standard, or your children are able to support you, the application may be turned down. Once it is approved there are other benefits as well: there is a subsidy for resident medical insurance (resident medical insurance: see item 9), medical care can go through medical assistance (medical assistance: see item 10), and applying for legal aid does not require a check on financial hardship (legal aid: see item 3).

### 8. If you hold a disability certificate, apply for the two disability subsidies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Take your disability certificate to the sub-district office or the township and submit one application.
- In plain language: A disabled person in a household on means-tested income support can receive a living allowance. A disabled person whose level is graded one or two and who needs long-term care can receive a care allowance. Both can be received at the same time. Each province sets the standard and the differences are large, so ask the sub-district office. Take the second-generation disability certificate and go once to the sub-district office or the township.
- Benefit: The State Council set down two subsidies. The first is the living allowance for disabled people in difficulty, and the document defines who it is for as「低保家庭中的残疾人，有条件的地方可逐步扩大到低收入残疾人及其他困难残疾人」. That means disabled people in means-tested households come first, and where local resources allow it can be extended to low-income disabled people and other disabled people in difficulty.
