# 32. Studying abroad: status, work, insurance and certification on return

This section measures money and personal freedom. It does not discuss whether you should go abroad, and it does not compare schools. Four things below cost you if you do not know them: when your visa status counts as unbroken, where the ceiling on working hours is, which insurances and fees you must pay, and whether this diploma is recognised once you are back home.

It covers four countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia, with the figures given country by country. The sources are the official pages of the immigration departments of each country and of the Ministry of Education of China. **Study-abroad policies are changed frequently in each of these countries, more often than China's own rules are. Every figure in this section is as at 2026-09. Before you leave, and before every renewal, go through the links in the Sources field and check again yourself.**

How to reach an embassy or consulate if something happens to you while you are abroad, what 12308 can do, and how to buy medical and evacuation insurance abroad, see section 21. The account of studying or working, see section 23. A stolen account, see section 14, and telecom fraud, see section 8. None of these is written out again here.

### 1. Before you pay tuition, check whether the school is on the accredited institutions list of the Service Center for Scholarly Exchange
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Type the name of the school once into the Service Center's "Accredited Institutions Search"「认证院校查询」; it takes a few minutes.
- In plain language: Once you are back home, if you want to use this diploma to sit the civil service examination, get into a state-owned enterprise, settle a household registration or apply for a professional title, it has to go through a certification first. The certification is done by the Service Center for Scholarly Exchange of the Ministry of Education. If the school is not on the list that can be certified, the tuition and the time are wasted and the diploma is of no use at home. Checking the list costs nothing; check before you pay the tuition.
- Benefit: The Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange has an accredited institutions search. You can search by country or by the name of the school. What you are checking is whether the qualifications and degrees this school awards fall inside the scope of certification. In addition, the Ministry of Education's Network for the Supervision and Administration of Education Involving Foreign Parties publishes regulatory information on Sino-foreign cooperative education and on running educational institutions abroad
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange（中国（教育部）留学服务中心）. Accredited Institutions Search（认证院校查询）. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>；Ministry of Education Network for the Supervision and Administration of Education Involving Foreign Parties（教育部教育涉外监管信息网）. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Notes: What an agency tells you in words, that it is "recognised by the Ministry of Education", does not count; go by what you find yourself when you look it up online. The list changes: a school can be on it when you enrol and off it when you graduate, so check once a year while you are studying. Sino-foreign cooperative education programmes have a separate system of certification and registration. For those who enrolled after 2008, you can look up your own registration number on that network using your name and your identity card number.

### 2. Watch the court case over the new F-1 rule in the United States: "at most four years, and leave within 30 days of finishing" was to take effect on 2026-09-15 and was postponed by the court the day before, so for now it is still "until you finish"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Copy the programme end date on your I-20 into your calendar and set a reminder 90 days ahead. The I-20 is the certificate of eligibility for study that the school gives you. Also look at the notices from the school's international student office once a term to see whether there is any new development in the case
- In plain language: The United States was going to change F-1 to at most four years, with 30 days to leave after finishing. A federal court postponed it the day before it was to take effect. So for now the old rule still stands: you stay as long as you study, and you have 60 days after finishing to get ready to leave. A postponement is not a repeal; the government can still appeal, and the rule can change at any time.
- Benefit: The Department of Homeland Security published a rule on 2026-7-17 that was to take effect on 2026-9-15. It changes F-1 students from entering "until you finish" to a fixed period, at most four years, after which a separate extension has to be applied for. After finishing a programme or an internship there would be only 30 days in which to prepare to leave. On 2026-9-14, Judge Saylor of the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, under item 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act, deferred the rule from taking effect. The postponement applies nationwide and to the rule as a whole. The court did not vacate the rule, the request to vacate it was denied, but a further request may be made later. The case is still being heard. Under the order of the court, people on F, J and I visas still enter "until you finish", the 60-day grace period for departure still applies, and no separate extension is needed
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: DHS (2026). Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors（published 2026-07-17, to take effect 2026-09-15）. <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/17/2026-14439/establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and-an-extension-of-stay-procedure-for-nonimmigrant>；Presidents' Alliance on Higher Education and Immigration v. U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026), court docket. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>；Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). Important Update: Court Action on the D/S Rule. <https://oiss.yale.edu/news/important-update-court-action-on-the-ds-rule>；AILA (2026). One Day Before Taking Effect, Federal Court Postpones the F, J, and I Fixed Admission Period Rule. <https://www.aila.org/blog/think-immigration-one-day-before-taking-effect-federal-court-postpones-the-f-j-and-i-fixed-admission-period-rule>；8 CFR 214.2(f). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>
- Notes: This item is as at 2026-09-25. The government can appeal to the Court of Appeals for the First Circuit, and the Department of Homeland Security can also amend the rule and publish it again. So if you are studying for several years, do not assume the old rule will last. The court has set 2026-10-02 for a further hearing on how both sides have got on, and after that date look at the notice from your school once more. If the new rule does take effect later, an extension has to be applied for separately before the period ends; it does not carry on by itself. Under the old rule, the departure deadline is different if you stop studying before finishing, so ask the international student office first. The J visa is in the same rule and the details are not the same as for F-1, so look at your own visa category.

### 3. The hours you may work are a hard limit written into the rules of all four countries, and one hour over is a breach of your visa conditions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Keep a record of your own hours week by week. The hard part is that employers say "nobody checks a few extra hours", and that is not true
- In plain language: All four countries have put a ceiling on how long a student visa holder may work. Go over it and you have breached the conditions of your visa; your visa may be cancelled, you may be removed, and a later application will be asked about it. The ceilings are: the United States 20 hours a week; the United Kingdom 20 hours a week on a degree course and 10 hours a week below degree level; Canada 24 hours a week; Australia 48 hours a fortnight. If you hold two jobs the hours are added together.
- Benefit: For F-1 students in the United States, on-campus work must not exceed 20 hours a week during term time. Approved off-campus part-time work is also 20 hours a week. Full-time work is allowed during the holidays. The basis is 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9) of the United States federal immigration regulations. For the student visa in the United Kingdom, on a degree course or above it is 20 hours a week during term time, and below degree level 10 hours a week. Part-time courses may not be worked at all. You also may not be self-employed (working for yourself), and may not do professional sport or entertainment work. The basis is the part of the United Kingdom immigration rules that deals with students, appendix Student ST26.1 and ST26.5. In Canada a qualifying full-time student may work off campus up to 24 hours a week, under paragraph 186(v) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations. For the student visa in Australia it is at most 48 hours a fortnight while the course is running. Research masters and doctoral students and their dependants are not subject to this limit
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1、ST26.5）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>；IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: Off-campus work in the United States has to be authorised first. While the I-765 application for the work permit is still being processed, you may not start work. Canada's older permit says 20 hours a week on it; as long as you still meet the conditions, 24 hours is in fact possible, and the current rules govern. The United Kingdom's prohibition on self-employment is drawn widely: taking private jobs, running an online shop and working as a freelancer all count.

### 4. The root of the status is "studying full time": during a leave of absence, a withdrawal or the gap between schools you may not work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you stop classes, take a leave of absence or change schools, go to the school's international student office and ask what your status is
- In plain language: Whether you may work depends on whether you are in fact studying full time. Once you take a leave, withdraw, are expelled, or are not attending classes during a period of transfer, the right to be paid to work ends at that moment. Carrying on then is working illegally. A lot of people think it is all right as long as the visa has not run out; in fact the status was broken long before that.
- Benefit: Canada states expressly that off-campus work is not permitted during an authorised leave of absence. Nor is it permitted while you are not studying during a period of transfer. You may only start working again after you have resumed your studies. In the United States, the right to work on campus is given only to F-1 students whose status in SEVIS is Active. SEVIS is the United States system of records for the status of international students, and Active means enrolled and valid. These students also have to keep up a full-time course. In the United Kingdom the work permission follows the type of course, and part-time courses may not be worked
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Notes: If the academic pressure is heavy and you are thinking of reducing your course load, ask first: how little still counts as full time. This one bites earlier than working does. If you are expelled by the school, or the programme is terminated, the status is normally broken the same day. Do not wait for an official letter from the school before you act.

### 5. United States: you must report your new address within 10 days of moving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Submit the change of address online once; it takes ten minutes
- In plain language: In the United States, when you move, the law requires you to notify the immigration authorities within 10 days. Not reporting is not a small matter. First, it is itself unlawful. Second, letters sent to you by the immigration authorities will go astray.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1 of the United States federal immigration regulations provides that a foreign national who is required to register under the law shall report within 10 days of the address having changed. It is reported to USCIS (the United States immigration authorities) in the way USCIS requires, reporting both the fact that the address has changed and the new address
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- Notes: Even after you have told the immigration authorities, the SEVIS record at the school (your file of international student status) has to be updated separately. The other three countries also require you to notify the immigration authorities or the school when you move. The deadline and the method differ from country to country; follow the page of the immigration department of the country you are in.

### 6. Before you leave and while you are studying, take a look at the Ministry of Education's study-abroad warnings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Look once before you leave, and once a term after that
- In plain language: The Ministry of Education issues a warning about a particular country or region, saying that public security or policy there is unfavourable to Chinese students at present. This is an official public risk notice, in writing and open to checking, and it is more reliable than what an agency tells you. Taking a look costs nothing.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Education publishes study-abroad warnings through the Network for the Supervision and Administration of Education Involving Foreign Parties. In 2025 it issued four in all, as warning item 1, item 2, item 3 and item 4. Item 1 warned that higher education legislation passed by certain states of the United States contains provisions that are negative towards China (2025-04-09). Item 2 and item 3 warned on two separate occasions that cases against Chinese citizens are frequent in the Philippines and that the environment for studying there is poor (2025-07-18, 2025-08-30). Item 4 warned that the public security situation and the environment for studying in Japan are poor, and advised planning study trips to Japan carefully (2025-11-16)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Education Network for the Supervision and Administration of Education Involving Foreign Parties（教育部教育涉外监管信息网）. Warning information（预警信息）. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>；Ministry of Education issues the fourth study-abroad warning of 2025（教育部发布 2025 年第 4 号留学预警）. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>；Warning item one（第 1 号）. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Notes: A warning is not a ban, and it does not mean you cannot go; it gives you an official basis for weighing the risk there. The list of warnings changes with the situation, and this section does not keep it up to date over time; go by the page current at the time on the website. Personal safety abroad and consular protection, see section 21.
### 7. Australia: OSHC has to cover the whole stay with no gap in the middle, and without proof of insurance you may be refused entry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Several thousand yuan up to more than ten thousand yuan. Buy it in one go for the length of the visa; if you are there several years, buy several years' cover
- In plain language: Australia has written the health insurance for international students into the visa conditions, so it is not something you may or may not buy. The insurance has to cover your whole stay. When you renew the visa and change policy there must be no gap in between. If you cannot produce proof of insurance on entry, you may be refused entry there and then. When family members come to accompany you, each of them has to buy a policy of their own.
- Benefit: The Australian Department of Home Affairs requires applicants for a student visa and their dependants to hold Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), and to hold it for the whole time they are in Australia, with no break. The insurance has to be provided by an insurer recognised in Australia. Cases that qualify for an exemption from having to buy it are excepted. There may be no gap between the new insurance and the insurance under the previous visa. If proof that you are insured cannot be produced on entry, you may be refused entry. Where you arrive earlier than the start of the course, the insurance start date has to be the day you actually arrive in Australia
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: Where the school arranges it, ask for the name of the insurer, the start and end dates of the policy and the policy number; these go into the visa application. Buying too short a period causes trouble with the renewal, so it is better to buy a little past the date you graduate.

### 8. United Kingdom: besides the visa fee there is a health surcharge charged by the year; a student pays 776 pounds a year, paid in full at the time of application
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Visa fee 558 pounds. Health surcharge 776 pounds a year. Both are paid in full at the time of application
- In plain language: The money you pay to go to the United Kingdom is not only the visa fee. You also have to prepay a health surcharge according to the number of years on your visa, and only with it do you get the National Health Service. A two-year visa means paying 1552 pounds in one go. Add the visa fee and this whole amount has to be paid before you leave, so do not leave it out of the budget.
- Benefit: The cost of a student visa in the United Kingdom is 558 pounds. Applied for outside the United Kingdom, and for extensions or changes inside the country, the figure is the same. There is also the health surcharge, without which you cannot use the National Health Service, prepaid once and in full at the time of application according to the number of years on the visa. The student and their dependants pay 776 pounds a year, so a 2-year visa is 1552 pounds. Other categories of applicant pay 1035 pounds a year. Where the visa is longer than 6 months and shorter than 1 year, it is charged as a whole year
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>；UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Notes: Both the amounts and the exchange rate change, so work from the current figures on the two pages in the sources. A degree course, and anyone aged 18 or over, is normally allowed at most 5 years; below degree level it is 2 years. Use that number of years to work out how much surcharge to allow for.

### 9. Leave time for the certification before you come back: certification by the Service Center takes 10 to 20 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Apply and pay online. Preparing the documents plus the assessment and the review, counted in working days, comes to around a month in all
- In plain language: To use a qualification gained abroad once you are back home, you first have to have it certified. The assessment alone takes 10 to 20 working days. If it falls in the job-hunting season, or your documents are returned, it takes longer. The autumn recruitment round, the registration for the civil service examination and settling a household registration all have deadlines, so do not wait until you need it to start the process.
- Benefit: Certification of qualifications obtained abroad and outside the territory is done in the online service hall of the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. The process has four steps: register and complete identity verification, submit the application and the documents online, pay online, and wait for the assessment and the review. The published time limit for certification is 10 to 20 working days. Working days do not include weekends and public holidays, and this is the time the Service Center takes to review
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange（中国（教育部）留学服务中心）. Online Service Hall · Academic Qualification Certification（网上服务大厅·学历学位认证）. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: The documents you have to hand in are these: the diploma, your passport or travel permit, your residence card or visa endorsement, a photograph of your identity document, and a statement of authorisation. Entry and exit records for the period you were abroad are pulled by the system itself, so do not throw away the visa pages you were carrying while you were abroad. The Service Center also issues announcements on its website saying which certificates have been invalidated and whose applications for certification have been suspended. Before you start, check whether your own school is one of them.

### 10. More schools every year are put under "enhanced review" of certification, and the route of buying a diploma no longer works
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you sign up, search the name of the school once in the Service Center's announcements
- In plain language: The Service Center names a batch of foreign institutions and applies enhanced review to the certification of their qualifications on its own; it also suspends the acceptance of certification applications for individual schools. The ones it names are often exactly the kind advertised as "no language test, no going abroad, a master's degree in a few months". Searching the name of the school once before you sign up saves a great deal of trouble compared with appealing afterwards.
- Benefit: The Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange has been issuing announcements applying enhanced certification review to the academic qualification certification of some foreign institutions (a separate extra check on the diplomas of these schools). The most recent is number nine (2025-10-28). The Service Center has also issued announcements suspending the acceptance of certification applications for individual overseas institutions, and reminders that people have used academic qualification certification from abroad and outside the territory to carry out fraud
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange（中国（教育部）留学服务中心）. Announcement on Applying Enhanced Certification Review to the Academic Qualification Certification of Some Foreign Institutions (Nine)《关于对部分国外院校学历学位认证加强认证审查的公告（九）》(2025-10-28). <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>；Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange（中国（教育部）留学服务中心）. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: "Enhanced review" does not mean the certification will definitely be refused, but it does mean you will be asked for more documents and that it will take longer. Any agent promising "certification guaranteed" or "expedited through inside channels" is a scam; the Service Center has issued specific warnings about fraud. Having somebody write your thesis or take your online courses for you across the border, if it is found out, not only voids the qualification but may also affect your visa record.
