# 8. Do not put yourself at risk: law and property safety

This section counts money and personal freedom, and is not converted into lifespan. It covers three things: what responsibility the law places on you, how much compensation you save by following it, and what rights you have in procedure once something has happened. Every article number has been checked against the official text. The order is this: items where one accident costs the most and the action takes the least effort come first. Items that have to be arranged in advance come next. Items resting on experience alone, with no official document behind them, come last.

### 1. After a traffic accident, stop, help the injured, call the police, and do not drive away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Stop, put on your hazard lights, help the injured, call the police, and wait for the traffic officer. That takes an hour or two at the most. If nobody was hurt in a minor accident and both sides agree on what happened, take photographs first. Then write down the other driver's name, phone number, plate number, driving licence number, insurance document number, and which parts of the vehicle you struck. Once both sides have signed, you can leave the scene and sort it out between yourselves.
- In plain language: If you drive away, the accident counts as entirely your fault by default. Your driving licence is revoked and you can never sit the driving test again. There is a more practical layer on top of that: commercial vehicle damage cover and third-party liability cover pay nothing at all for fleeing the scene, so a claim of several hundred thousand yuan comes out of your own pocket. Staying to help the injured and call the police takes an hour or two at the most.
- Benefit: The side that drives away carries the whole responsibility for the accident. Responsibility can only be reduced if you can prove that the other side was also at fault. Driving away also means your driving licence is revoked and you can never sit the test again. If the accident amounts to the crime of causing a traffic accident, driving away carries 3 to 7 years. If a death results from driving away, the sentence is 7 years or more. Insurance does not cover it either: in commercial motor insurance, vehicle damage cover and third-party liability cover pay nothing for 「交通肇事逃逸」「故意破坏、伪造现场、毁灭证据」, so a claim of several hundred thousand yuan comes out of your own pocket (nationwide)

- Notes: If anyone is hurt, help them first and then call the police. If you have to move the scene in order to help someone, mark where the vehicle and the person stood to begin with. 「I brushed it and did not feel a thing, so I drove off」 can still be counted as fleeing afterwards, so call the police if you are unsure. The insurance point quotes the model clauses, which are a standard template for the industry. What your own claim actually pays out depends on the policy in your hands.
### 2. If you find out you have been scammed, call 110 or 96110 at once and ask for the payment to be stopped. Do not investigate it yourself first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Make one call and give the time of the transfer, the amount, and the account it went to. Keep the transfer screenshot and the chat records.
- In plain language: The short period right after the money leaves your account is the only window you get. During that period the police can stop the payment urgently and freeze the funds quickly, and the bank must comply. Once the money has been passed on and scattered, it is basically unrecoverable. So call 110 or 96110 first, and do not start looking into it yourself. When 96110 rings you, a police officer is trying to stop you from being scammed. Answer it.
- Benefit: The police have the power to look up the funds involved immediately, stop payment urgently, and freeze them quickly. Banks and payment institutions must comply. Money that has been frozen is returned to the victim through the proper procedure. Once the money has been transferred on and scattered, the chance of recovering it drops sharply, so the earlier the stop is asked for the better it works. 96110 is the national anti-fraud warning hotline, and a call from it means the police are trying to stop you, so answer it (nationwide, in force from 2022-12)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2022). Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Telecom and Online Fraud, 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml> ; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (a provincial public security page)
- Notes: Anyone who claims to be from the courts, the procuratorate or the police and tells you to move money into a 「安全账户」 is running a scam. The law only says 「建立止付冻结制度」, which means letting the bank hold the money down. How much comes back to you depends on whether the money was still on the account when you reported it. The description of the 「国家反诈中心」 app and of 96110 is taken from the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department page, because the Ministry of Public Security's own page would not open.
### 3. Learn the four hard anti-fraud rules: do not trust a caller, do not reveal information, do not open links, check again before any transfer. The seven commonest scams all have this shape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Learn the 「三不一多」 rule, read through the seven categories below, and install the 「国家反诈中心」 app. Always answer 96110. If anyone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, install an unfamiliar app, open a link, or read out a verification code, hang up first and then call the official number to check. The hard part is that the other person usually pushes hard, and you have to hold out against that and hang up.
- In plain language: If anyone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, install an unfamiliar app, open a link, or read out a verification code, hang up first and then call back on the official number to check. The seven commonest categories are task-based rebates, fake investment and wealth management, online loans, impersonating customer service, impersonating the courts, the procuratorate or the police, pig-butchering romance scams, and sex-bait task scams. All of them start by getting you to transfer money. Recognising one before it happens costs far less than chasing the money afterwards.
- Benefit: The law requires the government and the departments concerned to explain the methods of telecom and online fraud to the public. The police set up a warning and deterrence system together with the financial, telecommunications and cyberspace departments, and warn people in time when they are about to be defrauded. 96110 is that national warning and deterrence hotline, and calls from it must be answered. The Ministry of Public Security reported in 2026-6 that the 10 most frequent types of fraud account for 85% of all telecom fraud cases. Task-based rebates are the most frequently reported, at 25%. Fake online investment and wealth management causes the largest losses, about 40% of all losses. The Fujian Provincial Public Security Department sets out the identifying signs category by category. Task-based rebates: 「喊你一起赚钱的大概率是想赚你的钱」, and the principal is not refunded if you never finish the task list. Fake investment and wealth management: the group's 「水军」 post returns, small rebates come first and then you are told to put in more, and withdrawal requires 「缴税」. Online loans: 「贷款并不需要交保证金、做银行流水账，一旦遇上了，一定是假的」. Impersonating customer service: you are told your membership will deduct automatically and affect your credit record, and you are pushed into transferring money to cancel it. Impersonating the courts, the procuratorate or the police: 「公检法机关不存在所谓的『安全账户』，凡是通过电话、QQ、微信、网络等办案、做笔录的『公检法机关』，都是诈骗」. Pig-butchering romance scams: 「对于从未见过面，只见到照片或者视频的『恋人』，不可轻信」, and the person never appears on video, has never met you face to face, and always has an urgent need for money. Sex-bait task scams: the meeting is proposed and then there is a 「做任务」 to do, and the more you pay the more they ask for. Recognising a scam before it happens costs far less than stopping the payment afterwards. How much a stop can recover depends on whether the money was still on the account when you reported it. How to ask for a stop: see item 2 in this section (call 110 or 96110 and ask for a stop) (nationwide, in force from 2022-12)

A real loan asks for no deposit, and the courts, the procuratorate and the police have no 「安全账户」. Remembering those two sentences blocks off most of it. The routine is the same in every case, and it ends with you being asked to transfer money.
### 4. A face on a video and a voice on the phone are not verification. Hang up before any transfer and call back on an old number from your own contacts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One extra phone call, a few minutes of waiting. Post fewer full-face videos and long voice messages on public accounts. The hard part is that the other person often pushes hard, and you have to hold out against transferring on the spot.
- In plain language: Seeing a face and hearing a voice do not count as verification. As soon as money is involved, hang up first, then call back on an old number from your own contacts, or ask someone who knows you both. If the other person says the 「信号不好」「在开会」 and refuses to do a live video call, treat it as a scam straight away.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Public Security's cyber security bureau gives three methods. The first is action verification: ask the person to turn their head in one continuous movement, or to cover their face completely with a palm and then move it away quickly. The original text says 「目前的 AI 技术在模拟这些复杂的面部遮挡和光影变化时，容易出现卡顿、模糊或扭曲」. The second is listening to the details of the voice: 「AI 生成的语音有时会缺乏自然的呼吸停顿、情感起伏，或者带有轻微的机械感、背景杂音异常」. The third is checking through several channels. The original text calls this 「最核心的一招」: 「挂断后直接拨打对方常用且你熟悉的电话号码（不要回拨可疑来电号码），或者通过你们共同认识的第三人进行侧面核实」. The slogan is 「视频语音莫轻信，动作验证多核实」. Legally, face swaps and cloned voices have long been banned for fraud. Face generation, face replacement, synthesised speech and voice imitation all have to carry a prominent label. No organisation or individual may maliciously delete, alter, forge or conceal that label. But the platforms are the ones who follow the rule, and a fraudster will not add one, so the absence of an AI label cannot be turned into evidence that the person is real. The Ministry of Public Security's criminal investigation bureau has published a case: a 14-person gang was found with more than 50,000 synthesised dynamic face videos (5 万多条) and more than 500,000 records of citizens' personal information (50 多万条), and the police seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide, the labelling duty applies from 2025-9-1)
- Evidence grade: B

- Notes: When you call back, use an old number from your own contacts. Do not call back the number that appeared on the display, and do not use a new number the other person sends in the chat. If they cite a 「信号不好」「在开会」 and refuse a live video call or the action you asked for, treat it as a scam. Action verification is only an aid. The AI keeps getting better, and the only steady method is the third one, checking through several channels. ### 5. If you are accused or summoned, get a lawyer first. Do not settle privately and do not delete records
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A fair amount of money. Legal fees start at several thousand yuan. If you cannot afford it you can apply to a legal aid organisation for a free lawyer. When you are questioned, answer only what concerns this case and leave the rest to the lawyer. The hard part is staying calm on the spot instead of rushing to explain yourself.
- In plain language: The first time you are called in for questioning you can already have a lawyer. Once the lawyer asks to see you, the detention centre must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the conversation is not monitored. Questions that have nothing to do with this case, you may decline to answer.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation, or from the moment a compulsory measure is taken against you (your personal freedom being restricted), you have the right to appoint a lawyer. While the case is still with the public security organ for investigation you may appoint a lawyer, and nobody other than a lawyer can act as your defender. When the lawyer applies to see you, the detention centre must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. Nobody may be forced to give evidence that they are guilty. You have the right to refuse to answer questions that do not relate to the case. A summons to appear, and a compulsory summons, last at most 12 hours, and at most 24 hours in particularly major and complicated cases. A summons to appear is a notice telling you to come and be questioned; a compulsory summons takes you there by force. You may not be summoned over and over until you amount to someone being held in custody. Fabricating facts to frame somebody else and get them held criminally liable carries 3 years or less, and 3 to 10 years where serious consequences follow. If the case is withdrawn after you have been detained or arrested, if no prosecution is brought, or if you are acquitted, you may apply for state compensation, calculated day by day at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2018). Criminal Procedure Law, 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml> ; National People's Congress (全国人大) (1997). Criminal Law, 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2012). State Compensation Law, 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html> (reprinted by the National Bureau of Statistics)
- Notes: Do not go and find the other side privately, and do not go to their family. Any private settlement can be read as you admitting the facts. Your chat records, call records and travel records are the material that proves you innocent, and deleting them deletes your own evidence. If you got the facts wrong, or if the report you made did not hold up later, that is not the crime of framing. This item makes no distinction between men and women. For the three items it opens out into, see items 33, 34 and 35 in this section (the party that fabricated the facts, insufficiency of evidence means an acquittal with appeal and retrial, and state compensation after custody calculated by the day).
### 6. Go and explain yourself as soon as something happens: a voluntary surrender cuts the sentence by up to 40%, and letting the case go out of time is a dead end
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Go yourself to the public security organ to report, or stay where you are and call the police, and then describe what happened truthfully. The hard part is holding out against the thought that you should wait a while longer and nobody will ever look into it.
- In plain language: A voluntary surrender can cut the sentence by up to 40%, and where the circumstances are minor the punishment can be remitted altogether. If you only tell the truth after they have come for you, that still cuts the sentence by up to 20%. Running away and refusing to admit anything gets you neither. Hiding for a few years so the case goes stale does not work either: once a case has been opened, the time you spend in hiding does not count towards the limitation period.
- Benefit: Article sixty-seven of the Criminal Law: a person who reports himself of his own accord after committing a crime and truthfully states his own crime has surrendered voluntarily. A person who surrenders voluntarily may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment, and where the crime is minor the punishment may be remitted. A person who did not report himself but truthfully stated his own crime (a confession) may be given a lighter punishment; where a truthful statement prevents especially serious consequences, the punishment may be mitigated. The two supreme courts' sentencing guidelines set out the percentages in black and white. The baseline sentence is the term that comes out of the facts of the case first, and each percentage below is a reduction from that baseline. A voluntary surrender can reduce the baseline sentence by up to 40%; where the crime is minor it can reduce it by more than 40% or the punishment may be remitted under the law. Within a confession, truthfully stating your own crime reduces it by up to 20%, truthfully stating a same-kind but heavier offence that the judicial organs do not yet know about reduces it by 10% to 30%, and truthfully stating in a way that prevents especially serious consequences reduces it by 30% to 50%. A guilty plea coupled with a request for punishment reduces it by up to 30%; where there is also a voluntary surrender, a major confession, restitution or compensation, or forgiveness by the victim, it reduces it by up to 60%, and where the crime is minor by more than 60% or the punishment may be remitted under the law. The limitation period for prosecution falls into four bands according to the maximum statutory sentence: 5 years, 10 years, 15 years and 20 years (Article eighty-seven of the Criminal Law). But Article eighty-eight of the Criminal Law states that once the procuratorate, the public security organ or the state security organ has opened a case for investigation, or once the court has accepted the case, evading investigation or trial is not subject to the limitation period (nationwide, the sentencing ranges in force from 2021-7-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大). Supreme People's Court Gazette. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Notes: The person who benefits from this item is you. A voluntary surrender is only a mitigating circumstance, the crime is still found, and the court decides how much comes off; the sentencing guidelines require leniency to be applied strictly for violent crimes and drug crimes. Traffic accidents have their own calculation. Staying at the scene, helping the injured and reporting to the public security organ counts as reporting yourself of your own accord, but because these are statutory duties in any case, leniency is applied strictly. Reporting yourself after driving away still counts as a surrender, but the heavier of the two statutory bands is used as the baseline, and only then is it decided whether to reduce it and by how much. How to do that: see item 1 in this section (after a traffic accident, stop, help the injured and call the police). Getting a lawyer first and telling the truth do not conflict: see item 5 in this section (get a lawyer first if you are accused). What the lawyer checks for you is whether the written record is right, not whether you can come up with a better story.
### 7. After drinking, do not touch the steering wheel, not on an electric scooter and not even to move the car a short way
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A little money. A hired driver or a taxi costs several tens of yuan. Agree before the gathering who is not drinking that day. The hard part is holding your ground when people at the table press you to drink.
- In plain language: Drive after drinking and your licence is taken away for 6 months and you are fined one or two thousand yuan. A second offence brings detention and revocation. Drunk driving revokes the licence straight away, bars you from taking the test again for 5 years, and is pursued as the crime of dangerous driving, which leaves you with a criminal record. That record affects political review and some jobs. Insurance does not cover it either: if you drive after drinking, car insurance pays nothing. 「Just moving it a short way」 counts as driving.
- Benefit: Driving after drinking results in your driving licence being withdrawn for 6 months and a fine of 1000 to 2000 yuan. A repeat offence brings detention of up to 10 days and revocation of the licence. Drunk driving revokes the licence outright, bars you from taking the test again for 5 years, and is pursued as the crime of dangerous driving, carrying a short custodial sentence and a fine; a short custodial sentence means a few months locked up. Commercial drivers get heavier treatment: drink driving brings 15 days of detention, a 5000 yuan fine and a 5-year ban on driving; drunk driving brings a 10-year ban, and after that you can never drive commercially again. If drinking or drunk driving causes a serious accident that amounts to a crime, the ban on driving is for life. Commercial motor insurance pays nothing for driving after drinking or after taking drugs (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

### 18. Write a clear IOU when you lend money, and before you guarantee somebody else's loan decide whether you are willing to repay it for them

### 19. Enforcing your rights has a time limit: 3 years for the statute of limitations in a civil case and 1 year for labour arbitration, and the other side only has to say 「too late」

- In plain language: The statute of limitations in a civil case is 3 years, running from the day you knew your rights had been infringed. Once it has passed the court will not act of its own accord, and all the other side has to do is say 「too late」 in court: the money is still there, it just cannot be recovered. One demand message that leaves a trace is enough to restart the 3 years. Labour disputes have their own rules, and the arbitration limitation period is 1 year.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that「向人民法院请求保护民事权利的诉讼时效期间为三年」. It runs from「权利人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害以及义务人之日起计算」, that is, from the day you knew you had been wronged and knew whom to go to. The longest period is 20 years from the day the right was infringed. Once the period has expired,「义务人可以提出不履行义务的抗辩」. The court will not examine the limitation period of its own accord, but if the other side raises it in court it takes effect: the money is still there, it just cannot be recovered. ### 33. If somebody reports you on facts they made up, you can ask for them to be pursued: 5 days of detention where an administrative penalty applies, and 3 years or less where a crime applies

### 34. Insufficient evidence should mean an acquittal, and a confession forced out of you should be excluded; and after a conviction there is still a petition and a retrial
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Going through the procedure costs nothing, but a petition and a retrial both take a great deal of time. You normally need a lawyer, and legal fees start at several thousand yuan. If you cannot afford it you can apply for legal aid: see item 5 in this section (get a lawyer first if you are accused). The hard part is going on waiting round after round without giving up.
- In plain language: The threshold for a conviction is written into the Criminal Procedure Law: the evidence on which the case is decided must be proved true through the statutory procedure, and reasonable doubt has to be excluded taking all the evidence in the case together. Where that cannot be done, an acquittal is what should follow. A confession obtained by torture and testimony obtained by violent threat have to be excluded and cannot be the basis of a judgment. After the judgment takes effect, you, your legal representative and your immediate family can still petition.
- Benefit: Article fifty-five of the Criminal Procedure Law: evidence must be true and sufficient, which means that the evidence on which a conviction rests has been proved true through the statutory procedure, and that the facts found have, taking all the evidence in the case together, excluded any reasonable doubt. Excluding reasonable doubt means that, taking all the evidence together, the facts found must not leave open another explanation that could still be argued. Article fifty-six: statements by a suspect or defendant obtained by torture and other illegal methods, and witness statements and statements by victims obtained by violence, threat and other illegal methods, must be excluded. Where evidence that ought to be excluded is found during investigation, examination for prosecution or trial, it must be excluded in accordance with the law and may not be used as the basis of a recommendation to prosecute, a decision to prosecute or a judgment. Article two hundred, paragraph (three): where the evidence is insufficient and the defendant cannot be found to have committed the crime, an acquittal must be pronounced on the ground that the evidence is insufficient and the charge cannot be made out. Article two hundred and fifty-two gives you a route to a petition. A party, their legal representative, or an immediate family member may lodge a petition with the people's court or the people's procuratorate against a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect. But a petition does not suspend the enforcement of the judgment or ruling. Article two hundred and fifty-three lists the circumstances in which the people's court must retry the case (nationwide)

### 35. If the case is withdrawn, no prosecution is brought or you are acquitted after custody, apply for state compensation, and it is paid by the day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Apply to the organ that took the decision to detain you, arrest you, prosecute you or convict you.
- In plain language: Where a case is withdrawn after you have been detained or arrested, where no prosecution is brought, or where you are acquitted, you can apply for state compensation. The days you were held are paid day by day, and from 2026-5-18 that is 495.94 yuan a day, or about 180,000 yuan a year. Where the consequences are serious, a payment for mental distress is added on top. Where the offence was so trivial that it was not prosecuted, nothing is paid. Nor is anything paid where the limitation period has expired, where you were below the age, or where you gave a false statement yourself.
- Benefit: The State Compensation Law sets out several circumstances in Article seventeen in which the victim has the right to compensation. The first is detention: a citizen was detained in breach of the Criminal Procedure Law, or was lawfully detained but the statutory time limit was exceeded, and afterwards it is decided to withdraw the case, not to prosecute, or a judgment declares the person not guilty and ends the criminal pursuit. The second is arrest: after an arrest measure is taken against a citizen, it is decided to withdraw the case, not to prosecute, or a judgment declares the person not guilty and ends the criminal pursuit. The third is retrial: the case is retried through the trial supervision procedure and the person is found not guilty although the sentence in the original judgment has already been carried out. The trial supervision procedure is the procedure for taking a judgment that has already taken legal effect and hearing it again. Article thirty-three: where a citizen's personal freedom is infringed, the daily compensation is calculated at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees. Article thirty-five: where mental harm is caused, the person responsible must, within the scope of the effect of the infringing act, eliminate the effect, restore the person's reputation and apologise, and where serious consequences result must pay a corresponding payment for mental distress. Article nineteen lists six circumstances in which the state does not compensate. First, the person was detained or convicted because they gave a false confession themselves or fabricated other evidence of guilt. Second, a person who bears no criminal responsibility under Article seventeen or eighteen of the Criminal Law was detained, such as a person below the age of criminal responsibility, or a person with a mental disorder who causes harm while unable to recognise or control their own conduct. Third, a person whom the Criminal Procedure Law provides is not criminally responsible was detained. This includes cases where the circumstances are plainly trivial and the harm is small, so that it is not treated as a crime, and where the limitation period for prosecution has expired, and where the suspect has died. It also includes a minor offence after which the procuratorate decides not to prosecute, which is what is usually meant by relative non-prosecution, also called discretionary non-prosecution. It also includes non-prosecution after a minor has passed the probationary period of conditional non-prosecution, and non-prosecution after a settlement is reached with the victim. Fourth, personal conduct of case-handling personnel that is unrelated to the exercise of their duties. Fifth, harm caused by deliberate acts of your own, such as injuring or harming yourself. Sixth, any other circumstance provided by law. Article seven of the 2015 judicial interpretation on criminal compensation from the two supreme courts confirms it again: where a person who bears no criminal responsibility and a person dealt with by relative non-prosecution are held in custody, the state does not compensate. But where, after prosecution, the court wrongly sentences the person to criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment and that has been carried out, the period of continued detention after the judgment became final must still be compensated. Article eight of that interpretation: where the case-handling organ refuses to compensate on the ground that 「you gave a false confession yourself」 or 「you injured yourself yourself」, the burden of proof is on the case-handling organ. ### 36. As the victim claiming compensation, go through 12315, a lawsuit or a lawyer; do not go on your own to an appointment the other side sets, and do not tie "I will pay" to "I will not make it public" in one sentence

- Cost: No money. You put it in through 12315, by suing in court, or by instructing a lawyer. If you meet the other side to talk, record the whole conversation. The hard part is holding back and not talking to the other side alone.

So use a route that leaves a record, do not go on your own to a place the other side names, and do not say out loud "I will pay" and "I will not make it public" as one condition.

### 38. The route of 「buy the insurance first, then do it」 is blocked from end to end in law: you get not one yuan of the money, and intentional homicide and insurance fraud are punished together

Where the applicant deliberately causes the death of the insured person, the insurer does not pay. Deliberately killing an heir loses the right of inheritance. The Criminal Law also makes this a separate offence of insurance fraud, punished together with intentional homicide, with the death penalty available at the top. You get not one yuan of the money.
- Benefit: Article thirty-four of the Insurance Law: a contract whose condition of payment is the death of the insured person is void unless the insured person has consented and recognised the amount of the insurance. The applicant is the person paying for the insurance, and the beneficiary is the person who receives the money when something happens. Article forty-three deals with deliberate harm. Where the applicant deliberately causes the death, disability or illness of the insured person, the insurer is not liable to pay the insurance benefit. Where the applicant has paid premiums for two years or more, the cash value is refunded to the other rights holders. Where a beneficiary deliberately causes the death, disability or illness of the insured person, or deliberately attempts to kill the insured person, that beneficiary loses the right to benefit. Where it is especially especially large, it is 10 years or more. Paragraph two: where conduct of that kind also constitutes another crime, it is punished under the rule of combined punishment for several crimes, which means the sentences for several crimes are worked out together. ### 40. Do not hand money or gift cards to the people handling a case or enforcing the law: bribery is punished for the giver too, and bribing supervision, enforcement or judicial staff is one of the cases treated as heavier

- In plain language: "Spend a little money and sort it out" is a separate crime under the Criminal Law, and the person caught is not only the one who took the money. Bribery carries 3 years or less. A serious case carries 3 to 10 years, and a particularly serious case 10 years or more or life imprisonment. Bribing supervision, administrative enforcement or judicial staff is one of the cases the law expressly treats as heavier. Money handed over because you were extorted, where you received no improper benefit, is not bribery.
- Benefit: Article three hundred and eighty-nine of the Criminal Law defines bribery:「为谋取不正当利益，给予国家工作人员以财物的，是行贿罪」. ### 41. Turn on the recorder for phone calls and meetings that could turn: you do not need the other person's consent first for a conversation you are party to
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- In plain language: For a loan, a debt owed to you, compensation, a refund or a labour dispute, what was said on the phone can be clear at the time and leave nothing behind, and a few months later the two sides can each tell a different story. Article ninety: audio-visual materials and electronic data that raise doubts may not be used on their own to find the facts. ### 42. When something happens at a scene, film the wide shot first, then how things are positioned, then the damage and the injuries, and do not delete the original photographs and video
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Hold your phone and film continuously for several tens of seconds to a few minutes. Make sure you are safe first, and do not delay helping or dealing with it.
- In plain language: If you only film a close-up of one scratch on a car or one object on the ground, nobody will know a few days later where it was. First film a wide shot that takes in the junction, the house number, the vehicle, the people and the surroundings, then film how things are positioned in relation to each other, then film the details. If you have to move anything or move the vehicle, film it before you do. Keep the original photographs and the original video on your phone, and do not keep only a copy that has been through a messaging app.
- Benefit: Article sixty-six of the Civil Procedure Law lists physical evidence, audio-visual materials and electronic data as evidence. Article fourteen of the Supreme People's Court rules on evidence in civil litigation lists images, audio and video as electronic data. Article fifteen requires the original of electronic data to be produced, so using a video as evidence means producing the original carrier. Photographs and video preserve the state of a scene while it is still changing, and can then be matched against the police report, the repair record, the medical record and what witnesses say. The order of 「wide shot, positions, detail」 comes from experience; the law does not prescribe it (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2023, amended, in force 2024-1-1). Civil Procedure Law, 民事诉讼法（第六十六条）, full text reprinted by the Shanghai Municipal Development and Reform Commission. <https://fgw.sh.gov.cn/ys-syjf-zc-2.4.1-h5/20240408/073febdb2b04464390546232d4089f51.html> ; Supreme People's Court (2019, amended). - Notes: The people who benefit are you and your family. The grade is C because the order of filming is only drawn from experience; the article only says that photographs and video can be evidence and that the original has to be produced. If somebody is hurt, if there is a fire or a gas leak, or if any other danger is still there, help first and get clear first, and do not delay that to take photographs. For what to do at the scene of a traffic accident, see item 1 in this section (after a traffic accident, stop, help the injured and call the police).
### 43. If you are being abused at home: call the police first so that there is a record of the officers sent, then apply to the court for a personal safety protection order; you do not have to divorce first, and it costs nothing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Applying for a personal safety protection order costs no court fee, and no security has to be deposited. What it costs is the time spent calling the police, having an injury assessed and writing the application. The hardest part is saying this out loud to your own family.
- In plain language: Among women killed worldwide, more than one third were killed by a partner, and it is often the end of long-term domestic abuse. Among women who are beaten, those whose throats have been squeezed are more than 7 times as likely to be killed later as those whose throats have not been. So call the police after an assault, and then apply to the court for a protection order. The court generally answers within 72 hours.
- Benefit: Start with the risk. Taking the data of 66 countries together, which is what a systematic review does. Of the cases of women killed, 38.6% were done by an intimate partner, and the figure for men is 6.3%. The authors say that murders of this kind are usually the last step in a long period of abuse. Another study compared 506 homicide and attempted homicide cases with 427 women who were abused but did not come to that. Women whose partner had previously strangled them were 6.70 times as likely to face an attempted killing as women who had not been strangled (OR 6.70, 95% CI 3.91-11.49). The risk of being killed was 7.48 times (OR 7.48, 95% CI 4.53-12.35). Now the law. A victim and an immediate family member may report to the police. The public security organ must send officers in good time, stop the abuse, investigate and collect evidence, and help with medical treatment and injury assessment. Where the circumstances are relatively minor, the public security organ may issue a warning letter to the other party. A person who is suffering domestic violence, or who faces a real risk of it, may apply to the court for a personal safety protection order. Once the court accepts the case it must rule within 72 hours, and within 24 hours where the situation is urgent. The order may prohibit violence, and may prohibit harassment, following and contact with you and your immediate family, and it may require the other party to move out of your home. The order lasts at most 6 months, and it can be extended on application before it runs out. The application does not depend on a divorce. The court may issue it as soon as it finds a domestic violence 「存在较大可能性」. Where the other party breaches the order, the court may impose a fine of up to 1000 yuan and detention of up to 15 days. Where it amounts to a crime, it is dealt with as the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling. Regular insults, threats, following and harassment, and being left without food or warmth, also count as domestic violence
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2015). Anti-Domestic Violence Law, 反家庭暴力法（第二、十三、十五、十六、十九、二十三、二十八、二十九、三十、三十四条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7f9d60889> ; Supreme People's Court (2022). Provisions on Several Questions Concerning the Application of the Law in Handling Personal Safety Protection Order Cases, 关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定（法释〔2022〕17 号，第一、三、六、十、十二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/366021.html> ; Supreme People's Court (2016). Reply on Procedural Questions Concerning Personal Safety Protection Order Cases, 关于人身安全保护令案件相关程序问题的批复（法释〔2016〕15 号，第一、二项）, full text reprinted by the Pingyuan County People's Court. <http://qdzy.sdcourt.gov.cn/dzpyfy/393141/393283/1461545/index.html> ; Stöckl H et al. (2013). The global prevalence of intimate partner homicide: a systematic review. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(13)61030-2> ; Glass N et al. (2008). Non-fatal strangulation is an important risk factor for homicide of women. Journal of Emergency Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jemermed.2007.02.065>
- Notes: The people who mainly benefit are you and your family. The homicide figures come from outside China, and there are no published Chinese statistics on the same measure. Build up the evidence: the record of officers sent, a warning letter, the result of an injury assessment, medical records, a written guarantee from the other person, chat records and recordings can all be used. For recordings, see item 41 in this section (recordings). A protection order does not need a lawyer, and there is usually an answer within days. Divorce or compensation is a separate case, where six months or more in the ordinary procedure at first instance is normal and legal fees are yours to pay. A victim of domestic violence may apply for legal aid, and the court may defer, reduce or waive the court fee. A protection order is not a guarantee of personal safety; if there is danger in front of you, call 110 first. Where the victim is forced or frightened and cannot apply themselves, an immediate family member, the public security organ, the women's federation, the residents' committee or the villagers' committee may apply on their behalf. If the person being abused is a friend or a colleague, you can go with them to the police and to hospital. If you see somebody in a stranger's home being beaten, calling 110 is enough. Do not step in and pull them apart yourself, and the reason is in item 10 in this section (call the police when a conflict starts).
### 44. If a member of your family has run up gambling debts, do not rush to pay them for them: the law does not protect gambling debts, and money borrowed to gamble is not a joint marital debt
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is holding out when the creditor comes to the door and your family cries and pleads. If the creditor really does sue, you have to respond to the case.
- In plain language: Money owed at a gambling table is not protected by the courts. Nor is money that a creditor lent knowing it was going to be used for gambling. Money one spouse borrowed for gambling cannot be counted as a debt the two of you have to pay together. Without a trial, the court cannot make the spouse who did not borrow the money answer for it.
- Benefit: A 2017 notice from the Supreme People's Court sets out four points. A debt incurred by one party in gambling, drug taking and other illegal and criminal activity is not protected by law. Where the creditor knew or should have known that the money was borrowed to gamble or to take drugs, and lent it anyway, it is not protected by law. Where one party borrows in their own name and uses the money for illegal and criminal activity, and demands that it be treated as a joint marital debt, the court does not support that. Without a judicial procedure, the party who did not borrow cannot be required to bear civil liability (nationwide, in force from 2017)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Supreme People's Court (2017). Notice on the Proper Handling of Cases Involving Marital Debt According to Law, 关于依法妥善审理涉及夫妻债务案件有关问题的通知（法〔2017〕48 号，第二、四条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/36982.html>
- Notes: The grade is B because this is an internal court notice with no figures in it, and whether the creditor knew has to be shown with evidence. The notice was issued under the Marriage Law as it stood at the time; for how a joint marital debt is recognised after the Civil Code came into force, see item 12 in section 10 (a large sum borrowed by a spouse). Money the creditor did not know was going to gambling still has to be repaid, only the person who borrowed it repays. To show that the money went on gambling you need the transfer records, the records of the gambling platform, or the materials from the public security organ, so keep them as you go. If the creditor really does sue, six months or more in the ordinary procedure at first instance is normal, and three months under the summary procedure. Each side pays its own legal fees, and the losing side does not bear the other's. If the creditor blocks the door or detains somebody, see item 15 in section 9 (do not detain anybody to collect a debt) and call the police. If the person himself has gambled to the point of wanting to die, see item 37 in section 1 (gambling, borrowing to keep gambling, call 12356 first). The people who mainly benefit are the spouse and immediate family.
