# 1. Do not die young

This section covers three things: accidental and violent deaths such as car crashes, fires and poisoning; the handful of vaccines and screenings with the hardest evidence behind them; and which signals from your body mean you should go and get checked. The figures in the benefit column come from trials that randomised people into groups, from studies that followed a large number of people for many years, or from official statistics. The cost column is the author's rough estimate at going-market prices, there for comparing options against each other; do not quote them.

### 1. Wear a seat belt, in the front and in the back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. It adds 2 seconds each time you get in the car.
- In plain language: A front-seat passenger with a seat belt on has about half the chance of being killed in a crash. In the front seat of a light truck the fall is 60%. In the United States in 2022, half of the people who died in a vehicle and whose belt use could be determined were not wearing one.
- Benefit: The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) estimates that a seat belt lowers the risk of fatal injury for a front-seat car passenger by 45%. For the front seat of a light truck it is 60%. Among people who died in a passenger vehicle in the United States in 2022, 50% of those whose belt use could be determined were not wearing one. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts the reduction in death risk for people inside the vehicle at up to 50%. WHO estimates that 248,099 people died from road traffic injuries in China in 2021, which is 17.4 per 100,000 people
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries> ; WHO GHO RS_196/RS_198 (China, 2021). <https://ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/RS_196?$filter=SpatialDim%20eq%20%27CHN%27>
- Notes: The back seat needs one too. In the same report, 60% of the people killed in the second row were not belted. The two figures, 45% and 60%, are estimates the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) calculated from its crash database, citing Kahane 2015. They are not from a randomised trial. WHO's China death count is modelled, and it runs several times higher than the figure registered by the public security traffic police. The two cover different populations, so only the WHO figure is used here.
### 2. Wear a helmet on a motorcycle or an electric bike, and fasten the strap
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A helmet costs 100 to 300 yuan. Putting it on takes 5 seconds each time.
- In plain language: A motorcycle rider with a helmet and a fastened strap has about 40% lower risk of dying in a crash. Head injuries are about 70% lower. The strap has to be tight. A helmet hanging loose on the head does not count.
- Benefit: Cochrane pooled several studies together. Helmet use among motorcycle riders lowered the risk of death by 42%. The original value is OR 0.58, credible range 95% CI 0.50 to 0.68. Head injury fell 69% (OR 0.31, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.38)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Liu BC et al (2008). Helmets for preventing injury in motorcycle riders. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004333.pub3>
- Notes: The strap has to be fastened. A helmet left hanging on the handlebars does not count. The figures above come from motorcycles. There is no direct study of electric bikes, so they are carried over, because head injuries after a crash happen the same way.
### 3. Fit smoke alarms; add a carbon monoxide alarm if you burn coal or gas indoors in winter
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A smoke alarm costs 30 to 100 yuan. A carbon monoxide alarm costs 50 to 150 yuan. After it is fitted, replace the battery once a year.
- In plain language: A working smoke alarm cuts the chance of dying in a home fire by about 60%. In 40% of fires people are asleep when the fire starts, and smell alone will not wake them. If you burn coal or gas indoors in winter, add a carbon monoxide alarm. In 2018 about 11,500 people in China died of carbon monoxide poisoning, and 70% of those deaths in the three winter months happened at home.
- Benefit: A study in North Carolina took a batch of residential fires and looked back at the households that had one and the households that did not. A working smoke alarm in the home lowered the risk of death by about three fifths. The original value is OR 0.39, credible range 95% CI 0.18 to 0.83. In fatal fires in occupied homes in the United States from 2018 to 2020, 24% of the homes had no smoke alarm, and 41% of the people were asleep when the fire started. China reported 11,523 carbon monoxide poisoning deaths in 2018. Of those deaths, 72.59% happened at home in month 12, 67.42% in month 1 and 66.48% in month 2
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Marshall SW et al (1998). Fatal residential fires: who dies and who survives? JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.279.20.1633> ; USFA (2022). Fatal Fires in Residential Buildings (2018-2020), Topical Fire Report Series 22(2). <https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v22i2.pdf> ; You J et al (2020). Number of Deaths due to Carbon Monoxide Poisoning by Month and by Place of Death — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2020.008>
- Notes: The reason for the B: on the smoke alarm side there is only one study, and its method was to look back at households that had a fire and households that did not. It was not a randomised trial. There is no study of carbon monoxide alarms lowering mortality on their own. The benefit here is inferred from the pattern that poisoning deaths cluster in winter and in the home.
### 4. Replace gas hoses and cookers when they reach end of life, do not repipe the gas yourself, and turn down the gas company when it comes selling
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A compliant gas hose costs a few tens of yuan. Replace the cooker every few years. Check whether it has passed its expiry date while you are at it.
- In plain language: A compliant hose costs a few tens of yuan. Replacing it at the end of its life is a duty of the user written into the Regulations on the Administration of Urban Gas. Repiping the gas yourself or taking out the gas meter is forbidden by the same regulations. Those regulations also say a gas company cannot require you to buy its designated products and services. A salesman at your door saying the hose "must be replaced" can be refused outright.
- Benefit: The regulations make it a duty of the user to "replace in good time gas appliances and connecting pipes that the state has ordered withdrawn or that have reached the end of their service life": once a cooker or a hose reaches end of life, or the state withdraws it, you have to replace it. The regulations also list "installing, modifying or removing indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices without authorisation" as a forbidden act, and that covers repiping the gas and taking out the meter yourself. The regulations also state that a gas operator may not require a user to buy the products it designates, and may not require a user to accept the services it provides
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2010). Regulations on the Administration of Urban Gas, 城镇燃气管理条例（国务院令第 583 号）. Article 27: 「燃气用户应当遵守安全用气规则，使用合格的燃气燃烧器具和气瓶，及时更换国家明令淘汰或者使用年限已届满的燃气燃烧器具、连接管等」 — gas users must follow safe-use rules, use qualified gas appliances and cylinders, and replace in good time any gas appliance or connecting pipe that the state has ordered withdrawn or that has reached the end of its service life. The prohibited acts in Article 28 include 「（一）擅自操作公用燃气阀门」「（二）将燃气管道作为负重支架或者接地引线」「（四）擅自安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施和燃气计量装置」 — operating a public gas valve without authorisation, using a gas pipe as a load-bearing support or as an earthing conductor, and installing, modifying or removing indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices without authorisation. Article 20 says a gas operator may not 「（六）要求燃气用户购买其指定的产品或者接受其提供的服务」 — require a gas user to buy its designated products or accept the services it provides. Article 29 lets a user complain to the gas authority about charges and service, and 「有关部门应当自收到投诉之日起15个工作日内予以处理」 — the department must handle it within 15 working days of receiving the complaint. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1758214.htm> ; Shenzhen Municipal Housing and Construction Bureau (深圳市住房和建设局) (2024). Gas safety notes, 安全用气小知识（二）: 「立即关闭家里的燃气总阀门」「迅速开门、开窗进行通风」「一定不要开、关家里的电灯、抽油烟机等电器」「人员应该迅速撤离，转移到安全的地方，并且及时拨打燃气公司的报警电话」 — close the main gas valve at home, open the door and windows to ventilate, never switch the lights or the cooker hood on or off, and move people out to a safe place and call the gas company's emergency number. <https://zjj.sz.gov.cn/zcjzts/content/post_11882410.html> ; Zhangjiagang Municipal Emergency Management Bureau (张家港市应急管理局) (2024). Gas safety knowledge, 燃气安全使用知识: 「不要情急之下打开排气扇进行通风」「应远离现场之后才能打电话求助」 — do not open the exhaust fan in a hurry, and call for help only after you are clear of the scene. <https://www.zjg.gov.cn/zjgszwz/yjaqzs/202411/72b4fad7399c48378302460c6ce2d55b.shtml>
- Notes: The text of the regulation can be checked word for word, but it only says that replacing the hose on time is your duty. It does not say how many fewer accidents you get that way. No official figure was found for how much replacing hoses on a schedule cuts gas accidents, so this is graded C only. Some people come to the door in the name of a "safety inspection" and, once they have finished looking, sell a self-closing valve and an alarm. Article 20, paragraph (6) bars a gas company from making you buy the products it designates. You can refuse, and you can also complain. If you smell gas, close the main gas valve first, and for bottled gas close the valve on the cylinder. Then open the door and windows to ventilate, and do not open the exhaust fan. Do not switch lights, the cooker hood and other appliances on or off, and do not touch a lighter. Then get everyone out, leave the scene, and only then call the gas company's emergency number. Carbon monoxide poisoning is another matter, see section 13.

### 5. Do not pick, buy or eat wild mushrooms; no local way of telling poison works
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is giving up a seasonal wild food.
- In plain language: In 2025 alone there were 828 mushroom poisonings nationwide, 2165 people affected and 13 deaths. That year 34 species of mushroom not previously recorded as poisonous were identified. A silver needle does not detect poison, a mushroom that insects have eaten is not safe, and only brightly coloured mushrooms are poisonous. None of these claims holds.
- Benefit: China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents in 2025, involving 2165 people and 13 deaths. The case fatality rate was 0.6%, which means 0.6% of the people poisoned died. From 2019 to 2024 there were 276 to 676 incidents a year, and the case fatality rate ranged from 0.87% to 2.86%. In 2025 alone 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, of which 34 were recorded in China for the first time as causing poisoning
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Mushroom Poisoning Outbreaks — China, 2025. China CDC Weekly (2026): 「In 2025, China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents across 27 provincial-level administrative divisions (PLADs), affecting 2,165 individuals and causing 13 deaths - a case fatality rate of 0.6%, the lowest of the past six years. In total, 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, including 34 newly recorded in poisoning incidents in China.」「From 2019 to 2024, the annual number of incidents ranged from 276 to 676, and the case fatality rate ranged from 0.87% to 2.86%.」<https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2026.120> ; the 2024 annual report in the same journal <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2025.106>
- Notes: If you eat the wrong mushroom, induce vomiting at once, then take the rest of the mushroom, or a photograph of it, to hospital. Poisoning by toxic mushrooms comes in several types, and identifying which mushroom it was decides how the doctor treats you. New species are recorded as poisonous every year, so the assumption that "local people can tell them apart" does not hold in the first place. A silver needle test, boiling it with garlic, the claim that a mushroom insects have eaten is safe, and the claim that only bright colours are poisonous are all wrong.

### 6. Do not take an electric bike into the corridor or the lift, and do not charge it at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is parking at the shared charging point downstairs every time and walking a few tens of metres further.
- In plain language: The Ministry of Emergency Management's Regulations on Fire Safety Management of High-Rise Civil Buildings forbid taking an electric bike into a corridor or a lift, and forbid charging it at home. Failing to correct this brings a fine of 500 to 1000 yuan on an individual and 2000 to 10000 yuan on a business. The fine is the smaller problem. The larger one is that this fire burns along your only escape route.
- Benefit: The regulation of the Ministry of Emergency Management states the ban in writing: 「禁止在高层民用建筑公共门厅、疏散走道、楼梯间、安全出口停放电动自行车或者为电动自行车充电」, which means electric bikes may be neither parked nor charged in the public entrance hall, the escape corridor, the stairwell or the emergency exit of a high-rise civil building. For failure to correct it, a unit or individual not in business is fined 500 to 1000 yuan and a business is fined 2000 to 10000 yuan
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Emergency Management (应急管理部) (2021). Regulations on Fire Safety Management of High-Rise Civil Buildings, 高层民用建筑消防安全管理规定（应急管理部令第 5 号，第三十七条、第四十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/01/content_5628815.htm> ; Ministry of Emergency Management (应急管理部) (2023). Reply letter on proposal No. 00969 of the Fifth Session of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, 关于政协第十三届全国委员会第五次会议第00969号提案答复的函: 「2021年，全国共接报电动自行车火灾1.8万起，造成65人死亡、222人受伤，直接财产损失达2.21亿元，其中发生在建筑物内3782起，造成60人死亡、175人受伤」 — in 2021 the country handled 18,000 electric bike fires, causing 65 deaths, 222 injuries and direct property losses of 221 million yuan, of which 3,782 began inside buildings and caused 60 deaths and 175 injuries. <https://www.mem.gov.cn/gk/jytabljggk/zxwytadfzy/2022zx/202301/t20230119_440918.shtml> ; Ministry of Emergency Management (应急管理部) (2025). Press conference on day 15 of month 1 in 2025: 「火灾起数同比下降42.2%，伤亡人数下降超过90%」 — the number of fires fell 42.2% year on year, and the number of casualties fell by more than 90%. <https://www.mem.gov.cn/xw/xwfbh/2025n01y15xwfbh_6337/>
- Notes: Do not buy a modified battery, do not use a no-name charger, and do not leave it charging all night. Once a lithium battery catches fire, the whole thing is alight within seconds and it gives off severely toxic smoke. A bike parked in a corridor sets that fire on your only escape route. The figures the Ministry of Emergency Management published are 18,000 electric bike fires nationwide in 2021 and 65 deaths. Of those, 3,782 started inside buildings and killed 60 people, and nearly all the dead were indoors. After the 2024 campaign the number of fires fell 42.2% on the year and casualties fell by more than 90%, and the absolute figures for that year were not published.
### 7. Measure your blood pressure, and take drugs to bring it down to target if it is high
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A home blood pressure monitor costs 100 to 200 yuan. One reading takes 1 minute. Most blood pressure drugs cost a few yuan to a few tens of yuan a month. The hard part is carrying on with them when you have no symptoms.
- In plain language: For every 10 mmHg that blood pressure falls, the chance of a major cardiovascular event falls by about a fifth. Stroke falls 27%, heart failure 28%, and the chance of death 13%. Among people aged 35 to 75 in China, 44.7% have high blood pressure and only 7.2% have it properly controlled.
- Benefit: 123 trials with more than 610,000 people were pooled. For every 10 mmHg that systolic blood pressure falls, major cardiovascular events are about a fifth lower. The original value is RR 0.80, credible range 95% CI 0.77 to 0.83. Systolic blood pressure is the higher of the two numbers in a reading. Stroke is about 27% lower (RR 0.73). Heart failure is about 28% lower (RR 0.72). All-cause mortality fell 13% (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.84 to 0.91). China screened 1,700,000 people aged 35 to 75: 44.7% had high blood pressure. Among them, 44.7% knew they had it, 30.1% were being treated, and only 7.2% were under control
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ettehad D et al (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> ; Lu J et al (2017). Prevalence, awareness, treatment, and control of hypertension in China (China PEACE Million Persons Project). Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32478-9>
- Notes: Measure your blood pressure once first. Of every 10 people in China with high blood pressure, fewer than 1 is under control, so that single reading is one of the highest-value actions in this book. What counts as target (130 or 140) is still argued over, but "knowing you have high blood pressure and bringing it down" is not disputed.
### 8. After 35, if you are overweight, get one fasting glucose test, and repeat it every three years even if it is normal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A fasting glucose test costs a dozen-odd yuan. A glycated haemoglobin test costs a few tens of yuan. One trip to the hospital to draw blood is all it takes.
- In plain language: Drawing one tube of blood for a few tens of yuan can find prediabetes while you have no symptoms at all. The official United States screening advice is this: people aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese should be tested. If the result is normal, test again every three years. In the United States overweight starts at a body mass index of 25, and the line comes down to 23 for people of Asian descent; the Chinese standard counts 24 as overweight.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force is the body that sets official screening advice in the United States. Its current recommendation is to screen adults aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, even when they have no symptoms at all. Overweight means a body mass index ≥25, and obese means ≥30. For Asian Americans it recommends bringing the line down to a body mass index ≥23, because at the same body mass index people of Asian descent have a higher risk of diabetes. The Chinese industry standard counts a body mass index of 24.0 to 28.0 as overweight and ≥28.0 as obese. That recommendation carries a grade B. It also states: "for adults with normal blood glucose, screening every 3 years is reasonable"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Screening for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-for-prediabetes-and-type-2-diabetes>：「at a lower BMI (≥23) if the patient is Asian American」 ; General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2024). Explanation of the Core Knowledge on Managing Residents' Weight, 2024 edition, 居民体重管理核心知识（2024 年版）释义: 「根据《成年人体重判定》（WS/T 428-2013）行业标准……24.0≤BMI＜28.0 为超重，BMI≥28.0 为肥胖」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202407/P020240703481519509521.pdf>
- Notes: Finding prediabetes does not mean you have to take drugs. A good share of people can reverse it with lifestyle changes alone. Among Chinese adults only a minority have ever known they had diabetes. Many find out only once complications appear

### 9. Do not speed, and do not drink and drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Driving a little slower takes a few extra minutes on each trip. The hard part is drinking less at a dinner.
- In plain language: Every 1% higher average speed raises the chance of killing someone in a collision by about 4%. For alcohol there is no safe line where a little is fine. Risk starts to climb when the alcohol concentration in the blood is still low.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization (WHO) puts it this way: every 1% rise in average speed raises the risk of fatal accidents by 4%. The risk from drink-driving starts to rise from a very low blood alcohol concentration
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: The 1% and 4% for speed and death come from an accident model, not from a randomised trial. On drink-driving WHO gave a direction but no numbers. This section has not checked the original literature on how much drinking raises the risk by how much.
### 10. Use a child car seat for children under 4, and do not hold them in your arms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One costs 300 to 2000 yuan and lasts for years. Buckling the child in adds 1 minute on each trip.
- In plain language: An infant under one year old in a car seat has about 70% lower risk of fatal injury in a crash. A child aged 1 to 4 has about half the risk. Holding the child in your arms gives no such protection, and in a hard stop you cannot hold on either.
- Benefit: The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) estimates that a car seat lowers the risk of fatal injury for an infant under one year old in a car by 71%. For a child aged 1 to 4 the figure is 54%. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts the reduction in infant deaths at 71%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: Fix the seat as the instructions say. A wrong fit is the same as no fit. Under the age of one the seat goes in facing backwards, with the child looking toward the rear of the car. The figures above are the official United States estimates calculated from crash data.
### 11. If there are children at home, fit window and balcony limiters; window screens do not count as protection
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A window limiter or a child safety lock costs a few tens of yuan. Fitting it takes ten minutes.
- In plain language: A window limiter costs a few tens of yuan. New York City fits window guards free of charge in high-risk housing and sends someone to explain them. In one borough, reported child falls dropped by half over three years. New York then passed a law requiring landlords to fit guards in homes where children live. A window screen opens with one push. It does not count as protection.
- Benefit: New York City ran a programme called Children Can't Fly from 1972. It gave out window guards free in high-risk districts and sent someone to explain them. Reported falls in the Bronx fell 50% from 1973 to 1975. The city then amended its health code. Landlords must now fit window guards in any apartment where a child aged 10 or younger lives
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Spiegel CN, Lindaman FC (1977). Children can't fly: a program to prevent childhood morbidity and mortality from window falls. American Journal of Public Health: "Significant reduction in falls resulted, particularly in the Bronx, where reported falls declined 50 percent from 1973 to 1975"; the 1976 amendment to the New York City health code "to require that landlords provide window guards in apartments where children ten years old and younger reside". <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.67.12.1143>; (2021). Unintentional Window Falls in Children and Adolescents. Academic Pediatrics: 38,840 emergency department visits recorded by the national electronic injury surveillance system from month 1 of 2007 to month 8 of 2017, "The majority of falls occurred in children under the age of 6 and were related to falls from a second story or below". <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acap.2020.07.008>
- Notes: Do not put a bed, a sofa, or boxes where a child can climb up next to a window or a balcony. A window screen only keeps out mosquitoes. It comes off with one push, so do not treat it as a guard. High-rise living is not the only reason to fit one. In the US data, most falls happened on the second floor or lower.

### 12. Keep children in sight whenever they are near water; wear a life jacket when boating and swimming in open water
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A life jacket costs 50 to 200 yuan. It also takes the attention you spend watching the child.
- In plain language: A child in a life jacket who falls in the water has about half the chance of drowning. Drowning is still the leading cause of death for Chinese children aged 1 to 14. The rural drowning mortality rate is about twice the urban rate.
- Benefit: A study using United States Coast Guard data matched comparable people in pairs. Among recreational boaters who fell in the water, those wearing a life jacket had about half the risk of drowning death. The adjusted value is RR 0.51, credible range 95% CI 0.35 to 0.74. In China, drowning mortality under age 20 fell from 6.60 per 100,000 people in 2013 to 3.28 per 100,000 in 2021. The rural rate is about 2 times the urban rate. Drowning is still the leading cause of death for children aged 1 to 14. In 2021, drowning caused 31.1% of injury deaths among people aged 0 to 19, and road traffic caused 27.9%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Cummings P et al (2011). Association between wearing a personal floatation device and death by drowning among recreational boaters. Injury Prevention. <https://doi.org/10.1136/ip.2010.028688> ; Li Z et al (2023). Unintentional Drowning Mortality Among Individuals Under Age 20 — China, 2013–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2023.198> ; Zhou J et al (2024). Injury Mortality of Children and Adolescents Aged 0–19 Years — China, 2010–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2024.057>
- Notes: The grade A covers only the life jacket. "Keeping children in sight" is a general consensus. On its own it is only grade C, with no randomised trial, and there will never be one. In China, drowning deaths cluster in rural areas, in summer, and among males aged 15 to 19. Going onto the ice to play in winter: see item 41 (bore a hole and measure the thickness before you step onto outdoor ice).

### 13. Over 60, train balance and leg strength; fix up the bathroom and stairs at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Train 2 to 3 times a week, 30 minutes of tai chi or balance work each time. A non-slip mat, grab bars, and a night light in the bathroom cost a few tens to a few hundred yuan. The hard part is keeping it up for years.

### 15. Punctured by a nail or a splinter, or a wound with soil in it: get it treated the same day and ask about a tetanus shot

Blood in the urine that is visible only under a microscope carries the same meaning. The odds ratio is 20, credible range 95% CI 12 to 33. Over 60 that share is 1.6%, credible range 1.2 to 2.1
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Price SJ, Shephard EA, Stapley SA, Barraclough K, Hamilton WT (2014). Non-visible versus visible haematuria and bladder cancer risk: a study of electronic records in primary care. British Journal of General Practice, 64(626), e584-e589. <https://doi.org/10.3399/bjgp14X681409>
- Notes: "No pain" is the key point. ### 28. If erectile function fails, check the cardiovascular system first, and do not treat it as only "that kind of problem"

### 30. Use a condom for the whole of sex, and never share needles
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One costs a yuan or two. The hard part is not the money. It is using one every time, and putting it on before you start.
- In plain language: Using a condom for the whole of sex each time cuts the chance of passing HIV between men and women by more than 70%. "Most of the time" works about as badly as not using one at all. It also blocks syphilis, gonorrhoea and the like.
- Benefit: One study pooled 25 studies with 10676 heterosexual couples. In each couple one partner had HIV and the other did not, which is called serodiscordant. Compared with people who never use a condom, people who used one every time had about 71% lower risk of HIV transmission. The value is RR 0.29, credible range 95% CI 0.20 to 0.43. Compared with people who use them only now and then, the risk was about 77% lower (RR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13 to 0.40). The authors conclude that condoms are not perfect, but consistent use "can cut HIV transmission by more than 70%". The same protection covers syphilis, gonorrhoea, chlamydia and other sexually transmitted infections
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Giannou FK, Tsiara CG, Nikolopoulos GK, et al. (2016). - Notes: The gap between irregular use and no use at all is small. A condom works only if you use one every time, for the whole of sex. Sharing needles is another route of transmission, independent of sex and highly efficient. Injecting drugs, tattoos and body piercing all count. People at high risk can also use preexposure prophylaxis (PrEP), which means taking medicine beforehand to block infection. Pooling 18 studies, in every trial where more than 70% took the pills on time, people taking PrEP had about 70% lower risk of HIV infection than people taking a placebo. The value is a risk ratio of 0.30, credible range 95% CI 0.21 to 0.45. In the trials where fewer people took the pills on time, no protective effect showed. PrEP is a prescription drug. ### 33. Do not treat surviving as a safety net: after drinking pesticide or inhaling coal gas, emergency care can save the life but not the lungs or the brain

### 36. Do not pick up, take apart or sell metal parts of unknown origin, and keep away from anything carrying the trefoil warning sign

- In plain language: Radiation has no smell, no colour and no temperature, and most radioactive sources are a piece of ordinary metal, so "it looks all right" is not a test. In 1987 in Brazil someone cut up a radiotherapy machine from a disused clinic as scrap, and 4 people died in the end.

### 41. Bore a hole and measure the thickness before you step onto outdoor ice; below 15 cm do not go on, and stay off river ice and the edge of ice-fishing holes

- Notes: Every official guideline says there is no ice that is a hundred per cent safe. Meeting the thickness is only a threshold, not a guarantee. The most dangerous times are the first cold of early winter, when the ice has just formed, and the first thaw of early spring. If you do go through, do not take your clothes off: the air held in thick clothing helps keep you up. Turn back the way you came, because the ice there is the strongest. Put an arm on the ice, drive the spike into it, push with your feet and drag yourself out. Once you are out, do not stand up; roll flat away from the hole, then get warm as fast as you can, and go to hospital if you are badly frozen. If you see someone go through the ice, do not run out and pull them out yourself. What to do is in item 25 in section 13 (do not go into the water yourself when you see someone drowning): shout for help, call 110, and then hand over a pole or throw a rope. The people who benefit are mainly you, and any children you take out onto the ice.
