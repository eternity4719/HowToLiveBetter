# 13. 紧急情况：先做什么

本节讲出了事先做什么、再做什么、什么不要做。急救和野外的条目算的是特定死因和活下来的比例。威胁和骗局的条目算的是钱和人身自由。两类数字不互相换算。条目的顺序按「这事多常见 × 做对做错差多少条命」从高到低排。这些条目首先是给你自己和配偶、直系亲属用的。心脏骤停有 79.2% 发生在家里，你最可能救的就是家里人。对朋友同事次之。对陌生人也不是一点好处没有，但这一档最低，还可能被讹、被卷进案子。所以这类条目把保护自己的动作和免责的条款写在前面。

本节只收几分钟内要做的动作。认出骗局、被人拿把柄要钱，处置要按天算，不在这一节。反诈的硬规则和七种高发骗局见第 8 节第 3 条（反诈硬规则）。被人拿隐私照片、裸聊录像要钱见第 8 节第 32 条（对方拿把柄来要钱）。钱已经转出去了，立刻打 110 或 96110 要求止付，见第 8 节第 2 条（止付）。

# 13. Emergencies: what to do first

This section says what to do beforehand, what to do next, and what not to do. The first aid and outdoor entries measure specific causes of death and the share of people who survive. The threats and scam entries measure money and personal freedom. The two sets of numbers are not converted into each other. The entries are ordered by how common the emergency is and how many lives the right or wrong action saves. These entries are written first for you, your spouse and your immediate family. 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home, so the person you are most likely to save is someone in your own home. Friends and colleagues come next. A stranger still gains something, but that group is the lowest, and you can be extorted or pulled into a case. So these entries put the steps that protect you, and the clauses that limit your liability, first.

This section only covers actions that take minutes. Recognising a scam, or being blackmailed with money, is handled on a scale of days and does not belong here. See item 3 in section 8 (hard anti-fraud rules) for the hard rules and the seven most common scams. See item 32 in section 8 (being blackmailed with private material) for demands for money backed by private photos or nude videos. If the money has already gone out, call 110 or 96110 at once and ask for the payment to be stopped. See item 2 in section 8 (stopping a payment).

### 1. Someone collapses and is not breathing: press hard on the centre of the chest, send someone else to call 120 and fetch an AED
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. A cardiopulmonary resuscitation course from the Red Cross or a hospital runs half a day to a day, and one course is enough for years. You can do it without any training: stack your hands at the midpoint of the line between the nipples, press down hard about 2 times a second, and do not stop.
- In plain language: The person you are most likely to press on is your own family. 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home. Where someone presses, the share discharged alive rises from 3.9% to 16.1%. If they do not answer and are not breathing, press straight away. Put your hands at the midpoint between the nipples, stack them, press hard, 2 times a second, and do not stop. Let the second person call 120 and fetch the AED. Your hands do not stop.
- Benefit: Pooling 79 studies and 142,740 people together (meta-analysis): among people whose heart stopped outside hospital (out-of-hospital cardiac arrest), only 7.6% in total were discharged alive. Where a bystander performed cardiopulmonary resuscitation, the share discharged alive rose from 3.9% to 16.1%. 53% had someone present who saw the collapse happen, and only 32% actually received compressions from a bystander. China's BASIC-OHCA registry recorded 38,227 emergency cases taken on by ambulance crews. In 30,282 of them (79.2%) the cardiac arrest happened at home. Among this group the bystander compression rate was 20.3%, and 1.2% were discharged alive. The order is: compress first, then let the second person call and fetch the AED. Once the machine is switched on, do what its voice tells you
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Sasson C et al (2010). Predictors of survival from out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576> ; Zheng J et al (2023). Incidence, process of care, and outcomes of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in China: a prospective study of the BASIC-OHCA registry. The Lancet Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00173-1> ; National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典第一百八十四条. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: Do not pinch the philtrum first, do not look for the family first, and do film it first. Do not hold back from pressing hard because you are afraid of breaking ribs. Article 184 of the Civil Code states that a person who gives emergency rescue voluntarily and injures the person helped bears no civil liability.

### 2. An elderly person falls or someone collapses: kneel down and speak to them, call 120, and do not lift them straight away. Walking away from a stranger is legal too, and if you do stop, do not move them by hand
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Kneeling down to ask one question and making one call takes a few minutes. The hard part is holding back the hand that reaches out to lift them. With a stranger there is one more cost to count: calling 120 leaves your number, and you may get a call back or be questioned once. If the family really does target you, a court case runs from several months upwards. And even if you win in the end, you pay the legal fees yourself.
- In plain language: The main case here is an elderly person in your own family or someone you know. First check whether they are breathing. If not, press the chest as in item 1 in this section (pressing hard on the centre of the chest). Do not lift them even if they are breathing: they may have had a stroke or fainted before they fell, and lifting them roughly makes it worse. Kneel down and call to them, and ask whether they remember how they fell. Do not move anyone with back or lower back pain, or with a suspected fracture. Call 120 where they are.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Health (卫生部) guideline Technical Guidelines for Intervention in Falls among Older Adults (《老年人跌倒干预技术指南》) says two things. One is 「跌倒是我国 65 岁以上老年人伤害死亡的首位原因」 — falls are the leading cause of injury deaths among people over 65 in China. The other is 「发现老年人跌倒，不要急于扶起，要分情况进行处理」 — when you find an elderly person who has fallen, do not lift them in a hurry, handle the case according to its type. If the person is not conscious, call the emergency number at once. If there is vomiting, turn the head to one side and clear the mouth and nose. If breathing and heartbeat have stopped, start chest compressions at once. 「如需搬动，应保证平稳，尽量平卧」 — if the person has to be moved, keep the movement smooth and lay them flat. If the person is conscious, first ask whether they remember the fall, because 「如不能记起，可能为晕厥或脑血管意外」 — if they cannot remember it, it may be fainting or a cerebrovascular accident. With a severe headache, or a crooked mouth, unclear speech, or weakness in the limbs, 「立即扶起老年人可能加重脑出血或脑缺血，使病情加重」 — lifting the person up at once may worsen a brain haemorrhage or brain ischaemia and make the condition worse. With signs of a fracture, or back pain, or loss of bladder or bowel control, 「如无相关专业知识，不要随便搬动」 — without the relevant professional knowledge, do not move them at will. All of these cases require an immediate call to the emergency number. If the elderly person wants to get up on their own, you can help them stand up slowly, then 「坐、卧休息并观察，确认无碍后方可离开」 — let them rest sitting or lying down and watch them, and only leave once you have confirmed they are all right. On the fear of being extorted, the law says this. Article ninety of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China has two sentences. The first is 「当事人对自己提出的诉讼请求所依据的事实……应当提供证据加以证明」 — a party must provide evidence for the facts on which its own claims for relief are based. The second is 「在作出判决前，当事人未能提供证据或者证据不足以证明其事实主张的，由负有举证证明责任的当事人承担不利的后果」 — if, before the judgment is made, a party has not produced evidence or its evidence is insufficient to prove its factual claims, the party carrying the burden of proof bears the adverse consequence. Article one thousand one hundred and ninety-eight of the Civil Code reads: 「宾馆、商场、银行、车站、机场、体育场馆、娱乐场所等经营场所、公共场所的经营者、管理者或者群众性活动的组织者，未尽到安全保障义务，造成他人损害的，应当承担侵权责任。」 — where the operator or manager of business premises or a public place, or the organiser of a mass activity, has failed to meet the duty of safety protection and has caused harm to another person, it bears tort liability. Article one hundred and eighty-four of the Civil Code: 「因自愿实施紧急救助行为造成受助人损害的，救助人不承担民事责任」 — where harm is caused to the person helped by a voluntary emergency rescue, the rescuer bears no civil liability. The Supreme People's Court has published one typical case. A pharmacy gave cardiopulmonary resuscitation to a person in cardiac arrest and broke the person's ribs. The person helped was in hospital for 18 days and claimed more than 9,000 yuan. The court ruled that the rescuer bore no civil liability
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Ministry of Health (卫生部) (2011). Technical Guidelines for Intervention in Falls among Older Adults (《老年人跌倒干预技术指南》), on handling an elderly person after a fall. Full text PDF at the Guangdong Provincial Center for Disease Control and Prevention <http://cdcp.gd.gov.cn/gcdcdfiles/editor/uploadfile/20111027094832117.pdf>, and the full on-site handling section at the Guinan County People's Government reprint <https://www.guinan.gov.cn/lnb/zc1__zwgk/zc/aztfl/qt/content_27609778> ; National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典第一百八十三、一百八十四条. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (2023). Typical cases of the people's courts securing justice and efficiency and putting the core socialist values into practice (case thirteen, Qi v. Sun, dispute over the right to health, Kangping County People's Court, Liaoning). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/408162.html> ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (second revision of 2022, in force from 2022-4-10). Interpretation on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, 第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/353651.html> ; Article one thousand one hundred and ninety-eight of the Civil Code, full text reprinted above by the Supreme People's Procuratorate ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (revision of 2023, in force from 2024-1-1). Full text reprinted by the Shanghai Municipal Development and Reform Commission <https://fgw.sh.gov.cn/ys-syjf-zc-2.4.1-h5/20240408/073febdb2b04464390546232d4089f51.html> ; State Council (国务院) (2006). Measures on the Payment of Litigation Costs (State Council Order No. 481, articles six and twenty-nine). <https://www.gov.cn/ziliao/flfg/2006-12/29/content_483682.htm> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2025). - Notes: The B is because the guideline only says how to handle the case, and gives no figure for how many fewer deaths following it would prevent. Article 184 of the Civil Code covers harm caused while giving rescue, on the condition that you acted voluntarily and the situation was urgent. It does not cover 「自作主张把人塞进私家车送医」 — putting someone into your own car and driving them to hospital on your own initiative. Waiting for 120 is safer. After you call 120, do not hang up, and do what the dispatcher on the line tells you. This line of judgement is written first for elderly people in your own family and for people you know. For someone you meet on the street and do not know, walking away is not illegal, and it is also the least trouble: the law does not require you to rescue anyone. If you do stop, count what you pay layer by layer: the time, leaving your number, being found by the family, being sued, the case reaching your workplace, being filmed and put online. If you do decide to stop, the one thing you must not do is **move them by hand**, and the reason is medical: lifting someone roughly can worsen a brain haemorrhage or a spinal injury. There is one exception. If you are the one running the place or managing it, or you organised the activity, walking away can make you liable. The duty of safety protection in Article 1198 of the Civil Code holds you responsible for safety for the people in the room. For fall prevention itself, see item 13 in section 1 (training balance and leg strength). For the delayed symptoms after an elderly person has hit their head, see item 10 in this section. For the money after a rescuer is injured, see item 39 in this section. For keeping evidence and calling the police after being piled on online, see item 16 in section 8.
### 3. A crooked mouth, one weak arm and unclear speech: call 120 at once. Do not wait, and do not drive there yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Three actions, and one time through is enough to learn them: have him smile, raise both arms straight out, and say one complete sentence.
- In plain language: A crooked mouth, one arm that will not lift, and unclear speech: call 120 at once. If the drug that opens the blocked vessel is given within 3 hours of onset, the chance of recovering to the point of basically managing on your own is about 1.75 times that without the drug. Wait until 3 to 4.5 hours and it falls to 1.26 times. Past 4.5 hours no difference can be seen. Write down the exact time the symptoms started. Do not give water or medicine by mouth, and do not drive there yourself.
- Benefit: Individual patient data from 16 randomised trials of intravenous thrombolysis, covering 6,756 patients, were pooled (meta-analysis). Intravenous thrombolysis means giving a drug that opens the blocked vessel. Within 3 hours of onset, thrombolysis with alteplase gave an odds ratio of 1.75 for recovery to basically managing on your own (modified Rankin score 0–1), 95% CI 1.35 to 2.27 (credible range). The chance of that outcome is about 1.75 times that without thrombolysis. At 3 to 4.5 hours it is 1.26 (1.05 to 1.51). Past 4.5 hours it is 1.15 (0.95 to 1.40), and statistically no difference can be seen
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Emberson J, Lees KR, Lyden P, et al. (2014). Effect of treatment delay, age, and stroke severity on the effects of intravenous thrombolysis with alteplase for acute ischaemic stroke: a meta-analysis of individual patient data from randomised trials. Lancet, 384(9958), 1929-1935. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(14)60584-5>
- Notes: The earlier the thrombolysis, the better it works. Past 4.5 hours it is close to useless. Call 120 rather than driving yourself, because the ambulance takes you straight to a hospital with a stroke centre and warns them in advance so they can prepare. Write down the exact time the symptoms started, because the doctor needs it to decide whether thrombolysis is possible. Do not give water or medicine by mouth, and do not wait to 「休息一下看看」 — rest a bit and see how it goes

### 4. A sudden spinning feeling, unsteady standing, seeing double or one eye going black, or being unable to touch your own nose: treat it as a stroke and call 120
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. On top of the three actions of a crooked face, arms raised and speech (item 3 in this section, a crooked mouth and unclear speech), add two more. One is to have the person stand upright with the eyes closed and see whether they sway, then ask whether they see double or whether part of the field of view is missing. The other is to point at your own nose with a finger when you suspect a stroke, then close your eyes and point again.
- In plain language: Screening on the three signs of crooked face, weak arm and speech alone misses a bit over a tenth of strokes, because some strokes start with neither a crooked mouth nor a weak arm. Add unsteady walking, double vision, or a missing patch in the field of vision, and the missed rate falls to 4.4%. A sudden spinning feeling, standing unstably, seeing double, or being unable to touch your own nose all mean the same thing: call 120 and write down the time.
- Benefit: In 2014 the stroke centre at the University of Kentucky reviewed the records of 736 cases of acute ischaemic stroke (a stroke caused by a blocked vessel). At onset, 14.1% of the patients had none of the three symptoms of crooked face, weak arm and slurred speech (FAST). Adding unsteady walking, weak legs and visual symptoms to the screening brings the missed rate down from 14.1% to 4.4%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Aroor S, Singh R, Goldstein LB (2017). BE-FAST (Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time): Reducing the Proportion of Strokes Missed Using the FAST Mnemonic. Stroke, 48(2), 479-481. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.116.015169>
- Notes: A blocked vessel in the area of the cerebellum and brainstem (posterior circulation infarction) often causes neither a crooked mouth nor a weak arm. It only causes spinning, walking to one side, diplopia (seeing double), and choking on water or food, and it is most often mistaken for a neck problem, low blood sugar or an inner ear disorder. Missing the nose with your finger and not being able to stand with your eyes closed are signs that the cerebellum is involved. But no action rules a stroke out in reverse: if it comes on suddenly and does not settle, call 120 and write down the exact time of onset. The study looked at the records of one hospital only, and the authors say themselves that it needs further verification
### 5. One eye suddenly goes black as if a blind had been pulled down, even if it clears within minutes: go to the emergency department as a stroke the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Emergency assessment and the blood vessel checks take up half a day to a day.
- In plain language: One eye suddenly going black as if a blind had been pulled down means a vessel at the back of the eye is blocked, and the longer it stays blocked, the less of the retina can be saved. It is also a form of brain ischaemia, and a stroke is more likely over the next few days. Where people are assessed and start treatment on the same day, the share having another stroke within 90 days falls from 10.3% to 2.1%. Go that day to an emergency department with a stroke centre. Do not wait until tomorrow to book an eye clinic.
- Benefit: A 2021 scientific statement from the American Heart Association on central retinal artery occlusion (the main vessel at the back of the eye is blocked) says: 「急性视网膜中央动脉阻塞是急症」 — acute central retinal artery occlusion is a medical emergency. A care system should recognise it as an emergency and put it in order by severity. The longer the blood is cut off, the less of the retina can still be brought back. The Oxford EXPRESS study looked at two kinds of patient: minor stroke, and brain ischaemia whose symptoms clear on their own (called a transient ischaemic attack in medicine). After this group was switched to assessment on the same day and treatment starting on the same day, the share having another stroke within 90 days fell from 10.3% (32/310) to 2.1% (6/281)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Mac Grory B, Schrag M, Biousse V, et al. (2021). Management of Central Retinal Artery Occlusion: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Stroke, 52(6), e282-e294. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000366> ; Rothwell PM, Giles MF, Chandratheva A, et al. (2007). Effect of urgent treatment of transient ischaemic attack and minor stroke on early recurrent stroke (EXPRESS study): a prospective population-based sequential comparison. Lancet, 370(9596), 1432-1442. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(07)61448-2>
- Notes: The B is because EXPRESS was not a randomised trial. It compares two periods, before and after the change of practice, in the same population, and other conditions may also have changed between the two periods, which cannot be separated out. A black-out of vision lasting a few minutes that then clears by itself is most often put down to being tired or to low blood sugar. It is a form of transient ischaemic attack, and the next few days are exactly the window when strokes cluster. Losing vision in one eye with no pain, no redness and no watering of the eye deserves particular attention. Do not wait until tomorrow to book an eye clinic. Go straight to the emergency department of a hospital with a stroke centre.

### 6. One eye swollen and painful and red, with a ring of rainbow around lights, plus headache, nausea and vomiting: go to the eye emergency department the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Measuring the pressure inside the eye and examining the anterior chamber at the eye emergency department takes half a day.
- In plain language: This is an acute attack of glaucoma. The fluid inside the eye cannot drain away and the pressure shoots up. The longer you wait, the more of the optic nerve the pressure damages, and damage there does not come back. During the Wuhan lockdown, patients waited an average of 241 hours before they were treated, and 21.87% of them went blind. In the same period of the following year there was no lockdown, the average wait was 121 hours, and 7.84% went blind.
- Benefit: An eye hospital in Wuhan compared two groups of patients with acute primary angle-closure glaucoma over two periods. In the 76 days of the 2020 lockdown there were 54 people and 64 eyes, and in the same period of 2021 there were 46 people and 51 eyes, with the two groups similar in their baseline characteristics. From the onset of symptoms to the start of treatment, the lockdown group took 241.84±211.95 hours and the comparison group 121.53±96.12 hours (P=0.001). The pressure inside the eye at the visit was 52.63±12.45 mmHg against 45.16±9.79 mmHg (P=0.001). The share going blind was 21.87% against 7.84%. Glaucomatous changes in the optic nerve were found in 20/64 eyes (31.25%) against 7/51 eyes (13.73%) (P=0.03). A hospital in Korea reviewed 50 eyes that had had one attack and later had the lens removed. A year later 25 of them (50%) had lost part of the visual field, to varying degrees. The longer the stretch from the onset of symptoms to getting the pressure down, the more likely the loss of field is to persist (P=0.005). A separate prospective study laid 43 people with shallow anterior chambers face down in a dark room for 1 hour. The pressure rose clearly in both eyes (P<0.01). In the dominant eye the median rise was 3.60 mmHg, and in the other eye 2.70 mmHg (P<0.05)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhou L, Wu S, Wang Y, Bao X, Peng T, Luo W, Ortega-Usobiaga J (2022). Clinical presentation of acute primary angle closure during the COVID-19 epidemic lockdown. Frontiers in Medicine, 9, 1078237. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2022.1078237> ; Sung MS, Kim HJ, Park SW (2023). Predictors of long-term visual field outcome after an episode of acute primary angle closure. Clinical & Experimental Ophthalmology, 51(4), 291-299. <https://doi.org/10.1111/ceo.14206> ; Wang J, Wang J, Ng TK, Huang C (2025). Asymmetric intraocular pressure changes in dominant and contralateral eyes: the dark room prone provocative test. Seminars in Ophthalmology, 40(4), 325-331. <https://doi.org/10.1080/08820538.2024.2443972>
- Notes: There are two reasons for the B. First, the two Wuhan groups were compared by going back through old records, and the lockdown itself brought other differences that cannot be separated from delay. Second, the Korean group is only 50 eyes, and it is retrospective as well. The trigger is not screen time. It is two things together: the pupil dilates in the dark, and looking down or lying face down for a long time pushes the lens forward. People who already have a shallow anterior chamber are easily triggered this way, so the real high-risk group is people over 50, people who are far-sighted, and people with a family history of angle-closure glaucoma. Young people playing games through the night hardly ever have an attack like this. The most common misjudgement during an attack is to treat it as gastroenteritis, migraine or high blood pressure, because the headache and vomiting are more obvious than the eye symptoms. Losing vision in one eye with no pain and no redness is a different matter, see item 5 in this section (one eye suddenly going black). For whether blue-light glasses are worth anything, see item 16 in section 6 (blue-light glasses).

### 7. Chest pressure, tightness or squeezing that does not ease after more than 15 minutes: call 120, do not put up with it and do not drive yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Chest pressure, tightness or squeezing that does not ease after more than 15 minutes: call 120. Where the vessel is opened within 55 minutes of arrival at hospital, 30-day mortality is 2.8%. Past 55 minutes it is 9.7%. Every stretch of delay kills off another patch of heart muscle. Do not drive yourself, because losing consciousness on the road is a second accident.
- Benefit: Israel ran a nationwide survey of acute coronary syndrome. What it analysed was patients with ST-elevation myocardial infarction, which is one kind of heart attack. The interval measured was from arrival at hospital to opening the blocked vessel with a balloon (balloon angioplasty). Where the interval was ≤55 minutes, 30-day deaths were 5/178 (2.8%). Where the interval was >55 minutes, 30-day deaths were 15/155 (9.7%)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Karkabi B, Meir G, Zafrir B, et al. (2021). Door-to-balloon time and mortality in patients with ST-elevation myocardial infarction. European Heart Journal - Quality of Care and Clinical Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjqcco/qcaa037>
- Notes: What the study compares is the delay after arrival at hospital, not the stretch from onset to arrival. The reasoning is the same either way: every extra stretch of delay kills off another patch of heart muscle. The pain can sit in the upper abdomen, the back or the chin, and the left arm can go numb. Diabetics and older people may have no pain at all, only breathlessness. Do not drive yourself, because losing consciousness on the road is a second accident

### 8. Sudden tearing pain that travels from the chest through the back and on towards the waist: call 120 and say clearly that the pain is moving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Tearing pain that travels from the chest through the back and on towards the waist: when you call 120, say straight out, 「像撕开一样，疼在往下走」 — like being torn apart, the pain is moving downwards. Without surgery and on drugs alone, this condition kills 58% of patients. Its handling is the opposite of a heart attack: the doctor has to think of it before ordering a contrast CT, instead of giving you anticoagulants and thrombolytics. Say as well if the blood pressure differs a lot between the two arms, or if no pulse can be felt in one of them.
- Benefit: The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD) collected 464 cases from 12 centres. Aortic dissection is the condition that causes tearing pain in the chest and back. Among the symptoms patients report first, the most common is a sudden severe sharp pain. All-cause mortality during the hospital stay was 27.4%. In type A dissection without surgery, on drugs alone, mortality was 58%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Hagan PG, Nienaber CA, Isselbacher EM, et al. (2000). The International Registry of Acute Aortic Dissection (IRAD): new insights into an old disease. JAMA, 283(7), 897-903. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.283.7.897>
- Notes: The handling of a dissection is exactly the opposite of that of a heart attack. A heart attack needs anticoagulants and thrombolytics, drugs that stop the blood clotting, and using them on a dissection is like letting the tear bleed. So at the hospital say 「撕裂样，疼在往下走」 — tearing, the pain is moving downwards, so that the doctor thinks of a contrast CT. A large difference in blood pressure between the two arms, and no pulse in one of them, are the clues. People whose blood pressure is poorly controlled, people with Marfan syndrome, and people with an aortic aneurysm or dissection in the family are at higher risk. Again, do not drive yourself, and do not put up with it until tomorrow

### 9. A sudden "the worst headache of my life" that reaches its worst within an hour: go to the emergency department for a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The emergency visit itself costs very little. One head CT in the emergency department costs several hundred yuan.
- In plain language: What matters is how fast the pain gets to its worst. A severe headache that peaks within an hour is a bleed from an artery on the surface of the brain in about 6% of cases. If you are over 40, have a painful or stiff neck, were seen to pass out, developed it during activity, felt it explode instantly, or your neck will not bend forward, any one of these means go to the emergency department for a head CT. Most of the people who go and get checked turn out to be fine, but missing one and having a second bleed is death.
- Benefit: 10 emergency departments in Canada ran a prospective cohort study of 2131 cases of severe headache that reached its worst pain within an hour, with the criteria fixed in advance and the patients then followed and recorded throughout. 132 of them (6.2%) were subarachnoid haemorrhage, a bleed from an artery on the surface of the brain. The Ottawa SAH rule has six conditions: aged 40 or over, neck pain or stiffness, and a witnessed collapse. The other three are onset during activity, onset that explodes instantly, and limited neck flexion when the doctor examines you. Meeting any one of them means you need the scan. The rule picked out every person who bled, a sensitivity of 100%, 95% CI 97.2%–100.0% (credible range). The price is that it excludes only 15.3% of the people who did not bleed (specificity 15.3%)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Perry JJ, Stiell IG, Sivilotti MLA, et al. (2013). Clinical decision rules to rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage for acute headache. JAMA, 310(12), 1248-1255. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.278018>
- Notes: What matters is not how bad the pain is but how fast it reaches the worst. A headache that explodes within a few seconds to a minute is the danger signal; a migraine that builds up slowly is not. With vomiting, a stiff neck, photophobia or a brief loss of consciousness, take it more seriously still. A specificity of only 15.3% means that most of the people who go for a scan on the strength of this rule come out clear. But missing one and having a second bleed is death, and the scan is worth having. Do not take a painkiller to push it down and go back to sleep
### 10. Two or three weeks to a few months after an elderly person hits their head, if they become unsteady, slow, sleepy or weak on one side: get a head CT
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One head CT costs two or three hundred yuan.
- In plain language: After an elderly person hits their head, the blood usually does not collect until the third week. At the time of the knock there can be nothing wrong at all, and the person may not remember hitting their head. After surgery this condition comes back in up to 33% of cases and kills in up to 32%. If an elderly person suddenly becomes confused, walks unsteadily, keeps dozing off, or has weakness in one hand, first go back and ask whether they fell or hit their head in the last two or three months, then get the CT.
- Benefit: The Chinese Medical Association Branch of Neurosurgery and others have published the Expert Consensus of Chinese Experts on Drug Treatment of Chronic Subdural Haematoma (《慢性硬膜下血肿药物治疗中国专家共识》). A chronic subdural haematoma is a pool of blood that collects slowly between the layers of membrane around the brain. The technical description is a chronic space-occupying lesion formed by blood collecting between the arachnoid and the dura mater. It 「通常在颅脑外伤后第三周形成」 — usually forms in the third week after head injury — and it is common in elderly people. After surgery the recurrence rate can reach 33% and the fatality rate can reach 32%. Among patients over 90 years old, only 24% make a good recovery overall. A systematic review found 3.39 to 39.1 new cases per 100,000 people worldwide each year, and with age the figure can reach three times that over 80. The most common causes are injury and falls, followed by long-term heavy drinking, the use of drugs that stop the blood clotting (anticoagulants), and violence. A review in geriatric medicine says the presentation is 「典型包括意识模糊、认知下降、步态障碍或嗜睡，可能与其他老年综合征相似」 — typically confusion, declining cognition, gait disturbance or drowsiness, and it can resemble other syndromes of old age
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhang J et al (2021). Expert consensus on drug treatment of chronic subdural hematoma. Chinese Neurosurgical Journal, 7(1), 47. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41016-021-00263-z> ; Dziho A et al (2025). Global prevalence and incidence of chronic subdural hematoma: A systematic review. Brain and Spine, 5, 105893. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bas.2025.105893> ; Mathon B, Shotar E (2026). Diagnostic and therapeutic management of chronic subdural hematoma in elderly patients. Gériatrie et Psychologie Neuropsychiatrie du Vieillissement, 24(1), 30-41. <https://doi.org/10.1684/pnv.2026.1274>
- Notes: At the time of the knock there can be nothing wrong at all, and the elderly person may not even remember hitting their head. So when you notice at home that someone has recently become confused, walks unsteadily, keeps dozing off or has weakness in one hand, first go back and ask whether they have fallen or hit their head in the last two or three months, then get the CT. Do not put it straight down to senile dementia or to the after-effects of a previous stroke, because getting that wrong costs time. Elderly people taking aspirin, clopidogrel, warfarin or one of the new oral anticoagulants are at higher risk, and so are those who drink heavily over the long term. For fall prevention, see item 13 in section 1 (training balance and leg strength).

### 11. One leg suddenly swollen, tight and painful to press: get seen as soon as you can. Add sudden breathlessness or chest pain and call 120 at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An ultrasound of the blood vessels in the leg (a lower limb venous ultrasound) costs one or two hundred yuan.
- In plain language: One leg suddenly swollen, a full girth larger than the other, and painful when pressed: get a venous ultrasound of the leg as soon as you can. If sudden breathlessness, chest pain on a deep breath, a fast heartbeat and dizziness follow, the clot may already have travelled to the lung. Call 120 at once. Among people diagnosed with pulmonary embolism, 17.4% die within 3 months. Sitting still for more than 4 hours roughly doubles the risk, but the risk to any one person is still very low.
- Benefit: The International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER) collected 2454 patients with confirmed acute pulmonary embolism from 52 hospitals in 7 countries. A pulmonary embolism is a clot blocking the pulmonary artery. In this group, all-cause mortality within 3 months was 17.4% (426/2454). The World Health Organization's WRIGHT project found that travel of more than 4 hours without a break roughly doubles the risk of a clot forming in a vein and travelling to the lung (venous thromboembolism), 「大约翻倍」 — roughly doubles. But the probability for any one person is still very low: sitting still for more than 4 hours carries a risk of 「约为六千分之一」 — about one in six thousand
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Goldhaber SZ, Visani L, De Rosa M (1999). Acute pulmonary embolism: clinical outcomes in the International Cooperative Pulmonary Embolism Registry (ICOPER). Lancet, 353(9162), 1386-1389. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(98)07534-5> ; WHO (2007). Study results released on travel and blood clots. <https://www.who.int/news/item/29-06-2007-study-results-released-on-travel-and-blood-clots>
- Notes: Both legs swollen together is usually down to the heart, the liver, the kidneys or how well the veins are working. One leg suddenly swollen and a girth larger than the other is the typical picture of a deep vein thrombosis. The situations where this happens are long journeys by car or plane, being bedridden after an operation or a fracture, a plaster cast, pregnancy and the weeks around childbirth, cancer, and taking the oral contraceptive pill. A clot that breaks loose and blocks the pulmonary artery is a pulmonary embolism: sudden breathlessness, more chest pain when you breathe in deeply, a fast heartbeat, dizziness, and coughing up blood (haemoptysis). At that point call 120 at once. 17.4% is the death rate among diagnosed cases of pulmonary embolism, not the risk you carry because your leg is swollen. Do not frighten yourself with that number.

### 12. Heavy bleeding: press hard on the wound with your hand first, put a tourniquet on a limb if pressure will not hold, and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A windlass tourniquet costs from a few tens of yuan to a little over a hundred. Keep one in the car and one in the first aid kit. Learn the sequence 「扎在伤口上方、拧到血停、记时间」 — tie it above the wound, wind it until the bleeding stops, note the time. It takes a few minutes.
- In plain language: Press hard on the wound with your hand or with clothing and do not let go. If pressure will not hold on a limb, put a tourniquet on it, then call 120, and once it is on do not loosen it. Among battlefield casualties, nine tenths of those given a tourniquet before shock survived, only one in ten of those given one after shock survived, and all 5 who needed one and did not get it died. Tie it above the wound, wind it until the bleeding stops, and note the time. Do not substitute a shoelace or a length of rope. On the neck and the torso you can only press, you must never tie.
- Benefit: 11 level-one trauma centres in Texas, United States, collected 1,026 cases of vascular injury to the limbs over 6 years, of which 181 (17.6%) had a tourniquet put on before reaching hospital. Taking the figures as they stand, mortality was 3.9% where a tourniquet was used and 5.2% where it was not. Once the other conditions are evened out (multivariable adjustment), a tourniquet is still independently associated with survival. Adjusted OR 5.86, credible range 95% CI 1.41 to 24.47. At a field hospital in Baghdad, 232 casualties: survival was 90% where a tourniquet was put on before shock appeared, and only 10% where it was put on after shock. All 5 who needed a tourniquet and did not get one died, and survival where it was used was 87%. The order is: direct pressure first, clothing or a towel will do → a tourniquet on limb bleeding that pressure will not hold → call 120 → do not loosen it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Teixeira PGR et al (2018). Civilian Prehospital Tourniquet Use Is Associated with Improved Survival in Patients with Peripheral Vascular Injury. Journal of the American College of Surgeons. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jamcollsurg.2018.01.047> ; Kragh JF et al (2009). Survival with emergency tourniquet use to stop bleeding in major limb trauma. Annals of Surgery. <https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0b013e31818842ba> ; Chinese Medical Doctor Association Branch of Emergency Physicians (中国医师协会急诊医师分会) et al (2020). Expert consensus on emergency use of tourniquets. 中华急诊医学杂志 29(6):773-779: 「院前急救时，止血带一旦使用则不建议松开，除非得到可以替代的彻底止血的救治」 — in prehospital first aid, once a tourniquet has been used it is not recommended to loosen it, unless a treatment that stops the bleeding completely becomes available in its place, and 「最长使用时间不应超过2 h。但如果客观情况无法到医院救治或者无替代止血办法，则在得到正规救援前不解除止血带」 — the longest time of use should not exceed 2 h, but if the situation objectively makes it impossible to get to hospital for treatment and there is no alternative way to stop the bleeding, then do not release the tourniquet before proper rescue arrives. <https://doi.org/10.3760/cma.j.issn.1671-0282.2020.06.006>, full text <http://www.cem.org.cn/rhtml/20200606/index.htm>
- Notes: Do not use a rope, a shoelace or any other thin thing as a tourniquet. It will not grip an artery, and it cuts the skin. Once a tourniquet is on, do not loosen it now and then to let some blood through. Bleeding on the neck and the torso can only be pressed, never tied. In that battlefield dataset the tourniquet itself cost nobody an amputation, and there were 4 cases of short-lived nerve paralysis. The expert consensus of Chinese emergency physicians agrees: once it has been used before hospital, loosening it is not recommended unless another way of stopping the bleeding completely is available. For bleeding in the upper arm, put it on the upper third of the upper arm; for bleeding in the thigh, put it at the top of the thigh. Do not put it on the forearm, the lower leg, the elbow or the knee. The consensus recommends keeping the total time, up to arrival at hospital and after it, under 2 hours if at all possible, but where the hospital cannot be reached and there is no other way to stop the bleeding, do not release it until proper rescue arrives. A tourniquet improvised from a belt, a tie or a shirt sleeve will struggle to stop the bleeding on its own; you need a stick to wind it tight.

### 13. Bitten or scratched by a dog or a cat through the skin: rinse alternately with soapy water and running water for 15 minutes, and get the vaccine the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The rinsing is free. The full course of rabies vaccine costs several hundred yuan. Grade III exposure also needs rabies immunoglobulin (ready-made antibodies given by injection), which costs from several hundred yuan to over a thousand.
- In plain language: Once rabies symptoms appear, the fatality rate is almost 100%, and rinsing plus the vaccine is the only route. Rinse alternately with soapy water and running clean water for 15 minutes, then get the vaccine the same day. A bite that goes right through, or broken skin that is licked, is grade III exposure, and it also needs immunoglobulin. A domestic animal that has been vaccinated does not change the handling: the grade is set by the wound, not by where the animal came from.
- Benefit: The 2023 version of the standard from the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration (国家疾控局) and the National Health Commission (国家卫健委) says in its original text: 「狂犬病病死率几乎达 100%」 — the fatality rate of rabies is almost 100%. The handling is 「用肥皂水（或者其他弱碱性清洁剂）和一定压力的流动清水交替彻底冲洗所有咬伤和抓伤处约 15 分钟」 — wash all bite wounds and scratch wounds thoroughly for about 15 minutes, alternating soapy water (or another mildly alkaline cleaning agent) with running water at some pressure. A light bite on bare skin, or a scratch that does not bleed, is grade II exposure and needs the vaccine. A bite that goes right through, or broken skin that is licked, is grade III exposure, and 「应处置伤口并注射狂犬病被动免疫制剂和接种狂犬病疫苗」 — the wound must be treated, rabies immunoglobulin must be injected, and the rabies vaccine given. WHO: about 59,000 people die of rabies worldwide every year, and 99% of it is passed on by dogs
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the National Disease Control and Prevention Administration (国家疾控局综合司) and General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2023). Notice on issuing the working standard for the prevention and handling of rabies exposure (2023 version), 关于印发狂犬病暴露预防处置工作规范（2023 年版）的通知. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100014/common/content/content_1706569159854649344.html> ; WHO. Rabies fact sheet, 狂犬病 实况报道. <https://www.who.int/zh/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rabies>
- Notes: Do not skip the handling because the animal is a domestic one that has been vaccinated. The standard looks at the grade the wound has reached (the exposure grade), not at where the animal came from. Do not dress the wound yourself. The ten-day observation method can only decide whether to stop the injections, not whether to start them. The same handling applies to bites from wild animals and from bats.

### 14. After a scald: rinse immediately with cool running water for 20 minutes, and put on no toothpaste and no soy sauce

### 15. A rash over the whole body, breathlessness or dizziness: handle it as anaphylactic shock, call 120 at once and say what you think it is
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. A person already known to have a severe allergy pays for their own epinephrine pen, and it is a prescription medicine.
- In plain language: A rash over the whole body, plus breathlessness or dizziness, is anaphylactic shock. Call 120 at once, say straight out 「怀疑过敏性休克」 — I suspect anaphylactic shock — and ask the ambulance to bring epinephrine. Anti-allergy drugs and steroids act too slowly to stand in for epinephrine. Anyone who has had one of these should ask a doctor for an auto-injector. Inject into the outer mid-thigh, and it goes in through clothing.
- Benefit: Epinephrine is the first-choice drug (first-line medicine) for anaphylactic shock of grade II and above, and the guideline lists it as a strong recommendation. Anti-allergy drugs and steroids act too slowly to take its place
- Evidence grade: B (strong recommendation in the guideline, but the underlying evidence grade is low)

### 16. Someone has a fit and goes down: clear the area, lay them on their side, time it, and put nothing in their mouth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Move hard objects out of the way, lay them on their side with the face pointing down, and time the fit on your watch. Do not hold them down, do not pry the mouth open, and do not put anything in the mouth: that only damages the teeth and the jaw. Call an ambulance if the fit goes on for more than 5 minutes without stopping, if it starts again soon after stopping, if they cannot be woken afterwards or have trouble breathing, if it happens in water, or if this is the first fit they have ever had. Give water only once they are fully awake.
- Benefit: This avoids the damage to the teeth and jaw caused by prying the mouth open and putting hard objects in it. This avoids the fractures caused by holding the person down. A fit that does not stop after 5 minutes is the red line at which you must call an ambulance
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: US CDC. First aid for seizures: 「Don't hold the person down or stop their movements」「Don't put anything in their mouth. This can hurt their teeth or jaw」「Don't offer water or food until they are fully alert」「Time the seizure to track if it lasts more than 5 minutes. If it does, seek immediate medical attention or call 911」. <https://www.cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html>
- Notes: The same page lists several other situations that need an ambulance at once: another fit soon after the first one ends, not being woken afterwards or having trouble breathing, being hurt during a fit, a fit in water, and a first-ever fit. When the fit stops, lay them on their side with the face pointing down, and give water only once they are fully awake. In a household with an epileptic patient, these are the steps that get used most. Doing the same for a stranger is less likely to pay you back, but it does not make you liable either (Article 184 of the Civil Code, see item 2 in this section, do not lift them straight away).

### 17. A person with diabetes starts shaking, sweating cold and speaking unclearly: give 15 grams of sugar first, then re-test after 15 minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Carry a few sugar cubes or a small box of juice with you.
- In plain language: Give 15 grams of sugar first, sugar cubes or a small box of juice will both do. Test again after 15 minutes, and if the reading is still low, do it again. A blood glucose below 3.0 mmol/L is the low blood sugar that medicine takes seriously. Once it reaches the point where somebody else has to help you treat it, it can kill. Give an unconscious person nothing at all by mouth, because it goes into the airway. Call 120 at once.
- Benefit: A blood glucose below 3.0 mmol/L is clinically significant hypoglycaemia. Hypoglycaemia with a change in consciousness or behaviour, needing help from another person to treat it, is severe hypoglycaemia, and it can kill
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: American Diabetes Association (美国糖尿病学会) (2025). Standards of Care in Diabetes 2025, Chapter 6 Glycemic Goals and Hypoglycemia. - Notes: Give an unconscious person nothing to eat or drink, because it goes into the airway. Call 120 at once. The nasal spray and the pre-filled injector are more usable than one that has to be mixed at the time. ### 18. Someone is electrocuted: cut the power first, then move the source aside with a dry wooden stick or the like, never pull them with your hands
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Cut the power first, or use something dry such as a wooden stick to move the live source aside, and only then touch the person. Pulling them with your bare hands turns one casualty into two. If they are not breathing once they are clear of the current, start compressing the chest at once, as in item 1 (pressing hard on the centre of the chest). Do not hold back because the injury came from electricity. Do not deal yourself with a fallen high-voltage line or anything near a substation. Back away and wait for the electricity authority to cut the power.
- Benefit: This avoids the rescuer being caught in the current as well, which turns one casualty into two. Where someone is not breathing after being clear of the current, do cardiopulmonary resuscitation as in item 1 in this section (pressing hard on the centre of the chest). The US CDC first aid steps are: look first, do not touch, because the person may still be in contact with the current. Cut the power if you can, and if you cannot, move the source aside with something that does not conduct, such as cardboard, plastic or wood. Once the person is clear of the current, if breathing or the pulse has stopped, start cardiopulmonary resuscitation at once. One review says that people who go into cardiac arrest after electrocution and are brought back often do well afterwards
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: US CDC. ### 20. Swallowed cleaning agent, pesticide or medicine: do not induce vomiting, take the bottle with you to hospital at once. Splashed in the eye or on the skin: rinse with plenty of clean water for 15 minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Put cleaning agents, disinfectant, pesticides and medicines on a high shelf out of a child's reach, or in a locked cupboard. Never decant them into a drinks bottle.
- In plain language: Do not induce vomiting after someone swallows a cleaning agent, pesticide or medicine, because bringing a corrosive liquid back up burns the oesophagus a second time. First get them to drink a moderate amount of milk or egg white, then take the bottle and the instructions to hospital at once. If it is splashed on the skin or in the eye, rinse with plenty of clean water for a full 15 minutes, holding the eyelids open in the eye and not rubbing it. Mixing disinfectant with toilet cleaner gives off chlorine gas.
- Benefit: The China CDC Institute for Occupational Health and Poison Control (中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所) gives four kinds of handling. Swallowed (ingested into the digestive tract): get them to drink a moderate amount of milk or egg white at once, 「切记，不要催吐」 — remember, do not induce vomiting. Get to hospital as soon as possible, taking the packaging or the instructions with you, 「以便于医生快速判断成分并制定救治方案」 — so that the doctor can judge the ingredients quickly and work out a treatment plan. Inhaled (into the airway): move the person at once to a well-ventilated area, unbutton the collar and keep the airway clear; if there is severe coughing, breathlessness or difficulty breathing, get medical help at once. Skin contact: rinse the contact area at once with plenty of clean water for at least 15 minutes, taking care not to let the rinse splash into the eyes or onto other unbroken skin. Eye contact: rinse the eye at once with plenty of clean water or saline for at least 15 minutes. Hold the eyelids open while rinsing so the water reaches the eyeball and the inside of the lids, 「切勿揉搓眼睛」 — never rub the eyes. Go to the eye department at once afterwards. High-risk products are identified by their pH. Those with pH<3 are toilet cleaners, descalers, floor cleaners and stainless steel polishes. Some powerful drain unblockers also contain dilute sulphuric acid. Those with pH>11 are drain unblockers, heavy-duty kitchen degreasers, dishwasher powder, drain salts and cleaning powders. There is also a combination that causes harm on the spot: using disinfectant together with a toilet cleaner containing hydrochloric acid gives off chlorine gas, which irritates the airway and causes severe coughing and breathlessness, and can be fatal
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: China CDC Institute for Occupational Health and Poison Control (中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所) (2025). Holiday safety reminder: beware of strongly acid and strongly alkaline cleaning agents. <https://niohp.chinacdc.cn/kpdw/202507/t20250722_308659.htm>
- Notes: The B is because the official explainer says only how to handle it and how to recognise it, and gives no figure for how many fewer incidents following it would prevent. The reason not to induce vomiting is that a corrosive liquid coming back up burns the oesophagus, the tube from throat to stomach, a second time. If you cannot tell what was swallowed, take the bottle, the instructions and any vomit to hospital together. Prevention costs far less than first aid: store things by category, keep them away from food and drink, screw the cap back on and throw the empty bottle away, and never pour anything into a mineral water bottle. Wear rubber gloves and goggles when using these products, open the window, and never mix types. For carbon monoxide see item 19, and for burns and scalds see item 14.

### 22. Dizzy, nauseated, not sweating or not fully conscious in high heat: move to shade at once, strip and pour water over the person to cool them. Give an unconscious person nothing to drink, and call 120
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Skin hot and red, speech slurred and mind confused mean heat stroke. From dizziness to coma can take only a few dozen minutes, and the fatality rate is very high. Move the person at once to a cool, ventilated place, take off their clothes, cool them with cool water and a wet towel, and call 120 at the same time. Give an unconscious person not one mouthful of water, because it goes into the airway.
- Benefit: The US National Weather Service: heat stroke is 「严重的医疗急症」 — a serious medical emergency. It shows as a body temperature above 103°F (39.4°C), confusion, slurred speech, and skin that is hot and red. The handling is to call for emergency help at once, move the person somewhere cool, cool them with cool water and a wet cloth, and 「不要给液体」 — give no liquids. Heat exhaustion shows as heavy sweating, weakness, cold damp skin, and a fast weak pulse. The handling is to move the person somewhere cool, loosen their clothing, and give small sips of water; if there is vomiting, if the person worsens, or if it has not eased after 1 hour, get medical help. The Yunnan Provincial CDC (云南省疾控局) advises: move the person at once to a cool, ventilated place, cool them with a cold wet towel, give salt-containing drinks to anyone fully conscious, and call the emergency number at once if the symptoms are severe; severe heat stroke has a 「病死率极高」 — very high fatality rate. China CDC advises: cool first, transport second, and do the cooling within the 「黄金半小时」 — golden half hour — after the person loses consciousness. Where it is possible, healthy young and middle-aged adults with no underlying conditions can be cooled quickly by cold water immersion, while elderly people and those with underlying conditions are sprayed with water and cooled by evaporation. The Wilderness Medical Society's heat illness guidelines, updated in 2024, say cold water immersion is the most important treatment for severe heat illness and the fastest way to bring the temperature down. With no ice to hand, use the coolest water you have, and if immersion is not possible, spray with water and fan: that runs at about half the speed of immersion
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: US National Weather Service. Heat Related Illnesses. <https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat-illness> ; Yunnan Provincial CDC (云南省疾控局) (2026). With these symptoms you may have heatstroke, 出现这些症状，你可能是中暑了. <https://ynsjkj.yn.gov.cn/html/2026/jikongkepu_0719/2606.html> ; China CDC. In hot weather, China CDC reminds you to guard against heatstroke: 「如有条件可用冷水浸泡的方法对无基础疾病的青壮年快速降温。老人和有基础疾病的患者可向皮肤喷水通过蒸发降温。降温应遵循"先降温，后转运" 的原则」 — where it is possible, healthy young and middle-aged adults with no underlying conditions can be cooled quickly by cold water immersion. Elderly people and patients with underlying conditions can be sprayed with water and cooled through evaporation. Wilderness Medical Society Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of Heat Illness: 2024 Update. Wilderness & Environmental Medicine 35(1_suppl):112S-127S. <https://doi.org/10.1177/10806032241227924> (the text site refuses automated fetching, so the specific recommendations were checked against the update summary on the Wilderness Medical Society's own site: 「cold water immersion … is the most important method of treating severe heat illness」「If CWI is unavailable, evaporative cooling is the recommended second-line treatment. … generally half as fast as CWI」<https://wms.org/magazine/magazine/1540/Heat-Illness-CPG/default.aspx>) ; Lipman GS et al (2019). - Notes: Do not wait to see if it 「缓一缓」 — eases off a little. Heat stroke can go from dizziness to coma in a few dozen minutes. Do not pour water into an unconscious person, because it goes into the airway. Where to put the ice packs, the two sources differ. China CDC says the neck, the armpits and the groin. The Wilderness Medical Society says the research does not show any cooling effect from those sites. Its advice is that where ice packs are all you have, cover the whole body with them, and where there are not enough, put them on the cheeks, the palms of the hands and the soles of the feet. ### 23. Anyone with heatstroke who is conscious: small sips of cool dilute salt water or a sports drink, often and little. Do not gulp, and do not drink alcohol or high-sugar drinks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. A sports drink costs a few yuan a bottle.
- In plain language: Only a conscious person drinks. Take small sips often of cool dilute salt water or a sports drink, 500 to 1000 millilitres an hour. Do not gulp, because being sick and taking it into the airway kills faster. Do not drink alcohol or high-sugar drinks, because they make you lose more water. Cool the body first, with cool water on the skin and ice packs on the neck, armpits and groin, and only then replace the fluid. Give an unconscious person or one with slurred speech not one mouthful.
- Benefit: China CDC has published the Guide to Public Prevention and Emergency Handling of Heatstroke (2014 version). It says 「至少每小时喝 2~4 杯凉水（500~1000ml），水温不宜过高，饮水应少量多次」 — drink at least 2 to 4 cups of cool water an hour (500 to 1000ml), not too warm, and in small sips often. It also says 「不要饮用含酒精或大量糖分的饮料。这些饮料会导致失去更多的体液。同时，还应避免饮用过凉的冰冻饮料」 — do not drink drinks containing alcohol or a large amount of sugar, because they cause you to lose more body fluid, and avoid drinks that are too cold and frozen. For heat stroke it says plainly 「不要给病人喝水」 — do not give the patient water. US NIOSH: for heat exhaustion, 「鼓励小口频繁地喝凉水」 — encourage small, frequent sips of cool water; for heat cramps, give a sugary and electrolyte-containing sports drink every 15 to 20 minutes, and 「不要用盐片」 — do not use salt tablets
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: China CDC Institute for Occupational Health and Poison Control (中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所) (2014). Guide to Public Prevention and Emergency Handling of Heatstroke (2014 version). <https://niohp.chinacdc.cn/sndt/201405/t20140530_97623.htm> ; US CDC/NIOSH. Heat-Related Illnesses. <https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html>
- Notes: After heavy sweating, drinking plain water on its own dilutes the salt in the blood (low blood sodium), so the water needs a little salt in it. Drinking a large amount at once makes people vomit, and vomit going into the airway kills faster than the heatstroke itself. "Do not drink it ice cold" is advice from the 2014 version of the China CDC guide, on the grounds of fear of stomach spasm, and it comes with no quantified evidence. Do not misread it as "cold is not allowed". The ways of cooling are set out in item 22 in this section (moving to shade, stripping and pouring water to cool down when dizzy and nauseated in high heat): wiping the body down with cool water, cold water immersion, and ice packs on the neck, armpits and groin. These are exactly the first-line cooling methods the official sources recommend, and they go on the body rather than into the mouth. Cooling always comes before rehydrating. Give an unconscious person or one with slurred speech not one mouthful

### 24. In a fire: crawl along the ground, feel the door before opening it, and if the door is hot do not open it. Take the stairs, not the lift, and once you are out do not go back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Fit 1 to 2 standalone smoke alarms at home, a few tens of yuan. Look at the two ways out of the stairwell in advance.
- In plain language: Stay down on the floor and crawl towards the exit under the smoke. Before opening a door, feel the handle and the door itself. If it is hot, or if smoke is coming from the gap, do not open that door: take the second way out. Open the door slowly and shut it again the moment thick smoke comes through. Take the stairs and not the lift, because a power cut traps you inside. Once you are out, do not go back for your things. If your clothes catch fire, stop, lie down and roll, and cover your face with your hands.
- Benefit: The US FEMA text on escaping says: 「趴到地上，从烟下面爬向出口」 — stay down on the ground and crawl towards the exit under the smoke. Before opening a door, feel the handle and the door itself: 「如果是热的，或者门缝有烟，别开这扇门，用第二条路」 — if it is hot, or if smoke is coming from the gap, do not open that door; use the second way out. Open the door slowly and close it again as soon as there is thick smoke. If clothes catch fire, 「停下、倒地、打滚」 — stop, lie down, roll — and cover your face with your hands. If you cannot get out, close the door, block the gap under the door and any vents with cloth or tape, and phone in your location. The Ministry of Emergency Management's fire self-rescue guidance says the same thing: do not take the lift when escaping, run downwards as far as you can while the fire is small, cover the nose and mouth with a wet towel or a mask, and crawl or bend low as you leave
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: US FEMA Ready.gov. Home Fires. <https://www.ready.gov/home-fires> ; Ministry of Emergency Management (应急管理部) (2019). How to get yourself out of a fire in a public place: 「逃生时不要乘坐电梯，当火势不大时，要尽量往楼层下面跑」「可用湿毛巾或口罩蒙鼻，匍匐或弯腰撤离」 — when escaping, do not take the lift; when the fire is not severe, run towards the lower floors as far as you can; you can cover the nose with a wet towel or a mask, and crawl or bend low as you leave. <https://www.mem.gov.cn/kp/shaq/201904/t20190401_366015.shtml> ; Guizhou Provincial Fire and Rescue Corps (贵州省消防救援总队) (2020). Everyday fire knowledge: three points for escaping, 消防常识丨逃生三要点: 「湿毛巾对折3次（叠成8层），除烟率比较高，但烟和毒气浓度过高时，过滤效果就无法保证了。另外，毛巾过湿会导致呼吸困难」 — a wet towel folded 3 times (into 8 layers) filters smoke well, but when smoke and toxic gas are too thick to allow any filtering, and a towel that is too wet makes breathing difficult. <https://gz.119.gov.cn/xfkp/xfcs/202010/t20201026_71437855.html>
- Notes: Do not take the lift, because a power cut traps you inside. Do not go back for property. Do not run upwards unless you have confirmed the roof door is open. Do not jump. Fold a wet towel 3 times into 8 layers and do not put too much water in it, because a towel that is too wet makes breathing harder. When the smoke and toxic gas are too thick, a towel will not save you.

### 25. You see someone drowning: call for help and dial 110/120, reach in with a pole, throw something that floats, and do not go into the water yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is holding back the urge to jump in.
- In plain language: A drowning person pushes the rescuer under by instinct, so whoever goes in may drown too. The order is: shout for help → call 110/120 → pass over a long pole or a rope → throw a life ring, an empty bucket or a plastic bottle → when the person is out and not breathing, start compressions at once. Do not form a chain of hands and go into the water. About 300,000 people drown worldwide every year, and it is the fourth leading cause of death in children aged 1 to 4.
- Benefit: WHO: about 300,000 people drown worldwide every year, 92% of them in low- and middle-income countries. Drowning is the fourth leading cause of death in children aged 1 to 4 and the third in those aged 5 to 14. The intervention WHO recommends is 「安全救援和复苏培训」 — safe rescue and resuscitation training — which means learning first the way to help without going into the water. The order is: shout for help → make the call → pass over a long pole or a rope and throw a life ring, an empty bucket or a plastic bottle → when the person is out and not breathing, start compressions at once
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: WHO. Drowning fact sheet, 溺水 实况报道. <https://www.who.int/zh/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drowning>
- Notes: The C is because no official text could be found for the rule "do not go into the water". There is only WHO's death figures and WHO's direction of "safe rescue". Do not link arms and form a chain into the water. If you have not been trained, do not try to drag someone out, because a drowning person pushes the rescuer under. "Do not go into the water" is also a rule in your own interest: the person who goes in drowns too, whoever it happens to be. The person you are most likely to save is your own child, and item 12 in section 1 (keeping a child in sight near water) covers how to prevent it.

### 26. Someone is choking and cannot speak: stand behind them and give 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts, and if they collapse start cardiopulmonary resuscitation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Look at the movement once: make a fist above the navel, put your other hand over it, and thrust sharply inwards and upwards.
- In plain language: First ask 「你噎住了吗，能说话吗」 — are you choking, can you speak. If they can still cough hard and make a sound, do nothing and let them cough. If they cannot speak, stand behind them and give 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts, repeating until the object comes out or the person collapses. If they collapse, start cardiopulmonary resuscitation and send someone to call the emergency number. For a pregnant woman or a very large person, thrust on the chest instead. Do not reach into the mouth for something you cannot see.
- Benefit: The US National Library of Medicine text says: first ask 「你噎住了吗，能说话吗」 — are you choking, can you speak. 「能用力咳嗽、能说话的不要动手」 — if they can cough hard and can speak, do not intervene, because coughing itself usually brings the object up. If they cannot speak, 「反复做 5 次拍背接 5 次腹部冲击，直到异物出来或人失去意识」 — repeat 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts until the object comes out or the person loses consciousness. 「失去意识就开始心肺复苏」 — once they lose consciousness, start cardiopulmonary resuscitation — and send someone to call the emergency number. For a pregnant woman or someone with obesity, do chest thrusts instead
- Evidence grade: B

### 28. Shaking, slurred speech and unsteady walking mean hypothermia: change the wet clothing, wrap them up, warm the trunk first, give a hot sweet drink and no alcohol

- In plain language: A body temperature below 35℃ is hypothermia. The person shakes, speaks unclearly, walks unsteadily and cannot think straight. ### 29. Bitten by a snake: sit down and stay still, take off rings and watches, get to hospital quickly for antivenom. Do not cut, do not suck, do not tie it tight
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->

- In plain language: Sit down and stay still, and splint the bitten limb. Take off rings and watches first, because it is about to swell. Get to a hospital with antivenom quickly, because antivenom is the only thing that reverses most of the toxic effects. Do not cut, do not suck with your mouth, do not apply ice, and do not tie a rope until it turns purple. Do not drink alcohol and do not run about. Take a photo of the snake from a distance if you can, but do not go and catch it.

### 30. In an earthquake, first work out what building you are in: in a building built to seismic code, drop where you are. In an old building that will come down, the door is a few steps away, so go out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Look in advance at how many steps it is out of your own front door, which way you go, and which table is solid.
- In plain language: First work out what building you are in. In a building put up to code after 2001, if you have to go down the stairs or through the entrance hall to get out, drop where you are: cover your head and neck and get under a solid table, holding the table leg. In a single-storey house, an old brick house, or a self-built house with no seismic design, the door is a few steps away, so go out. In no circumstances jump from a window, take the lift, or go down the stairs while the building is shaking.
- Benefit: The China Earthquake Administration's explainer video Earthquake: hide or run? (《地震来了，是躲还是跑？》) gives the formula 「震时就近躲避，震后迅速撤离」 — during the shaking, take cover nearby; after the shaking, withdraw at once — and it states the limit explicitly: 「这里指的是在符合抗震设防要求的建筑内」 — this refers to being inside a building that meets seismic design requirements. The reason is that such buildings 「整体垮塌的可能性较小」 — are less likely to collapse entirely. The text says that 「坠落的建筑构件及装饰物品」 — falling structural members and fittings — 「才是对生命安全最大的威胁」 — are the greatest threat to life. And that 「震时无保护措施的盲目乱跑」 — running about blindly during the shaking without protection — 「反而容易被坠落的天花板、吊灯（扇）等物品砸伤」 — makes you more likely to be struck by a falling ceiling, a light fitting or a fan. To take cover, 「用手或者其他软物保护好头颈部」 — protect your head and neck with your hands or another soft object — 「并牢牢地抓住桌腿」 — and hold the table leg firmly. With no table, 「迅速贴紧承重墙蹲伏」 — crouch close to a load-bearing wall at once — 「避开外墙、窗户、阳台」 — and keep away from outer walls, windows and balconies. 「如果在户外」 — if you are outdoors — you should 「应该第一时间疏散到空旷的地方避震」 — move to open ground at once to ride out the shaking. Look at it the other way round. The 1988 Armenian earthquake brought down whole Soviet-style prefabricated blocks. The case-control study from that time says that 「第一下震动之后离开建筑是保护性行为」 — leaving the building after the first shock is protective behaviour. The chance of being hurt while staying indoors was 4.40 times the chance for someone who got out (odds ratio 4.40), 95% CI 2.24 to 8.71 (credible range). The chance of being hurt inside a building was 12.20 times that of being outside (3.62 to 63.79). In a building of more than 5 storeys it was 3.65 times the chance in a lower building (2.12 to 6.33)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: China Earthquake Administration (produced by the Anhui Provincial Earthquake Administration). Earthquake: hide or run? (explainer video, subtitle transcript), 地震来了，是躲还是跑？. <https://www.cea.gov.cn/cea/dzpd/dzcs/5537260/index.html> ; Armenian HK, Noji EK, Oganesian AP (1992). A case-control study of injuries arising from the earthquake in Armenia, 1988. Earthquakes. <https://www.ready.gov/earthquakes> ; Mahue-Giangreco M, Mack W, Seligson H, Bourque LB (2001). - Notes:Disputed, and the two conclusions are about two different kinds of building. The China Earthquake Administration itself makes "take cover" conditional on 「符合抗震设防要求的建筑内」 — being inside a building that meets seismic design requirements. It also calls for 「因地制宜」「因人而异」 — adapting to the place and to the person — so you weigh up whether the building resists earthquakes, where you are standing, and whether you can run at all. The Armenian buildings came down whole, so there was nowhere to take cover and going out was the better option. What US FEMA gives for housing in the United States is "drop, cover your head and neck, hold on, and do not run outside", and the injuries there come mainly from falling objects and from falls while moving during the shaking. The Northridge study also warns that reaching out to catch something, or to hold up something that "looks steady", makes the injury worse. So asking only "do I run" is no use. You have to judge whether the building will come down and whether you can reach open ground within seconds. In a building put up to code after 2001, where you have to go down the stairs or through the entrance hall, drop where you are. In a single-storey house, an old brick house, or a self-built house with no seismic design, the door is a few steps away, so go out. The Armenian study compared only survivors who were hurt with survivors who were not, did not count the dead, and does not say whether the effect of storey height was adjusted for. People who can run may well have lived on the ground floor, so read the figures down. In no circumstances jump from a window, take the lift, or go down the stairs while the building is shaking. Once outside, keep clear of tall buildings, overpasses, electricity poles, glass curtain walls and advertising hoardings.

### 31. Bears, wild boar and a pack of wild dogs: do not run, do not scream, do not stare them down, and back away slowly side-on
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Take a walking pole into the hills, walk in company, and make some noise so the animals know you are coming. The hard part is standing still instead of running.

Stand still, keep talking to it in a low voice, and when it stops moving back away slowly side-on. If a brown bear charges, lie down and play dead, with your hands clasped behind your neck and your legs apart. If a black bear charges, do not play dead: run for the car or the house, and if you cannot get there, fight back with whatever is to hand. For a scratch or a bite from a dog, rinse and get the injection as in item 13.
- Benefit: The US National Park Service text on bears has six sentences. One is 「不要跑，熊上下坡都能跑得像赛马一样快」 — do not run, a bear can run as fast as a racehorse up and down a slope. One is 「保持冷静，大多数熊不想攻击你，只想被放着不管」 — stay calm, most bears do not want to attack you, they only want to be left alone. One is 「用低沉的声音持续跟熊说话」 — keep talking to the bear in a low voice. ### 32. Caught in a thunderstorm in the wild: get into a vehicle or building with a hard roof, and keep away from ridges, lone trees, open water and metal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The price is that the trip is interrupted for half an hour or more. The hard part is turning back while the thunder is still far off.
- In plain language: Outdoors with a thunderstorm in the area, there is nowhere safe. Get into a vehicle or building with a hard roof. A convertible, a motorcycle, a tent, a shelter, the foot of a tree and the underside of an overhanging rock do not count. Keep away from open ground, from the top of a ridge, from a lone tall tree, from open water and from iron railings. If a group cannot get out, spread out and stand separately. Leave as soon as you hear thunder, and it is only safe 30 minutes after the last thunderclap.
- Benefit: Outdoors with a thunderstorm in the area, there is nowhere safe. It is only safe 30 minutes after the last rumble of thunder
- Evidence grade: B

### 33. Going up to a high plateau: climb slowly by sleeping altitude (how high you sleep that night), come back down if the symptoms get worse, and do not push through
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One to two extra days for acclimatisation. The preventive drug is a prescription medicine, so ask a doctor before you leave. The hard part is keeping those days free when the itinerary is tight.
- In plain language: What counts is how high you sleep, not how high you got to during the day. Sleeping above 2450 metres puts you at risk of altitude illness. Above 3000 metres, raise the height you sleep at by no more than 500 metres a night, and allow one extra day of acclimatisation for every 1000 metres of climb. A headache with nausea, unsteady walking, a change in consciousness, or breathlessness even at rest means come back down. Coming down comes before oxygen and before medicine.
- Benefit: Sleeping above 2450 metres puts you at risk of altitude illness. High-altitude cerebral oedema and pulmonary oedema, where fluid collects in the brain and the lungs, kill if you do not come down to lower ground in time
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US CDC. ### 34. A tick has attached itself: pull it straight out with fine-tipped tweezers held against the skin, and put on no oil and use no flame
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pair of fine-tipped tweezers.
- In plain language: Grip the head of the tick with fine-tipped tweezers held against the skin, and pull it straight out. Do not put on oil, do not use a flame, and do not twist as you pull: that agitates the tick and squeezes fluid carrying the pathogen into the skin, and it also leaves the mouthparts buried in the flesh. Write down the date afterwards. If you get a fever, a rash or weakness in the weeks that follow, tell the doctor you were bitten by a tick.
- Benefit: Oil, flame and twisting as you pull all agitate the tick and squeeze pathogen-bearing fluid into the skin. Doing it this way also makes it easy to leave the part of the tick's mouth that is buried in the flesh inside. The fever with thrombocytopenia syndrome that ticks carry in China had 6602 cases and 306 deaths reported nationwide in 2024, the highest year since the disease was discovered. The average fatality rate from 2010 to 2024 was 4.79%, it rises with age, and in those aged 85 and over it is 9.15%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: US CDC.
