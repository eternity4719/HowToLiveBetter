# 18. Is raising children worth it

This section works out two accounts, money and time. As in section 10, where the marriage account is worked out, this section only breaks the account down for you and does not draw the conclusion for you. If you are already pregnant and have to follow the procedure step by step, see section 27.

### 1. Count the money you can receive first: the national child-rearing subsidy is 3600 yuan per child per year, paid until the age of 3
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Apply at the place where the child's hukou is registered, and one trip finishes it
- In plain language: If the child was born on or after 2025-1-1 and is not yet 3 years old, you can receive this money. One of the parents applies at the place where the child's hukou is registered. It is 3600 yuan per child per year, and it is paid until the age of 3. This money is not subject to individual income tax. When you apply for means-tested income support, it is not counted as part of your household's income.
- Benefit: The plan issued by the General Offices of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council provides that for infants born from 2025-1-1 who are under 3 years old, the payment is 「每孩每年 3600 元」, 3600 yuan per child per year. It is claimed by one of the child's parents or by another guardian. This subsidy is 「免征个人所得税」, exempt from individual income tax. When means-tested income support is granted, it 「不计入家庭或个人收入」, is not counted as household or personal income
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Offices of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council (中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅) (2025). Implementation Plan for the Child-Rearing Subsidy Scheme, 育儿补贴制度实施方案. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12206/202508/content_7035435.html>
- Notes: This is the nationwide minimum. Some provinces and cities have their own local subsidy on top, and you can receive both. Three years come to 10800 yuan, which is a very small part of the cost of raising a child, so do not use it as the reason for deciding whether to have one

### 2. Maternity leave is 98 days, and the maternity allowance is paid by the maternity insurance fund at the employer's average monthly wage for the previous year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The employer makes the declaration, so you do not have to go anywhere
- In plain language: Giving birth earns you at least 98 days of maternity leave, and 15 of them can be taken before the birth. A difficult birth adds 15 days. Each further child adds another 15 days. The money paid for this period is called the maternity allowance, and the maternity insurance fund pays it. If your employer has not enrolled you in maternity insurance, the employer pays it out of its own money.
- Benefit: The number of days is set by the State Council. 「女职工生育享受 98 天产假，其中产前可以休假 15 天；难产的，增加产假 15 天；生育多胞胎的，每多生育 1 个婴儿，增加产假 15 天。」 — a female employee who gives birth is entitled to 98 days of maternity leave, of which 15 may be taken before the birth; a difficult birth adds 15 days; and for a multiple birth each additional baby adds 15 days. The money received during maternity leave is called the maternity allowance. Where you are already enrolled in maternity insurance, it is 「按照用人单位上年度职工月平均工资的标准由生育保险基金支付」, paid by the maternity insurance fund at the standard of the employer's average monthly employee wage in the previous year. If you are not enrolled, the employer pays it at your wage level before the maternity leave
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2012). Special Provisions on Labour Protection for Female Employees, 女职工劳动保护特别规定（国令第 619 号，第七条、第八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-05/07/content_6584.htm>
- Notes: 98 days is the minimum set by the state. Most provinces add a reward leave of more than 60 days for childbirth in their population and family planning regulations, so 158 days in total is the usual starting point. Which party pays the wages during that reward leave is set differently in each province

### 3. Know this rule: your pay may not be cut and you may not be dismissed because of pregnancy, childbirth or breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- In plain language: During pregnancy, childbirth and breastfeeding, an employer that cuts your pay or dismisses you is breaking the law, and it is fined 1000 to 5000 yuan for each person. While the child is under one year old you have one hour a day for breastfeeding, and the employer may not arrange overtime or a night shift for you. For how to claim, see section 19.
- Benefit: The State Council provides: 「用人单位不得因女职工怀孕、生育、哺乳降低其工资、予以辞退、与其解除劳动或者聘用合同。」 — an employer may not reduce a female employee's pay, dismiss her, or terminate her employment or engagement contract because of pregnancy, childbirth or breastfeeding. In these three periods the employer may not cut your pay, dismiss you or end the contract. A female employee breastfeeding a baby under 1 year old has one hour of breastfeeding time each day. The employer may also not arrange for her to work longer hours, and may not arrange a night shift. An employer that breaks any of the above is fined from 1000 yuan to 5000 yuan for each person
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2012). Special Provisions on Labour Protection for Female Employees, 女职工劳动保护特别规定（国令第 619 号，第五条、第九条、第十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-05/07/content_6584.htm>
- Notes: If you are really dismissed in breach of the law, the route to a claim is in section 19. The evidence to collect is the written notice of a transfer to another post or a pay cut, together with the chat records, and you should keep both

### 4. Count the time account by 「whose time is taken up」, not by 「how hard it is」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money; what it costs is time. Before the age of 3 the child takes up nearly the whole day. The hard part is that it is every single day, and there is no grinding it out for a few days and being finished
- In plain language: Convert the hours spent looking after the child each day and the number of times you are woken at night into time, and multiply by the hourly wage of the person doing the work. The figure that comes out is usually far larger than the money for formula and nappies. So it is best to settle who does what before the birth.
- Benefit: Convert the time spent looking after the child each day and the number of times you are woken at night into hours, and multiply by the hourly wage of the person doing the work. The number that comes out is usually far larger than the money for formula and nappies. Work that nobody pays for, such as looking after the child, cooking and getting up in the night, is counted into the cost in the same way. The method is the same as the one section 10 uses for housework. Settle who does what first, and only then decide whether to have a child
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature ; for the measure used see section 10
- Notes: The figures do not have to be exact. What is useful is that the two of you sit down with the same table before the birth and agree who does what, so that there is no argument afterwards

### 5. Split the money account into three stages: 0 to 3 years old, compulsory education, and after compulsory education
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: There is no fixed figure for what it costs; different cities and different ways of looking after the child are far apart. The hard part is sitting down quietly and working out the three stages separately, instead of guessing a number
- In plain language: For 0 to 3 years old the money goes mainly on how the child is looked after: by you, by the grandparents, or by a paid carer. From primary school to middle school it goes mainly on housing and on activities outside lessons. After that it goes mainly on the route into higher education. The three stages are spent in completely different ways, and mixing them only produces one alarming total.
- Benefit: The main cost from 0 to 3 years old is the way the child is looked after: by you, by the grandparents, or by a paid carer. In the compulsory education stage, primary school to middle school, the main costs are housing and activities outside lessons. After that the main cost is the route into higher education. Where the money goes is completely different across the three stages, and working them out together only gives one alarming total
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature
- Notes: First count the money you are certain to receive, such as the child-rearing subsidy, the maternity allowance and medical insurance reimbursements, and only then see what is still missing. Do not base a decision on the 「how many tens of thousands of yuan it takes to raise a child」 figures that circulate online; the measure behind them is not stated, so you do not know what they counted or what they left out

### 6. Having a child for the sake of the older generation, for the sake of the marriage, and for the sake of your own old age: keep a separate account for each
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money; what it costs is one serious conversation. The hard part is saying it straight rather than going round it
- In plain language: Other people hoping you have a child can be one factor in your decision. But the time and the money are entered in your own account. 「Raising a child to support you in old age」 has to be costed on its own, because it is a bet on another person's financial capacity and willingness twenty years from now. Write down the consequences of not having a child as well, and set the two side by side.
- Benefit: The line of thought is the same as in section 10. Other people's expectations can be one factor in your decision. But the cost goes in your own account, not in theirs. 「Raising a child to support you in old age」 above all has to be costed on its own, because it assumes another person's financial capacity and willingness twenty years from now
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature ; for a comparable analysis see section 10
- Notes: Write down 「the consequences of not having a child」 as well, and look at it side by side with 「the consequences of having a child」
