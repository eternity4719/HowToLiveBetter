# 33. Living after a disability

This section is about the days after a disability has happened. How to prevent one is in section 1 and section 13, and is not repeated here.

Four measures are counted separately and are not converted into each other. The items on emergencies and complications are counted in mortality. The items on subsidies, benefits and lawsuits are counted in money. The items on schooling and paperwork are counted in time. The items on psychiatric admission, capacity for conduct and guardianship are counted in personal freedom.

The World Health Organization's briefing gives three numbers. About 1.3 billion people worldwide have a significant functional impairment, 16% of the world's population, roughly one in every six people. Some disabled people die up to 20 years earlier than people who are not disabled. Disabled people have twice the risk of depression, asthma, diabetes, stroke, obesity or poor oral health. A large part of those gaps is not caused by the disability itself but by not being able to see a doctor, not being able to go to school, not being able to find work and not being able to get out of the house. So half the items in this section are about the body and half are about paperwork and rights.

Five things already written up elsewhere are only pointed to here, not repeated. How to get a disability certificate, the seven categories and how levels one to four are assessed: see item 10 in section 24 (disability certificate). When to have a disability assessed: see item 9 in section 24 (disability assessment). How to claim the two disability subsidies: see item 8 in section 7 (the two subsidies). How a work injury's assessment of labour capacity and disability level convert into money: see item 14 in section 19 (assessment of labour capacity). How to apply for long-term care insurance for severe loss of ability: see item 7 in section 17 (long-term care insurance). That item is written in the section on having elderly at home, but this benefit has no age limit and young people can get it too.

In most items in this section the beneficiary is you and your family, which puts them in the top two tiers. Where other people are involved, that is written separately in the notes.

### 1. After a spinal cord injury, a sudden severe headache and sweating: sit the person up straight first, loosen the clothing, and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Three moves, done in a few tens of seconds.
- In plain language: After a spinal cord injury a section of the body's alarm wiring is cut. A full bladder or tight clothing may go unnoticed by the person, while the blood pressure surges. The signal is a sudden severe headache and sweating. Sit the person up straight, loosen anything tight, check whether they are holding urine, and call 120 at the same time.
- Benefit: This kind of attack is called autonomic dysreflexia. A systematic review searched four databases, Medline, CINAHL, EMBASE and PsycINFO, and included 31 studies, 6 of which were randomised controlled trials. The review states that the non-drug treatment during an attack is three steps: put the patient in an upright position, loosen tight clothing, and remove the trigger. The basis for these three steps is clinical consensus and physiological data, and they count as level 5 evidence. A blood-pressure drug is used only when the blood pressure stays high; for prazosin there is level 1 evidence, and for nifedipine and prostaglandin E2 level 2 evidence. The review also states that strategies to prevent attacks mainly target the two common triggers of the genitourinary system and of surgery, and that most are supported only by level 4 and level 5 evidence
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Krassioukov A, Warburton DE, Teasell R, Eng JJ; Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation Evidence Research Team (2009). A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury. Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, 90(4):682-695. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017>
- Notes: The grade is B because those three moves at the scene have no randomised controlled trial behind them, only consensus and physiological reasoning. The review itself says that most of these measures are supported only by uncontrolled studies. The drugs are the doctor's business; do not give a blood-pressure drug at home yourself. Laying the person flat will push the blood pressure higher, so sit them up straight. Family and care workers need to know these three steps too. Do not just ride it out as an ordinary headache.
### 2. The first ten years after becoming disabled are the high-risk period for suicide; do not treat it as something to think your way out of
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Make one phone call, or register once at a hospital.
- In plain language: In the first years after becoming disabled, it is common for you or your family to break down emotionally; that is not a question of willpower. Long-term follow-up at two spinal cord injury centres in the United Kingdom found that this group dies by suicide at five times the ordinary rate. More than nine in ten suicides happen in the first ten years after the injury. Where the injury itself was a suicide attempt, the risk is higher again.
- Benefit: The study followed 2304 patients with traumatic spinal cord injury who were newly admitted between 1991 and 2010, survived the first year after the injury, and had a neurological deficit at discharge, following them to the end of 2014. In 63 of the cases (2.7%) the cause of the injury was itself a suicide attempt. By the end of the follow-up 533 people had died, of whom 4.2% died by suicide, and 91% of the suicides happened in the first ten years after the injury. The age-standardised suicide mortality was 62.5 per 100,000 people per year (95% CI 36.4–88.6, credible range), five times the rate of the general population of England and Wales in 2014 (12.2 per 100,000 people). For the people whose injury came from a suicide attempt, compared with other causes of injury, the odds ratio for death was 4.32 (P<0.001) and for suicide 9.46 (P<0.001)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Savic G, DeVivo MJ, Frankel HL, Jamous MA, Soni BM, Charlifue S (2018). Suicide and traumatic spinal cord injury - a cohort study. Spinal Cord, 56(1):2-6. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sc.2017.98>
- Notes: This study looked only at spinal cord injury, so applying it to blindness, amputation, burns and other causes of disability is an extrapolation. What it can tell you is the time window: the risk does not end at discharge, and you have to watch it for ten years. To have somebody to talk to, call 12356: see item 11 in section 29 (12356). Do not stock sleeping pills and pesticide at home: see item 25 in section 1 (thoughts of suicide). If grief and mood are stuck in the same place after half a year, register at a psychiatry or clinical psychology department: see item 8 in section 29 (grief past half a year).
### 3. Psychiatric admission follows the principle of voluntariness; only two circumstances allow it without the person's consent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Remember the two circumstances so that you can say them when you need them.
- In plain language: One word from the family or the employer cannot keep a person in a psychiatric hospital. The law recognises only two situations: the person has already harmed themselves or is at risk of doing so, and the person has already endangered others or is at risk of doing so. The first requires a guardian's consent before the person may be admitted. If the diagnosis is disputed, a fresh diagnosis may be requested within three days, and after that the person can commission an appraisal themselves.
- Benefit: Article thirty of the Mental Health Law (《精神卫生法》) states 「精神障碍的住院治疗实行自愿原则」, that is, admission for the treatment of a mental disorder follows the principle of voluntariness. Inpatient treatment may be carried out only where the diagnostic conclusion and the assessment of the condition show that the patient has a severe mental disorder and one of the following applies. One: 「已经发生伤害自身的行为，或者有伤害自身的危险的」, that is, the person has already carried out an act of self-harm, or is at risk of self-harm. Two: 「已经发生危害他人安全的行为，或者有危害他人安全的危险的」, that is, the person has already endangered the safety of others, or is at risk of doing so. Article thirty-one: in the first case the medical institution may admit the person only with the guardian's consent, and may not admit them if the guardian does not agree. Article thirty-two: in the second case, where the person or the guardian objects, a fresh diagnosis may be requested; the request must be made within three days of receiving the diagnostic conclusion, and must be made by two practising psychiatrists other than the doctor who made the first diagnosis; where there is still an objection to the fresh diagnosis, the person may commission an appraisal body lawfully qualified to appraise to carry out a medical appraisal of the mental disorder. Article thirty-five: where the fresh diagnosis or the appraisal report shows that the person cannot be established as having a severe mental disorder, or that inpatient treatment is not needed, the medical institution may not carry out inpatient treatment. Article seventy-eight: a person who deliberately has a patient without a mental disorder sent to a medical institution for treatment as though they had one bears liability in accordance with the law (nationwide, in force from 2013-5-1, amended 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Mental Health Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国精神卫生法）(2018 amendment, articles thirty, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-five and seventy-eight). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7448a066d>
- Notes: The other way round, not just anybody can be sent in either. Article twenty-eight provides that where a person suspected of having a mental disorder has already carried out an act of self-harm or of endangering another's safety, or is at risk of doing so, their close relatives, their work unit or the local public security organ must immediately take measures to stop it and send them to a medical institution for a diagnosis of mental disorder. A medical institution that receives such a person may not refuse to make a diagnosis. So "sent in for a diagnosis" and "kept in for admission" are two doors: the first is low, the second is high. Article seventy-five lists the medical institution's own liability; carrying out restraint or isolation against the rules, or diagnosing a person without a mental disorder as having one, leads to discipline up to withdrawal of the practising certificate. What to do when somebody near you has an urge to hurt someone: see section 8.
### 4. The person who cares long-term for a disabled family member needs somebody watching their own health
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Hand a share of the caring work to somebody else, and get a check-up yourself.
- In plain language: A carer collapsing is not self-indulgence. A United States study followed more than eight hundred older people for four years. The ones who were caring for a disabled spouse and also reported mental strain had about sixty per cent higher death risk than people who did not care for anyone. Carers who did not feel strained showed no such rise. So what needs watching is not the caring itself, but that long-term tension.
- Benefit: The study was a prospective population cohort, run in four communities in the United States between 1993 and 1998, with an average follow-up of 4.5 years. There were 392 carers and 427 non-carers, aged 66 to 96, all living with a spouse. Within four years 103 people (12.6%) died. After adjusting for socio-demographic factors, existing illness and subclinical cardiovascular disease, people who were caring and reported mental or emotional strain had a death risk 63% higher than the non-caring controls (RR 1.63, 95% CI 1.00–2.65, credible range). Carers who did not report strain (RR 1.08, 95% CI 0.61–1.90) and people whose spouse was disabled but who did not do the caring themselves (RR 1.37, 95% CI 0.73–2.58) showed no rise in adjusted mortality
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Schulz R, Beach SR (1999). Caregiving as a risk factor for mortality: the Caregiver Health Effects Study. JAMA, 282(23):2215-2219. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.282.23.2215>
- Notes:Disputed: The lower bound of the credible range in this study sits exactly on 1.00, so the possibility of "no difference" has not been ruled out. The population is older Americans living with a spouse, caring for an older spouse who is disabled; applying it to people caring for a younger disabled relative is an extrapolation. What it can tell you is the direction: the people who find it a strain run higher risk. Hand over as much of the caring as you can; ask the local federation of disabled persons and the civil affairs department about respite and day-care services. The carer should not stop their own chronic illness medication: see item 1 in section 16 (take the medicine as prescribed). The person who benefits from this item is yourself.
### 5. If you sit in a wheelchair long-term, switch to a pressure-relief cushion and check your sitting bones and tailbone every day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pressure-relief cushion runs from a few hundred yuan to over a thousand. One look a day, one minute.
- In plain language: An ordinary foam cushion presses on the skin long enough to break it, and once broken it heals slowly and easily gets infected. Older people in nursing homes who use pressure-relief cushions have clearly fewer pressure ulcers over the sitting bones, but once the tailbone is counted in there is no difference. The wheelchair itself also has to be fitted to the person's measurements; do not settle for a standard model.
- Benefit: This randomised clinical trial was done in 12 nursing homes, recruiting 232 residents between 2004 and 2008 who were over 65, sat in a wheelchair more than 6 hours a day, and scored below 18 on the Braden scale. Everyone was first fitted with a well-fitting wheelchair, then randomised into two groups. One group used a skin-protection cushion (air, viscous fluid with foam, or gel with foam), the other a 7.6 cm thick segmented foam cushion. Follow-up was 6 months, or until a pressure ulcer appeared. Pressure ulcers at the ischial tuberosity: 8 cases (6.7%) in the segmented foam group, 1 case (0.9%) in the skin-protection cushion group, P=0.04. Counting ulcers at the ischium and the sacrococcygeal area together: 21 cases (17.6%) in the segmented foam group, 12 cases (10.6%) in the skin-protection cushion group, P=0.14, which showed no difference
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Brienza D, Kelsey S, Karg P, et al. (2010). A randomized clinical trial on preventing pressure ulcers with wheelchair seat cushions. Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, 58(12):2308-2314. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2010.03168.x>
- Notes: There are two reasons for the grade B. First, the sample was only 232 people, the P value of 0.04 on the primary endpoint had just crossed the line, and the secondary endpoint showed no difference. Second, the population is very old nursing-home residents, so applying it to young wheelchair users is an extrapolation. In the trial everyone was first fitted with a well-fitting wheelchair, so "fits" and "cushion" work together; do not change only the cushion. For pressure ulcers during long bed rest, an air mattress and turning on a schedule: see item 8 in section 17 (pressure ulcers). How to apply for the subsidy for fitting basic assistive devices: see item 7 in this section (ask everything at the county federation).
### 6. Do not buy therapies and devices that "can cure paralysis, blindness or deafness"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, it saves money. The hard part is that the family is holding on to hope and cannot be talked out of it.
- In plain language: The Advertising Law has settled the wording. Advertisements for medical treatment, drugs and medical devices may not guarantee the effect, may not state a cure rate or an effective rate, and may not use people giving their own testimony. So "stand up after three courses of treatment" or "how many people have already recovered" is itself an unlawful advertisement, and there is no need to examine the theory it puts forward. Something that is not a medical device may not even mention treating illness.
- Benefit: Article sixteen of the Advertising Law (《广告法》) provides that advertisements for medical treatment, drugs and medical devices may not contain the following: 「（一）表示功效、安全性的断言或者保证；（二）说明治愈率或者有效率；（三）与其他药品、医疗器械的功效和安全性或者其他医疗机构比较；（四）利用广告代言人作推荐、证明」, that is, assertions or guarantees of efficacy or safety; statements of a cure rate or an effective rate; comparisons with the efficacy or safety of other drugs or devices, or with other medical institutions; and recommendations or endorsements by advertising spokespeople. Article seventeen provides that, apart from advertisements for medical treatment, drugs and medical devices, 「禁止其他任何广告涉及疾病治疗功能，并不得使用医疗用语或者易使推销的商品与药品、医疗器械相混淆的用语」, that is, no other advertisement may concern the treatment of disease, and none may use medical terms, or terms likely to confuse the goods being promoted with drugs or medical devices. Article four provides that advertisements may not contain false or misleading content, may not deceive or mislead consumers, and the advertiser is responsible for the truth of the content (nationwide, amended 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国广告法）(2021 amendment, articles four, sixteen and seventeen). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Notes: There is only one test: see whether it dares to promise everything. The rehabilitation department of a proper hospital will not promise you that you will stand up after a number of courses of treatment. When you meet this kind of advertising, report it to 12315 or to the local market regulator. Health supplements use the same script: see item 10 in section 6 (health supplements). If you have already bought and want your money back, follow the method for online shopping and pre-paid consumption: see item 29 in section 5 (online shopping follows the platform's rules). The people who benefit from this item are yourself and your family.

### 7. Once the disability certificate is issued, go to the county federation and ask what you can apply for, all in one visit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One trip to the county federation, and one morning of asking. There is more than one department to go to afterwards.
- In plain language: The disability certificate pays nothing itself; it is a key. What hangs off it is several benefits, each independent of the others, and you have to apply for each one yourself; none of them comes to you. On your first visit to the federation, go through the items on the list one by one and ask about them. That saves you several trips back and forth.
- Benefit: At least six benefits hang off the certificate, and each has its own basis. First, the living allowance for disabled people in difficulty and the care allowance for severely disabled people: see item 8 in section 7 (the two subsidies). Second, rehabilitation assistance for disabled children: see item 8 in this section (rehabilitation assistance). Third, the subsidy for basic assistive devices, whose basis is Article twenty-six of the Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities（残疾预防和残疾人康复条例）. Fourth, the subsidy for adapting the home to be barrier-free: see item 9 in this section (ramps, handrails and bathroom modifications). Fifth, quota employment and employment services: see item 10 in this section (say up front that you hold the certificate). Sixth, the reduction in individual income tax: see item 11 in this section (individual income tax reduction). Article twenty-six of those Regulations also states that the state provides basic rehabilitation services for disabled people in difficulty in urban and rural areas and for severely disabled people by carrying out key rehabilitation programmes, and 「按照国家有关规定对基本型辅助器具配置给予补贴」. Article twenty provides that the relevant departments of people's governments at county level and above and the federations shall make use of community resources, set up rehabilitation premises or purchase services, and carry out community rehabilitation work such as rehabilitation guidance, training in the activities of daily living, rehabilitation care, fitting of assistive devices, information and referral (nationwide, in force from 2017-7-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2017). Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities（残疾预防和残疾人康复条例）(Articles twenty, twenty-six). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: The procedure for getting the certificate itself is in item 10 in section 24 (disability certificate). The subsidy standards and the detailed measures are set mostly at provincial level, and the same item can differ severalfold between two provinces. As you ask, write down the name of each item, and at home look up the original document by that name on the local federation's website and on the government's website; do not rely only on what the counter says. Do not expect to get it all in one visit; go back and ask again a year later, because the policies change.

### 8. If the child is under 7 and has a disability or autism, apply to the county federation for rehabilitation assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Submit one application at the county federation, then choose one institution from the designated list.
- In plain language: The state has built a rehabilitation assistance system for children with disabilities and children with autism aged 0 to 6, covering surgery, assistive devices and rehabilitation training. Families in difficulty are served first; where local finances allow, the age range and the household income conditions may be relaxed. This is the window in which rehabilitation works best — do not wait until it is time for school. Institutions must be chosen from the federation's designated list.
- Benefit: The State Council's Opinions on Establishing a Rehabilitation Assistance System for Children with Disabilities (《国务院关于建立残疾儿童康复救助制度的意见》) states that the beneficiaries are eligible children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical or intellectual disabilities, and children with autism. These include children with disabilities from families on the urban or rural minimum living allowance, from registered poor households, children with disabilities raised in child welfare institutions, disabled orphans, children with disabilities within the scope of support for persons in extreme poverty, and children with disabilities from other financially strained families. The specific criteria for "other financially strained families" are laid down by local people's governments at county level and above. The document also states 「有条件的地区，可扩大残疾儿童康复救助年龄范围，也可放宽对救助对象家庭经济条件的限制」, that is, areas with the means may widen the age range of the assistance and relax the household income conditions for beneficiaries. Funding standards are set by people's governments at county level and above according to local finances, the number of beneficiaries and the category of disability, with a dynamic adjustment mechanism. The higher-level basis is Article twenty-six of the Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities: the state establishes a rehabilitation assistance system for children with disabilities, 「逐步实现0—6岁视力、听力、言语、肢体、智力等残疾儿童和孤独症儿童免费得到手术、辅助器具配置和康复训练等服务」, that is, gradually ensuring that children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical or intellectual disabilities and children with autism receive surgery, assistive-device fitting and rehabilitation training free of charge (nationwide, from 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2018). Opinions on Establishing a Rehabilitation Assistance System for Children with Disabilities (《国务院关于建立残疾儿童康复救助制度的意见》) (Guo Fa [2018] No. 20). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-07/10/content_5305296.htm>; State Council (2017). Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities (《残疾预防和残疾人康复条例》) (article twenty-six). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: The assistance standards are set by each province; first search the local federation's website for 「残疾儿童康复救助实施办法」. The designated-institution list is also in the federation's hands. The document requires the federation, together with the relevant departments, to tighten supervision of entry and exit for designated rehabilitation institutions, establish regular inspection and comprehensive evaluation mechanisms, and set up a blacklist. For an institution not on the list you pay every yuan yourself, and if something goes wrong it is hard to hold anyone to account. The beneficiary of this item is your child, which puts it in the second tier. Institutions that promise to "guarantee a cure" are handled under item 6 in this section (can cure paralysis).
### 9. Ramps, handrails and bathroom modifications at home can be subsidised by a government at county level or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Applying costs nothing. Most of the work itself is subsidised, but you may still have to cover part of it. You will have to visit the federation and the subdistrict office.
- In plain language: The Law on Barrier-free Environment (《无障碍环境建设法》) has a clause: governments at county level and above shall support and guide barrier-free home modifications, and shall give appropriate subsidies to qualifying households with disabled or elderly members. These projects are generally led by the federation and run on annual quotas, so register early. How much is paid and which items are covered is decided locally.
- Benefit: Article nineteen of the Law on Barrier-free Environment: 「县级以上人民政府应当支持、指导家庭无障碍设施改造。对符合条件的残疾人、老年人家庭应当给予适当补贴。」, that is, people's governments at county level and above shall support and guide barrier-free home modifications, and shall give appropriate subsidies to qualifying households with disabled or elderly members. Article eighteen provides that, for existing residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transport facilities, urban and rural roads and the like that do not meet the barrier-free facility engineering construction standards, people's governments at county level and above shall, in light of actual circumstances, formulate targeted barrier-free facility modification plans and organise their implementation. Article sixty-two provides that any organisation or individual has the right to put opinions and suggestions to the relevant competent departments of the government on strengthening and improving the barrier-free environment, and to complain or report behaviour that violates this Law; a competent department that receives such a complaint or report 「应当及时处理并予以答复」, that is, shall handle it promptly and reply. Article sixty-three provides that where conduct in violation of this Law harms the public interest, the people's procuratorate may raise a procuratorial suggestion or bring public interest litigation (nationwide, in force from 2023-9-1)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Barrier-free Environment Construction Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国无障碍环境建设法）(articles eighteen, nineteen, sixty-two and sixty-three). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff80818188d7430b0189018493370940>
- Notes: The grade is B because the law only writes 「应当给予适当补贴」 — shall give appropriate subsidies; how much is paid and who counts as qualifying is handed entirely to the localities, with no nationally uniform number. First search the local federation's website for 「困难重度残疾人家庭无障碍改造」. Quotas are mostly allocated by year, so queue early. Adding a lift to a residential building and modifying stairwells is a different process, through the housing and urban-rural development department. Where the barrier-free facilities in a public place fall short, complain to the competent department under Article sixty-two — it has to answer you.
### 10. When job hunting, say up front that you hold the certificate; hiring you offsets a payment for the firm
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One extra sentence in the interview. The hard part is bringing it up.
- In plain language: The proportion of disabled employees a work unit arranges may not be lower than 1.5% of its total on-the-job staff, with the exact proportion set by the province. Fall short and the unit has to pay the disability employment security fund on the head-count gap, each head calculated on the unit's average annual staff wage of the previous year. So hiring you is not just a favour — it saves real money. Say clearly in the interview that you hold the certificate, and the other side can do the arithmetic itself.
- Benefit: Article eight of the Regulations on Employment of Persons with Disabilities (《残疾人就业条例》): 「用人单位安排残疾人就业的比例不得低于本单位在职职工总数的1.5%。具体比例由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地区的实际情况规定。」, that is, the proportion of disabled employees a unit arranges may not be lower than 1.5% of its own total on-the-job staff, and the specific proportion is set by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality according to local circumstances. Article nine: a unit that fails to reach the proportion set by the provincial people's government where it is located shall pay the disability employment security fund. ### 16. Do your rehabilitation in a proper institution that has a rehabilitation medicine department, and let the therapist set the intensity; more is not better

### 17. If your hearing is going, get a hearing aid fitted; the reason is to hear, not to prevent dementia
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->

- In plain language: The claim that 「戴助听器能防痴呆」 does not hold up at the moment. A three-year trial of close to a thousand people showed no difference overall. - Benefit: The ACHIEVE trial was carried out in 4 communities in the United States and included 977 people aged 70 to 84 with untreated hearing loss and no obvious cognitive impairment. The participants who came from that long-term cardiovascular health study were older, had more risk factors for cognitive decline and had lower baseline cognitive scores. The authors' conclusion is that a hearing intervention may reduce the 3-year change in cognition among people at higher risk of decline, and does not among those at lower risk. The trial did not report serious adverse events attributed to the study
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Lin FR, Pike JR, Albert MS, et al.; ACHIEVE Collaborative Research Group (2023). The other way round, if an adult's intelligence or mental health recovers, an application can be made for a determination that he has regained capacity. This procedure is a restriction and a protection at the same time.
- Benefit: Article twenty-one of the Civil Code《民法典》says: 「不能辨认自己行为的成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」 Article twenty-two says: 「不能完全辨认自己行为的成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为。」 Article twenty-four says: 「不能辨认或者不能完全辨认自己行为的成年人，其利害关系人或者有关组织，可以向人民法院申请认定该成年人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人。」 The same article also provides that once a determination has been made, on application by the person himself, by an interested party or by a relevant organisation, the people's court may, according to the state of his recovery in intelligence and mental health, determine that he has regained capacity for civil conduct as a person with limited capacity or as a person with full capacity. Relevant organisations include: residents' committees, villagers' committees, schools, medical institutions, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, lawfully established organisations for the elderly, and the civil affairs departments. Article twenty-three says that the guardian of a person with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity is his legal representative (nationwide, in force from 2021-1-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Articles twenty-one, twenty-two, twenty-three and twenty-four)《中华人民共和国民法典（第二十一条、第二十二条、第二十三条、第二十四条）》. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: The application goes through the court's special procedure, which is faster than an ordinary civil case but still takes several months. You have to submit a diagnosis from a hospital, and in most cases the court will also commission a judicial assessment. The assessment fee is usually several thousand yuan, advanced by the applicant. Signing for someone, or disposing of his property, without going through this procedure may later be found invalid, and a flat that has been sold may have to be returned. One more point to keep in mind: limited capacity for civil conduct does not mean nothing can be done. Things that match his intelligence and mental condition, and things that bring him pure benefit, he can do himself. Do not act for him across the board. For how the guardian is determined see item 19 in this section (guardian)
### 19. A guardian for an adult is determined in the order set by law; if the person under guardianship injures someone else, the guardian pays
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Where there is a dispute you go to the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, the civil affairs department or the court
- In plain language: When an adult loses capacity for civil conduct, the guardian follows in order: the spouse, parents and children, other close relatives, and last any other person or organisation willing to take the role. Where they dispute it, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee or the civil affairs department designates the guardian, and anyone who objects can go to the court. While the person is still of sound mind he can nominate in writing who his future guardian will be. If the person under guardianship injures someone else, the guardian pays.
- Benefit: Article twenty-eight of the Civil Code《民法典》says that an adult with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity shall have as guardian, in order, one of the following who has the capacity to be a guardian: 「（一）配偶；（二）父母、子女；（三）其他近亲属；（四）其他愿意担任监护人的个人或者组织，但是须经被监护人住所地的居民委员会、村民委员会或者民政部门同意。」 Article thirty-one says that where there is a dispute over the determination of the guardian, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee or the civil affairs department of the place where the person under guardianship resides designates the guardian, and anyone who objects to the designation may apply to the people's court for a designation, or apply to the court directly. In making the designation it 「应当尊重被监护人的真实意愿，按照最有利于被监护人的原则」. Where the person's rights and interests were left unprotected before the designation, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, an organisation provided for by law, or the civil affairs department acts as temporary guardian. Article thirty-three says: 「具有完全民事行为能力的成年人，可以与其近亲属、其他愿意担任监护人的个人或者组织事先协商，以书面形式确定自己的监护人」. Article one thousand one hundred and eighty-eight says: 「无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人造成他人损害的，由监护人承担侵权责任。监护人尽到监护职责的，可以减轻其侵权责任。」 Where the person has property, the compensation is paid out of his own property first and the guardian pays what is left. Article one thousand one hundred and eighty-nine says that where a guardian entrusts the duties of guardianship to another person, the guardian still bears tort liability, and the person entrusted bears the corresponding liability where he is at fault (nationwide, in force from 2021-1-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Articles twenty-eight, thirty-one, thirty-three, one thousand one hundred and eighty-eight and one thousand one hundred and eighty-nine)《中华人民共和国民法典（第二十八条、第三十一条、第三十三条、第一千一百八十八条、第一千一百八十九条）》. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: Article thirty-three is voluntary guardianship. Fixing the person in writing while he is still of sound mind saves most of the disputes that follow; for how to write it see item 1 in section 17 (voluntary guardianship). Be careful with the compensation part: handing the person over to a care home or a relative to look after him does not take the responsibility off the guardian, and the person entrusted answers for his own fault only. Guardian liability insurance for the person under guardianship is therefore worth having. Article seventy-eight of the Mental Health Law《精神卫生法》also says that a guardian who abandons a patient, or who fails in his duties and causes harm, bears compensation liability under the law
### 20. Refused a job, kept off the permanent staff or underpaid because of a disability: you can sue, but work out the cost of the process first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Legal fees run from several thousand to several tens of thousands of yuan, and you pay them yourself. An ordinary procedure at first instance takes 6 months or more, and can be extended. You have to preserve the evidence yourself.
- In plain language: The law forbids discrimination against disabled people in recruitment, confirmation of permanent status, promotion, grading of professional title, pay and social insurance. You can complain to the competent authority, and you can sue in the court. But if you win the case, the other side does not pay your legal fees. So complain first, apply for legal aid at the same time, and only then think about going to court.
- Benefit: Article three of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities《残疾人保障法》says: 「禁止基于残疾的歧视。禁止侮辱、侵害残疾人。」 Article thirty-eight says: 「在职工的招用、转正、晋级、职称评定、劳动报酬、生活福利、休息休假、社会保险等方面，不得歧视残疾人。」 The same article also provides that the unit where a disabled employee works shall, according to the characteristics of the disabled employee, provide suitable working conditions and labour protection, and shall, as actually necessary, adapt the workplace, the equipment and the living facilities. Article sixty-four says: 「违反本法规定，在职工的招用等方面歧视残疾人的，由有关主管部门责令改正；残疾人劳动者可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。」 Article four of the Regulations on the Employment of Persons with Disabilities《残疾人就业条例》says: 「禁止在就业中歧视残疾人。」 Article thirteen says that an employer shall not discriminate against disabled employees in promotion, advancement, grading of professional title, pay, social insurance, living benefits and the like. Article forty-two of the Legal Aid Law《法律援助法》says that an applicant who has documents proving that he belongs to a specific group such as 「无固定生活来源的未成年人、老年人、残疾人等特定群体」 is exempted from any check on whether he is financially in difficulty. Article forty-five says that where a legal aid institution provides legal aid services to elderly people or to persons with disabilities, it shall provide accessible facilities, equipment and services as the circumstances require (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities (Articles three, thirty-eight and sixty-four)《中华人民共和国残疾人保障法（第三条、第三十八条、第六十四条）》. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f1d134c88132b> ; State Council (国务院) (2007). Regulations on the Employment of Persons with Disabilities (Articles four and thirteen)《残疾人就业条例（第四条、第十三条）》. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6646.htm> ; Legal Aid Law of the People's Republic of China (Articles forty-two and forty-five)《中华人民共和国法律援助法（第四十二条、第四十五条）》. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817b6472a3017b6577820f0061>
- Notes: The cost of the process has to be worked out first. An ordinary procedure at first instance takes 6 months or more and can be extended; a simplified procedure takes 3 months. Legal fees are not among the litigation costs listed in Article six of the Regulations on the Payment of Litigation Costs《诉讼费用交纳办法》, and the 「败诉方负担」 in Article twenty-nine does not include legal fees. So take the two cheap routes first: complain to the human resources and social security department or to the disabled persons' federation, which must order the discrimination stopped; and apply for legal aid at the same time, because Article forty-two exempts disabled people from the check on financial hardship. Collecting evidence is the hardest part. When you are turned down at an interview the other side seldom leaves a written reason, so keep a recording and keep the messages. Once you are employed, the employment dispute goes to labour arbitration; for keeping evidence and the time limit see item 8 in section 19 (keep the evidence before you leave). The person who benefits from this entry is you yourself
