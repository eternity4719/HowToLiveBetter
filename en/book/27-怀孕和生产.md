# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: from test to paperwork

The first half of this section counts the risk of death for the pregnant woman and the fetus. The second half counts money and time spent on errands. The two are not converted into each other. The text only says what to do and when. Pregnancy nutrition and prenatal education have no hard evidence behind them or make very little difference, so they are left out. For the money side of maternity allowances, maternity leave and raising a child see section 18; for caring for the baby after birth see section 20; for emergencies see section 13.

The numbers the World Health Organization gives: in 2023 about 260,000 women worldwide died in pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% of those deaths came from five causes: severe bleeding after birth, infection, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (the disorders in which blood pressure rises after conception, including pre-eclampsia and eclampsia), complications during delivery, and unsafe induced abortion. The World Health Organization also says that 「大多数孕产妇死亡是可以预防的，因为预防和处理并发症的医疗手段都是已知的」. The entries with the largest effect in this section are the ones that catch these problems early.

### 1. Start 0.4 mg of folic acid every day before conception and keep taking it through the first 3 months of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan for a bottle. Rural women pay nothing, because the national free folic acid supplementation programme provides it. One tablet a day. The hard part is remembering it every day
- In plain language: One folic acid tablet a day before and during conception cuts the chance of a neural tube defect by about seven tenths. Neural tube defects are birth defects such as anencephaly and spina bifida. If you have already had one child with such a defect, the chance that the next child has one falls to about one third of what it was.
- Benefit: The Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials and 6708 deliveries, and rates the quality of the evidence high. Taking folic acid every day before and during conception gives RR 0.31 for fetal neural tube defects (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, credible range), about a seven-tenths reduction. For women who have already had a child with a neural tube defect, the risk that the next child has one is RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), roughly one third of what it was. Broken down by population group, 0.4 mg and higher doses make no difference. Folic acid on its own and folic acid inside a multivitamin make no difference either
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Notes: What matters is starting before conception. The fetal neural tube closes within 28 days of fertilisation. By the time a pregnancy test turns positive, most of that window has gone. So if you plan to have a child, start 3 months beforehand. The same pooled review found no clear effect of folic acid on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease or miscarriage. Do not expect it to treat anything else
### 2. Before 13 weeks of pregnancy open a Mother and Child Health Handbook at the community health centre, and use up your free prenatal checks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. It is part of the national basic public health service programme. You have to make several trips according to how far along you are
- In plain language: At least five prenatal checks during pregnancy, plus one home visit after the birth and one check at 42 days postpartum, all paid for by the state. The way to claim them is to open a Mother and Child Health Handbook《母子健康手册》at the community health centre before 13 weeks of pregnancy. Without the handbook you simply lose those free checks.
- Benefit: The National Standard for Basic Public Health Services (Third Edition)《国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）》sets out maternal and child health management services. Any woman living in the area who is pregnant can have them: 1 health check in the first trimester, 2 in the second trimester, 2 in the third trimester, 1 postpartum visit and 1 health check at 42 days postpartum. You open the handbook before 13 weeks of pregnancy and have the first prenatal check at the same time. The two checks in the second trimester fall between 16 and 20 weeks and between 21 and 24 weeks. The two in the third trimester fall between 28 and 36 weeks and between 37 and 40 weeks. Locally published lists of services also include the full set of lab tests at the first visit and screening for postpartum depression
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National Health and Family Planning Commission (国家卫生计生委) (2017). National Standard for Basic Public Health Services, Third Edition（国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版））. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf> ; Jingjiang Municipal People's Government (靖江市人民政府). National Basic Public Health Service Programme — Maternal and Child Health Management Services (local published service list)（国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开））. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: Those 5 checks are the free floor, not the whole of prenatal care. The hospital will add ultrasound, blood glucose and fetal heart monitoring where it sees fit, and you pay for those. The earlier you open the handbook, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading is worked out. Pregnancy risk grading puts pregnant women into tiers by risk level. Unless a high-risk tier is found, you cannot be referred in time to a hospital that can take the case. If you live in a district other than your registered one, open the handbook where you live now. There is no need to go back to your registered address
### 3. Test for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B at the first prenatal check, and free blocking treatment is available if any is positive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The programme for preventing mother-to-child transmission provides the tests free
- In plain language: Have all three tested for at the first prenatal check, and the tests cost you nothing. A positive result is not a dead end. The state supplies the medicine and the regimen free, to block the route from mother to child. That is called blocking of mother-to-child transmission.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission means passing an illness from mother to child. The Working Specification on the Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis and Hepatitis B (2020 Edition)《预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）》sets requirements for it. In the first trimester, or at the first prenatal check, the hospital must tell you about this and 「提供适宜、规范的免费检测」, so the three tests cost you nothing. It then gives post-test counselling or a referral depending on the result. A positive result is not a dead end. Article forty-four of the Regulations on the Prevention and Control of AIDS《艾滋病防治条例》says that a pregnant woman infected with HIV is 「免费提供预防艾滋病母婴传播的治疗和咨询」. Article forty-three requires blocking (stopping the illness from passing to the child), treatment, a postpartum visit, follow-up of the baby and testing. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the newborn gets both the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immune globulin at birth (see item 2 in section 20)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2020). Working Specification on the Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis and Hepatitis B (2020 Edition)《预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）》. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml> (reprinted by the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region Health Commission (新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会)) ; State Council (国务院) (2006). Regulations on the Prevention and Control of AIDS (Articles forty-three and forty-four)《艾滋病防治条例》. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html> (reprinted by the Beijing Municipal Health Commission (北京市卫生健康委员会))
- Notes: The earlier these three are tested, the better, because blocking and treatment both take time. Article three of the same regulations also states that 「任何单位和个人不得歧视艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属」. The lawful rights to marry, to find work, to see a doctor and to go to school are protected by law. For everyday prevention and testing see section 1. For post-exposure blocking after high-risk behaviour see section 13
### 4. Not one cigarette and not one drink during pregnancy, and nobody in the household smokes indoors
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money, and you also save the money you would spend on tobacco and alcohol. The hard part is getting everyone you live with to stop as well
- In plain language: Smoking during pregnancy raises the chances of preterm birth, low birth weight and cleft lip and palate. It doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and childbirth. It also raises the risk of sudden infant death syndrome. Smoke breathed in at home by family members counts the same. Quitting at any point still helps.
- Benefit: The US CDC (美国疾病控制与预防中心) puts it this way. Smoking during pregnancy means a baby may be 「足月出生也可能过小」 and may be 「可能早产」. It 「使孕期和分娩期异常出血的风险翻倍」. It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It also 「损害胎儿正在发育的肺和大脑，损害可持续到儿童期」. Babies of mothers who smoke during pregnancy, and babies exposed to smoke after birth, are at higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice is 「最好在怀孕前就戒烟，但如果已经怀孕，戒了仍然有用」 and 「什么时候戒都不晚」.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Notes: Smoke from someone else indoors counts for the fetus, because it is the pregnant woman who breathes it in. So family members should not smoke indoors either. Alcohol is the same: no amount of drinking is known to be safe, and the simplest rule during pregnancy is to drink nothing at all. For what tobacco and alcohol cost an adult in their own right see section 2
### 5. If you have risk factors for pre-eclampsia, start one low-dose aspirin tablet a day after 12 weeks of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Tens of yuan. One tablet a day, taken right through to the birth. The hard part is never forgetting
- In plain language: Pre-eclampsia is a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy. You count as high risk if you had it in a previous pregnancy, are carrying twins or more, have chronic high blood pressure, had diabetes before pregnancy, or have kidney disease or an autoimmune condition. Starting one low-dose aspirin tablet a day at 12 weeks of pregnancy cuts pre-eclampsia by about a tenth and a half, and cuts both preterm birth and the child's death around the time of birth by about a fifth. Everyone else should not take it on their own.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a grade B recommendation: to prevent pre-eclampsia in people at high risk, start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after 12 weeks of pregnancy. Pre-eclampsia is a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy. Pooling several studies gives the following results, where the 95% CI is the credible range. Pre-eclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), about 15% lower. Preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), about 20% lower. Perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), about 21% lower; perinatal death means death in the period around childbirth. Small for gestational age / fetal growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), about 18% lower; this means the fetus grew smaller than usual for its gestational age
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: The high-risk categories are: pre-eclampsia in a previous pregnancy, twins or more this time, and chronic high blood pressure. Also type 1 or type 2 diabetes before pregnancy, kidney disease, and autoimmune conditions such as systemic lupus erythematosus or antiphospholipid syndrome. If you are in none of these categories, do not take it on your own. A doctor has to prescribe this, so give your full medical history at the prenatal check and let the doctor judge. What is sold in China is mostly the 100 mg enteric-coated tablet. Take it as the doctor instructs
### 6. Have one screening for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later, and do not begrudge the sugar drink
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Tens to a little over a hundred yuan. You go fasting and give several blood samples. It takes half a morning
- In plain language: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. After 24 weeks, half a morning with one sugar drink and a few blood samples will find it. Leave it unchecked and the fetus grows too large, labour becomes difficult, you may need an operation, and the risk of pre-eclampsia and of low blood sugar in the newborn both rise.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a grade B recommendation: screen for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later in women with no symptoms (who have noticed nothing wrong themselves). Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. For anyone who screens only once, that one screen should be at 24 weeks or later. On whether screening before 24 weeks helps, the task force concluded that the present evidence is not enough to tell the benefits from the harms (grade I). Untreated gestational diabetes raises the risk of macrosomia (a fetus that grows too large), difficult labour, caesarean section, pre-eclampsia and low blood sugar in the newborn
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Notes: Among the women who test positive, most can keep their blood sugar under control by changing diet and exercise, and only a minority need insulin. Do not skip this one test because you are afraid of what a positive result would mean. After the birth you have to test your blood sugar again: women who have had gestational diabetes are clearly at higher risk of type 2 diabetes later
### 7. Memorise this list of 「立刻去医院」 signs; it counts during pregnancy and for a year after the birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Five minutes to memorise it, and it is best if the people you live with know it too
- In plain language: If any one of these appears, go to hospital that day. Do not sit at home for a night first. A headache that does not lift or keeps getting worse, vision changing, a fever of 38 °C. Severe swelling of the hands or face, breathlessness, chest pain or a fast heartbeat. Severe abdominal pain that does not ease, the baby moving normally and then stopping or clearly less often, vaginal bleeding or fluid loss. One arm or one leg swollen and painful, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. All of it counts for a year after the birth too.
- Benefit: The US CDC lists these urgent maternal warning signs: a headache that does not lift or keeps getting worse, dizziness or fainting, vision changing, a temperature of 38 °C or above; severe swelling of the hands or face, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby, difficulty breathing, chest pain or a very fast heartbeat; severe nausea and vomiting, severe abdominal pain that does not ease, and during pregnancy the baby moving normally and then stopping or clearly less often; vaginal bleeding or fluid loss during pregnancy, or abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after the birth; one arm or one leg severely swollen, red or painful. All of these can happen within a year after the birth. Getting out of the maternity ward does not make you safe
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html> ; World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: What this list is for is telling 「再忍忍」 apart from 「必须马上走」. A headache together with blurred vision and pain in the upper abdomen points to pre-eclampsia (the high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy). One lower leg swollen and painful points to deep vein thrombosis (see section 13). By the World Health Organization's account, severe bleeding after birth can take the life of a healthy woman within hours. Go straight to hospital as soon as any one item appears, and do not spend a night at home first

### 8. If your waters break, lie down flat where you are, raise your hips and call 120; do not walk about and do not bathe
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money
- In plain language: When the waters break you do not pack slowly and then set off. The right move is to lie down where you are, raise your hips and call 120. Standing and walking makes the fluid drain faster, and the umbilical cord can be washed out with it. That is an emergency that can kill the baby.
- Benefit: The handling given by the Beijing Municipal Health Commission (北京市卫生健康委员会) is this: once the waters have broken, 「马上找个地方平躺下来，防止羊水流出来」 and 「不要随意走动」, and 「立即拨打 120 急救电话」. While you wait for the ambulance, tell your family to bring your things and meet you at the hospital. Material from medical institutions explains why: fluid drains faster when a woman is standing, and the umbilical cord can come out with it. That is called cord prolapse, and it is an emergency that puts the fetus at risk. So lie down and raise your hips. A left lateral position is one way to do that
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Beijing Municipal Health Commission (北京市卫生健康委员会). Weekly first aid topic: what to do when a pregnant woman's waters break early（每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html> ; Beijing Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital (北京市顺义区妇幼保健院). What to do when the waters break at home in late pregnancy（孕晚期在家破水怎么办？）. <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: The three signs that labour is close are regular contractions, the bloody show, and the waters breaking. You can pack calmly for the first two. Breaking of the waters is the only one that means lying down on the spot and calling for a car. Bathing, squatting on the toilet and driving yourself after the waters break are all classic mistakes. Write down the time the waters broke and the colour of the fluid, and tell the doctor. Yellow or green fluid, or fluid with flakes of meconium in it, points to fetal distress (the fetus is short of oxygen inside the uterus). If regular contractions, a clear drop in the baby's movement, and fever with shivering are happening at the same time, stress to the doctor that this is an emergency
### 9. If you want pain relief in labour, say so directly. It does not raise the risk of caesarean section
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Several hundred to over a thousand yuan, and the price differs from place to place. You have to ask the hospital and the anaesthesia department in advance whether they can do it. The hard part is raising it yourself
- In plain language: Pain relief in labour is an injection of anaesthetic into the back that pushes down the pain of giving birth. It works better than other ways of easing pain, and it does not make you more likely to have an operation. The claim that 「打了无痛腰疼一辈子」 is no better founded. It now has a separate billing item of its own, so hospitals are more willing to provide it.
- Benefit: The Cochrane systematic review concludes that epidural analgesia is more effective than methods without an epidural at relieving labour pain and at raising how satisfied a woman is with the analgesia. Pain relief in labour is exactly epidural analgesia. The same review also says that 「硬膜外镇痛对剖宫产风险和长期腰痛没有影响」. The rise in instrumental birth (using instruments to deliver the baby) is no longer seen in studies from 2005 onwards. From 2024, the project of the National Healthcare Security Administration for setting up prices for obstetric medical services gives 「分娩镇痛」, 「导乐分娩」 and 「亲情陪产」 items of their own. The reason is precisely that without a separate price item before, hospitals had little incentive to offer pain relief in labour
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4> ; National Healthcare Security Administration (国家医保局) (2024). Rebuilding the obstetric service price items to help build a childbirth-friendly society《重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会》. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: The two claims that spread widest are that pain relief in labour means a back problem for life, and that it makes the birth hard so an operation is needed. Neither is founded. Cochrane pooled epidural analgesia with the other methods and concluded that it has no effect on the risk of caesarean section and none on long-term back pain. The real side effects are these: low blood pressure, fever, weakness in the legs, difficulty passing urine, and a longer first and second stage of labour with more likelihood of needing oxytocin. Whether to have it is yours to decide, but do not give it up on the strength of a rumour. Whether it is available also depends on whether the hospital has an anaesthetist on site 24 hours a day. Ask when you go for the prenatal check
### 10. Without a medical indication do not ask for a caesarean section, and do not have an operation to pick an auspicious date
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is standing your ground against what the family says
- In plain language: The World Health Organization's position is that once the caesarean rate in a place passes 10%, deaths among mothers and newborns stop falling any further. Where there is a reason to operate, operate. Having an operation to pick a date, or because you are afraid of pain, buys you one large abdominal operation for nothing.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization's 2015 statement: when the caesarean rate in a population rises to about 10%, deaths among mothers and newborns fall. Past 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves any further. The World Health Organization also stresses that every effort should be made to give a caesarean section to the women who need one, rather than to reach some particular rate
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates（WHO/RHR/15.02）. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Notes: When there is a real medical need, a caesarean section is an operation that saves lives. Do it, and do not tough it out. What to avoid is an operation that is not medically needed, whether out of fear of pain, to pick an auspicious date, or to fit in with the age at which the child starts school. A caesarean section is a large abdominal operation, recovery is slower, and the next pregnancy carries higher risks of placenta previa, placenta accreta and uterine rupture. That bears directly on whether you still want a second child
### 11. Check your maternity insurance status before conception; a spouse who is not working can also claim the maternity medical costs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One call to the social insurance or medical insurance enquiry line
- In plain language: When an employer pays maternity insurance, the medical costs of the birth and the allowance during maternity leave come out of that fund. If the spouse does not work, the medical costs can be claimed as well. Whether you have paid in for enough months, and whether a birth outside the area has to be filed for first, both have to be asked in advance. Neither can be put right afterwards.
- Benefit: Article fifty-four of the Social Insurance Law《社会保险法》says: where an employer has already paid maternity insurance contributions, its employees enjoy maternity insurance benefits. Where an employee's spouse does not work, the spouse enjoys the maternity medical cost benefits as the state provides, and the money is paid out of the maternity insurance fund. Put plainly, once the employer pays maternity insurance the employee qualifies for the benefits, and the medical costs of the birth of that employee's non-working spouse come out of the same fund. The benefits have two parts. One is the maternity medical cost, which is the medical cost of the birth itself. The other is the maternity allowance, the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article fifty-six sets out the three cases in which the maternity allowance can be received: a female employee giving birth and taking maternity leave, taking leave for a family planning operation, and other cases set out in laws and regulations. The maternity allowance is 「按照职工所在用人单位上年度职工月平均工资计发」, which works out at the average monthly wage of the employees of your employer last year, not at your own wage
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2010). Social Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China (Articles fifty-four and fifty-six)《中华人民共和国社会保险法（第五十四、五十六条）》. <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml> (reprinted by the Guangdong Provincial Tax Service, State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局广东省税务局))
- Notes: Two things have to be asked in advance, because neither can be put right afterwards. The first is whether the number of consecutive months of contributions is enough: every place sets a minimum, and below it you get nothing. The second is whether a birth outside the place of enrolment has to be filed for in advance: without the filing you may have to pay the money yourself first and claim it by hand afterwards, and sometimes you cannot claim it at all. For the number of days of maternity leave and how the maternity allowance is worked out see item 2 in section 18
### 12. Get the Medical Certificate of Birth done before you leave the hospital; decide the name in advance and do not get a character wrong
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. It is done as part of the discharge formalities
- In plain language: This certificate is a precondition for household registration, for insurance and for school, and only the institution that delivered the baby can issue the first one. If you go back for it after leaving the hospital, you go through a separate set of procedures and hand in more documents. Check first that the characters in the name can be produced by the household registration system.
- Benefit: Article twenty-three of the Law on Maternal and Infant Health《母婴保健法》says: medical and health institutions and the persons who carry out home births shall, in accordance with the rules of the health administrative department of the State Council, issue the uniformly produced and issued Medical Certificate of Birth for the newborn. This certificate is the precondition for everything that comes after it: household registration, insurance, a medical insurance card, and kindergarten and school. And the first issue can only be done by the institution that delivered the baby. If you go back for it after leaving the hospital, you have to follow a different set of procedures and supply extra supporting documents
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (1994, amended 2017). Law of the People's Republic of China on Maternal and Infant Health (Article twenty-three)《中华人民共和国母婴保健法（第二十三条）》. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Notes: Before filling it in, check the parents' names, their identity card numbers and the child's name character by character. The characters used in the name have to be ones the household registration system can produce, and rare characters make every later errand harder. Changing the name or reissuing the certificate means going through a supplementary or a replacement issue, which is far more trouble than checking it on the spot. Keep the certificate on its own once you have it, as carefully as the household register and the identity card
### 13. Do not refuse either the newborn heel-prick screening or the hearing screening
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free in most regions, or tens of yuan. Some extended items are yours to pay for. The baby's heel is pricked once after birth
- In plain language: One prick of the heel and one hearing test can find hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria and hearing loss while the child still looks completely normal. By the time the symptoms show, the damage to the child's intellect cannot be taken back.
- Benefit: Article three of the Measures for the Administration of Screening for Neonatal Diseases《新生儿疾病筛查管理办法》states: 「全国新生儿疾病筛查病种包括先天性甲状腺功能减低症、苯丙酮尿症等新生儿遗传代谢病和听力障碍。」 These two kinds of illness have one thing in common. In the early stage nothing abnormal can be seen, and by the time the symptoms are obvious the damage to the intellect is already irreversible. Early detection and early intervention work well. The methods are thyroid hormone replacement, special formula, a hearing aid, or a cochlear implant. Article ten says that where a screening result is positive, the institution shall notify the guardian in good time to have a confirmatory diagnosis
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Health (卫生部) (2009). Measures for the Administration of Screening for Neonatal Diseases (Ministry of Health Order No. 64, Articles three, ten and eleven)《新生儿疾病筛查管理办法（卫生部令第 64 号，第三、十、十一条）》. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Notes: Article eleven requires that before the screening the items, the conditions, the method, the sensitivity (how much it can find) and the cost are all explained truthfully to the guardian, who then signs consent. So you will be given an informed consent form. Read it before you sign: which conditions are the ones the state requires, and which are extra items you pay for. A positive first screen is not a diagnosis. Most are ruled out at the repeat test, so do not fall apart at this stage. If the first hearing screen is not passed, go for the repeat test when you are told to. Do not decide on your own that the child can hear well enough
### 14. Enrol the child in resident medical insurance as soon as you have the Medical Certificate of Birth; do not wait for household registration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Resident medical insurance is paid once a year, a few hundred yuan
- In plain language: Once you have the certificate you can enrol the child online, with no need to wait for household registration. Enrolled, the inpatient bill from the hospital stay can be settled on the spot. A newborn's inpatient bill is not small, and being enrolled or not makes a large difference.
- Benefit: The State Council's official website (中国政府网) puts it this way in 2024: 「新生儿凭出生医学证明就可以在线上参保」, with no need to register the household first. 「新生儿出院以后就能够直接报销医药费用」. The old practice was that you had to register the household before you could enrol, and the inpatient bill had to be paid in full up front and claimed afterwards. The average time to process an enrolment and a payment has also come down from 28.7 working days at the start of the year to 6.4 working days
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council official website (中国政府网) (2024). Regular policy briefing on a sound long-term mechanism for basic medical insurance enrolment《健全基本医疗保险参保长效机制国务院政策例行吹风会》. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Notes: Preterm birth, jaundice needing phototherapy and newborn pneumonia are the common reasons for a newborn to be admitted, and they run into thousands of yuan. Being enrolled or not makes a large difference. The rules on how many days after birth you must enrol in order to get cover from the day of birth itself are not the same everywhere. For how it is actually applied, follow what the local medical insurance authority says. One call to 12393 before you leave the hospital settles it
### 15. Go to the police station to register the birth within one month of the child being born
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. One trip to the household registration window at the police station
- In plain language: The law gives you one month, not "when there is time". Household registration holds up the vaccinations, the kindergarten, the school and the identity card that come after it. The longer you put it off, the more documents you have to supply later.
- Benefit: Article seven of the Regulations on Household Registration《户口登记条例》says: 「婴儿出生后一个月以内，由户主、亲属、抚养人或者邻居向婴儿常住地户口登记机关申报出生登记。」 That is a deadline set by law, not a suggestion. Household registration is the precondition for what follows: vaccinations, kindergarten, school, the identity card. The longer you put it off, the more documents you have to supply
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (1958). Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Household Registration (Article seven)《中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第七条）》. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html> (reprinted by the Guangdong Provincial People's Government (广东省人民政府))
- Notes: Bring all of these: the Medical Certificate of Birth, both parents' identity cards and household registers, and the marriage certificate. Whether to register with the father or with the mother is yours to choose, but the rules of the two places differ and both the school catchment and the place of insurance are affected. Decide before you go; changing it later is a nuisance. A child born outside marriage can be registered in the same way

### 16. Do not skip the 42-day postpartum check; it is also the screening for postpartum depression
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money, it is part of the basic public health service. It takes half a day. The hard part is that you have just left the maternity ward and cannot be bothered
- In plain language: This check is free. It covers a full blood count and a routine vaginal swab, and it includes one screening for postpartum depression. Serious problems can still happen within a year after the birth, so do not assume you are safe once the maternity ward is behind you.
- Benefit: The National Standard for Basic Public Health Services (Third Edition)《国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）》sets out maternal and child management, with 1 postpartum visit and 1 health check at 42 days postpartum. Locally published lists of services show that this check includes lab tests (a full blood count and a routine vaginal swab) and 1 screening for postpartum depression. Serious complications can occur at any time within a year after the birth. In the list 「立刻去医院」 in item 7, 「有伤害自己或伤害孩子的念头」, 「产后阴道出血或异常分泌物」 and 「一侧肢体严重肿痛」 are all postnatal
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National Health and Family Planning Commission (国家卫生计生委) (2017). National Standard for Basic Public Health Services, Third Edition（国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版））. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf> ; Jingjiang Municipal People's Government (靖江市人民政府). National Basic Public Health Service Programme — Maternal and Child Health Management Services (local published service list)（国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开））. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: Postpartum depression is not fussiness, and a word of encouragement from the family does not fix it. It has its own screening questionnaire, it can be treated, and it can be recovered from. For what to do if you have thoughts of suicide see section 1. For the lawful way out when a baby really cannot be brought up see item 20 in section 9. This check also looks at how the wound has healed, how the uterus has recovered and how the pelvic floor is working. Contraception is discussed at this visit too: no periods after the birth does not mean you cannot get pregnant
