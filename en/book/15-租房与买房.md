# 15. Renting and buying a home

This section measures money. The places where renting loses you the most are the deposit and the agent. The places where buying a home costs the most are the loan interest, and the things the seller knows about and you do not.

### 1. The amount of the deposit, when it is returned, and the cases in which it can be reduced must go into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. It costs another ten minutes when you sign. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord
- In plain language: How much the deposit is, when it comes back, and what may be taken off it all have to be in the contract before you sign. A reason for deduction that is not in the contract cannot be used against your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is explicit: 「出租人收取押金的，应当在住房租赁合同中约定押金的数额、返还时间以及扣减押金的情形等事项。除住房租赁合同约定的情形以外，出租人无正当理由不得扣减押金。」 — where a landlord takes a deposit, the lease contract has to set out the amount, the time of return, and the cases in which the deposit may be reduced, and apart from the cases the contract provides for, the landlord may not reduce the deposit without a proper reason. Three things go into the contract: how much deposit is taken, when it is returned, and what it may be reduced for. A reason that is not in the contract cannot be used to take your money
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2025). Regulations on Housing Leasing, 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On the day you move out, take photographs and video together with the landlord. Record the readings on the water, electricity and gas meters, and photograph the walls and the floor as well. If the deposit is reduced without reason, complain first to the local housing leasing administration. If the amount is small, go through small-claims procedure: see section 8
### 2. If the water or power is cut, the locks are changed, or you are threatened at the door, call the police and keep the record: the rules ban these ways of making you move out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One telephone call to the police, which you can make on the spot
- In plain language: A landlord may not cut the water or the power, change the locks, or threaten you at the door in order to force you to end the contract or move out, and the rules ban it outright. If it happens, call the police first. The police attendance record is your evidence when you later claim for the loss. Do not confront them yourself.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is explicit: 「出租人不得采取暴力、威胁或者其他非法方式迫使承租人解除住房租赁合同或者腾退租赁住房。」 — a landlord may not use violence, threats or any other illegal means to force a tenant to terminate the lease or move out of the rented housing. Moving the tenant out is what 腾退 means. When this happens, call the police first and keep the evidence. Do not confront them yourself
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2025). Regulations on Housing Leasing, 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: You call the police so that there is an attendance record, and it serves as evidence when you later claim for the loss. Notify the local housing leasing administration at the same time
### 3. An agent may not collect and pay the rent and deposit for you: the money goes straight to the landlord
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Check the payee before you pay, which takes a few minutes. The hard part is standing firm when the agent says you should transfer the money to it
- In plain language: Under the rules, the rent and the deposit may not be collected and paid on a tenant's behalf by an agent; the money goes straight to the landlord. Every time the money passes through the agent's hands you gain another chance for it to take the money and run. If it does run, you have paid the rent for nothing and the landlord still turns you out. Before you pay, check that the payee is the name on the title certificate.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists 「代收、代付住房租金、押金」, collecting and paying housing rent and deposits for others, among the acts a real estate agency is prohibited from, so an agent may not take your rent and deposit on the landlord's behalf. Every time the money passes through the agent's hands you gain another chance for it to run off with it
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2025). Regulations on Housing Leasing, 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before you pay, check that the payee is the name on the title certificate. If it is not the owner, ask questions and get a written authority. An agent may only charge the agency fee for bringing the two sides together
### 4. Before you rent a long-term serviced apartment, check its escrow account, and do not pay a year up front to save money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One check takes a bit over ten minutes
- In plain language: Paying a year up front buys you a small discount. If the company folds, your deposit and the remaining months of rent go together. A rent-linked loan is worse: if the operator runs off, you still finish paying the loan every month. Before you sign, check whether it has published an escrow account for the rent.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation requires a housing leasing business that operates by subletting to 「设立住房租赁资金监管账户并向社会公示」, set up an escrow account for housing lease funds and publish it. Subletting means the business rents the whole flat from the landlord and then sublets it to you. The small discount you get from paying a year up front does not cover the risk of losing the deposit and the remaining rent together if the business runs off. The risk is larger if you have also signed a rent-linked loan
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2025). Regulations on Housing Leasing, 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Check at signing whether a loan is attached. If an instalment app pays the apartment a year's money in one go and you repay it monthly, you keep paying after the operator runs off. Do not sign that kind of contract
### 5. If the flat is sold during the lease, the lease stays valid and you do not have to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- In plain language: If the flat is sold during the lease, your lease is still valid. The new landlord has to let you stay until the end of the term under the original contract. You do not move, and you do not renegotiate the price. The condition is that you are living there lawfully, so keep the contract, the transfer records and the evidence of when you moved in.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called 「买卖不破租赁」, a sale does not break a lease. The words of the article are: 租赁物在租赁期限内发生所有权变动的，不影响租赁合同的效力 — a change of ownership of the leased property during the lease term does not affect the validity of the lease contract. That means that if the flat changes hands during the term, the lease you have signed is still valid. The new landlord has to let you stay until the end of the term under the original contract
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: The condition is that you have taken possession lawfully, which is what the article calls lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, the transfer records and the evidence of when you moved in
### 6. Before you sign, check the title certificate and the mortgages, and pay every sum by transfer with a note of the purpose
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One check takes half an hour
- In plain language: The two most common ways to lose everything are paying rent to somebody who is not the owner, and renting a flat that was already mortgaged to a bank and later sealed up. Before you sign, check the ownership and the mortgages once at the real estate registration centre. Pay every sum by transfer, and write in the note 「rent for a certain flat for a certain month」, because in a dispute that note is direct evidence.
- Benefit: The two most common ways to lose everything are paying rent to somebody who is not the owner, and renting a flat that was already mortgaged to a bank and later sealed up. When you transfer the money, note 「rent for a certain flat for a certain month」, because in a dispute that note is direct evidence
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature ; the same reasoning about keeping evidence and noting the purpose of a transfer is in the two items in section 8 on bride price and promissory notes
- Notes: You can look up the ownership information at the local real estate registration centre. At signing, have the landlord attend in person; if the landlord cannot come, get a written power of attorney. If a second landlord sublets to you, you need the original landlord's written consent
### 7. If an agent collects the purchase money for a second-hand flat, it must go through the agent's dedicated client settlement account at the bank
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account, which may mean arguing with the agent for a while
- In plain language: The purchase money runs from several hundred thousand to several million yuan, and it is the single largest sum in this book. Where an agent collects it, the rules require it to go through the dedicated account the agent has opened at the bank, and it may not be transferred by WeChat to an individual broker. Two agents working one deal together may also charge only one commission, while arranging a loan or handling the transfer of title needs a separate contract with the charges stated in advance.
- Benefit: The rule is explicit: where the parties to a property transaction agree that a real estate agency collects and pays the money, it should be transferred through the client transaction settlement account for deposits that the agency has opened at a bank. That means the purchase money goes into a regulated dedicated account, not the agency's own account, and not the private account of an individual broker
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (住房城乡建设部), National Development and Reform Commission and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (人力资源社会保障部) (2011). Measures for the Administration of Real Estate Brokerage, 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」 第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条 on arranging loans, registering property and other services「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: The purchase money runs from several hundred thousand to several million yuan, and it is the single largest sum in this book, so do not transfer it by WeChat to a broker. There are two more common ways of being overcharged. The first is two agencies working the same deal together, which may carry only one commission. The second is arranging a loan or handling the transfer of title, which is a different service and needs its own contract with the charges told to you in advance. See item 6 in this section for paying every sum by transfer with a note of the purpose (check the title certificate and the mortgages before you sign).
### 8. Do not rent a partitioned flat: the smallest unit for rent is a room as originally designed, and kitchens, toilets and balconies may not be lived in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. What it costs you is that on the same budget you either live further out or live in a smaller place
- In plain language: Under the rules, the smallest unit that can be rented out is a room as originally designed. Kitchens, toilets, balconies and underground storage rooms may not be lived in. If such a flat is inspected, you may have to move out. The landlord also may not raise the rent during the lease just because he says so.
- Benefit: The rule is explicit that the smallest unit for rent is a room as originally designed, and one room may not be partitioned again and let separately in several parts. The floor area per person may not be below the minimum standard set locally. Kitchens, toilets, balconies and underground storage rooms may not be let for people to live in. During the lease the landlord may not raise the rent unilaterally at will
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (住房和城乡建设部) (2010). Measures for the Administration of the Leasing of Commercial Housing, 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」 第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」 <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal partition is inspected, it is the tenant who moves, and the deposit and the rent already paid are often not recovered. It is also worse for safety: partition walls often block the escape route, and several people in one flat share one electric line. Look at three things when you view a flat: whether the walls were built later, whether the room has a window, and whether the meter can carry that many people.

### 9. If you have an urban hukou and want to live in the countryside, rent a village house and do not buy a homestead or a house on one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. What it costs you is that the house is not yours, one lease runs at most 20 years, and at the end of it you start again with the landlord
- In plain language: A person with an urban hukou may not go into the countryside to buy a homestead, a farmer's house or a 「小产权房」, and state documents forbid it outright. It does not help when a villager says 「a written contract is enough」: once the money is paid, the house still will not be yours. If you want to live in the countryside for a long time, sign a lease, at most 20 years at a time, and renew it at the end.
- Benefit: The State Council General Office document is explicit: 「城镇居民不得到农村购买宅基地、农民住宅或『小产权房』。」 — urban residents may not go to the countryside to buy homesteads, farmers' houses or 「小产权房」. The same document also provides that once a farmer has sold or rented out the house, a later application for a homestead will not be approved. A 2019 document from the Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs said the same again: 「严禁城镇居民到农村购买宅基地。」 — urban residents are strictly forbidden to buy a homestead in the countryside. The same document sets out the lawful route: urban residents may rent a village house to live in or to run a business, the term of the lease may not exceed twenty years, and the two sides may agree otherwise when it ends
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council General Office (国务院办公厅) (2007). Notice on Strictly Enforcing the Relevant Laws and Policies on Collective Construction Land in the Countryside, 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm> ; Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部) (2019). Notice on Further Strengthening the Administration of Rural Homesteads, 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: A homestead is a plot of land the village allocates to its own villagers to build on, and it can only go to villagers. When you rent a village house, sign a written contract with the farming household that rents it out, and set out the term, the rent, whether you may fit it out or rebuild, and how the money spent on the fit-out is counted up when the term ends. The documents set 20 years as the maximum term, so do not sign anything that says 「rent for 50 years」 or 「rent for 70 years」, and do not pay several years of rent in one go. The documents also forbid building villas, walled courtyards or private clubs on a homestead in the countryside, so do not pay a villager to 「build it jointly with you」.
