# 28. 别为了外形把身体搞坏

本节说的是「为了改变外形而对身体动手」的几种做法：饿肚子、吃药、打针、开刀。这几件事，决定多半是和别人比较之后做的，代价却全落在自己身上，而且相当一部分改不回来。

本节不评价任何人想把自己变成什么样子，那是各人自己的事。同一个目标，可以找「有处方、有资质、有复查」的，也可以「网购、美容院、自己加量」，本节只比这两种做法的风险差多少。算账主要看身体上实际出了什么事，比如失明、住院、死亡。医美那两条还涉及非法行医的刑事责任。

# 28. Do not wreck your body for the sake of looks

This section covers several ways of operating on the body to change how you look: starving yourself, taking pills, injections and surgery. The decision behind these things is usually made after comparing yourself with other people, but the price falls entirely on you, and a good part of it cannot be put back.

This section does not judge what anyone wants to look like; that is each person's own business. The same goal can be reached through "a prescription, a qualified practitioner and follow-up visits", or through "buying online, a beauty salon, raising the dose yourself". This section only compares how much risk the two routes carry. What is counted is what actually happens to the body, such as blindness, hospital admission and death. The two cosmetic-medicine items also touch criminal liability for practising medicine without a licence.

### 1. Do not control your weight with extreme dieting, fasting or vomiting; if you must lose weight, lose it on the exercise side
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. It also saves the money you would spend on diet products.
- In plain language: People with anorexia nervosa die at about 5.9 times the rate of others their age. Follow 1000 people for one year and 5.1 of them die. One in five of those deaths is suicide. The way in is dieting: students at 44 secondary schools were followed for three years, and girls who dieted severely were 18 times as likely to develop an eating disorder as girls who did not. If you want to control your weight, move more, and do not eat so little that you are hungry.
- Benefit: 36 studies pooled. The anorexia nervosa group was followed for 166,642 person-years in total (the years each person was followed, added up). Their standardised mortality ratio is 5.86, that is, their probability of dying is about 5.9 times that of others their age. Deaths are 5.1 per 1000 person-years, that is, follow 1000 people for one year and 5.1 of them die. About 1/5 of the people who die, die by suicide. The standardised mortality ratio for bulimia is 1.93, and for other eating disorders 1.92, each about 1.9 times that of others their age. Another study was done in 44 secondary schools in Victoria, Australia: students aged 14–15 followed for 3 years, and the same group surveyed in 6 waves over the three years. New eating disorders among girls were 21.8 per 1000 person-years, that is, follow 1000 girls for one year and 21.8 new cases appear. Girls who dieted severely had 18 times the risk of those who did not diet, and moderate dieters 5 times. The analysis then adjusted for previous dieting and psychiatric symptoms, taking out the effect of "had dieted before" and "already had psychiatric symptoms". After that adjustment, BMI (the measure of fatness worked out from weight divided by height squared), amount of exercise and sex no longer predicted new eating disorders. The authors conclude that "using exercise rather than food restriction to control weight in adolescents appears to carry a lower risk of eating disorders"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Arcelus J et al. (2011). Mortality rates in patients with anorexia nervosa and other eating disorders. A meta-analysis of 36 studies. Arch Gen Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74>；Patton GC et al. (1999). Onset of adolescent eating disorders: population based cohort study over 3 years. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.318.7186.765>
- Notes: "Severe dieting" here means keeping food far below what the body needs over a long period, not skipping one meal or cutting out late-night snacks. If you have already started vomiting, feel guilty after bingeing, or cannot stop weighing yourself, go to a psychiatry department or the clinical psychology department of a general hospital. This is an illness with standard treatment; it is not a question of willpower. For BMI and mortality see item 33 in section 2; that item is about the long-term weight range, not about ways of losing weight.

### 2. Before injections, thread embedding or surgery, check two things: whether the clinic's licence lists "medical cosmetology", and whether the person operating is the designated attending physician
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Checking the two certificates takes only minutes. The institution should hang its Medical Institution Practice Licence in the hall. The doctor's qualifications can be checked in the health authority's licensed physician search.
- In plain language: A needle, a scalpel or a medical device that breaks the skin or goes inside the body counts as medical treatment. It may only be done at an institution whose licence lists "medical cosmetology", and the person doing it must be a registered licensed physician. Having it done in a beauty salon, a studio or a hotel room is practising medicine without a licence. Where the circumstances are serious, the person doing it faces up to 3 years; if someone is hurt, 3 to 10 years; if someone dies, 10 years or more.
- Benefit: Article two of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Cosmetology Services (《医疗美容服务管理办法》) defines medical cosmetology. The original wording is 「运用手术、药物、医疗器械以及其他具有创伤性或者侵入性的医学技术方法对人的容貌和人体各部位形态进行的修复与再塑」, that is, the repair and reshaping of a person's appearance and the form of each part of the body by surgery, drugs, medical devices and other traumatic or invasive medical techniques. In plain terms, once the skin is broken, a needle goes in, or something goes inside the body, it counts as seeing a doctor. Articles eight and twenty-four require an institution to obtain a Medical Institution Practice Licence first, and to have the medical cosmetology subject approved by the registering authority, before it may offer the service. If the licence does not list medical cosmetology, it may not do it. Article eleven requires the designated attending physician (the doctor who does the work) to hold a licensed physician qualification and to be registered. Cosmetic surgery also requires more than 6 years of clinical work in the relevant specialty, cosmetic dentistry more than 5 years, and cosmetic traditional Chinese medicine and cosmetic dermatology 3 years each. Article twenty requires written notice before the procedure of the indications, contraindications (when it is suitable and when it is not), medical risks and precautions, and a signed consent. Article three hundred and thirty-six of the Criminal Law (《刑法》) governs practising medicine without a licence. Practising medicine without a licence means a person without a licensed physician qualification giving injections, operating or treating patients. In serious circumstances the penalty is fixed-term imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention or public surveillance, together with or instead of a fine. Where the patient's health is seriously damaged, 3 to 10 years of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine. Where the patient dies, 10 years or more of fixed-term imprisonment plus a fine
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Health (卫生部) (2002). Measures for the Administration of Medical Cosmetology Services（医疗美容服务管理办法）(Ministry of Health Order No. 19, articles two, eight, eleven, sixteen, twenty and twenty-four). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62198.htm>；National People's Congress (全国人大) (1997). Criminal Law（刑法）(article three hundred and thirty-six). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Article sixteen of the same Measures also provides that a cosmetic procedure may only be done in a medical cosmetology institution, or in a hospital or clinic that has a medical cosmetology department. So injections given at someone's home, or work done privately in a studio, already fail on the location alone. Article twenty states that a minor or anyone else who cannot decide for themselves may have cosmetic medicine only with the consent of a guardian, signed by a parent. Facials, massage and nail care are everyday beauty services and are not covered by these rules. But once a needle or a scalpel is involved, it counts as medical cosmetology.

### 3. For fillers, avoid the bridge of the nose, the space between the eyebrows and the forehead, and never use an unlicensed injector to save money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. You simply inject somewhere else, or not at all.
- In plain language: Among people blinded by filler injections, more than half were injected in the nose, then the space between the eyebrows, the forehead and the smile folds, and more than eight in ten were injected with hyaluronic acid. Only two in ten of those blinded recovered their sight fully, a little over one in ten recovered partly, and nearly two in ten also had brain injury. No rescue treatment works reliably, so the only defence is to avoid these areas beforehand.
- Benefit: Cases reported in journals worldwide were pooled and counted. From 2015-1 to 2018-9 there were 48 new cases of partial or complete loss of vision after a filler injection. The site with the most accidents was the nose, at 56.3%. Next came the space between the eyebrows at 27.1%, the forehead at 18.8%, and the nasolabial fold (the crease of the smile line) at 14.6%. In 81.3% of cases the filler was hyaluronic acid, the same substance. Skin changes appeared in 43.8%. Central nervous system complications (a problem in the brain) appeared in 18.8%. 10 cases (20.8%) recovered vision fully, 8 cases (16.7%) recovered partly, and the rest did not recover. The authors conclude that practice varies enormously between clinics, and no treatment has proved consistently effective
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Beleznay K et al. (2019). Update on Avoiding and Treating Blindness From Fillers: A Recent Review of the World Literature. Aesthet Surg J. <https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjz053>
- Notes: These numbers were counted case by case from cases reported in journals. They contain only the number of people who were hurt, not the total number of injections given. So you cannot work out "how likely one injection is to cause blindness"; you can only see which sites go wrong most often and how little can be done afterwards. That is also why the evidence is graded B. What a proper institution does to lower risk is routine anyway: first map the vessels under the place to be injected, use a blunt needle rather than a sharp one, inject small amounts while withdrawing, and have hyaluronidase ready beforehand. Every one of them requires the person doing it to be a trained doctor, which is the reason not to use an unofficial channel. Before you go, check it against item 2 in this section (check the clinic's licence and the attending physician).

### 4. Do not buy diet pills, diet coffee, slimming sweets or enzyme plums that promise "fast weight loss"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. It also saves the money you would spend on these products. The hard part is accepting that there is no such thing as dropping several jin in a week.
- In plain language: The substance most often found smuggled into these products is sibutramine. It was originally a diet drug and was stopped in China in 2010. Overweight people with heart disease or diabetes who took it for more than three years had about 30% more non-fatal heart attacks and about 36% more strokes. They lost only 1.7 kg more. By the time a spot check finds it, you have already swallowed it.
- Benefit: The SCOUT trial enrolled 9,804 people. All were over 55, overweight or obese, and had cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes. They were randomised into a group taking the drug and a group taking none, with an average of 3.4 years of treatment. The primary endpoint was a set of bad events agreed in advance: non-fatal heart attack, non-fatal stroke, cardiac arrest survived, and cardiovascular death. These occurred at 11.4% vs 10.0%. HR 1.16 (95% CI 1.03–1.31, P=0.02), so the people taking the drug were about sixteen per cent more likely to have one of them. The true value lies roughly between 3% higher and 31% higher. Non-fatal heart attack 4.1% vs 3.2%, HR 1.28 (1.04–1.57), about thirty per cent higher. Non-fatal stroke 2.6% vs 1.9%, HR 1.36 (1.04–1.77), about thirty-six per cent higher. Cardiovascular death and all-cause death did not increase; all-cause means every cause added together. After the randomisation, the sibutramine group lost an average of 1.7 kg more. The former State Food and Drug Administration held, on the basis of its safety assessment, that "the risks of weight-loss treatment outweigh the benefits", and in month 10 of 2010 ordered the production, sale and use of sibutramine preparations and the raw drug to stop. Both sibutramine and phenolphthalein are banned from food, health food included
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: James WPT et al. (2010). Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003114>；Tianjin Municipal Commission for Market Regulation (天津市市场监督管理委员会). Beware of "sibutramine hydrochloride" and "phenolphthalein" illegally added to some foods（警惕部分食品中违法添加「盐酸西布曲明」和「酚酞」）. <https://scjg.tj.gov.cn/tjsscjdglwyh_52651/hdpt/cjwtyxfts/202007/t20200720_2972549.html>
- Notes: SCOUT enrolled people who already had heart disease or diabetes, a high-risk group. The trial did not show that healthy young people would have the same events. But because nobody can tell who is high-risk, the regulator simply took it off the market. Another substance often smuggled in is phenolphthalein; it was originally a laxative, and taking too much of it over a long period upsets the body's electrolytes and can bring on an arrhythmia. How to tell: a proper diet drug is a prescription drug, and its approval number can be looked up at the pharmacy and the hospital. Anything sold under the flag of "coffee", "sweets", "plums" or "meal replacement" that promises to drop several jin in a week should by definition have no drug effect; if it has one, something has been added. The original text of 国食药监办〔2010〕432 号 is on the National Medical Products Administration website; opening it on this machine has always returned error 412, so a provincial regulator's page repeating it is cited instead. The two sentences "the risks outweigh the benefits" and "production, sale and use stopped in October 2010" can be checked word for word there.

### 5. Do not use anabolic steroids ("muscle-building injections", "oral steroids") to build muscle
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money, and it saves the money for the drugs. The hard part is accepting that muscle grows slowly.
- In plain language: Men who test positive for steroids in gym doping tests die at 3 times the rate of others their age, and go to hospital more than twice as often each year. Acne, gynecomastia and erectile dysfunction each hit at least one in ten users. In China these are prescription drugs, so any channel able to sell them to you is itself breaking the law.
- Benefit: Denmark went back through its records and gave every user a same-age control. The users were 545 men who tested positive for androgenic steroids in gym doping tests between 2006-01-03 and 2018-03-01, matched with 5,450 controls. A second run checked them again against 644 people who refused to be tested and 6,440 controls. The results: users' mortality was 3 times that of the controls, HR 3.0 (95% CI 1.3–7.0), with the true multiple lying roughly between 1.3 and 7. The median number of hospital admissions or visits a year was 0.81 vs 0.36 (P<0.0001). The median means half the people are above that number and half below it. That P value means the difference is almost impossible to be chance. Acne (spots), gynecomastia and erectile dysfunction each affected more than 10% of users, that is at least one in ten users, and all were clearly higher than in the controls (P<0.0001). The second batch of records gave the same result. Article seven of the Regulations on Anti-Doping (《反兴奋剂条例》): the state applies strict control to the banned substances listed on the doping list, and no entity or individual may illegally produce, sell, import or export them. The same regulations put anabolic agents and peptide hormones under prescription-only control, that is, they may be bought only against a doctor's prescription; anabolic agents is what the regulations call this class of drug
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Horwitz H et al. (2019). Health consequences of androgenic anabolic steroid use. J Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12850>；State Council (国务院) (2004). Regulations on Anti-Doping（反兴奋剂条例）(State Council Order No. 398, articles seven and nine). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63129.htm>
- Notes:Disputed: This is a study that followed a group of people and looked at what happened to them, not a randomised trial. Users may take more risks in other ways as well, so the figure of 3 times cannot all be laid at the drug's door. The true multiple may be as low as 1.3 or as high as 7.0, a very wide range. But "acne, gynecomastia and erectile dysfunction each hit more than a tenth of users" are side effects you can see with your eyes, and they are much less affected by this bias. In regulations these drugs are written as "anabolic agents"; those four Chinese characters mean this drug. Ordinary protein powder and creatine are not covered by this item. The trouble is that products claiming to build muscle very fast may smuggle ingredients in. The way to tell is the same as for the diet products (item 4, do not buy diet pills that promise fast weight loss): a proper drug is a prescription drug with an approval number you can check. Anything sold as "mass gainer" or "nutrition product" that promises results in a few weeks, if it really works, has had something added.

### 6. If you are going to take diet pills, get a prescription at the hospital; do not buy from an online shop that ships without one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One registration, plus the trip there and back. A few dozen yuan, half a day.
- In plain language: A prescription drug must be sold against a prescription, and that applies online too: there must be a real prescription and real-name registration. A shop that does not ask, wants no prescription and ships the moment you order is either not selling what it claims to sell, or is breaking the rules. When something goes wrong, nobody is answerable for your dose or your contraindications. A registration costs a few dozen yuan, and a doctor then takes that gate for you.
- Benefit: The Measures for the Administration of the Quality Supervision of Drug Distribution and Use (《药品经营和使用质量监督管理办法》) require retail drug businesses to follow the classified management system for prescription and non-prescription drugs. That system divides medicines into two kinds: one may only be bought against a doctor's prescription, the other may be bought on your own. A pharmacy must sell prescription drugs against a prescription as required, and keep the prescription for no less than five years. When the pharmacist or other pharmaceutical technician is off duty, a notice must be displayed, and prescription drugs may not be sold without their review. Prescription drugs may not be sold on open shelves, that is, left on a shelf for customers to take themselves. Article nine of the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales (《药品网络销售监督管理办法》) provides that prescription drugs sold to individuals over the network must come from a genuine and reliable prescription, and real-name registration must be used. Article ten requires risk warning information to be shown prominently under each drug's display page. Before the prescription has passed review, the instructions may not be shown and no purchase service may be offered; until the prescription has been reviewed the page should not even offer a buy button. Article eight provides that drugs under special state control may not be sold over the network. Such drugs include vaccines, blood products, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, toxic drugs for medical use, radioactive drugs, and drug-type precursor chemicals
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2023). Measures for the Administration of the Quality Supervision of Drug Distribution and Use（药品经营和使用质量监督管理办法）(SAMR Order No. 84). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10846/202311/content_6917322.html>；State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2022). Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales（药品网络销售监督管理办法）(SAMR Order No. 58, articles eight to ten). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Notes: The law can be checked word for word, but nobody has run a quantitative study on how much fewer goes wrong when you take a medicine as prescribed than when you buy it and take it yourself, so the grade is B for "hard to quantify". The steroids and the sex hormones covered in items 5 and 7 in this section follow the same rules. When it really is time to rely on medicine to lose weight, the doctor first looks at your body mass index, whether you have other illnesses, and whether there is any reason you cannot take the drug, then decides whether to prescribe and which one. That judgement cannot be bought.

### 7. Use sex-hormone drugs only when a doctor prescribes them and you have regular check-ups; do not buy online, do not raise the dose yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Regular registrations, plus blood tests for the check-ups. A few hundred yuan a year, taking up several half-days.
- In plain language: This item does not discuss whether you should use them, only how. Transgender women who use oestrogen get blood clots in their vessels noticeably more often than people their age: about 4 extra per 1000 people over two years, and about 17 extra over eight years. Risks like these have to be watched by regular check-ups; with drugs bought online and doses adjusted by yourself, nobody runs those checks for you.
- Benefit: The researchers followed a group of people taken from the electronic medical records of the Kaiser Permanente health group: 2,842 transgender women and 2,118 transgender men. Average follow-up was 4.0 years and 3.6 years respectively. Each person was matched with about 10 times as many cisgender controls on year of birth, race, clinic site and time of entry, giving 48,686 cisgender men and 48,775 cisgender women in all. Venous thromboembolism (a clot forming in a vessel and blocking it) was more common in transgender women than in the controls. Compared with cisgender men, at 2 years it was 4.1 per 1000 people (95% CI 1.6–6.7). At 8 years it was 16.7 per 1000 (6.4–27.5). Worked out, that is 4.1 extra cases per 1000 people over two years and 16.7 extra over eight years. Compared with cisgender women, the two figures were 3.4 (1.1–5.6) and 13.7 (4.1–22.7). Ischaemic stroke (the kind of stroke caused by a blocked vessel) and heart attack were close across the groups overall. But among transgender women who only started hormone therapy during the follow-up period, the differences in venous thrombosis and ischaemic stroke were more marked. The evidence on transgender men is not enough to draw a conclusion. The authors conclude that "these results may suggest a need for long-term vigilance about the vascular side effects of oestrogen in transgender hormone therapy"
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Getahun D et al. (2018). Cross-sex Hormones and Acute Cardiovascular Events in Transgender Persons: A Cohort Study. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2785>；State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2022). Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales（药品网络销售监督管理办法）(SAMR Order No. 58, article nine). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Notes: The researchers set out the weaknesses of the study themselves: how many people bought their drugs outside the hospital system they cannot see, so the line of "was the drug taken or not" was never clean. That is all the more reason to put the use of the drug inside regular follow-up. This study only looks at whether the vessels had an accident; it does not answer whether hormone therapy should be used or what it buys you, and that is another body of evidence. Every place where sex hormones are used is the same, including the pill, hormone replacement around menopause, and testosterone replacement. Anyone who buys online, doses from an internet post, and never has clotting, liver and kidney markers checked, runs the risk in the stretch where nobody is watching, and that has nothing to do with why the drug was taken. The proper route in China is the endocrinology department, or the relevant specialty, of a proper medical institution: assessment, prescription, follow-up.

### 8. If "thinking you are ugly" has got to the point of checking the mirror repeatedly and wanting to change repeatedly, get a body-image assessment before talking about surgery
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One registration at a psychiatry department or a clinical psychology department, a few dozen yuan. The hard part is making that trip before you book the operation.
- In plain language: About 15% of people going for plastic surgery have body dysmorphic disorder, which means magnifying a flaw others can barely see until it affects your life. About 13% of people who go to dermatology wanting to look better have it, and in both groups more than seven in ten are women. Someone like this finishes one area and then fixes on the next; surgery does not solve it. Get one registration first to see whether this is you, then decide about the operation.
- Benefit: Following the rule, 33 studies were gathered and then pooled. Among plastic surgery patients, the prevalence of body dysmorphic disorder (magnifying a flaw others can barely see until it affects your life) was 15.04%, with individual studies ranging from 2.21% to 56.67%. In headcount, about 15 in every 100 people who go for surgery fit the description, while individual studies range from 2 to 57. This group had an average age of 34.54±12.41 years, the second figure being the margin either side, and 74.38% were women. Dermatology patients were 12.65%, with individual studies from 4.52% to 35.16%, an average age of 27.79±9.03 years, and 76.09% women. The authors suggest that plastic surgeons and dermatologists assess proactively, identify the high-risk people, and arrange for several departments to manage them together
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Ribeiro RVE (2017). Prevalence of Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Plastic Surgery and Dermatology Patients: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Aesthetic Plast Surg. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00266-017-0869-0>
- Notes: The proportions measured by the individual studies run from 2.21% all the way to 56.67%, which is an enormous spread. The reason is that the questionnaires and the populations surveyed were all different. So all you can read off it is "this proportion is not low, and it is worth screening once first"; you cannot take 15.04% as an exact figure, and that is also why it is graded B. The great majority of people who want cosmetic work have no psychological problem. This item is aimed at the small group for whom it is "repeated, persistent, and already affecting work and social life"; surgery does not solve their problem. The signs to judge by are these: spending a long part of the day checking or covering one area, and so avoiding social contact or photographs, and finishing one area only to fix on the next.
