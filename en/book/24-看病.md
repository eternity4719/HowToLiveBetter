# 24. Medical care: spending less and going fewer rounds

This section measures money and time. It covers how medical care itself works: which hospital to go to first, how to be referred upwards, and where the money can be saved. It also covers how emergency care is ordered, and which assessments and certificates you need after serious injuries are treated. For what you can claim, see section 7. For managing a chronic illness over the long term, see section 16. For what to do first at the scene of an emergency, see section 13.

This section rests mainly on the Measures on Accelerating the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System (《关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施》), issued in April 2026 by the General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅). Tiered diagnosis and treatment means deciding which level of hospital to go to first according to how serious the illness is. That document ties going to the grassroots level directly to saving money. Local implementation is at different stages, and the text gives the national rules. For your own pooling region, follow what the local medical insurance and health authorities say. The pooling region is where your medical insurance is enrolled, usually one city.

### 1. For an ordinary illness, start at the community clinic and go up by referral step by step from the grassroots level, and the inpatient deductible carries on
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You have to make one trip to the community clinic first. The hard part is holding back the urge to go straight for a specialist's appointment.
- In plain language: Go to the community clinic first, and let the community clinic refer you upwards to the big hospital for the admission. The part of the bill you pay yourself counts once only. If you go straight to a tier-three hospital for the admission and are then referred back to the community clinic for rehabilitation, you may pay that part twice. Where you are referred back from the big hospital to the community clinic and stay there, it is not counted a second time for the same illness within that period.
- Benefit: For insured patients referred up step by step through primary care, the inpatient deductible at the higher hospital can be counted continuously. The deductible is the part of each admission that you pay yourself, up to the point at which medical insurance starts paying. Counting it continuously means the two hospitals together count it once, and you do not have to pay it again from scratch. An inpatient referred back down from the higher hospital to the grassroots level is not given a separate deductible for the same period of illness
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅) (2026). Measures on Accelerating the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System (《关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施》). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: 「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」 — for insured patients in the pooling region referred up step by step through primary care, the inpatient deductible at the higher hospital may be counted continuously; an inpatient referred back down to the primary care institution is not given a separate inpatient deductible within the same period of illness.
- Notes: If you go straight to a tier-three hospital for the admission and then go back to the grassroots level for rehabilitation, you may pay that part twice, whereas going by referral counts it once. Referral rules are set separately by each province, each city and each medical consortium, so ask the community clinic before you go. Do not go to the big hospital any more for prescriptions in the stable phase of a chronic illness: this kind of ordinary outpatient service is being cut back at the big hospitals, and the community clinic can prescribe 12 weeks of medicine at a time; see item 5 in section 16 (the community clinic prescribes 12 weeks of medicine at a time).

### 2. In the same pooling region (where your medical insurance is enrolled), the lower the level of the hospital the higher the share reimbursed, with a gap of about 10 percentage points
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is holding back the urge to run to the big hospital.
- In plain language: On the same bill for an admission, the lower the level of the hospital, the more is reimbursed. Each place has to spread the levels apart, with about ten percentage points between each step up. Take a hospital bill of 10,000 yuan: if the reimbursed share falls from 80% to 70%, what you pay yourself rises from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. Newly added medical insurance funds also tilt towards primary care.
- Benefit: The document requires each place to spread apart the inpatient reimbursement level of medical institutions of different grades, in principle by about 10 percentage points per step. On that gap, going up one hospital level for the same bill adds about 10% of the reimbursable amount to what you pay yourself. Take a reimbursable bill of 10,000 yuan: the reimbursed share falls from 80% to 70%, so what you pay yourself rises from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. The extra 1000 yuan is a tenth of the total, but half more than what you paid before. The actual ratios differ from place to place, and the deductible and the ceiling also change the difference; the figure here is only an illustration based on the 10 percentage points in the document. Newly added annual medical insurance funds also tilt towards primary care
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅) (2026). Measures on Accelerating the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System (《关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施》). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: 「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」 — in line with local conditions, appropriately widen the gap in inpatient reimbursement between medical institutions of different grades, in principle about 10 percentage points per step within the pooling region; where conditions allow, widen the gap in outpatient reimbursement between grades at the direction of tiered diagnosis and treatment.
- Notes: On the same bill for an admission, the difference between paying at the grassroots level and paying at a tier-three hospital can be several thousand yuan. Where the illness can be dealt with at the grassroots level, do not run upwards. Not every illness has to be seen at the community clinic.

### 3. If you need a large hospital, go through a grassroots referral or the hospital's referral centre, and do not buy appointments from scalpers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. You have one more referral procedure to get through.
- In plain language: Large hospitals must set aside a share of their appointments and beds for people referred up from the grassroots level. Hospitals must also set up a referral centre, or name one fixed department to run referrals, with full coverage by 2027. Failing to get an appointment does not mean there is no route in. A scalper's appointment is not only expensive; it may also land you in a department that does not match your illness.
- Benefit: The lead hospital of a tightly integrated medical consortium must reserve a set share of appointment slots and beds for the grassroots level and see referred patients promptly. A tightly integrated medical consortium is a group of hospitals and community health institutions tied together to work as one. So a referral up from the community has its own appointments and beds, instead of queueing with the people fighting for appointments in the lobby. Healthcare institutions must set up a referral centre or name a fixed department, with full coverage achieved by 2027
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅) (2026). Measures on Accelerating the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System (《关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施》). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: 「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」, that is, the lead hospital must reserve a set share of appointment slots and beds for primary healthcare providers and see patients referred up from them promptly. 「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」, that is, healthcare institutions must strengthen unified management of referral services, set up a referral centre or name a fixed department to take on patient referral work, reaching full coverage by 2027.
- Notes: The reserved appointments are for referred patients only, so if you cannot get one, go through a referral. A scalper is not only expensive; you may also end up in a department that does not match. If you cannot find the referral centre, ring the hospital's service line and ask.
### 4. Before going out of province for treatment, ask at home first; in principle the need is assessed by a deputy chief physician or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One more question before you set off.
- In plain language: Whether you should go elsewhere for treatment is, in principle, decided by a deputy chief physician or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital. How much you get back for temporary treatment away from your enrolled area differs from a hospital of the same level inside it, and the local rules set the figure. Asking once at home before you leave beats arriving somewhere else and finding out nothing can be claimed back.
- Benefit: The need for treatment across pooling regions and across provinces is, in principle, assessed by staff at deputy chief physician level or above at secondary and tertiary hospitals. Going across pooling regions means leaving the area where your medical insurance is enrolled. Whether you travel for treatment is decided by a doctor of sufficient rank. The reimbursement level for people going out of province temporarily for treatment keeps a reasonable difference from the level at the same grade of provider inside the enrolled area. The same illness treated elsewhere gets less money back than it does at home
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅) (2026). Measures on Accelerating the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System (《关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施》). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: 「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」, that is, in principle the need for a patient to receive treatment across pooling regions and across provinces is assessed by staff at deputy chief physician level or above at secondary and tertiary hospitals. 「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」, that is, further strengthen direct settlement of treatment away from the enrolled area, keeping the reimbursement level for people going out of province temporarily for treatment at a reasonable difference from the level at providers of the same grade inside the enrolled area.
- Notes: Temporary treatment away from home and long-term residence away from home are two different registrations, and they reimburse at different levels. For how to register and which illnesses can be settled directly, see section 16. Do not travel first and think about reimbursement afterwards.
### 5. After every visit, keep your own copy of the records, the test reports and the images
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. A hospital may charge a copying fee under the rules, and the rate has to be published. The hard part is remembering to go and copy them after every visit.
- In plain language: Reading and copying your own medical records is a right the law gives you. Outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab sheets, images, operation and anaesthesia records, pathology material and itemised bills can all be copied. The hospital runs a dedicated window for the requests and may charge only a copying fee. Changing hospital, seeing another doctor, claiming on private insurance or fighting a dispute all rest on this pile of paper.
- Benefit: The regulation makes reading and copying medical records an explicit patient right. The scope covers outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab sheets, medical images, operation and anaesthesia records, pathology material, medical costs and all the other material. Changing hospital, seeing a second doctor, claiming on private insurance or fighting a dispute all rest on these
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2018). Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (《医疗纠纷预防和处理条例》) (State Council Order No. 701), Article sixteen: 「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」, that is, patients have the right to read and copy their outpatient records, inpatient notes, temperature charts, medical orders, lab sheets (test reports), medical imaging materials, consent forms for special examinations, consent forms for surgery, operation and anaesthesia records, pathology material, nursing records, medical costs and all other material belonging to the records as required by the health authority of the State Council. 「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」, that is, where a patient asks for the records to be copied, the healthcare institution must provide the copying service and stamp a certifying mark on the copy; it may charge a copying fee, and the rate must be published. 「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」, that is, where the patient has died, close relatives may read and copy the records under these Regulations. Article fifteen: 「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」, that is, no unit and no individual may alter, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical records. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission and National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine (国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局) (2013). Administrative Provisions on Medical Records in Healthcare Institutions (2013 edition) (《医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）》): healthcare institutions 「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」, that is, must designate a department, or a member of staff full-time or part-time, to take the applications to copy records, and the copy is made with the applicant present and stamped with a certifying mark. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Notes: There is a dedicated window for copying, so you do not have to plead with a doctor. The list of what you can ask for is in Article sixteen of these Regulations, item by item. First the outpatient records, inpatient notes, temperature charts, medical orders, lab sheets and imaging. Then the operation and anaesthesia records, pathology material, nursing records and the itemised bill. Put what you get into one folder in date order. The most important items inside are the discharge summary, the operation record, the pathology report and the key images. Keep the readings for a chronic illness in a single notebook; see section 16.
### 6. If you doubt the diagnosis or treatment, ask on the spot for sealing the records, with both sides present, a list drawn up and one copy each
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask the hospital to seal the records and wait for both sides to turn up and complete the formalities. The hard part is saying that sentence in front of a doctor.
- In plain language: If you have doubts about the treatment, you can ask for the records to be sealed. Sealing requires both the patient side and the doctor side to be present, and a list to be drawn up, signed and stamped, with one copy each. Where the records are not finished, seal the part already completed. If you suspect an infusion, a blood transfusion, an injection or a drug caused the harm, the bag of fluid, the vial and the giving set have to be sealed as well.
- Benefit: The regulations provide that "where records must be sealed or unsealed because a medical dispute has arisen, this must be done with both the patient side and the doctor side present". What is sealed may be the original or a copy, and the healthcare institution keeps it. Where the records are not complete and sealing is required, the completed part is sealed first, and the rest is sealed once it is finished. The healthcare institution must draw up a list of the sealed records, signed or stamped by both sides, with one copy each. Where an infusion, a blood transfusion, an injection or a drug is suspected of having caused harm, both sides must jointly seal and unseal the physical items at the scene. Where testing is needed, the two sides must jointly appoint an institution with testing qualifications. Where they cannot agree on a joint appointment, the health authority of the county-level people's government where the institution is located designates one. Where a transfusion is suspected of having caused harm and the blood has to be sealed, the institution must notify the blood station to send someone to attend. There is also a hard limit: "no unit and no individual may alter, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical records". Once the dispute is resolved after sealing, or 3 years pass with no further request to resolve it, the institution may unseal the records itself (nationwide, in force from 2018-10-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2018). Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (《医疗纠纷预防和处理条例》) (State Council Order No. 701, Articles fifteen, twenty-four and twenty-five). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: Sealing and copying are two different things. Copying is what you should do as a matter of course; see item 5 in this section (keeping your own copy of the records and images). Sealing fixes the evidence when a dispute arises, and it guards against entries written or changed afterwards. If you suspect an infusion, a blood transfusion or a drug caused the harm, seal the bag of fluid, the vial and the giving set along with the records, and do not let anyone carry them off and bin them. Healthcare institutions must publish in a prominent place the routes, the procedures and the contact details for resolving medical disputes, and must set up a single complaints department. When something happens, go to that department rather than arguing with the doctor on duty.

### 7. For serious injuries go straight to the emergency triage desk, not to the registration queue
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. A few more steps inside the door to find the triage desk. Say the most dangerous symptom in one sentence.
- In plain language: Emergency care is not ordered by who arrived first. Patients are sorted into four levels by how serious they are, and the sorting happens at the triage desk at the emergency entrance, not at the registration window. Skip the triage and you have no level in the system, however badly you are hurt, so you move along the queue at the speed of the queue. On arrival, report the most dangerous thing first: heavy bleeding, breathlessness, loss of consciousness, something embedded in the body, crushing pain in the chest.
- Benefit: The 2024 edition of the emergency medicine quality control indicators from the National Health Commission requires emergency triage to 「按病情危重程度将患者分为四级」, that is, sort patients into four levels by how critical they are. 「I 级为急危患者，需要立即得到救治」, that is, level I is for critical patients who need treatment immediately. 「II 级为急重患者，往往评估与救治同时进行」, that is, level II is for seriously ill patients, where assessment and treatment often go together. 「III 级为急症患者，需要短时间内得到诊治」, that is, level III is for acute patients who need diagnosis and treatment within a short time. 「IV 级为非急症患者或亚急症患者，在普通诊疗区顺序就诊」, that is, level IV is for non-acute or sub-acute patients, seen in order in the general treatment area. Triage means a member of staff assesses how serious you are once you come into the emergency department, and sets the order of treatment. Beijing's implementing standard sets out the response time for each level. Level 1: 「立即进行评估和救治，安排患者进入复苏室或抢救室」, that is, assess and treat at once, and move the patient into the resuscitation or resuscitation room. Level 2: 「立即监护生命体征，10 分钟内得到救治，安排患者进入抢救室」, that is, start monitoring vital signs at once, treat within 10 minutes, and move the patient into the resuscitation room. Level 3: 「先于 4 级患者优先诊治，安排患者在普通诊疗区候诊；若候诊时间大于 30 分钟，需再次评估」, that is, see the patient in preference to those at level 4, and have them wait in the general treatment area; if the wait exceeds 30 minutes, assess again. Level 4: 「顺序就诊，除非病情变化，否则候诊时间较长；若候诊时间大于 4 小时，可再次评估」, that is, seen in order, with a longer wait unless the condition changes; if the wait exceeds 4 hours, assess again. Triage has to 「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则」, that is, keep to the principle that critical cases are seen first, and a triage area must be set up in a prominent position at the entrance to the emergency department (the four levels are the national standard, the response times are Beijing's)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2024). Notice on Issuing the Medical Quality Control Indicators for Six Specialties including Emergency Medicine (2024 edition) (《关于印发急诊医学等6个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024年版）的通知》(国卫办医政函〔2024〕150号)), Annex 1, indicator four, 「急诊分级分诊执行率」, that is, the rate at which emergency graded triage is carried out. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>; Beijing Municipal Health Commission (北京市卫生健康委员会) (2019). Beijing Plan for Strengthening Emergency Pre-triage and Grading, and its Annex, the Beijing Standard for Emergency Pre-triage and Grading in Hospitals (Trial) (《北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案》and 《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》): 「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」, that is, keep to the principle that critical cases are seen first; according to how serious the patient's condition is, follow the order from severe to mild and from rapidly changing to relatively stable, and arrange the order of treatment accordingly. 「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」, that is, set up an emergency triage area in a prominent position at the entrance to the emergency department. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Notes: The four levels are the same nationwide. How long each level waits before being seen is set by each province and city, and the Beijing text is a version you can check word for word. The level turns on readings such as pulse, blood pressure and blood oxygen, and on the symptoms, not on how much pain you feel. Level 1 has hard criteria, and these are some of them. A heart rate above 180 per minute, or below 40 per minute. A systolic blood pressure below 70 mmHg, which is the upper number in a blood pressure reading. Blood oxygen below 80%. Shock, a confirmed heart attack, or an acute impairment of consciousness. If you think the triage has put you too low, add the most dangerous symptom and say it again. If your condition changes while you wait, go straight back to the triage desk to be assessed again. When things are genuinely critical, ringing 120 is faster than going yourself: the paramedic assesses you against the same standard first and tells the hospital to prepare (see section 13).

### 8. No money, no papers, and nobody who can say who you are: emergency care still has to come first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: The state has an emergency medical assistance fund that pays emergency treatment charges for two kinds of people: those whose identity cannot be established, and those who cannot pay. A hospital may not refuse or delay resuscitation, and an ambulance service may not decline to send an ambulance over money. So do not sit at home and endure it because you have no money, and do not be afraid to call an ambulance and owe the bill. This fund covers emergency treatment only. An admission afterwards still goes through medical insurance and medical assistance.
- Benefit: The emergency medical assistance scheme covers 「在中国境内发生急重危伤病、需要急救但身份不明确或无力支付相应费用的患者」, that is, patients who suffer a serious or critical injury or illness in China, need emergency treatment, and either cannot be identified or cannot pay. The fund pays two kinds of charge: 「无法查明身份患者所发生的急救费用」, that is, the emergency treatment charges of patients whose identity cannot be established, and 「身份明确但无力缴费的患者所拖欠的急救费用」, that is, the emergency treatment charges owed by patients whose identity is known but who cannot pay. The document also states that 「各级各类医疗机构及其工作人员必须及时、有效地对急重危伤患者施救，不得以任何理由拒绝、推诿或拖延救治」, that is, healthcare institutions at every level and their staff must treat patients with serious or critical injuries promptly and effectively, and may not refuse, shirk or delay treatment on any grounds. Pre-hospital emergency care has its own rules: the national pre-hospital emergency number is 「120」. An emergency centre (station) 「不得因指挥调度原因拒绝、推诿或者延误院前医疗急救服务」, that is, may not refuse, shirk or delay pre-hospital emergency care on grounds of dispatch or scheduling, and also 「不得因费用问题拒绝或者延误院前医疗急救服务」, that is, may not refuse or delay pre-hospital emergency care over money. Transfer follows 「就近、就急、满足专业需要、兼顾患者意愿的原则」, that is, the principles of going to the nearest place, going to the most urgent place, meeting professional need and taking the patient's wishes into account (nationwide, since 2013)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅) (2013). Guiding Opinions on Establishing the Emergency Medical Assistance Scheme (《关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见》(国办发〔2013〕15号)). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission (国家卫生和计划生育委员会) (2014). Measures for the Administration of Pre-hospital Medical Emergency Care (《院前医疗急救管理办法》(委令第3号), Articles thirteen, twenty-two, twenty-three and twenty-five). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: The fund pays only the emergency treatment part, and it is the hospital that applies to the fund afterwards, so there is nothing for you to collect at a window. If you have medical insurance, use it first as usual. The application process and the ceiling differ by province. In the transfer principles, the nearest place and the most urgent place come before the patient's wishes, so the ambulance does not necessarily take you to the hospital you named. When things are critical, the nearest place that can treat you is what counts. Where what you pay yourself afterwards is still too heavy, use medical assistance; see item 10 in section 7 (medical assistance).
### 9. Get the disability assessment after treatment has finished, because doing it early gets you a lower grade
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The assessment fee runs from a few hundred yuan to two thousand yuan, and the party that applies usually pays it up front. You have to wait until treatment has finished or the clinical outcome has settled, usually three to six months after the injury and sometimes longer. The hard part is resisting the urge to settle it while the injury is still healing.
- In plain language: How much you get paid depends mainly on the grade, and the grade depends on how much functional impairment is left once treatment has finished. The standard says the assessment must wait until treatment has finished or the clinical outcome has settled. Go for an assessment while the bone has not healed or the plate is still in place and you are assessed as you are that day, which means lowering your own grade. An ordinary fracture that has healed without affecting function does not reach any grade.
- Benefit: The Standard on the Grading of Disability from Human Body Injury states that the timing of the assessment "should be after treatment of the primary injury and of complications clearly related to it has finished, or after the clinical treatment outcome has settled". The standard "divides the degree of disability from human body injury into 10 grades, from grade one (a disability rate of 100%) to grade ten (a disability rate of 10%), with 10% between each grade". It turns on "structural damage to tissues and organs, functional impairment, and the degree of dependence on medical and nursing care, with due consideration of the effect of the disability on social interaction and psychological factors". The fracture-related clauses among the ten grades include these. First: "compression fracture of one vertebral body (compression of 1/3 or more) or a comminuted fracture". Second: "more than two fractures of the pelvis or a comminuted fracture, with malunion". Third: "loss of more than 25% of the function of any major joint of the limbs, excluding the ankle". Disability at the same site and of the same kind may not be counted twice. Where there is more than one disability, the grades must be stated separately (nationwide, in force from 2017-1-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of State Security and Ministry of Justice (最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部) (2016). Announcement on the Issue of the Standard on the Grading of Disability from Human Body Injury (《关于发布〈人体损伤致残程度分级〉的公告》, published 2016-4-18, in force 2017-1-1), clauses 4.2, 4.4, 4.5, 5.10.6 and 6.2 of the standard. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Notes: There are three different disability assessments, and they do not substitute for one another. Assault and other personal injury, and traffic accidents, go through the judicial Standard on the Grading of Disability from Human Body Injury, which is used to work out the disability compensation (see section 9). A work injury goes through the assessment of capacity for work, which uses a different set of ten grades and governs the benefits for a work injury (see section 19). To claim the disability benefits available to disabled people you need a disability certificate as well (see item 10, disability certificate). Have the records, the operation record and the follow-up images ready before the assessment (see item 5, keeping your own copy of the records and images); missing images are the commonest reason a grade comes out low. Where this injury is mixed up with a long-standing condition, the assessment opinion also has to say how much of the resulting disability this injury accounts for. That line has a direct effect on how much you are paid.

### 10. If functional impairment really is left after treatment, apply for a disability certificate at the county-level disabled persons' federation where your household registration is
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The certificate itself carries no handling fee. The assessment fee and the photo fee are in principle yours to pay, and you can apply for a reduction if you are in difficulty. Take your identity card, your household register and 3 photos two inches in size on a white background to the county-level disabled persons' federation where your household registration is. Once the assessment passes, the result is posted in the village or community for 5 working days, and the federation finishes its review within 10 working days. The hard part is making those trips and waiting out that period.
- In plain language: A disability certificate is the threshold for claiming disabled persons' benefits. It is not a certificate of injury. There are seven categories: vision, hearing, speech, limbs, intellectual, mental and multiple disabilities, each graded from one to four. The process is application, acceptance of the application, assessment at a designated institution, posting of the result, review and approval, and issue of the certificate. It is valid for ten years and is replaced free of charge when it expires. If you do not qualify, you do not qualify. This is not something an acquaintance can fix for you.
- Benefit: The disability certificate "is the legal proof for determining that a person is disabled and of their category and grade of disability, and is the important basis on which disabled people lawfully enjoy the preferential policies of the state and of local governments". The assessment standard is the national standard 《残疾人残疾分类和分级》 (GB/T 26341-2010). The certificate rests on two rules: applying is voluntary, and it is administered where the applicant lives. The seven categories are vision, hearing, speech, limbs, intellectual, mental and multiple disabilities, and the disability grade runs from one to four. The county-level disabled persons' federation takes the application and issues the certificate, on the basis of the assessment conclusion made by a hospital or professional body designated by the province. Where the conclusion meets the standard, it is posted in the village or community for five working days, and the county-level federation "finishes its review within ten working days". "The issue of a disability certificate carries no handling fee". The assessment and photo fees "are in principle to be borne by the applicant personally; where local conditions allow they may be subsidised by the local finance, and for applicants in special difficulty the relevant departments shall be coordinated to reduce or waive them". The certificate is valid for ten years and is replaced free of charge when it expires. Where the category or the grade changes, you may apply for a fresh assessment. Where you dispute the conclusion, you may apply to the prefecture-level or city-level disabled persons' federation for a fresh assessment within ten working days. If you are still dissatisfied, the provincial expert committee on disability assessment makes the final decision (nationwide, in force from 2018-1-1)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: China Disabled Persons' Federation and National Health and Family Planning Commission (中国残疾人联合会、国家卫生和计划生育委员会) (2017). Measures for the Administration of the Disability Certificate of the People's Republic of China (《中华人民共和国残疾人证管理办法》, published 2017-5-27, in force 2018-1-1; Articles two, three, five, seven, nine, seventeen, nineteen, twenty-two and twenty-five). <https://www.bdpf.org.cn/cms68/web1459/subject/n1/n1459/n1508/n1509/n1512/c68465/content.html>
- Notes: Work out the money before you decide whether to apply. The certificate itself pays nothing out. What it connects you to is the two allowances for disabled people: a living allowance for disabled people in means-tested income support households, and a care allowance for those at grade one or two who need long-term care; see item 8 in section 7 (the two allowances). The certificate also connects you to the local concessions on rehabilitation, assistive devices, taxes, employment and transport. These standards differ a great deal between provinces, so look them up on the local federation's website before you decide whether the trip is worth making. It is one of three different things, along with the disability grade from a judicial assessment and the assessment of capacity for work after a work injury, and none of them substitutes for another (see item 9, get the disability assessment after treatment has finished). The certificate is for the holder only and may not be lent to anyone else. A disability that no longer meets the standard has its certificate cancelled, and no fresh application is allowed within one year of the cancellation. Where someone has multiple disabilities, the grade is set by the most severe category among them.

### 11. Thanking the doctor who saved your life: go by thank-you letter, banner and satisfaction rating, and not by red envelope, because the code bans property, not thanks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Writing a thank-you letter, or filling in one rating on the satisfaction platform, takes a little over ten minutes.
- In plain language: Paying money in advance buys no priority. Appointment slots, beds, drugs in short supply and arrangements for surgery are all forbidden as something to exchange for a favour. Afterwards, if you want to thank the doctor who saved your life, you may do so, but do not send tobacco, alcohol or a red envelope: if he accepts, that is a breach of the rules. There are three forms of thanks that really land on him: fill in the satisfaction rating carefully, write a thank-you letter by name or send a banner, and go back for the follow-up the doctor asked for.
- Benefit: The Nine Codes for Clean Practice by Staff of Medical Institutions (《医疗机构工作人员廉洁从业九项准则》, 国卫医发〔2021〕41 号), seventh item: "maintain the order of diagnosis and treatment, and do not undermine fairness in access to care. Uphold the principle of equality and build an environment of fair access together. It is strictly forbidden to accept benefits by using appointment slots, bed sources, drugs and consumables in short supply and other medical resources, or examinations, surgery and other arrangements for diagnosis and treatment, and to seek private gain at public expense". The eighth item: "build harmonious relations, and do not accept the patient's red envelope. Keep to medical ethics and strict self-discipline". The same item also states that it is "strictly forbidden to ask for or accept from patients and their relatives gifts, cash, gift cards, negotiable securities, equity, other financial products and other property". A meal or entertainment paid for by the patient side is the same: it is "strictly forbidden to take part in banquets, travel, fitness, entertainment or other activities arranged, organised or paid for by them". The document also requires medical institutions to carry out their main responsibility, run inspections and "firmly investigate and punish violations". Satisfaction ratings were written into the performance assessment of tertiary public hospitals. The basis is the Opinions of the General Office of the State Council on Strengthening the Performance Assessment of Tertiary Public Hospitals (《国务院办公厅关于加强三级公立医院绩效考核工作的意见》, 国办发〔2019〕4 号). The document says: "the performance assessment indicator system of tertiary public hospitals consists of indicators in 4 areas: quality of care, operational efficiency, sustainable development and satisfaction rating". It also states: "hospital satisfaction consists of two parts, patient satisfaction and staff satisfaction……" and goes on to say that the purpose is to "measure patients' sense of gain and the motivation of staff through satisfaction ratings of outpatients, inpatients and staff". This rating also has a national platform: "the state establishes a satisfaction rating system for public hospitals……". The timetable is: "by the end of March 2019, all tertiary public hospitals nationwide are brought into the satisfaction survey platform of the National Health Commission. Localities are to apply the National Health Commission satisfaction survey platform and bring the survey results into the performance assessment of tertiary public hospitals". Hospitals also have to handle complaints. The basis is the Measures for the Administration of Complaints in Medical Institutions (《医疗机构投诉管理办法》, National Health Commission Order No. 3, in force 2019), Article 2. Complaint management means that patients, over the medical service itself, the medical management and the quality and safety of care, "put forward opinions, suggestions or a complaint request". The hospital is responsible for investigating, handling and giving feedback. "Medical institutions at grade two and above shall set up a doctor-patient relations office or a designated department (collectively the complaints department) to take on complaint management in a unified way". The duties of the complaints department include "receiving complaints in a unified way, investigating and verifying the matters complained of, putting forward handling opinions, and replying to the patient in good time". Medical institutions "should encourage staff to collect patients' opinions and suggestions on medical services and on the quality and safety of care actively" (nationwide)

Notice on Issuing the Nine Codes for Clean Practice by Staff of Medical Institutions (《关于印发医疗机构工作人员廉洁从业九项准则的通知》, 国卫医发〔2021〕41 号). - Notes: The people who benefit are you and the person who saved you. Wanting to say thank you after an all-night resuscitation is human. You can thank them; only the form has to change. A red envelope, tobacco and alcohol push the risk onto the other side, while a thank-you letter, a banner and a satisfaction rating are the forms the system recognises. Where you have already handed something over and they pushed it back, do not read it as them being short of money or playing hard to get: they have to do that under the rules. And keep two things apart. A red envelope to a doctor is a matter of professional discipline and of the hospital, while handing property to the officials handling or enforcing a case is bribery under the Criminal Law. The two are not on the same scale, and the second is item 40 in section 8. Hospitals widely accept thank-you letters and banners, but the claim that they "go into the personal and department record" rests on experience and no document requires it. What is set down in a document is the rule bringing the satisfaction survey results into the performance assessment of tertiary public hospitals. Where you have doubts about the diagnosis or treatment itself, ask on the spot for sealing the records (see item 6 in this section, sealing the records), and keep your own copy of the records and images (item 5, keeping your own copy of the records and images)
