# 9. 普通人容易踩的法律红线

这一节算两笔账：你的人身自由，和你的钱。下面这些事，普通人常以为「没什么」，实际会被拘留、罚款，重的还会判刑。每条都给出法条里的具体数字，以及官方渠道公布过的案例。本节引的治安管理处罚法是 2025 年修订版，2026 年 1 月 1 日起施行，条款号和旧法不一样。刑法引的是北京市公安交管局转载的「根据刑法修正案（十一）修正」的整合文本，正文里会注明对应的修正案。条目的顺序，按「最容易不小心踩到」加「后果最重」从高到低排。

# 9. Legal lines ordinary people cross

This section counts two things: your personal freedom and your money. In the items below, ordinary people commonly think 「it is nothing」, and in fact end up with detention, a fine, and in the heavier cases a prison sentence. Each item gives the specific figures in the articles of law, together with the cases published through official channels. The Law on Penalties for Administration of Public Security cited in this section is the version revised in 2025 and in force from 2026-1-1, and its article numbers differ from those of the old law. The Criminal Law cited here is the consolidated text as amended by Criminal Law Amendment Eleven, reprinted by the Beijing Municipal Public Security Traffic Management Bureau, and each entry says which amendment is involved. The items are ordered from high to low by 「easiest to step on by accident」 plus 「heaviest consequences」.

### 1. Do not repost disaster, epidemic or police messages of unknown truth in a group chat, do not doctor photographs, and do not use AI to fake a scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you repost, look at it and see whether it is an official notice. If you are unsure, do not repost it, and do not doctor the picture or add 「it is said that」. The hard part is holding out against the urge to be the first to pass on the news.
- In plain language: For a disaster, epidemic or police message, knowingly passing it on when you know it is false is punished as heavily as writing it yourself. The same goes when you did not write it yourself: 5 to 10 days of detention and a fine of 1000 yuan. Disturbing public order carries 3 years or less, and serious consequences carry 3 to 7 years. Doctored pictures and fake scene images made with AI both count as writing it yourself.
- Benefit: Deliberately spreading a rumour, or falsely reporting a danger, an epidemic, a disaster or a police incident, and disturbing public order, carries 5 to 10 days of detention, and a further fine of up to 1000 yuan may be imposed. The lighter band is up to 5 days of detention, or a fine of up to 1000 yuan. Fabricating a false danger, epidemic, disaster or police incident and putting it online for spread carries 3 years or less. Knowingly passing on something false as though it were true falls in the same band. The condition is that public order is badly disturbed, which the article words as 「严重扰乱社会秩序」. Where serious consequences result, it carries 3 to 7 years (nationwide)

### 2. Do not post or repost pictures, stickers or comments that insult martyrs, and do not make fun of the national flag or the national anthem
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. On content involving martyrs, the national flag, the national emblem or the national anthem: do not comment, do not doctor the picture, do not repost. The hard part is holding back from joining in when somebody in the group continues a joke.
- In plain language: Making fun of martyrs, the national flag or the national anthem carries 5 to 10 days of detention, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. The heavier cases carry 10 to 15 days of detention. Where it amounts to a crime it carries 3 years or less. One man posted 2 messages defaming Yuan Longping in a 499-member villagers' WeChat group and was sentenced to 6 months' public surveillance: he was not sent to prison, but his movement was restricted.
- Benefit: Where a person's name, likeness, reputation or honour as a hero or martyr is infringed by means such as abuse or fabricating facts, and the public interest is harmed, the band below applies: 5 to 10 days of detention, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. Desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of martyrs, or producing and spreading remarks, pictures, audio or video that glorify a war of aggression, and disturbing public order, falls in the same band. The heavier band is 10 to 15 days of detention, and a further fine of up to 5000 yuan may be imposed. Where it reaches 「情节严重」, it amounts to a crime and carries 3 years or less. Insulting the national flag or the national emblem in a public place, altering the words or music of the national anthem, or singing the anthem in a distorted and disparaging way, where the circumstances are serious, also carries 3 years or less. The official case: Xiao posted 2 messages defaming and insulting Yuan Longping in a 499-member villagers' WeChat group and was sentenced to 6 months' public surveillance, which is a punishment that does not mean going to prison but does restrict your movement. Qiu used a Weibo account with 250 余万 followers to post 2 pieces of content distorting the deeds of the soldiers guarding the border, and was sentenced to 8 months, and had to make a public apology in national media (nationwide)

### 3. Close the page once you have read something on an overseas site: do not screenshot it, repost it or move it into a group chat, and do not join in under the comments
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Close the page and the matter is finished. Do not archive it, do not repost it, do not move it onto a domestic platform. Do not use an account that can be matched to your own identity to post political content on an overseas platform over the long term. Do not accept fees or funding from overseas institutions, organisations or individuals. The hard part is holding back from screenshotting and sharing content that startles you.
- In plain language: These provisions punish passing things on, not looking at them. Reposting, moving into a group chat and joining in under the comments all count as spreading when a case is handled. Where it goes as far as inciting subversion or inciting division, it carries 5 years or less, and 5 years or more for those who lead it. Books and audio or video about terrorism and extremism are the exception: knowingly still holding them, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less.
- Benefit: Using rumour, defamation or any other means to incite others to subvert the state power of the country or to overthrow the socialist system carries 5 years or less. Those who lead it, whom the article calls 「首要分子」, and those whose crimes are serious, carry 5 years or more, with fixed-term imprisonment reaching at most 15 years. Inciting the division of the country or undermining national unity carries the same terms. Where this is done in collusion with an overseas institution, organisation or individual, the punishment is heavier. Where an institution, organisation or individual inside or outside the country pays to fund somebody else's conduct, the person directly responsible carries 5 years or less, and where the circumstances are serious, 5 years or more. Stealing, spying on, buying or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence to an overseas institution, organisation or person carries 5 to 10 years. In this item, especially serious circumstances carry 10 years or more or life imprisonment, and relatively minor circumstances carry 5 years or less. Crimes endangering national security all carry an additional deprivation of political rights of 1 to 5 years, during which the right to vote and the right to hold public office are taken away. A fine and confiscation of property may also be imposed, taking the person's property into state ownership. There are provisions on administrative penalties as well. No individual or organisation may use the internet to carry out activities endangering national security, honour and interests. Nor may they incite subversion of the state power of the country or overthrow of the socialist system. Nor may they incite the division of the country or the undermining of national unity. Whoever publishes or transmits this kind of information is punished under the relevant laws and administrative regulations (nationwide). There is also a category that is complete without any spreading at all. Knowingly and illegally holding material that promotes terrorism or extremism, where the circumstances are serious, is enough for conviction. The material here includes books, audio and video materials, or other items. The sentence is up to 3 years' fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, which is short-term confinement, or public surveillance, which is confinement-free but restricts your movement. A fine may be imposed in addition or instead, so the fine can come with the sentence or on its own. The basis is Article one hundred and twenty of the Criminal Law, item six, added by Criminal Law Amendment Nine
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (1997). Criminal Law, 刑法（第四十五、五十五、五十六、一百零三、一百零五、一百零六、一百零七、一百一十一、一百一十三条，这几条未经修正案修改）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2025). Cybersecurity Law, 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第十三条第二款、第七十一条第一项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (Nine), 刑法修正案（九）（新增第一百二十条之六）, full text reprinted by the National Petition Office. <https://www.gjxfj.gov.cn/gjxfj/fgwj/flfg/webinfo/2017/03/1601761496614423.htm>
- Notes: The provisions cited in this item punish inciting others, passing things on, and providing things to other people. Merely looking is not among them. Material about terrorism and extremism is the exception written into the article, and simply holding it can already amount to a conviction (Article one hundred and twenty, item six), so this category must never be stored. Once you have seen it, that is where it ends: do not screenshot and store it, do not repost it, do not add a comment, and do not pull other people into reposting it. Both reposting and joining in under the comments count as spreading when a case is handled, and 「I was only reposting it」 is no excuse. Content that comes from an overseas platform may also attract a heavier penalty. If you are unsure whether something is true, do not join in either: see item 1 in this section (do not repost messages of unknown truth). For how the circumvention tools themselves are punished, see item 11 in section 11. Passing this kind of content on brings you no practical good, while the price is a prison sentence, deprivation of political rights and confiscation of property. Publicly checkable cases of this kind appear only in overseas media and in documents released by families, such as a blogger who had long posted political commentary on an overseas platform being sentenced to 7 years in 2023. This book has not obtained a judgment that can be checked through official channels, so it writes from the articles alone and attaches no case.

### 4. Pornographic video is yours alone to watch: do not post it in a group chat, do not sell 「the material」, and do not set up a group
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Do not repost it, do not save it into a group chat's files, and do not become the owner or an administrator of that kind of group.
- In plain language: Watching it yourself is not within the penal provisions. Posting it into a group chat once counts as spreading it to the whole group, and carries 10 to 15 days of detention and a further fine of up to 5000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious it carries 2 years or less. Selling 「the material」 to make money carries 3 years or less, and at the top life imprisonment. Setting up a distribution group of more than 30 people, the person who sets it up, the administrators, and the people who mainly post into it are all treated as committing the crime.
- Benefit: Spreading obscene information online carries 10 to 15 days of detention, and a further fine of up to 5000 yuan may be imposed. The lighter band is up to 5 days of detention, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. Where the material involves minors, the punishment is heavier. Spreading obscene material at the level of 「情节严重」 carries 2 years or less. Selling or spreading it in order to make money carries 3 years or less and a fine. In this item, serious circumstances carry 3 to 10 years, and especially serious circumstances carry 10 years or more or life imprisonment. Setting up a group whose main purpose is spreading obscene electronic information, with 30 or more members, is handled as a crime. Causing serious consequences is the same. The person who set the group up, the administrators, and the people who mainly posted into it are all convicted of the crime of spreading obscene material (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2025). Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security, 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml> ; National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Criminal Law, as amended by Criminal Law Amendment Eleven, 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百六十三、三百六十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html> ; Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate (2010). Interpretation (Two) on Several Questions Concerning the Application of the Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Producing, Copying, Publishing, Selling or Spreading Obscene Electronic Information by Means of the Internet, Mobile Communication Terminals and Voice Messaging Platforms（第三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201002/t20100204_25001.shtml>
- Notes: Both of these laws punish 「spreading」, and watching privately is not within them. But posting into a group chat once already counts as spreading to the whole group. What 「情节严重」 means in terms of quantity is set by the judicial interpretation, and this section has not verified the specific figures.

### 5. If a 「side job」 wants you to receive money, take out cash or make transfers through your own card, do not do it whatever fee you are offered
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Turn down that kind of 「a few hundred a day for carrying a card to the bank」 work.
- In plain language: This kind of side job earns a few hundred yuan, and the price is a criminal record. One man went to an agreed place with his identity card and 1 bank card, helped with the transfers and withdrawals, and earned a little over 500 yuan. The procuratorate did not prosecute him, but the police still detained him for 10 days and fined him 1000 yuan. Another handed his own and other people's bank cards and phone cards to a gang to receive money and take out cash, and was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months with a fine of 22,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Knowing that money and goods come from a crime and still helping to transfer, buy or sell them, or helping to conceal them in some other way, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, so the fine can come with the sentence or on its own. The official case: Wang, on instructions, took his identity card and 1 bank card to an agreed place and helped with the transfers and withdrawals, and earned a little over 500 yuan. The procuratorate did not prosecute him, but the police detained him for 10 days under the Law on the Prevention and Control of Telecom and Online Fraud and fined him 1000 yuan. Man handed his own bank cards and phone cards, and those of other people, to the gang to receive money, transfer it and take out cash, and was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months with a fine of 22,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

- Notes: For the consequences of lending your bank card to somebody and of passing transactions through it for them, see item 8 in section 8 (lending a bank card to somebody). A fee that is absurdly high, being asked to verify with your face, and transactions that look abnormal however you look at them are enough between them to establish that you did in fact know. The lightest outcome is still 10 days of detention and a criminal record.

### 6. If somebody draws you into 「packaging the materials」 for a loan and offers you a cut of the loan amount, do none of it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Simply say no. Do not lend your identity card or your own credit record to somebody else, do not sign a loan contract you have not read, and do not go to the bank to sign on somebody else's behalf.
- In plain language: Helping somebody to 「package the materials」 to get a loan and taking a share of it is handled as fraud. In one case a gang of 126 people packaged people with no job as 「high-quality customers」 and fraudulently obtained more than 30 million yuan in loans from more than 80 banks. The people who came forward to sign took three tenths of the loan amount as a fee, and of the 80 people sentenced, 76 got 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months. The debt belongs to whoever the loan is in the name of.
- Benefit: Obtaining loans, bill acceptances, letters of credit, guarantees and the like from a bank or another financial institution by deceptive means. Where this causes major losses to the bank or another financial institution, it carries 3 years or less. A fine is imposed in addition or instead, so the fine can come with the sentence or on its own. Where especially major losses result, or where there are other especially serious circumstances, it carries 3 to 7 years and a fine. The threshold at which the police open a case and start to investigate is a direct economic loss of 500,000 yuan or more. Where the intention from the start was not to repay, and the loan is obtained by fabricating a reason for bringing in capital, a false economic contract, false supporting documents or a false title deed, that is the crime of loan fraud. In this item, obtaining 50,000 yuan is enough to open a case, and it carries 5 years or less with a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan. An especially large amount, or other serious circumstances, carries 5 to 10 years, and an especially especially large amount carries 10 years or more up to life imprisonment. Forging, altering, buying or selling official documents, certificates or seals of state organs carries 3 years or less, and where the circumstances are serious, 3 to 10 years. The official case: a gang of 126 people packaged people with no job as 「high-quality customers」 across 16 provinces and cities, and fraudulently obtained more than 3000 余万元 in loans from more than 80 bank branches. The 「blank customers」 who came forward to take out the loans took three tenths of the loan amount as a fee. The procuratorate pursued 105 of the 「blank customers」. Of the 80 people sentenced at first instance in 2026, 76 were sentenced for the crime of loan fraud to 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months, together with a fine. Another 4 people, because they took out cash for the gang, were convicted of loan fraud and of money laundering together, with the two crimes worked out as one sentence. Those 4 were sentenced to 2 years and 4 months to 7 years and 6 months (nationwide). The crime of fraudulently obtaining loans in this item follows the text of the Criminal Law as it has stood since 2021-3 under Criminal Law Amendment Eleven

- Notes: In that case, the statement 「I meant to repay it」 was not accepted. The procuratorate found that these people had no job and no fixed assets, could not possibly repay, and had spent the money they were given, and on that basis found that they had never intended to repay. People who took out cash for the gang may also pick up a separate count of money laundering. Whoever's name the loan is in, and whoever signed the contract, is the borrower. Even where it is not a crime, the debt and the entry in your credit record are yours. Taking the money and running abroad settles nothing: once a case has been opened, however many years you stay away it can still be pursued. If you have been sentenced and have not served your sentence, or you are being treated as a suspect or a defendant, you may not leave the country. ### 7. Do not withdraw money from a bank card you pick up; contact the owner or hand a phone or wallet you find to the police, and do not pocket it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, and it takes a few minutes. Make one call to find the owner, or take the thing to the police station.
- In plain language: Keeping something you found and using it yourself, where the amount reaches 「数额较大」 and you refuse to hand it over, carries 2 years or less, and an especially large amount carries 2 to 5 years. Using a bank card you found to take money out of an ATM is heavier: it counts as using another person's credit card, and a relatively large amount carries 5 years or less with a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan. For this one the police open a case directly; the owner does not have to sue.
- Benefit: Where you find something somebody has lost, you must return it to the owner, notify them promptly so that they can collect it, or hand it to the public security organ or another department. Keeping something somebody has left behind for your own use, where the amount reaches 「数额较大」 and you refuse to hand it over, constitutes the crime of embezzlement and carries 2 years or less. An especially large amount carries 2 to 5 years. Using another person's credit card that you found on an automated teller machine falls under 「冒用他人信用卡」. Where it amounts to a crime it is handled as credit card fraud: a relatively large amount carries 5 years or less, with a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan. An especially large amount, or other serious circumstances, carries 5 to 10 years, with a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan (nationwide)

### 8. Claim a discount in your own real identity, do not buy virtual numbers to register batches of accounts, and do not rely on a loophole to claim it again and again

- In plain language: Claiming a coupon once, in your own real identity, on one account, is not within the penal provisions. Registering fake users in bulk, or placing orders yourself and then taking the orders yourself in order to claim more, is handled as fraud: 3000 yuan is enough for 3 years or less, and 30,000 yuan or more is 3 to 10 years. Where you earned nothing, or earned less than 50,000 yuan, the fine is up to 500,000 yuan, and in serious cases there is detention of up to 15 days. ### 22. Do not sell your own organ, and do not help other people find a donor: a kidney fetches a little over 20,000 yuan, the same kidney resold goes for 200,000 yuan, the money is confiscated and a fine of 10 to 20 times the transaction amount is imposed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, no time, and you simply do not do it.
- In plain language: A living organ may only be donated to a spouse, a direct blood relative or a collateral blood relative within three generations, and buying or selling is illegal either way. In one case in Beijing the person selling a kidney got a little over 20,000 yuan, while the same kidney sold to a patient fetched 180,000 to 220,000 yuan. The money is confiscated and a fine of 10 to 20 times the transaction amount is imposed. For what losing a kidney costs the body afterwards, see item 35 in section 1 (the remaining kidney does the work of two).
- Benefit: Article five of the Regulations on the Donation and Transplantation of Human Organs:「任何组织或者个人不得以任何形式买卖人体器官，不得从事与买卖人体器官有关的活动」. Article ten:「任何组织或者个人不得获取未满 18 周岁公民的活体器官用于移植」. Article eleven:「活体器官的接受人限于活体器官捐献人的配偶、直系血亲或者三代以内旁系血亲」. Article thirty-seven sets out how buying and selling human organs is punished. Buying or selling human organs, or engaging in activity connected with buying or selling human organs, is investigated by the health commission of a local people's government at county level or above. First the illegal gains are confiscated, taking away the whole of the money from the transaction. Then a fine of 10 times or more and not more than 20 times the transaction amount is imposed. Where medical personnel take part, their practice certificates are revoked and they are permanently barred from working in health care. Where a crime is constituted, criminal liability is pursued under the law (nationwide, in force from 2024-5-1, and the former Regulations on Human Organ Transplantation is repealed at the same time). Article two hundred and thirty-four-one of the Criminal Law:「组织他人出卖人体器官的，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；情节严重的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产」. There are three further circumstances, convicted and punished under Article two hundred and thirty-four and Article two hundred and thirty-two of the Criminal Law. First, taking somebody's organ without their consent. Second, taking the organ of a person under eighteen years old. Third, forcing or deceiving somebody into donating an organ. These are handled as intentional injury or intentional homicide. The first case: the Zheng Wei case, 15 defendants, heard on final instance by the Beijing First Intermediate People's Court on 2014-8-20, involved 51 kidneys and an amount of more than 1034 万元 (10,340,000 yuan). What the person selling the kidney, the donor side, got was, in the words of the judgment, 「换来的仅仅是 2 万到 2.5 万元不等的报酬」, a payment ranging from 20,000 to 25,000 yuan. What the waiting patient's side, the recipient side, paid was 「以前是一个肾 18 万或 19 万，后来是 21 万或 22 万」. The place the kidneys were taken out in was a four-storey building rented at 7,500 yuan a month, and the nurse in charge of the gang testified that the place was 「脏乱不堪，连抢救设备都没有」. The organiser Zheng Wei was sentenced to 12 years' imprisonment for the crime of organising the sale of human organs, and the other 14 got 3 and a half years to 9 years. The second case: the 「boy sells his kidney for an Apple phone」 case published by the Supreme People's Procuratorate. Wang, aged 17, had his right kidney removed in Chenzhou on the evening of 2011-4-28. The operation was carried out in a department run by a contractor inside a hospital with no transplant qualification. On 5-2, when he was discharged, he received 22,000 yuan, and the recipient side paid the middleman 150,000 yuan and 10,000 US dollars, with the principal offender making more than 56,000 yuan. ### 23. Do not buy sex: the default band is 10 to 15 days of detention, not a fine and nothing more

- Cost: No money, no time, and you simply do not do it.
- In plain language: Getting caught is not a matter of paying a fine. The default band written into the article is 10 to 15 days of detention, and a further fine of up to 5000 yuan may be imposed. Only where the circumstances are relatively minor is it up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1000 yuan. And if you go while knowingly having syphilis or gonorrhoea, that is a crime carrying 5 years or less.
- Benefit: Article seventy-eight of the Law on Penalties for Administration of Public Security: a person who sells sex or buys it is detained for not less than ten days and not more than fifteen days, and may additionally be fined up to five thousand yuan. Where the circumstances are relatively minor, the detention is up to five days or the fine up to one thousand yuan. Soliciting in a public place is punishable by up to five days' detention or a fine of up to one thousand yuan. Article seventy-nine: a person who lures, harbours or introduces another to sell sex is detained for not less than ten days and not more than fifteen days, and may additionally be fined up to five thousand yuan. Article eighty-seven: where personnel of the hotel, catering, cultural and entertainment, or taxi businesses, or of similar units, tip off the person breaking the law or provide the conditions for the above activities in any other way while the public security organ is investigating prostitution, the detention is not less than ten days and not more than fifteen days.
