# 25. 人走了以后要办什么

口径：金钱。本节只讲手续怎么办、钱怎么拿，包括人刚走时先做什么、哪些费用有清单可以对。还有哪些账户里的钱能取回来，哪些事近亲属有权出面办。前五条按事情发生的先后排，依次是现场、死亡证明、遗体和火化、对死因有异议怎么办、销户。后面几条按性价比排。老人生前该做的安排，比如意定监护、遗嘱、账户，见第 17 节。因工死亡的三笔待遇见第 19 节。

殡葬这部分依据新版《殡葬管理条例》，2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行。养老保险的丧葬补助金和遗属抚恤金给多少，由人力资源社会保障部门另外规定。这次没拿到能逐字核对的原文，所以正文不写具体金额。

# 25. What to do after someone dies

This section measures money. It covers only the paperwork and the money: what to do first after someone dies, which charges you can check against a published list, which accounts still hold money you can get back, and which steps a close relative is allowed to handle in person. The first five entries follow the order in which things happen: the scene, the death certificate, the body and the cremation, what to do if you dispute the cause of death, and the cancellation of the household registration. The rest are ordered by value for money. The arrangements an older person should make while they are still able, such as voluntary guardianship, a will and accounts, are in section 17. The three payments for a death caused by work are in section 19.

The funeral side follows the new Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration (殡葬管理条例), in force from 2026-3-30. The amounts of the funeral subsidy and the survivor's pension under the old-age insurance are set separately by the human resources and social security departments. This time I could not get the original text to check word for word, so the items below give no figures.

### 1. If someone dies at home or elsewhere, first tell an expected death from an unexpected one: an accident, an unknown cause, or a person living alone found late — call 110 at once, and do not touch the body or the scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. One phone call is all it takes.
- In plain language: Where the death was an accident, where nobody knows how it happened, or where someone living alone was found only after a long time, only the police can issue the death certificate. Once the scene has been disturbed, the account cannot be set out and the certificate is hard to issue as well. Without that sheet of paper, the cremation, the cancellation of the household registration and the insurance claim all stall. If you cannot tell which kind it is, call 110 first. Once the police have been there and finished their inspection, they will still issue the certificate.
- Benefit: The Regulations on the Registration of Households (户口登记条例), Article eight, paragraph two: 「公民因意外事故致死或者死因不明，户主、发现人应当立即报告当地公安派出所或者乡、镇人民委员会」 — where a person dies in an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the head of household and the person who found the body must report it at once to the local police station or the township or town people's government. The Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration (殡葬管理条例), Article twenty-three: 「自然人正常死亡的，医疗卫生机构应当及时出具死亡证明。涉及公安机关依法处置且未经医疗卫生机构救治的非正常死亡案（事）件的逝者，由公安机关出具死亡证明」 — for an ordinary death, the medical and health institution issues the death certificate in good time. Where the case involves a death that the police handle under the law and the person was not treated by a medical institution, the police issue the certificate. Only the police can issue the certificate for an unexpected death. If the scene has been disturbed, the account cannot be set out and the certificate is hard to issue. The cremation, the cancellation of the household registration and the insurance claim all stall on that one sheet of paper
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (1958). Regulations on the Registration of Households, 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (posted on the Guangdong Provincial Government portal); State Council (国务院) (2026). Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration, 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: Someone bedridden for a long time who dies at home counts as an expected death. You can ask the institution that treated him while he was alive to issue the certificate, or the community health centre or the township health centre for the area you live in. Whoever treated him issues the death certificate, see item 2 in this section (the death certificate). If you cannot tell which kind it is, treat it as unexpected and call 110 first; once the police have been there and finished their inspection, they will still issue the certificate. If you tidy the scene up before calling, nobody can help you afterwards. Do not move the body yourself, do not wash and dress the body, and do not take away the medicine bottles or the notes.

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: whoever treated the person issues it, for an expected death at home you go to the community health service institution, and it is issued within one day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: If the person died in hospital, ask the institution that treated them for the certificate. If they died at home in the ordinary way, ask the community health centre or the township health centre for the area you live in. When the cause of death is clear, the certificate is issued within one day. The cancellation of the household registration, the cremation, the withdrawal of the housing provident fund, the payments under social insurance, the insurance claim and the inheritance all need it. Get several copies as soon as you have it.
- Benefit: The Interim Standards for the Registration of Death Information (人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）) say that for a death inside a medical and health institution the certificate is 「负责救治的医疗卫生机构签发」 — issued by the institution that did the treating, so for a death in a hospital or a health centre you ask the one that treated the person. For an ordinary death at home or in another place it is 「由本辖区社区卫生服务机构或乡镇（街道）卫生院签发」 — issued by the community health service institution or the township (sub-district) health centre for the area. Where the police or a judicial authority judges the death to be unexpected, the police or judicial department handles it under the existing rules and procedures. A 2026 notice from five departments says that the death certificate 「纸质版与电子证照具有同等法律效力」 — the paper version and the electronic licence carry the same legal force, so the electronic one works exactly like the paper one. An ordinary death with a known cause must be issued within one day of the death. If the certificate is lost, you may apply once to the issuing institution for a replacement. If a basic detail is wrong, you may apply once, on the strength of your documents, for it to be issued again
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Office of the National Health and Family Planning Commission (国家卫生计生委办公厅) (2014). Interim Standards for the Registration of Death Information, 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html> (forwarded by the Shenzhen Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau); National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委), Ministry of Public Security (公安部), Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部), National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine (国家中医药局), National Administration of Disease Control and Prevention (国家疾控局) (2026). Notice on Strengthening the Registration of Medical Death Certificates of Residents and the Management of Electronic Licences, 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm> (forwarded by the Fujian Provincial Health Commission)
- Notes: The cancellation of the household registration, the cremation, the withdrawal of the housing provident fund, the payments under social insurance, the insurance claim and the inheritance all need it. Get it and make several copies at once, and ask for the electronic licence as well where one can be issued. You only get one chance of a replacement, so do not lose the original. If the name or the identity number is wrong, have it corrected on the spot, because finding it out when the inheritance is being processed means going round every counter again.

### 3. Collecting, storing and cremating the body can only be done by a funeral home, a hospital mortuary does not hold funerals, and storage is normally no more than 3 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain language: Collecting the body, storing it, treating it, restoring its appearance and cremating it can only be done by a funeral home (殡仪馆). A hospital mortuary does not hold funerals; at most it lets you leave the body there for a short time. A funeral home normally keeps a body for no more than 3 days, and to keep it longer you have to go and extend it. Cremation needs the death certificate and the cremation confirmation form. The cremation certificate you are given afterwards is still needed for the inheritance and for the insurance claim.
- Benefit: The Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration (殡葬管理条例), Article twenty-five: 「遗体接运、存放、防腐、整容、火化服务，由殡仪馆专门负责提供」 — the services of collecting, storing, treating, restoring and cremating the body are provided by the funeral home alone, and nobody else can do them. Article twenty-four: 「医疗卫生机构的太平间不得外包，不得开展殡仪服务，可以为遗体暂时停放提供便利服务」 — a hospital mortuary may not be outsourced and may not provide funeral services, and it may only make it convenient for a body to be kept there temporarily. Article twenty-seven: 「殡仪馆存放遗体一般不超过 3 日，需要延期存放的，丧属或者遗体移交方应当在殡仪馆办理延期存放手续」 — a funeral home normally keeps a body for no more than 3 days, and where longer storage is needed, the relatives or the party handing over the body must go to the funeral home and complete the extension formalities. Article twenty-eight: 「殡仪馆应当凭死亡证明和火化确认书火化遗体，并出具火化证明」 — the funeral home cremates the body on the strength of the death certificate and the cremation confirmation form, and issues a cremation certificate. You need both documents, and after the cremation you are given a cremation certificate. Article twenty-nine: 「在实行火葬的地区，遗体应当就地、就近在殡仪馆火化」 — in an area where cremation applies, the body is cremated at a funeral home in the place where the person died, or the nearest one
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration, 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: A 「one-stop service」 that turns up after someone dies in hospital is usually not from the funeral home. The collection itself is a basic item and it has a published price, see item 6 in this section (the list of basic items). Keep the cremation certificate safe; you need it for the inheritance, the insurance and moving the ashes. Whether the area you live in applies cremation or burial is fixed by the provincial government. The funeral customs of ethnic minorities are protected by Article 9 of the regulations. Where a body that should be cremated is buried instead, or is buried and a grave built outside a cemetery or an eco-burial area, the civil affairs department orders it corrected within a set period (that is, put it back the way the rule says).

### 4. If you dispute the cause of death, ask for the autopsy within 48 hours and have the medical records sealed at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is remembering to ask at that moment.
- In plain language: If you have a question about the cause of death, you have to ask for the autopsy within 48 hours of the death. Where the hospital can store the body frozen, the time can be extended to 7 days. An autopsy also needs the signature of a close relative. Without that signature, or if you let the time run out, the responsibility for not establishing the cause of death falls on whoever delayed. When you ask for the autopsy, ask at the same time that the medical records be sealed. Once the body is cremated, no autopsy is possible any more.
- Benefit: The time limit for the autopsy is in the Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (医疗纠纷预防和处理条例), Article twenty-six. The text is: 「患者死亡，医患双方对死因有异议的，应当在患者死亡后 48 小时内进行尸检；具备尸体冻存条件的，可以延长至 7 日」 — where the patient dies and the two parties disagree about the cause of death, the autopsy must be carried out within 48 hours of the death, and where the body can be kept frozen it may be extended to 7 days. So if you want the cause of death established, the autopsy has to happen within 48 hours of the death, and where the hospital has freezing conditions it goes to 7 days at most. The same article also says: 「尸检应当经死者近亲属同意并签字，拒绝签字的，视为死者近亲属不同意进行尸检」 — the autopsy requires the consent and the signature of a close relative of the dead person, and a refusal to sign counts as a refusal. If the family does not sign, it counts as a refusal. It also says: 「不同意或者拖延尸检，超过规定时间，影响对死因判定的，由不同意或者拖延的一方承担责任」 — whoever refuses or delays the autopsy past the time limit and thereby affects the finding on the cause of death bears the responsibility. Whoever refuses, and whoever delays past the deadline, is the party that bears the responsibility for a cause of death that cannot be established later. Article twenty-four: sealing and unsealing the medical records 「应当在医患双方在场的情况下进行」 — must be done with both parties present. You and the hospital both have to be there when the records are sealed and when the seal is broken
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2018). Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes, 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: Saying afterwards that something felt wrong from the start puts the responsibility on the party that delayed. Once the body is cremated there is no autopsy to be had, so this has to be settled before you get in touch with the funeral home. How the records are sealed is in section 24.

### 5. Cancelling the household registration has a statutory deadline: in a city before the burial, in the countryside within one month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Take the death certificate and the household register and go to the police station once.
- In plain language: In a city the registration is cancelled before the burial; in the countryside within one month. Take the death certificate and the household register to the police station. If you leave it undone, the transfer of inherited property, the payments you are entitled to and the repayment of the pension all get stuck on the household registration status.
- Benefit: The Regulations on the Registration of Households (户口登记条例), Article eight, paragraph one: 「公民死亡，城市在葬前，农村在一个月以内，由户主、亲属、抚养人或者邻居向户口登记机关申报死亡登记，注销户口。公民如果在暂住地死亡，由暂住地户口登记机关通知常住地户口登记机关注销户口」 — the meaning is: in a city it is done before the burial, in the countryside within one month. The head of household, a relative, a guardian or a neighbour may all go to the registration authority (the police station) to report the death and cancel the registration. Where a person dies while staying away from home, the police station where they were staying notify the police station of the registered residence, and the registration is cancelled there
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (1958). Regulations on the Registration of Households, 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (posted on the Guangdong Provincial Government portal)
- Notes: While the registration is still open, the transfer of inherited property, the payments you are entitled to and the repayment of the pension all stick, because every one of those counters looks at the registration status. If the pension keeps being paid, that is someone receiving it wrongly and it will have to be handed back eventually. While you are at the police station, ask as well: how the social insurance and the medical insurance payments are stopped, and how the funeral subsidy is claimed. Those payments are in item 9 in this section (the provident fund balance and the payments under social insurance).

### 6. Funeral services are split into basic items and non-basic items, there is a list of the basic items, and the charges are set by law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend a few minutes looking at the list before you set off.
- In plain language: Collecting the body, storing the body, the farewell ceremony, the cremation, the storage of the ashes and eco-burial are all on the national list of basic items. Ash-niche burial provided by a public institution is on the list as well. The price of these items is fixed by the rules. Outside the list you may not set up an item of your own and charge for it. Before you set off, ask which items count as basic items and what the local standard is, and only then talk about the rest.
- Benefit: Collecting the body, storing the body, saying farewell over the body, cremating the body, storing the ashes and eco-burial are all on the national list of basic items. Ash-niche burial provided by a funeral service institution run by the government is on the list as well. The charges for these items are set by law, the price is fixed by the rules and it is not for the funeral home to decide. The list of non-basic items is drawn up by the civil affairs department of a city or above (the civil affairs department at prefecture level and above)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration, 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Notes: A province, an autonomous region or a municipality may add items on top of the national list. Ask first which items count as basic items and what the local charge is, and only then talk about the rest. The price of an item on the list is fixed by the rules; the items off the list are yours to choose. Tell the two kinds apart and only then do you have anything to say about the part you have been overcharged. There is nothing wrong with giving someone a decent send-off, and no one is telling you to make do cheaply; where the money goes is your own decision.

### 7. If you are sold a bundle you did not ask for, charged above the posted price, or charged several times for one item split into several, write it down and complain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is still standing your ground at that moment.
- In plain language: All of these are price violations the regulations name: not displaying a price list, adding money on top of the posted price, tying the sale of funeral goods to the service, and making you accept another provider's paid service. Splitting one item into several and charging for each is a hidden price rise as well. Ask for the price list on the spot, and keep the receipts and the messages. Complaining afterwards is still in time. For prices go to the market regulation authorities, for the funeral service itself go to the civil affairs department.
- Benefit: The regulations list five kinds of price violation, and four of them are set out here. Not displaying a price list, and taking more money than the posted price, are a failure to mark prices clearly or an addition to the posted price. Not being allowed to have one thing unless you buy another is a tying arrangement, or the compulsory sale of goods by attaching unreasonable conditions. Having another provider's paid service pushed on you is the compulsory or disguised compulsory acceptance of a paid service from a third party. Taking more money under another name is a disguised increase of the charge standard. The regulations also forbid 「分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准」 — disguised increases of the charge standard by splitting items, widening the scope of the charge, and the like. Splitting one service into several and charging for them separately, or pushing the scope of the charge outwards, is among the forbidden things
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration, 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」
- Notes: Price violations are dealt with by the market regulation authorities, and the funeral service itself by the civil affairs department. Ask for a price list on the spot, keep the receipts and the messages, and complaining afterwards is still in time.
### 8. Funeral agents, agents who buy the goods, planners and hosts of the ceremony: intermediaries like these must file with the county civil affairs department
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One question is enough: has it filed?
- In plain language: Intermediaries in funeral agency, in buying the goods, in planning and hosting the ceremony and in giving information all have to file with the civil affairs department (民政部门) of the county. An intermediary that has not filed does not get into the funeral services information system run by the civil affairs department. Whether you can look it up yourself varies from place to place, and the surest way is to telephone the county civil affairs department. Anyone who uses deception to charge fees, or who sells on the information of the dead, will be dealt with under the law.
- Benefit: An intermediary that has not filed is not in the funeral services information system under the civil affairs department. Anyone who charges fees in breach of the rules by deceptive means, encourages extravagant and wasteful funerals, forces people to accept services, or sells on the information of the dead will be dealt with under the law, will be flagged in the system, and will be published for the public to see
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Regulations on Funeral and Interment Administration, 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Notes: Selling on the information of the dead is the commonest trap in this trade. If a pile of sales calls arrives the moment the paperwork is done, the information has almost certainly been sold on. Online memorial platforms are covered by the same rule. Article 45 of the regulations forbids them from pushing consumption, and forbids them from handling the information of the dead in breach of the law.

### 9. The money sitting in different places has to be collected one at a time: the provident fund balance, the payments under social insurance, the payments for a work injury
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, but you have to visit several counters. Before you go, prepare the death certificate and the proof of the relationship.
- In plain language: An heir can withdraw the balance in the housing provident fund, it does not sit in a bank account, and it is the one most easily forgotten. What is left in the personal account under the old-age insurance can also be inherited. Where a person dies of illness, the family receives a funeral subsidy and a survivor's pension of 3 to 24 months as separate payments. Where a person dies as a result of a work injury, there are the three payments under the work injury rules as well.
- Benefit: The balance in a housing provident fund account (what is left in that person's provident fund account) may be withdrawn by the heirs or by a legatee under the will. A legatee under the will is the person written into the will to leave the money to. Where a person dies as a result of a work injury, there are three further payments: the funeral subsidy, the pension for dependants and the one-off payment for a death from a work injury. The Social Insurance Law states that what is left in the personal account under the old-age insurance may be inherited. Where a person who took out the employee old-age insurance dies of illness or of something other than a work injury, the family can receive the funeral subsidy and the survivor's pension, which together are called the survivor benefits. For enterprise employees the standard follows one formula for the whole country. The funeral subsidy is 2 times the average monthly disposable income of urban residents in the province in the previous year. The survivor's pension is based on that monthly income and is paid for 3 to 24 months according to the years you have contributed: under 5 years, 3 months; 5 to 10 years, 6 months; 10 to 15 years, 9 months; over 15 years, 1 month more for every extra year, up to 24 months. For someone already retired, every year of pension already drawn reduces it by 1 month, down to a minimum of 9 months

### 10. The deceased's accounts and personal information: close relatives may ask to look at them, take a copy, have them corrected, or have them deleted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. You only need to report to the authorities or sue if the platform refuses to cooperate.
- In plain language: After someone dies, close relatives may, for their own lawful and proper interests, ask the platform to let them look at the deceased's personal information, take a copy, correct it or delete it. If the platform refuses, it has to give its reasons. You can also take the case to court. There is one exception: where the person arranged something in advance, for example by naming an account successor or leaving a clear statement of wishes, his arrangement is followed.
- Benefit: The law says plainly: where a natural person dies, close relatives, for their own lawful and proper interests, may exercise the rights set out in that chapter to look at, take a copy of, correct and delete the deceased's relevant personal information. Close relatives may ask the platform to let them see the information, take a copy, put right the errors and delete it
