# 11. Lines programmers and tech workers cross

The measures in this section are personal freedom and money, and what you get back is 「not being locked up」 and 「not having to pay」. People who write code and do technical work often treat the things below as 「only a technical problem」, but they correspond to a specific charge in the Criminal Law, or to a specific sum you have to pay in civil law. Each item gives the figures in the articles of law, together with the cases published on the websites of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme People's Court. The Criminal Law is cited from the consolidated text as amended by Criminal Law Amendment Eleven, reprinted by the Beijing Municipal Public Security Traffic Management Bureau. The items are ordered from high to low by 「what a technical worker is most likely to think is harmless × the heaviest consequences」. Item 1 is the general one: how to judge before you act, and what to do first once something has happened.

### 1. Ask three questions before you act: whose interests have you harmed, how capable the other side is of pursuing it, how much evidence have you left; and get a criminal lawyer as soon as you are pursued
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you act, replace the question 「can this be done technically」 with another one: will the vendor, the platform or the company report it to the authorities? The hard part is holding out against the thought that 「nobody will do anything about something this small」, because server logs, payment records and group chat records are all produced by default when a case is handled.
- In plain language: Before you act, ask one question: will the vendor, the platform or the company report it to the police? Server logs, payment records and group chat records can all be produced when a case is handled. If you are held in criminal detention, the decision on whether to arrest you is made within at most 37 days. From the first questioning you can already have a lawyer, and those 37 days are when getting a criminal lawyer does the most good.
- Benefit: Any unit or individual that finds facts of a crime has the right to report it to the authorities. Where the case-handling organ finds facts of a crime, a case must be opened. After you have been held in criminal detention, the police must apply for approval of the arrest within 3 days, that is, ask the procuratorate to arrest you formally. In special circumstances this may be extended by 1 to 4 days. In cases such as being on the run, where offences are committed in several places in succession, it may be extended to 30 days. The procuratorate then has 7 days to decide. Added together, the decision on whether to arrest is made within at most 37 days. From the first interrogation, or from the moment a compulsory measure is taken against you, that is detention or arrest, you may appoint a lawyer. While the case is still with the police for investigation you may appoint a lawyer. When the lawyer applies to see you, the detention centre must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest. Where the possible sentence is public surveillance, criminal detention or some punishment lighter than prison, or where only an additional punishment is imposed with no principal punishment, you may be released on bail pending trial: pay a deposit or find a guarantor and go home while the case is handled. Release on bail lasts at most 12 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2018). Criminal Procedure Law, 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十九、六十七、七十九、九十一、一百一十、一百一十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: Those 37 days are counted from detention to the procuratorate's decision on whether to approve the arrest; they are not a limit on how long you may be held. After the arrest is approved there is still the custody period during the investigation, and the counting continues from there. Those 37 days are also when a lawyer who knows the field is most useful: getting to see you, handing the procuratorate a written view that the arrest should not be approved, and applying for release on bail. Looking for a lawyer after the prosecution has begun is too late. Every other item in this section is about the same thing: whether the other side has grounds to report you.
### 2. Do not make or sell game cheats and cheats, including AI assistance that only reads the screen and never touches memory
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The price is giving up this kind of contract work and the income from 「small tools」. The hard part is that the money comes in quickly and it is not obvious who would come after you.
- In plain language: Selling cheats to 20 users, or earning 5000 yuan, is enough for 3 years or less, and especially serious cases carry 3 to 7 years. One man made wallhack and aimbot cheats, sold more than 80,000 yuan of them in 3 months, and was sentenced to 1 year suspended for 1 year with a fine of 5000 yuan. Another used server-side programs to run a private server, took in more than 20,000,000 yuan of top-ups, and got 4 years at the most, with a fine of up to 10,000,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Providing programs or tools made specifically for intruding into or illegally controlling computer information systems, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead. An additional fine means a fine on top of the sentence; instead means a fine alone. Especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years and a fine. The threshold for 「serious circumstances」 is providing the tool to more than 20 users, or illegal gains of more than 5000 yuan. Illegal gains means the money you earned out of it. Deliberately going around or breaking the technical protection measures the rights holder has put in place may also amount to the crime of infringing copyright, which carries 3 years or less, and 3 to 10 years where it is serious. The official cases: Wang and others made and sold cheats that read and modified the game's memory to give wallhacks and aimbots. They sold more than 80,000 yuan in 3 months and made over 30,000 yuan. He was sentenced to 1 year suspended for 1 year, with a fine of 5000 yuan. A suspended sentence of 1 year means he was sentenced to 1 year but does not go in at first, and if he does not offend again during that year he does not have to serve it. Xiang and others sold the 「TZ」 series of cheats with sales of more than 200,000 yuan, and Xiang was sentenced to 3 years suspended for 3 years with a fine of 20,000 yuan. Liu and others obtained the game's server-side program, changed the parameters and ran a private server, taking in more than 2000 余万元 in top-ups. The sentence was 4 years to 2 years and 6 months, with a fine of from 10,000,000 yuan down to 1,300,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). - Notes: The cheat cases that have been published are all the kind that read and write game memory and get round the anti-cheat. No official case has yet been seen for assistance that 「only takes screenshots and only simulates key presses」. But the ticket-grabbing case in item 3 shows that not touching memory and only sending requests can still amount to the crime of illegally obtaining data. Whether it is a crime in the end, and under which charge, is settled by the case-handling organ on the basis of the material handed in when the vendor reports it, and not by the author. Selling to 20 users is enough for 「serious circumstances」, and that is a small number.
### 3. Do not write or sell ticket-grabbing, flash-sale, order-brushing or coupon-collecting scripts, not even one that only 「clicks the button automatically」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, and no time either.
- In plain language: A script does not change memory and does not crack anything; it is simply faster at sending requests than a person, and it is still convicted. Earning 5000 yuan, or causing the other side a loss of 10,000 yuan, is enough. One man used a purchased 12306 account to grab more than 200 tickets on other people's behalf and earned more than 20,000 yuan, and was sentenced to 1 year and 6 months suspended for 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Another sold ticket-grabbing software for a ticketing app on an online shop, was found to have engaged in unfair competition, and was ordered to pay 20,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Intruding into a computer information system, or using other technical means to obtain the data inside it, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, and especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years. The threshold for 「serious circumstances」 is illegal gains of more than 5000 yuan, or a loss of more than 10,000 yuan. The official cases: one man used a cheat to import purchased 12306 accounts in bulk and submitted ticket requests densely with many threads. He grabbed more than 200 train tickets on other people's behalf and made over 20,000 yuan. The court convicted him of the crime of illegally obtaining computer information system data and sentenced him to 1 year and 6 months suspended for 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Zheng sold ticket-grabbing software for a ticketing app on an online shop, was found to have engaged in unfair competition, and was ordered to pay 20,000 yuan (nationwide)

### 4. Let a crawler crawl only public pages that need no login, do not get round the anti-crawler measures, do not touch personal information, and do not sell what you crawl
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. A crawler is a program that turns web pages automatically and pulls the data off them. Before you crawl, read the robots file and the interface authorisation note; robots is the file a site puts in its own root directory saying which pages may not be crawled. Do not crawl data that needs a login, needs a token, which is a temporary access credential, or that can only be got by cracking a signature. The hard part is that technically this data is there for the taking once you start, and holding out depends on nothing but your own restraint.
- In plain language: Data that needs a login, a token or a cracked signature counts as getting round the protection, and carries 3 years or less, with 3 to 7 years where it is serious. Crawling personal information is more troublesome. 50 records of movement, communications, credit reporting or property count as serious circumstances. 500 records of accommodation, health or transactions, 5000 records of anything else, or 5000 yuan earned, are the same.
- Benefit: The sentencing for getting round the protection in order to take data out of a system is the same as in item 3 in this section (ticket-grabbing, flash-sale and order-brushing scripts): 3 years or less, or 3 to 7 years. Illegally obtaining, selling or providing citizens' personal information amounts to 「serious circumstances」 on any one of the following: more than 50 records of movement trajectories, communications content, credit reporting or property information; more than 500 records of accommodation, call records, health or transaction information; more than 5000 records of any other personal information; and illegal gains of more than 5000 yuan. Serious circumstances carry 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, and especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years. The official cases: the software developer Li disguised a function that stole photographs from people's phone galleries as an 「attractiveness detection」 app, got 1751 face photographs, and also sold social engineering database material; he was sentenced to 3 years suspended for 3 years. A social engineering database is a database that collects personal information leaked from many places. One internet technology company used a crawler to take shop and order data from a food delivery platform, causing a loss of more than 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of illegally obtaining computer information system data (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Exporting a table of users to an outside partner at work falls under that same sentence. 5000 records is only the size of a small spreadsheet.
### 5. Do not take development contracts for gambling, fraud or pornography apps, and do not set up payment channels or unblock accounts for a 「client」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The price is turning down that kind of outsourcing work, where the quote is high and the requirement is vague. The hard part is that what the other side pays is often well above the going rate.
- In plain language: Taking that kind of work, where the quote is high and the requirement is vague, makes you an accomplice to a gambling gang. It is punished as the crime of opening a gambling house, which carries 5 years or less and 5 to 10 years where it is serious, and that is heavier than the 3 years at the most you get for merely helping. In one esports prediction app, 28 people who wrote the code were sentenced to 1 year to 3 years and 6 months. Unblocking QQ accounts for a fraud gang brought 1 year and 6 months and a fine of 20,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Knowing that somebody is using the internet to commit a crime and still providing them with technical support, payment settlement or help of that kind, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead. Where it also constitutes another crime, it is punished as the heavier one. Opening a gambling house carries 5 years or less and a fine, and serious circumstances carry 5 to 10 years. The official cases: after the Zhangsheng group developed gambling games it 「sold the network on as a package」 and took site-building fees and reporting fees, with nearly 7 billion yuan involved. Zhang Sheng was sentenced to 9 years and 9 months, with a fine of 50 million yuan. The 79 people responsible for the outsourced development, including Wang Qiang, got between 6 months and 9 years and 9 months. Chen Dong and others developed and ran an esports prediction app, with the technical team writing code in Hainan, and 28 people were convicted of the crime of opening a gambling house with sentences of 3 years and 6 months down to 1 year, together with fines. Zhang opened a studio that unblocked QQ accounts frozen by a fraud gang, and was sentenced to 1 year and 6 months with a fine of 20,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Criminal Law, as amended by Criminal Law Amendment Eleven, 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十七条之二、第三百零三条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html> ; Supreme People's Procuratorate (2023). 建赌博网站并出售，涉案近 70 亿元. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202307/t20230711_621248.shtml> ; Supreme People's Procuratorate (2022). 上海闵行区：办理一起新型开设网络赌场案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202208/t20220805_569952.shtml> ; Supreme People's Procuratorate (2025). 依法惩治帮助信息网络犯罪活动及相关犯罪典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202507/t20250728_702377.shtml>
- Notes: 「Knowing」 does not require the other side to have spelled out the purpose. A quote plainly above the market rate, features that are top-up and withdrawal plus odds, and a client who will not say what the thing is for are all used, when a case is handled, to work out that you knew. Technical staff who develop gambling apps are handled as accomplices to the crime of opening a gambling house, and not under the crime of assisting information network criminal activity, which is capped at 3 years. The crime of assisting information network criminal activity is short for 「the crime of assisting information network criminal activity」.

### 6. Hand over your account access when you leave: do not delete the database, do not leave a back door and do not change the password to lock the system, even if the company owes you money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. If the company owes you wages, get them back through labour arbitration rather than doing it yourself (for labour arbitration see section 7). The hard part is holding back, while you are angry, from touching the system.
- In plain language: The account and password were given to you by the company, which does not mean you can still use them after you leave; logging in counts as illegal control and carries 3 years or less. Deleting data so that the system will not run carries 5 years or less, and especially serious consequences carry 5 years or more. One senior engineer who was dismissed used the original administrator account to log into the company server and delete data and logs, causing a loss of more than 120,000 yuan, and was sentenced to 3 years suspended for 5 years with a fine of 30,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling a computer information system, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, and especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years. Deleting or modifying the functions or data of a system so that it cannot run normally, where the consequences are serious, carries 5 years or less, and especially serious consequences carry 5 years or more. The official cases: after the senior IT engineer Ly was dismissed he logged into the company shared server with the original administrator account. He changed the password, deleted data and logs, and caused a loss of more than 120,000 yuan. He was sentenced to 3 years suspended for 5 years, with a fine of 30,000 yuan. Bai, an employee of a software company, deliberately put faulty code into an upgrade program, so that a customer's system could no longer be logged into, causing a loss of more than 40,000 yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of destroying a computer information system (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). The loss is worked out as the cost of restoring the data, and a few tens of thousands of yuan is enough to open a case.
### 7. Do not take source code, customer lists or technical documents away when you leave, do not upload them to your own cloud drive, and do not reuse them at your next employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you leave, delete the company material from your own computer and cloud drive. The hard part is that some of those files you wrote line by line yourself and you are reluctant to let them go.
- In plain language: Code you wrote yourself is owned by the company as far as copyright goes, and a customer list counts as a trade secret, so taking either is taking the company's property. One employee who left took the source code of a security screening system to a new company, and he was sentenced to 5 years and fined 500,000 yuan, while the new company was fined 4,000,000 yuan. Another exploited a flaw in a system to download more than 33,000 design files and was sentenced to 1 year and 10 months. The company can also bring a separate civil claim against you.
- Benefit: Breaching a confidentiality obligation by disclosing trade secrets or using them yourself, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, and especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 10 years together with a fine. The Anti-Unfair Competition Law also lists two things as infringement: taking trade secrets by means of 「electronic intrusion」, which is intruding into a system by technical means; and inciting or helping other people to breach a confidentiality obligation. The official cases: Sun and others, who had left the company, took the source code and core algorithms of a security screening system and used them in their new company's products; Sun was sentenced to 5 years and fined 500,000 yuan, and the new company was fined 4,000,000 yuan. Tian exploited a flaw in a system to download more than 33,000 design files and was sentenced to 1 year and 10 months, with a fine of 100,000 yuan. The engineer Yang saved the source code of an app module on a personal computer and in the cloud, and after changing jobs made a similar app, and was prosecuted for the crime of infringing trade secrets (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Taking it is taking the company's property. A customer list is business information and is likewise a trade secret. On top of any prison sentence, the company can bring a separate civil claim.
### 8. Do not run your own programs on somebody else's computer, server or camera, and do not mine cryptocurrency on company machines
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, and no time either.
- In plain language: Controlling somebody else's computer, server or camera without permission is a crime at 20 machines or more, carrying 3 years or less, and especially serious cases carry 3 to 7 years. Where it does not reach a crime, there is still up to 5 days of detention, and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan is possible. Somebody who has been punished criminally is barred for life from key posts in network security management and network operations.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling a computer information system, where the circumstances are serious, carries 3 years or less, with a fine imposed in addition or instead, and especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years. Illegally controlling 20 or more machines counts as 「serious circumstances」. Where it does not reach a crime, the police confiscate the illegal gains, detain the person for up to 5 days, and may impose a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan. Where the circumstances are relatively serious, detention is 5 to 15 days and a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 yuan may be imposed. For 5 years after an administrative penalty, and for life after a criminal penalty, a person may not take a key post in network security management or network operations. The official cases: Li bought the IP addresses of network cameras, used software to control 105 cameras to spy on people, and was sentenced to 3 years suspended for 5 years with a fine of 20,000 yuan. Wu and others planted a trojan on phone motherboards, controlled more than 330 余万 phones and sent verification codes back, and were sentenced to 2 years to 4 years and 6 months (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). ### 9. Do not test somebody else's system without written authorisation, and 「out of good intentions」 and 「reporting it afterwards」 are not reasons for acquittal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before joining a vendor's SRC or a public bug bounty, get a document that sets out the scope of the authorisation, the list of targets and the period you may test for. An SRC is the security emergency response centre the vendor runs itself, which takes reports of vulnerabilities from outside. Once you have confirmed that the vulnerability exists, stop. The hard part is giving up having 「a quick try while you are at it」.
- In plain language: Whether you are convicted depends on whether you had authorisation and how much you got, not on your intention. Getting 500 sets of ordinary account details, or earning 5000 yuan, or causing the other side a loss of 10,000 yuan, is enough for 3 years or less. To prove a vulnerability people often export a table of users, and one export easily passes 500 sets at a time. Having account details in hand and using them outside the scope of the authorisation is still intrusion.
- Benefit: The systems of ordinary companies and individuals are computer information systems outside the fields of state affairs, national defence construction and frontier science and technology. Intruding into such a system, or using other technical means to obtain the data stored, processed or transmitted in it, where the circumstances are serious, carries fixed-term imprisonment of not more than 3 years or criminal detention, with a fine imposed in addition or instead. Especially serious circumstances carry 3 to 7 years and a fine. There are several standards for 「serious circumstances」: more than 10 sets of identity authentication information for online financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading and futures trading, meaning account details and the like; more than 500 sets of other identity authentication information; illegal control of more than 20 computer information systems; and illegal gains of more than 5000 yuan, or an economic loss of more than 10,000 yuan. 5 times the figures above is 「especially serious」. The official case: Procuratorate Case No. 36 settled a sentence,「超出授权范围使用账号、密码登录计算机信息系统，属于侵入计算机信息系统的行为」. That means that where you had the account details to begin with but used them outside the scope of the authorisation, the act itself counts as intrusion. In that case three people used accounts, passwords and tokens they had access to at work, logged into the company's internal system from another city, downloaded data that was not within the scope of their work, and sold it online, making illegal gains of 37000 yuan. The three were sentenced to 4 years, 3 years and 9 months, and 4 years, and each was fined 40,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Criminal Law, as amended by Criminal Law Amendment Eleven, 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第一款、第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html> ; Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate (2011). - Notes: Procuratorate Case No. 36 is itself a case about selling data for profit. It is cited only for the sentence 「logging in outside the scope of your authorisation is still intrusion」, and not to say that good-faith testing is punished just as heavily. What you meant to do, and whether you reported it afterwards, cannot cancel the charge; they can only affect how heavy the punishment is and whether there is a prosecution. A vendor thanking you first and reporting you afterwards are not contradictory, and being thanked is not authorisation. While this section was being written, no good-faith testing case that could be checked word for word was found on the websites of the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate, so only the articles and the penalty standards are written out. If you are going to do this, go through a vendor's public SRC, or a written engagement for the assessment, and put the scope, the targets and the time window into the document. Prove the vulnerability with the least evidence, and do not take data in bulk. The band below a crime still brings an administrative penalty and a bar on the work, and the amount of the fine and the period are the same as in item 8 in this section (do not run your own programs on somebody else's machines). For what to do after finding a vulnerability, see item 10 in this section (report it as the rules require).

### 10. Report a vulnerability as the rules require: before the fix, do not publish the details, do not release an exploit and do not hand it to anybody overseas
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Report the vulnerability to the product provider, or to an official platform. Until the vendor has fixed it, do not publish the details and do not release verification code.
- In plain language: Individuals are covered by the Provisions on the Administration of Security Vulnerabilities in Network Products as well. Until the vendor has come up with a fix, you may not publish the vulnerability, and you may not publish the details of a vulnerability in a system that is in use. You may not release programs and tools made specifically to exploit the vulnerability, and when you publish you must say at the same time how to fix it. A vulnerability that has not been made public may not be handed to any overseas organisation or individual other than the product provider.
- Benefit: This set of provisions covers three kinds of party: network product providers and network operators inside China, and organisations or individuals engaged in discovering, collecting and publishing vulnerabilities. No organisation or individual may use a vulnerability to carry out activities endangering network security, and may not unlawfully collect, sell or publish vulnerability information. Publishing vulnerability information to the public has to keep to five rules. First, you may not publish before the product provider has provided a fix. Second, you may not publish details showing that a network, information system or device in use by a network operator has a vulnerability. Third, you may not deliberately exaggerate the danger and the risk, and may not use vulnerability information for malicious hype or fraud. Fourth, you may not publish or provide programs and tools made specifically for exploiting a vulnerability to carry out activities endangering network security. Fifth, when you publish, you must publish the fix or the precautions at the same time. In addition, vulnerability information that has not been made public may not be given to any overseas organisation or individual other than the product provider. The provisions encourage reporting vulnerabilities to four official platforms. First, the network security threat and vulnerability information sharing platform of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. Second, the vulnerability platform of the National Centre for Reporting Network and Information Security Information. Third, the vulnerability platform of the National Computer Network Emergency Technical Response Coordination Center. Fourth, the vulnerability database of the China Information Security Evaluation Center. Where vulnerability information is collected or published in breach of the provisions, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Public Security deal with it in accordance with their respective duties under the law. Where a situation provided for in the Cybersecurity Law is constituted, it is punished under that law. Publishing network security information such as system vulnerabilities to the public must comply with the relevant state provisions. A breach leads to an order to rectify and a warning from the competent department, and a fine of 10,000 yuan up to 100,000 yuan may be imposed. Where the breach is not rectified or the circumstances are serious, the fine is 100,000 yuan up to 1,000,000 yuan. It may also order the relevant business to be suspended, order a cessation of operations, or order the website or the application to be closed. It may also revoke the relevant business licence or the business licence. The person in charge who is directly responsible and the other persons directly responsible are fined 10,000 yuan up to 100,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Cyberspace Administration of China and Ministry of Public Security (工业和信息化部、国家互联网信息办公室、公安部) (2021). Provisions on the Administration of Security Vulnerabilities in Network Products, 网络产品安全漏洞管理规定（工信部联网安〔2021〕66 号，第二、四、九、十、十四条，2021 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5641351.htm> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2025). - Notes: The provisions put individuals squarely among the parties they cover, and 「I am only an amateur」 is not one of the exceptions. Submitting a vulnerability in a domestic system to an overseas bug bounty platform falls squarely under 「an unpublished vulnerability may not be given to anybody overseas」. Whether you are entitled to test at all is one question, and see item 9 in this section (do not test somebody else's system without written authorisation); what to do with a vulnerability once you have it is a separate question. A vulnerability found lawfully can still be punished for the way it is published, and a vulnerability found without authorisation is a problem both in the testing and in the publishing. Of the reporting channels, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology platform and CNCERT are the most used. The provisions do not make a separate category for 「the vendor has been notified and still does not fix it」; in practice you keep a record of the negotiation and the reporting, and report to an official platform as well.
### 11. Do not sell circumvention tools or VPN accounts, and do not set up this kind of node for other people
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, and no time either.
- In plain language: Setting up your own line out of the country, or using an international channel other than the public channels of the state, can lead to an order to stop connecting to the internet and a warning, a fine of up to 15,000 yuan and confiscation of the illegal gains. Making a business out of it can be handled by the case-handling authorities as the crime of illegal business operation, and serious circumstances carry 5 years or less. This item is about selling and setting up nodes. For your own use, the ceiling on the penalty is a warning plus a fine.
- Benefit: Connecting directly to an international network must use an international entry and exit channel provided by the public telecommunications network of the state. No unit or individual may set up or use any other channel: you may neither build your own line out of the country nor use one somebody else has built. A breach leads to an order from the police to stop connecting to the internet and a warning, and a fine of up to 15,000 yuan may be imposed in addition, with the illegal gains confiscated. Making a business out of it can be handled by the case-handling authorities as the crime of illegal business operation. ### 12. If you have signed a non-compete, send a written demand when the company does not pay the monthly compensation after you leave, and you can terminate it once 3 months have gone by; and for a post that never touched trade secrets you can ask the court to rule the clause has no effect
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When you leave, keep the original of the non-compete and the evidence that the company has not paid you month by month.
- In plain language: A non-compete means you may not go and work for a competitor for a period after you leave, at most 2 years, and the company has to pay compensation month by month. Where no amount is agreed, it is 30% of the average wage over the 12 months before you left, and not less than the local minimum wage. If the company goes 3 months without paying for its own reasons, you can ask for it to be terminated. From 2025, for a post where you never knew and never touched a trade secret, you can ask the court to rule that the clause has no effect.
- Benefit: A non-compete can bind only senior management, senior technical staff, and other people who are under a confidentiality obligation. The period is at most 2 years. The company must pay economic compensation month by month over that period. Where no compensation amount is agreed, it is 30% of the average wage over the 12 months before you left, and not less than the local minimum wage. Where the company goes 3 months without paying for its own reasons, the worker may ask for the non-compete to be terminated. Where the company terminates it of its own accord, it must pay an extra 3 months' compensation. From 2025, a worker in a post where they did not know and did not touch a trade secret may ask the court to rule that the clause has no effect. Where the agreed scope goes beyond the part of the secret they actually knew, the part beyond it is void. The official case: a pharmaceutical company sued its chief technology officer Zheng for 7,100,000 yuan in liquidated damages. The court found that the products of his new employer were not substitutes for those of the original company, that the scope of the non-compete should be limited to the secrets he actually knew, and dismissed all the claims (nationwide)

- Notes: The other way round, where the agreement is valid, the company has paid month by month, and you still go to work for a competitor, you have to pay the liquidated damages as agreed. ### 13. Code written in working hours or with company resources belongs to the company; make your own open-source project with your own time and your own equipment, and do not mix in company code
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Read the intellectual property clause in the employment contract when you join. The hard part is that personal work and the company's work often share one computer and have to be kept apart.
- In plain language: Software written mainly with the company's equipment, intranet or business data, and for which the company carries the responsibility, is owned by the company as far as copyright goes; you keep only the right of authorship, and where the contract says so it is the same. Code you write after work is the company's too, if you used a company computer or business data. An invention made with company resources belongs to the company as far as the patent goes.
- Benefit: A work created to complete a task of the unit is a work of employment, that is, something made while doing your job. In the general case the copyright belongs to the author, the unit has the right of priority use within the scope of its business, and for 2 years the author may not licence a third party to use it in the same way. But there are two kinds of situation that are different. One is works such as engineering designs and software created mainly using the material and technical conditions of the unit and bearing its responsibility. The other is works that the contract agrees belong to the unit. In these two kinds the author keeps only the right of authorship. The material and technical conditions of the unit means the company's equipment, intranet and business data. An invention created by carrying out a task of the unit, or created mainly by using the material and technical conditions of the unit, is a service invention, and both the right to apply for a patent and the patent obtained belong to the unit (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2020). Copyright Law, 著作权法（2020 年修正，第十八条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2020). Patent Law, 专利法（2020 年修正，第六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308800.html>
- Notes: Software written straight into the category of 「copyright belongs to the unit」 almost never falls, for a programmer, into the category of 「copyright belongs to the author, and the unit has priority use for two years」. 「Written after work」 still counts as being made mainly with the equipment and materials of the unit as soon as a company computer, intranet or company business data is used. To keep a personal project clean, use your own machine, do not touch the company repository, and do not overlap with the company's business.
### 14. When you build a product on GPL and other open-source code, open the source as the licence requires or change the licence; do not treat it as 「nobody polices this」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you use somebody else's open-source code, read its licence once (the conditions the author has written down). The GPL family requires a product that uses it to release its own source as well, which clashes with selling closed-source software; where it clashes, change to another one. The hard part is giving up a library you have got used to.
- In plain language: Infringing copyright is made good by the other party's loss or by the money you earned, and where the infringement is deliberate and the circumstances serious the damages can reach 1 to 5 times that. One company hired a competitor's former employee and produced substantially similar software, arguing that the other party's software was under GPLv2 and should have been open source. The Supreme People's Court held that whether the other party had opened its source made no difference to its copyright, and still ordered the infringement to stop and awarded 500,000 yuan in damages.
- Benefit: For infringement of copyright, the damages are the right holder's actual loss, or the money the infringer earned. Where neither can be worked out, the figure is set by reference to what a normal purchase of that licence would cost. Where the infringement is deliberate and the circumstances serious, damages of 1 to 5 times may be awarded. Where the public interest is harmed, the illegal gains may also be confiscated and a fine imposed. Official case: (2021) Supreme People's Court Intellectual Property Chamber Final No. 51. The defendant hired the plaintiff's former employee and developed gateway software substantially similar to the plaintiff's software. The defendant argued that the plaintiff's software was based on GPLv2 and should have been open source in the first place. The Supreme People's Court held that 「开发者自身是否违反 GPLv2 协议和是否享有软件著作权是两个独立问题」, that is, whether the developer has himself breached the GPLv2 agreement and whether he holds the software copyright are two separate questions, and ordered the infringement to stop with 500,000 yuan in damages (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2020). Copyright Law, 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十三、五十四条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html> ; Supreme People's Court Intellectual Property Tribunal (最高人民法院知识产权法庭) (2022). Copyright infringement case involving open-source software（（2021）最高法知民终 51 号）. <https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3042.html>
- Notes: What the case shows is that 「the other side used GPL code」 is not a reason for you to copy it. The other way round, there is already a binding judgment in China from a GPL author suing a commercial user that had not opened the source, but this section did not find the text on the Supreme People's Court website, so nothing is cited. The grade is B because keeping to an open-source licence can only be governed through contracts and the general provisions of the Copyright Law; there is no dedicated article.

### 15. Collect only the personal information your app genuinely needs, get a guardian's consent for anyone under 14, and write the privacy policy clearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you ship, go through the list of permissions the app asks for alongside the privacy policy, and leave out any permission you do not need. The hard part is that the product side always wants to keep a little more data 「that might come in useful later」.
- In plain language: Collect only the information the business genuinely needs, and get a guardian's consent for anyone under 14. A breach first brings an order to rectify, and the app may be taken down. Refusing to rectify brings a fine of up to 1,000,000 yuan, and the person in charge 10,000 to 100,000 yuan. Serious cases bring a fine of up to 50,000,000 yuan or 5% of the previous year's turnover, and 100,000 to 1,000,000 yuan for the person in charge, and the people who write the code and build the product may count as the person in charge.
- Benefit: Processing personal information must have a clear and reasonable purpose, must be directly related to that purpose, and must use the method that affects the individual's rights and interests least. Processing personal information requires the individual's consent, or must fall within a statutory situation. For anyone under 14 years of age, the consent of a parent or guardian must be obtained. A breach leads the regulator to order rectification, give a warning and confiscate the illegal gains, and to order the offending app to suspend or stop providing its service. Refusing to rectify brings a fine of up to 1,000,000 yuan, and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan on the person in charge who is directly responsible and the other persons directly responsible. Serious circumstances bring a fine of up to 50,000,000 yuan, or up to 5% of the previous year's turnover, and 100,000 to 1,000,000 yuan for those responsible. Those people may also be barred for a set period from being directors, supervisors or senior executives of the relevant companies. Where selling or providing personal information amounts to a crime, see item 4 in this section (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2021). Personal Information Protection Law, 个人信息保护法（第六、十三、三十一、六十六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html>
- Notes: In practice,「the person directly responsible」very often lands on the people who write the code and build the product. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology regularly reports apps taken down for breaches; this section did not verify which particular batch of reports it was, so nothing is cited.

### 16. Before a website or app goes live, do the ICP filing first, and keep the logs for 6 months or more as the graded protection rules require
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The filing is free, and going through your cloud provider's process takes about a few weeks. The graded protection assessment is charged according to the level. Graded protection (等保) is a set of security measures the state grades by how important the system is and then requires a website or system to put in place; a small site generally does not need the assessment.
- In plain language: The filing costs nothing and the process through your cloud provider takes about a few weeks; a server inside China with no filing gets the site shut down. A filing that says it charges nothing but in fact charges a fee is fined 100,000 to 1,000,000 yuan. The logs have to be kept for a full 6 months, and a refusal to put it right, or consequences following from it, is fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Commercial internet information services are run under a licence, and non-commercial ones under a filing. Without a licence, or without a filing, you may not carry on at all. Where there is no filing, the telecommunications authority orders it to be put right, and where it is not put right the website is ordered closed. Operating without a licence brings confiscation of the illegal gains and a fine of 3 to 5 times the illegal gains, or a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 yuan. A website whose home page does not show the filing number is fined 5,000 to 50,000 yuan. A network operator has to work out security management rules under the graded protection system (等保), guard against attacks, keep network logs for not less than 6 months, and classify, back up and encrypt its data. Failure to comply brings an order to put it right and a warning. A refusal to put it right, or consequences following from it, is fined 10,000 to 100,000 yuan, and the person in charge 5,000 to 50,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

### 17. Before offering a generative AI service to the public, do the security assessment and the algorithm filing as the rules require, and label the generated content
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money, but you have to go through the process at the cyberspace administration department, which takes some time. A small tool of your own that is not open to the public does not trigger any of this.
- In plain language: A generative AI service aimed at the public that can affect public opinion or mobilise people needs a security assessment and an algorithm filing before it goes live, and if you change it or shut it down you have to do the change and the cancellation. Generated images and video have to carry a label. A small tool for your own use that is not open to the public does not have to do any of this. A breach is punished under the Cybersecurity Law and the other laws.
- Benefit: A provider of generative artificial intelligence services with the attributes of public opinion or the capacity for social mobilisation must carry out a security assessment in accordance with state provisions, and must go through the algorithm filing under the algorithm recommendation provisions, together with the later change and cancellation formalities. The attributes of public opinion or the capacity for social mobilisation mean being able to affect how the public sees something, or being able to organise and mobilise people. The provider must label generated content such as images and video. The measures came into force on 2023-8-15 (nationwide)
