# 21. 出国、旅行与境外安全

这一节算的是钱和人身自由，有几条还关系到人身安全。到了国外，管你的是所在国的法律，不是中国的。中国使领馆能帮你到哪一步，有明确的界线。

# 21. Travel and safety abroad

This section measures money and personal freedom, and several of the items also bear on personal safety. Once you are abroad, the law that governs you is that of the country you are in, not of China. There are clear limits to how far a Chinese embassy or consulate can go for you.

### 1. Check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs safety notices once before you leave, and do not go anywhere marked 「暂勿前往」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Open the Chinese Consular Affairs website and go through it once. It takes a few minutes.
- In plain language: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs sorts overseas safety risk into four bands, and the highest is 「暂勿前往」, do not travel. From 15 September 2026 the duty to stay away from high-risk countries and regions was written into an administrative regulation. When you apply for your documents and go through exit formalities, staff will also remind you, and where necessary try to talk you out of the trip. Read that page once before you leave. It takes a few minutes.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs publishes overseas safety notices in public, banded by risk level. From low to high they are 「注意安全」, take care of your safety, 「加强安全防范」, strengthen your precautions, 「谨慎前往」, travel with caution, and 「暂勿前往」, do not travel. The new rules, in force from 2026-9-15, also write the duty of the individual into administrative regulations. The text reads: 「中国公民应当关注国外安全提醒和旅游目的地安全风险提示，避免前往及驻留高风险国家或者地区」, that is, Chinese citizens should pay attention to overseas safety notices and safety risk warnings for travel destinations, and avoid travelling to and staying in high-risk countries or regions. The new rules also require the immigration authorities, the departments that issue entry and exit documents and run border checks, to remind anyone heading for a high-risk area when that person applies for documents and goes through the exit check. That requirement reads: 「对准备前往风险等级为最高级别或者严重危及人身安全案件突发高发的国家或者地区的中国公民，必要时应当劝阻其前往」, that is, for Chinese citizens preparing to travel to a country or region at the highest risk level, or where cases seriously endangering personal safety break out suddenly and often, the authorities should try to stop them going where necessary.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry, 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm> (Article two); State Council (国务院) (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance, 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Articles nineteen and twenty-one); Consular Affairs Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Safety notices, 安全提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/>
- Notes: The notices change with the situation, so this book does not copy the list. Check that page yourself before you travel. As of 7 September 2026 the column still carried these 「暂勿前往」 notices: Eswatini (2026-08-25), Palestine (2023-10-10), Sudan (2023-04-17). Four more were Afghanistan (2023-02-17), Syria (2023-01-04), Peru (2022-12-29) and Somalia (2022-11-24). There are also separate notices for northern Myanmar and for some provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Travel agencies have a duty to inform you as well. Before joining a group tour you can ask for a written statement of the risk at the destination.

### 2. Save 12308 and your local consular protection number in your phone, and copy them onto paper in your wallet
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Save two numbers and write one copy down. A few minutes.
- In plain language: There is one number to call when something goes wrong. In China, dial 12308. Abroad, dial +86-10-12308. Since the hotline opened it has taken more than 3 million calls. Over the same period the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and its missions abroad handled more than half a million consular protection cases.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs runs a global consular protection and assistance emergency line, answered 24 hours a day. In China, dial 12308. Abroad, dial +86-10-12308 or +86-10-65612308. Since the line opened in 2014 it has taken more than 300 million calls. Over the same period the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and its missions abroad handled more than 50 × 10,000 consular protection cases of every kind
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Chinese Consulate-General in Myanmar (外交部、中国驻缅甸使领馆) (2023). Consular reminder on the current security situation in northern Myanmar, 关于当前缅北地区安全局势的领事提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/202311/t20231109_11176682.shtml>; State Council Information Office (国务院新闻办) (2023). The State Council's regular policy briefing on consular protection and assistance. <https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2023zccfh/11/index.htm>
- Notes: The local embassy or consulate has its own consular protection number, and it is usually faster than the switchboard. Before you leave, look up the country on the Chinese Consular Affairs website and save that number alongside the switchboard number. If your phone is lost or runs flat, numbers saved only on it are no use. So write a copy on paper and keep it in your wallet, or give a copy to someone at home.

### 3. Know what consular protection can and cannot do: it can visit you, it cannot get you out, and you pay the bill
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. It takes a few minutes to read this boundary clearly.
- In plain language: If you are arrested abroad, a consulate can do this: check the situation with the local authorities, ask for handling that is lawful and fair, visit you, and observe the trial. It cannot change the verdict, and it cannot take you out of the country. Accommodation, transport and medical costs paid by someone else on your behalf come back to you in the end. So put the money and the insurance in place before you travel, and do not build a plan on the idea that the state will cover you if something happens.
- Benefit: When you are arrested, a consulate can do these things. It checks the situation with the relevant departments of the country you are in and asks for handling that is lawful and fair. The regulation reads: 「按照驻在国法律和我国与驻在国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约对其进行探视或者与其联络，了解其相关需求，要求驻在国有关部门给予该中国公民人道主义待遇和公正待遇」. In plain terms, it visits you and gets in touch with you under the local law and under the treaties signed by the two countries, asks what you need, and asks the local authorities to treat that Chinese citizen humanely and fairly. At the trial a consulate may sit in and may ask that your procedural rights be protected. If you are injured or caught in a disaster, it can press the local authorities for emergency help and can help the family handle what follows. On costs the regulation is just as clear: 「中国公民、法人、非法人组织在领事保护与协助过程中，得到第三方提供的食宿、交通、医疗等物资和服务的，应当支付应由其自身承担的费用」. Accommodation, transport and medical care paid for you by a third party are yours to pay
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance, 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第九、十、十四、二十六条）
- Notes: A consulate does not pay your fines, does not pay your medical bills and does not buy your ticket, and it cannot change the conclusion of a court in the country you are in. What it can give you is information on local lawyers, translators, medical care and funeral services, and help reaching relatives and finding relief. So put the money and the insurance in place before you travel, and do not build a plan on the idea that the state will cover you if something happens.

### 4. Buy a policy that covers overseas medical treatment and medical evacuation, not only flight-delay cover
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A short-term overseas travel policy costs a few tens to a few hundred yuan.
- In plain language: You pay for being treated abroad yourself. Chinese medical insurance basically does not cover it, and the consulate does not advance the money. What can bankrupt a family is not the clinic bill but the hospital stay after a serious injury, and getting the patient home, which takes an air ambulance or a medical escort. When you buy travel insurance, look at two limits: overseas medical expenses, and emergency medical evacuation and repatriation.
- Benefit: You pay for medical treatment abroad yourself. Chinese medical insurance basically does not reimburse it abroad. Consular protection does not advance this money either (see item 3 in this section, what consular protection can and cannot do, based on Article twenty-six of the regulations). What actually bankrupts a family is not the clinic bill but the hospital stay after a serious injury and the medical evacuation. Moving a patient from a foreign hospital back to China takes an air ambulance or a medical escort, and it is among the most expensive self-paid items. The US CDC puts it this way: moving a patient in an emergency from a remote area to a good hospital can, if you pay for it yourself, cost more than $100,000. When you buy insurance, look at two limits first. One is the limit on overseas medical expenses. The other is the limit on emergency medical evacuation and repatriation, which covers the most that will be paid for transferring to another hospital after an accident and for getting the patient home
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance, 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第二十六条）; US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (美国疾病控制与预防中心). Travelers' Health: Travel Insurance：「Medical evacuation insurance covers emergency transportation from a remote area to a high-quality hospital, which could otherwise cost more than $100,000.」. <https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/insurance>; the rest is the author's own experience, no direct literature
- Notes: The figure of $100,000 is a floor, not a range of costs. No official or industry published range of costs was found, so this book gives no specific range. Confirm three things before you buy. First, whether the country you are going to is covered. Second, whether high-risk activities such as diving, skiing and motorcycling are excluded. Third, whether the insurer settles with the hospital directly or pays you back after you pay up front. Buying a policy is just as necessary for visa-free countries.

### 5. Treat every 「境外高薪招聘」 offer as fraud, and know that being tricked into telecom fraud can cost you the right to leave the country
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is turning down with your own hand a job that looks very good.
- In plain language: A monthly wage so absurd it cannot be true, no questions about your education or experience, no proper contract, being told to gather in a border city first, and someone else keeping your passport. Take a job like that and neither you nor your passport is in your own hands. If you falsify your papers on the way out, border control will stop you and not let you go. Take part in crime abroad, and once you are back you can be barred from leaving the country for 6 months to 3 years.
- Benefit: The official description of these cases is blunt. In recent years there have been cases of 「上当受骗出境或者虚构事由非法出境从事跨境赌博、电信网络诈骗等违法活动，严重危害人民群众生命和财产安全」, that is, people deceived into leaving the country, or leaving illegally on a made-up pretext, to engage in cross-border gambling, telecom network fraud and other illegal activity there, seriously endangering the lives and property of the people. That covers both being tricked into going and inventing a reason to slip out to do gambling and telecom fraud. The new rules tighten both ends of this. On the way out: 「申请出境入境、停留居留的事由应当真实、合法」, that is, the stated reason for applying to leave or enter the country or to stay there must be true and lawful. If you submit false documents or tell lies, the immigration authorities may refuse to issue you a document and may refuse to let you leave. On the way back: 「中国公民在境外从事违法犯罪活动，危害国家安全和利益的，可以……决定自回国之日起 6 个月至 3 年以内不准其出境」, that is, where a Chinese citizen commits illegal or criminal acts abroad that endanger national security and interests, the authorities may decide, counting from the date of return, to bar that person from leaving the country for 6 months to 3 years. Where someone is given administrative detention for obtaining documents fraudulently or for leaving or entering the country illegally, the bar is likewise 6 months to 3 years
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry, 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、四条）; Ministry of Justice, Ministry of Public Security and National Immigration Administration (司法部、公安部、国家移民局) (2026). Questions and Answers on the Provisions of the State Council on the Administration of Exit and Entry. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202608/content_7077270.htm>
- Notes: The warning signs are more or less fixed: a monthly wage far above the same job, no requirement for education or experience, no proper contract, being told to gather in a border city first, visa handling done for you and your passport kept by them. There is already a dedicated consular reminder for northern Myanmar. If you do go, your personal freedom and your passport are in someone else's hands and you cannot leave when you want to.

### 6. Cash withdrawals abroad are capped at 100,000 yuan a year, counted across every card in your own name
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Estimate before you leave how much cash this trip needs, and use cards for the rest. The hard part is changing the habit: if a card will do, do not take out cash.
- In plain language: The cap on cash withdrawals abroad is not one card at a time. It is every bank card in your name, supplementary cards included, added together, at 100,000 yuan for each calendar year. Once you have used it up, you cannot withdraw cash abroad for the rest of that year or in the year after. Only withdrawals stop. Card payments are not affected, and they do not use up your annual quota of 50,000 US dollars for purchasing foreign exchange.
- Benefit: The basis is the Notice of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange on Regulating Large Cash Withdrawal Transactions with Bank Cards Abroad, 《关于规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易的通知》（汇发〔2017〕29 号，in force from 1 January 2018）. Article one: 「个人持境内银行卡在境外提取现金，本人名下银行卡（含附属卡）合计每个自然年度不得超过等值 10 万元人民币。超过年度额度的，本年及次年将被暂停持境内银行卡在境外提取现金」, that is, where a person withdraws cash abroad with a domestic bank card, the total across all bank cards in that person's name, supplementary cards included, must not exceed the equivalent of 100,000 yuan in a calendar year. Where the annual cap is exceeded, withdrawals abroad on domestic bank cards are suspended for that year and the year after. The second paragraph of the same article: 「个人不得通过借用他人银行卡或出借本人银行卡等方式规避或协助规避境外提取现金管理」, that is, a person may not borrow another person's bank card or lend out their own to get around, or to help someone else get around, the management of cash withdrawals abroad. Article two: the foreign exchange authority sends the suspension list to the issuing banks every day, and an issuing bank must, no later than 17 hrs Beijing time on that day, stop the people on the list from withdrawing cash abroad with that bank's cards. Article three: a person on the suspension list may take valid identity documents to a local branch of the foreign exchange authority and check their own record of cash withdrawals abroad. Article four: the daily cap per foreign currency card is raised from the equivalent of USD 1000 to the equivalent of 10,000 yuan; the cap for renminbi cards stays at the equivalent of 10,000 yuan per card per day. Article six: issuing banks, renminbi card clearing organisations in China and individuals who breach the provisions of this notice are punished under the relevant rules of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Administration. The foreign exchange authority's answers to reporters also made several points clear. The Notice does not affect spending abroad on a card. Ordinary transactions such as food, accommodation, transport and shopping on a trip abroad for tourism, business or study can all be paid by card. Those payments do not use up the individual facilitation annual quota of USD 50,000 for purchasing foreign exchange. The annual quota is worked out by the foreign exchange authority from data added up across the issuing financial institutions, because real-time control would slow transactions down: 「外汇局采用延时控制手段」, that is, the authority uses deferred control rather than stopping the payment on the spot. Separately, in 2016, 81% of cash withdrawals abroad on domestic bank cards were below 30,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局) (2017). Notice on Regulating Large Cash Withdrawal Transactions with Bank Cards Abroad, 关于规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易的通知（汇发〔2017〕29 号, Articles one, two, three, four, six and seven, in force from 1 January 2018）. <https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/21873.html>; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局) (2017). Answers by the responsible officials of the foreign exchange authority to reporters on the questions of regulating large cash withdrawal transactions with bank cards abroad. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2017-12/31/content_5251958.htm>; China UnionPay International (中国银联国际). ATM cash withdrawals abroad, 境外ATM取款. <https://m.unionpayintl.com/wap/cn/serviceCenter/cardUsingInstructions/805.shtml>
- Notes: The cap is counted by calendar year and starts again on 1 January. Only withdrawals abroad stop; card payments are not affected. If you really need a lot of cash abroad, buy the foreign exchange first under the Administrative Measures on Foreign Exchange of Individuals, then take the foreign currency notes out of the country. Withdrawal itself has two traps, and the UnionPay International page 「境外ATM取款」 sets them out. First, a dual-logo card cannot use the UnionPay channel at some ATMs in some countries. Second, some overseas ATMs show a surcharge on the screen, and that money goes to the local acquirer and is 「与银联无关」, nothing to do with UnionPay. The same page also warns: 「由于信用卡取款需支付透支利息，建议持卡人境外取款使用银联借记卡」, that is, because a credit card withdrawal incurs overdraft interest, holders are advised to use a UnionPay debit card for cash withdrawals abroad. If the card is lost, swallowed or used fraudulently, see item 5 in section 14 (report the card lost and freeze it before calling the police).

### 7. Photograph your passport, visa and identity card and keep a copy in the cloud, and if they are lost, report first and then get a travel document
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Take a few photographs before you leave.
- In plain language: If your passport is lost, a consulate can issue you a travel document for the journey home. You have to be able to prove who you are first. With photographs of the data page, of the visa pages and of your entry and exit records to hand, the replacement goes through much faster. You also have to report the loss locally and get a receipt, which the exit check and the insurance claim will both ask for. Phones are often lost together with the passport, so do not keep the photographs only on the phone.
- Benefit: If your passport is lost, the embassy or consulate abroad can issue you a travel document for the journey home, but you have to prove your identity first. With clear photographs of the data page, of the visa pages and of your entry and exit records to hand, the difference in how long the replacement takes is large. At the same time you have to report the loss locally and get a receipt. Many countries' exit checks and insurance claims ask for this document. The way into the consulate is the two consular protection numbers you saved in item 2 in this section
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance, 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十六条）; the author's own experience, no direct literature
- Notes: How to store them: take the photographs and put them in two different places, for example one copy in cloud storage and one sent to a relative. Print a paper copy as well and keep it away from the original. Photograph your bank cards and the prescriptions for the medicines you use regularly too. A phone and a passport are usually lost together, so storing them only on the phone is the same as not storing them.

### 8. Before driving abroad, check whether the destination recognises a Chinese licence; most 「国际驾照」 sold online are waste paper
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Check the rules of your destination once before you leave.
- In plain language: The 「国际驾照」 arranged for you online has no standing in mainland China. That document is a translation recognised between the parties to the 1949 Geneva convention, and mainland China is not among the 103 parties. So whoever arranges one for you in China gives you a document with no legal effect. Whether you may drive on a Chinese licence depends on the destination's own rules. If you have not confirmed those rules, do not drive.
- Benefit: The so-called international driving permit (IDP) is a translation recognised between the parties to the 1949 Convention on Road Traffic. The convention was concluded at Geneva on 1949-9-19 and entered into force on 1952-3-26. There are 103 parties at present, that is, the countries and regions that have signed. Mainland China is not on that list. So China does not issue this kind of international driving permit. Any agency offering to arrange an 「国际驾照」 inside China produces no legal effect. Whether you may drive on a Chinese licence depends on the rules of the destination country or region itself: some recognise a Chinese licence with a notarised translation, some require you to exchange it for a local licence, and some do not recognise it at all
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: United Nations Treaty Collection. Convention on Road Traffic, Geneva, 19 September 1949 — status of treaties. <https://treaties.un.org/pages/ViewDetailsV.aspx?src=TREATY&mtdsg_no=XI-B-1&chapter=11&clang=_en>
- Notes: In most countries driving without a licence is a criminal offence, or it carries a heavy penalty. And if there is an accident the insurance will very likely refuse to pay, which is the real cost. There are two ways to check: look up the destination country's 「中国公民须知」 on the Chinese Consular Affairs website, or ask the car hire company for a written confirmation. Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and some countries have separate arrangements.

### 9. Before you use an agency for a visa, study abroad or immigration, ask whether it is registered with the immigration authorities
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask once whether it is registered, and keep the written material.
- In plain language: From 15 September 2026, agencies that handle visas, study abroad and immigration have to register with the immigration authorities. Phrases like 「包过」, guaranteed approval, 「保签」, guaranteed visa, and 「材料我们帮你做」, we prepare the documents for you, are themselves expressly forbidden. Filling in a form wrongly yourself costs you at most a refusal this time round. Using false documents and being found to have submitted false information leaves a mark on your entry and exit record for years to come.
- Benefit: The licensing requirement for private entry and exit agencies was removed in 2018, so an agency no longer needs an approval before it opens. The number of agencies then grew fast, and problems appeared that 「机构底数不清、部分机构违法违规办理业务、损害出境入境人员合法权益」, that is, the true number of agencies is unclear, and some agencies handle business in breach of the law and damage the lawful rights of people entering and leaving the country. The authorities cannot even count them. From 2026-9-15 these agencies are put under registration. An agency must report to the immigration authorities for the place where it sits within 15 days of being set up. Agencies already operating must make up the registration within 90 days of the rules coming into force. The expressly forbidden practices fall into these categories. First, 「发布虚假信息，或者通过夸大宣传、误导性宣传等方式招徕服务对象」, publishing false information, or attracting clients by exaggerating or misleading publicity. Second, 「提供或者协助提供虚假材料，协助他人违规办理签证、停留居留证件、护照等出境入境证件或者手续」, supplying or helping to supply false documents, and helping others obtain visas, stay and residence documents, passports and other entry and exit documents or formalities in breach of the rules. Third, 「泄露、出售、非法提供……个人隐私或者个人信息」, disclosing, selling or unlawfully providing personal privacy information or personal information. Fourth, 「组织或者协助他人从事跨境违法犯罪活动」, organising or helping others carry out cross-border illegal and criminal activity. Overseas enterprises and institutions may not provide this kind of agency service inside China.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry, 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第七、八、十、十二、十三条）
- Notes: An agency that keeps saying 「包过」, guaranteed approval, 「保签」, guaranteed visa, and 「材料我们帮你做」, we prepare the documents for you, is already doing what is expressly forbidden. If it is caught, the mark is left on your visa record. A problem with an application you filled in yourself costs you at most a refusal this time round. Using false documents and being found to have submitted false information costs a good deal more. Policy advice and information lookup that are not done for money do not count as agency service.

### 10. Do not write an invitation letter for a stranger, and do not let anyone handle entry and exit formalities in your name
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is turning down a favour to someone's face.
- In plain language: If you write an invitation letter or application documents for someone else and the contents are untrue, the person who wrote it is the one who is fined. An individual is fined 5000 yuan to 10,000 yuan, and any illegal gains are confiscated as well. An organisation is fined 10,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan, and the person who handled it is fined 5000 yuan to 10,000 yuan on top. One favour ends up costing you the money yourself.
- Benefit: The person who issues an invitation letter is responsible for whether its contents are true. The text reads: 「个人为他人申请出境入境、停留居留出具虚假邀请函件或者其他申请材料的，由移民管理机构处 5000 元以上 1 万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得」, that is, where an individual issues a false invitation document or other application document for another person's exit, entry, stay or residence, the immigration authority shall impose a fine of 5000 yuan to 10,000 yuan, and any illegal gains shall be confiscated. Where an organisation issues false documents, the fine is 10,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan. At the same time a further fine of 5000 yuan to 10,000 yuan is imposed on the person in charge and the other people directly responsible, that is, the manager who signs it off and the person who handled it.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2026). Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry, 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、十一条）
- Notes: This is the same kind of risk as 「不帮陌生人带东西」 in section 8: once documents are issued in your name, the consequences land on you. Where a public servant or a member of the armed forces uses an agency to get foreign nationality or overseas residence status in breach of the rules, the agency may not only refuse; it also has to report the matter to the supervisory authority.

### 11. Before you go abroad, send your itinerary, address and travelling companions to one family member and agree how often you will make contact
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Send one message before you leave.
- In plain language: If a person goes missing abroad, family members can ask an embassy or consulate for help. The consulate will tell the family how to report to the police locally, and once a case is opened it will chase it. All of that depends on someone at home knowing where you should be and when you should make contact. Sending your flight number, address, the name and phone number of a travelling companion and the contact interval you agreed is enough.
- Benefit: When a Chinese citizen goes missing abroad, relatives may ask a diplomatic mission abroad for help. The regulation reads: 「驻外外交机构应当提供当地报警方式及其他获取救助的信息」, that is, diplomatic missions abroad shall provide the way to report to the police locally and other information for obtaining assistance. Once the local police have opened a case, the consulate presses them to deal with it in time. Whether any of this can be started depends on whether someone at home knows where you should be and when you should make contact.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2023). Regulations on Consular Protection and Assistance, 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十三条）; the author's own experience, no direct literature
- Notes: Four things are enough: the flight number, the address, the names and phone numbers of the people you travel with, and the contact frequency you agreed. If you are going somewhere with poor signal or poor public order, say plainly how long you can go without news before someone reports you. All of the rescue procedures above can only start once a family member knows your itinerary.
