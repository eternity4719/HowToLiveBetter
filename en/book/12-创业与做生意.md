# 12. 创业与做生意：别把家底赔进去

这一节只算钱和法律责任，不折算成寿命。开一家店、办一家公司，哪一步会把你自己的钱、家里的钱、配偶的钱一起赔进去？法律怎么规定，能不能提前隔开？最容易把家底赔进去、又最省事就能避开的条目，放在最前面。所以签字类的条目靠前，要自己动手做的方法靠后。只凭经验写、没有文献支撑的，标 C 级。

# 12. Starting a business: do not lose the family savings

This section counts money and legal liability, and is not converted into lifespan. When you open a shop or incorporate a company, which step puts your own money, your family's money and your spouse's money at risk together? What does the law say, and can you keep them apart in advance? The items that most easily lose the family savings and are also the easiest to avoid come first. So the items about signing papers come early, and the methods that need you to do the work yourself come later. Anything written from experience alone, with no literature behind it, is graded C.

### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose, and do not touch the family savings or borrow to open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you start, set yourself a number: losing all of it would not affect your mortgage payments, your children or your medical care. Do not invest more than that. Money from family members and borrowed money do not count towards that number. The hard part is holding back from putting in more when the business looks like it is taking off.
- In plain language: A sole proprietor's debts are paid out of the money and property in your own name. If it is not clear whether the business was run by the whole family, the family's assets are used. Incorporating a company is not a safe harbour either: when the company cannot pay a debt, the capital you promised to contribute and have not yet paid in can be demanded from you early. So decide how much to put in on the basis of 「all of it lost and I still pay the mortgage and my medical bills」.
- Benefit: Losing is not only about the money. Where a sole proprietorship business owes debts and the business was run by you alone, payment comes from the money and property in your own name (personal liability, 以个人财产承担). If the business was run by the whole family, or it is not clear who ran it, payment comes from the family's money and property (family liability, 以家庭财产承担). A debt signed by both husband and wife is a joint debt that both of them repay. One spouse's later agreement counts too. Money one spouse borrowed in their own name is still repaid by both, as long as the creditor can show the money went into a business the two of them ran together. On the company side there is a parallel rule: when the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, a creditor may demand early payment of the capital the shareholder subscribed to within the registered capital but has not yet paid in by the due date (认缴出资) (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2023). Company Law, 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: 「亏得起」 means this: if the money is lost entirely, you still do not have to sell your home or borrow from relatives to plug the hole. For how many businesses survive, and for how many years on average, no verifiable official statistics could be found on the National Bureau of Statistics (国家统计局) or State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) websites this time. So the text gives no figures and only the legal consequences. That is why the grade is B.

### 2. Do not sign a personal guarantee for company loans, and never let your spouse sign one either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. When a bank, a supplier or a landlord asks you to sign a 「股东个人连带担保」 or asks for 「配偶签字」, stop for a moment. Signing that page means you personally give up the protection that 「the company's debts do not reach the individual」 (giving up limited liability). Do not sign if you can avoid it. If you must sign, sign only a 「一般保证」 and write down the largest amount you are guaranteeing. The hard part is that the other side often says 「no signature, no loan」. You have to hold out against that.
- In plain language: What you signed is a 「连带责任保证」: when the company cannot pay, the creditor can bypass the company and come straight to you. With a 「一般保证」, the creditor has to sue the company or go to arbitration first, and only what is still missing after the company's assets are sold off comes to you. If your spouse signs as well, both families' assets go in together, and for you personally the 「limited」 in a limited liability company stops counting.
- Benefit: The sentence on the guarantee document means exactly this: 「the debtor does not pay, and you pay instead」. Where it is written as a 「连带责任保证」, the creditor does not have to approach the company and can come to your door. Where it is written as a 「一般保证」, the creditor has to sue the company or apply for arbitration first, then ask the court to sell off the company's assets in enforcement against the debt. Only the shortfall after the sale is yours to make up. A debt signed by both husband and wife is a joint debt that both of them repay. One spouse's later agreement counts too. Once your spouse signs, both families' assets go in together (nationwide, in force from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: When a bank lends to a small or micro business, it almost always asks a shareholder to sign a joint and several guarantee. You can still get the loan. What matters is understanding what that page gives up before you sign, and keeping the guaranteed amount inside the 「can afford to lose」 figure from item 1 in this section (start a business only with money you can afford to lose). For the general rules on promissory notes and guarantee documents, see item 18 in section 8.

### 3. Choose the right entity before you open: a sole proprietorship and an ordinary partner pay to the end, only a limited liability company is 「limited」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration is done on the local government services website and takes a few hours. After a limited liability company is set up you have to keep books and file tax returns every month, and that is a running cost: doing it yourself takes time, paying someone to keep the books costs money, and prices differ by place.
- In plain language: A sole proprietorship and an ordinary partner pay to the end: whatever is owed is paid. A shareholder of a limited liability company pays only the amount promised at registration. But whatever you promised has to be paid in real money within 5 years: put in 1,000,000 and you may owe 1,000,000. Mixing the company's books with your own also makes the 「limited」 part fail.
- Benefit: The debts of a sole proprietorship business are paid from the individual's money and property, or from the family's money and property. A partner in an ordinary partnership enterprise bears unlimited joint and several liability for the partnership's debts. That means whatever is owed is paid, and payment does not end until it is made. A creditor can also pick out one partner alone and demand the whole amount. A limited partnership is different: a limited partner inside it pays no more than the amount promised (以认缴出资为限). A shareholder of a limited liability company likewise pays no more than the amount promised (以认缴出资额为限). But the 「limited」 part fails in three situations. First, the money promised has to be paid in within 5 years of the company being established, and when the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, a creditor can demand early payment. Second, money already put in and then withdrawn (抽逃出资) has to be returned. Third, the company is used as a shell to hide from creditors. A company with only one shareholder that cannot show the company's money and the shareholder's own money are kept separate also counts as the third case. Under these two practices, the company's debts are yours to pay as well (joint and several liability, 承担连带责任) (nationwide, in force from 2024-7)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2006). Partnership Enterprise Law, 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm> ; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2023). Company Law, 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: The condition for the 「limited」 part is that company money and private money stay apart: do not mix the company account and your personal account, do not pay household expenses out of the company's money, and keep complete records. Do not put a large figure in the registered capital just because it looks better. Whatever you subscribe for has to be paid in real money within 5 years. Put in 1,000,000 and you have created a potential debt of 1,000,000.

### 4. Do not act as a nominal shareholder, and do not hold shares on someone else's behalf
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. If someone says 「lend me your ID to register as a shareholder」 or 「the shares go under your name for now」, refuse every time.
- In plain language: Once your name is on the register you are a shareholder in law. The money promised at registration has to be paid in on time, and if you cannot pay it you must make up the shortfall and also compensate the company for its loss. If another shareholder did not really pay in money when the company was set up, you share that shortfall as well. Someone else runs the business, and the debt sits under your name.
- Benefit: A shareholder on the register is a shareholder in law. You have to pay in the whole amount promised, to the last cent, by the deadline set in the company's articles of association (full payment of subscribed capital, 足额缴纳认缴出资). If you do not pay on time, you must make up the shortfall and also compensate the company for its loss. If a shareholder did not really pay in money when the company was set up, the other shareholders present at the time share that shortfall (joint and several liability, 承担连带责任). When the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, a creditor can demand early payment of money whose deadline has not yet arrived. The person actually running the business is not on the register, and the debt sits under your name (nationwide, in force from 2024-7)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2023). Company Law, 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (2020 revision). Provisions on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China (III), 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）（法释〔2011〕3 号，2014 年法释〔2014〕2 号、2020 年法释〔2020〕18 号两次修正，第二十四、二十五条）:「如无法律规定的无效情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效」「实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东……人民法院不予支持」「名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持」. In plain terms: unless the law gives a ground of invalidity, the contract is valid; an actual investor who asks the company to change the shareholder registration without the consent of more than half the other shareholders is not supported by the court; where a nominal shareholder disposes of the equity and the actual investor suffers a loss, the court supports the actual investor's claim for compensation against the nominal shareholder. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799def980179ac07a9ca117c> (National Laws and Regulations Database, 国家法律法规数据库) ; Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院) (2025). Interpretation on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China (draft for comment), 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的解释（征求意见稿）第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/477881.html>
- Notes: Between a nominal shareholder and the person who really put in the money, a nominee agreement holds unless the law makes it invalid. But if the person who really put in the money wants the registration changed to their own name, more than half of the other shareholders have to agree. If the nominal shareholder sells or pledges the shares, the court handles it under the good-faith acquisition rules of the Civil Code. Where the buyer was unaware, paid a reasonable price and completed the registration, the person who put in the money cannot get the shares back and can only claim compensation from the nominal shareholder. A draft for comment on a replacement for this judicial interpretation came out in September 2025, and the whole document is repealed once the final version takes effect. Use the 2020 revision, which was still in force as of September 2026. For the risk of being a nominal legal representative, see item 28 in section 8.

### 5. Before you take a franchise, check the Ministry of Commerce filing, get the written disclosure, and put the 「cooling-off period」 into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Go to the Ministry of Commerce's 「商业特许经营信息管理系统」 website and check whether the brand has filed. Then require the other side to give you, before signing, written material covering everything it has to disclose, together with the contract text. A few hours is enough.
- In plain language: Under the rules the brand itself must first run 2 company-owned stores and operate them for at least 1 year, and it must file with the commerce authorities within 15 days after signing. Before you sign, it has to give you written material on 12 items: the franchise fee, how the deposit is refunded, whether the existing stores make money, and so on. If you cannot find a filing, if they will not hand over the material, or if they want your money before letting you see the contract, walk away.
- Benefit: The rules require the brand (called the franchisor, 特许人, in the rules) to run at least 2 company-owned stores it operates itself, for more than 1 year. Counting from the day the first franchise contract is signed, it must file with the commerce authorities within 15 days. Failing to file, it is fined 10,000 to 50,000 yuan. Failing to file after the deadline, it is fined 50,000 to 100,000 yuan and the filing is published. Before you sign, it must hand you written material covering everything it has to disclose, 12 items in total. These include which kinds of franchise fee are charged and how much each is, the circumstances in which the deposit is refunded, how many franchise stores there are now and how the business is doing. They also include a summary of the audited accounts for the past 2 years, and the lawsuits and arbitrations of the past 5 years. The contract must also state that the franchisee can withdraw on its own say-so within a period after signing (unilateral termination, 单方解除). If the brand conceals information or gives false information, the franchisee can terminate the contract (nationwide, in force from 2007-5)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2007). Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Franchise, 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm> ; Ministry of Commerce (商务部) (2012). Measures for the Administration of Commercial Franchise Information Disclosure, 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm> ; Ministry of Commerce (商务部). 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: If you cannot find a filing, if they will not hand over the disclosure documents, or if they want your money before showing you the contract, walk away. The 「cooling-off period」 is the stretch after signing when you can still withdraw on your own say-so. The number of days is set by the contract; the regulation only says 「a certain period」, so ask for the number of days to be written into the contract when you sign. The 「average sales volume, gross profit and net profit of existing franchise stores」 the brand discloses should be questioned about their source, because the measures require the brand to explain where the figures came from.

### 6. Settle the name, premises, business scope and registered capital before you register, and with complete papers you get the licence on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The registration step itself is not charged for. The registration fee for enterprises and sole proprietorships has not been collected since 2015-1-1. Do it yourself on the local government services website, or go to the government service hall and do it there; either way it takes a few hours. Cutting the company seal, paying someone to keep the books, and renting a registered address (address hosting) are services you buy in the market, not fees the government charges.
- In plain language: The registration itself is free, and the registration fee stopped being collected in 2015. There are only five sets of documents to submit: the application form, proof of identity or qualification, documents for the premises, the articles of association or the partnership agreement (a sole proprietorship submits none of these), plus anything else the rules name. With complete papers the licence is issued on the spot; if it cannot be issued on the spot it is done within 3 working days. Whatever you were charged is most likely an agent's errand fee.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes the documents to be submitted at five. 「（一）申请书」. 「（二）申请人资格文件、自然人身份证明」. 「（三）住所或者主要经营场所相关文件」. 「（四）公司、非公司企业法人、农民专业合作社（联合社）章程或者合伙企业合伙协议」. 「（五）法律、行政法规和国务院市场监督管理部门规定提交的其他材料」. In plain terms: one application form; one set of documents showing who you are or that you are qualified to do this; one set of documents proving the shop or the office is yours to use; the articles of association or the partnership agreement; plus anything else the rules name. A company must also submit the appointment documents and identity proofs of its legal representative, directors, supervisors and senior managers. A sole proprietorship submits no articles. The name is 「由申请人依法自主申报」, that is, the applicant names and declares it themselves. The registered capital 「实行认缴登记制」, so you write down what you promise to contribute and do not pay it over on the spot. The business scope is chosen from the categories the registration authority publishes. Where the documents are complete and correctly formatted, they are 「予以确认并当场登记」. Where it cannot be finished on the spot, it is done 「3 个工作日内」, and where the circumstances are complicated, 3 further working days. 「营业执照签发日期为市场主体的成立日期」: the date printed on the licence is the day the company or the shop came into existence (nationwide, in force from 2022-3)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2021). Regulations on the Registration of Market Entities, 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2022). Detailed Rules for Implementing the Regulations on the Registration of Market Entities, 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; Ministry of Finance and National Development and Reform Commission (财政部、国家发展改革委) (2014). Notice on Cancelling, Suspending and Exempting a Batch of Administrative and Institutional Charges, 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself is not charged for, and whatever you were charged is most likely an agent's errand fee. The price of cutting the company seal is set by the market. The General Office of the State Council has expressly ordered 「严禁指定公章制作单位制作公章」, so when someone says 「you must go to the designated place to cut the seal」 you do not have to accept that. How much you put in the registered capital does not affect the shopfront. It only sets the ceiling on your liability, and it has to be paid in full within 5 years; see item 3 in this section (choose the right entity before you open). If the business scope contains items that need a licence, you cannot open until the licence comes through; see item 7 in this section (trades that need a licence)

### 7. In a trade that needs a licence, do not open before the licence comes through
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you open, find out whether the business you are starting needs a licence. Food service and selling food need a food business licence. Selling only whole packages made by someone else needs no licence, only a filing. Open for business once the licence is in place.
- In plain language: Opening without a licence means, at the mild end, being ordered to stop, having the money earned taken away, and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan. If what you are doing is an item under monopoly or exclusive sale, or securities, futures, insurance or funds payment and settlement, serious circumstances amount to the crime of illegal business operations, with 5 years or less as the starting point. Renovating first and getting the licence afterwards, then trading before it comes through, is the step people trip over most often.
- Benefit: Opening the door and trading without a licence is handled by whichever department the laws and regulations designate. Operating without a licence where the law does not say what the penalty is: ordered to stop, the money earned taken away (illegal gains), and a fine of up to 10,000 yuan. There is a heavier tier as well. Operating without a licence items that the laws and regulations put under monopoly, exclusive sale or restricted trading. Doing securities, futures, insurance or funds payment and settlement business without approval. Where the circumstances are serious, both amount to the crime of illegal business operations. The sentence is 5 years or less of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention (short confinement), plus a fine of 1 to 5 times the money earned. Where the circumstances are particularly serious, 5 years or more (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (国务院) (2017). Measures for Dealing with Operations Without a Licence or a Business Licence, 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm> ; State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) (2023). Measures for the Administration of Food Business Licences and Filings, 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html> ; National People's Congress (全国人大) (1997). Criminal Law, 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: The licensing conditions for finance, medical care and education and training each have their own dedicated laws; this item verified only the general enforcement rules and the Criminal Law article. A bubble tea shop or a snack stall counts as food service and needs a food business licence. Renovating first and getting the licence afterwards, then trading before it comes through, is a common trap. For how the food business is divided into tiers and whether selling fresh meat and vegetables needs a certificate, see item 8 (work out which tier of food business you fall into).

### 8. Work out which tier of food business you fall into: production and food service need a licence, selling only prepackaged food needs a filing, selling fresh meat and vegetables needs neither
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The licence itself is not charged for. But the premises and the equipment have to meet the standard, and the on-site inspection plus the paperwork takes several weeks. If you sell meat you also have to keep the quarantine certificate and the certificate of conformity for meat quality inspection for every batch.
- In plain language: Making things yourself to sell, such as a snack stall, braised food, baking or home-made pickled vegetables, all need a licence. Selling only whole packages made by someone else needs no licence, only a filing with the market regulation department at county level or above. Selling fresh meat and vegetables needs no certificate. Doing it without one brings a fine of at least 50,000 to 100,000 yuan, which is more than most small businesses put in.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: doing food business requires a licence first. Producing food yourself, selling food and running food service all have to obtain a licence as the law requires. 「但是，销售食用农产品和仅销售预包装食品的，不需要取得许可。仅销售预包装食品的，应当报所在地县级以上地方人民政府食品安全监督管理部门备案」. In plain terms: selling fresh agricultural products, and selling only prepackaged food, need no licence. Selling only prepackaged food requires a filing with the food safety supervision department of the local people's government at county level or above. Fresh agricultural products are things like fresh meat and vegetables that have not been processed. Prepackaged food is food somebody else made and sold with its packaging. 「食品生产加工小作坊和食品摊贩等的具体管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定」, so provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities set the rules for these two categories themselves. Doing food business without a licence: the money earned, the food, and the tools, equipment and raw materials are all confiscated. Where the total value of the goods at the selling price (货值金额) is under 10,000 yuan, there is a fine of 50,000 yuan or more and 100,000 yuan or less. Where the value is 10,000 yuan or more, the fine is 10 to 20 times the value. ### 10. Ordinary food may not say it can cure disease: labels, instructions, advertisements and livestream scripts all count
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Go through your slogans, your product description pages and your livestream scripts. The hard part is holding back from saying things like 「lower your three highs」 or 「cure constipation」.
- In plain language: The labels, instructions and advertisements of ordinary food may not say they prevent or cure disease. The fine is 1 to 3 times the advertising spend. Where the advertising spend cannot be worked out, the fine is 100,000 to 200,000 yuan. In serious circumstances the business licence can be revoked as well.

### 11. The food business has a criminal line: selling diseased or dead meat or goods over the limit is already enough, and adding toxic or harmful substances carries 5 years whatever the amount
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Do what item 8 (work out which tier of food business you fall into) says: keep the quarantine certificate and the purchase vouchers, and put only ingredients in the list whose approval number you can look up.
- In plain language: The earlier items punish money. This one takes people. Selling diseased or dead meat, meat that fails inspection, or food where pesticide residues, veterinary drug residues or heavy metals seriously exceed the limit is already a crime as long as it is 「sufficient」 to cause severe poisoning, without anyone actually falling ill. That carries 3 years or less. Adding a toxic or harmful non-food ingredient to food starts at 5 years or less, and it makes no difference how much you sold.
- Benefit: Article one hundred and forty-three of the Criminal Law covers this tier. It covers food you make or sell that does not meet food safety standards. As long as it is 「足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的」, that is enough for the crime. The sentence is 3 years or less of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention (short confinement), together with a fine. Where severe harm to people's health results, or there are other serious circumstances, the sentence is 3 years or more and 7 years or less, together with a fine. Where the consequences are particularly serious, the sentence is 7 years or more, up to life imprisonment, and a fine or confiscation of all the property held in the name. Article one hundred and forty-four: adding a toxic or harmful non-food ingredient to food you make or sell, or knowingly selling it after adding one, carries 5 years or less of fixed-term imprisonment together with a fine. Where severe harm to people's health results, or there are other serious circumstances, the sentence is 5 years or more and 10 years or less, together with a fine. Where someone dies, or there are other particularly serious circumstances, punishment follows Article one hundred and forty-one (10 years or more, life imprisonment or the death penalty). The Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate have issued an interpretation, known as the SPC-SPP interpretation. It sets out which situations count as 「足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性性疾病」. First, 「含有严重超出标准限量的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、生物毒素、重金属等污染物质」. Second, 「属于病死、死因不明或者检验检疫不合格的畜、禽、兽、水产动物肉类及其制品的」. Third, 「属于国家为防控疾病等特殊需要明令禁止生产、销售的」. Fourth, 「特殊医学用途配方食品、专供婴幼儿的主辅食品营养成分严重不符合食品安全标准的」. Using additives over the limit or in the wrong place, to the point where the consequences above follow, is convicted under Article one hundred and forty-three as well. What counts as a 「toxic or harmful non-food ingredient」 is not for you to decide. One group is substances the laws and regulations forbid adding to food. Another is substances on the lists announced by the relevant departments of the State Council, such as banned pesticides and drugs barred from use on animals raised for food. One more situation also counts as 「其他严重情节」 under Article one hundred and forty-four. It was made and sold to a value of 10,000 yuan or more but under 20,000 yuan. The location is a primary or secondary school campus, a childcare or elderly care institution, or the surroundings of either. The buyers are minors or elderly people. Slaughtering and selling outside a designated slaughterhouse, at a pig slaughtering site set up privately, is convicted as illegal business operations where the circumstances are serious. A turnover of 10,000 yuan or more, or illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, counts as 「情节严重」 (nationwide)

### 13. Issue invoices only for real transactions, and small-scale taxpayers should use the whole exemption threshold
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

### 15. Write 「定金」 when you take money, not 「订金」, put a figure on the liquidated damages, and vet a credit period as if it were a loan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. In the contract write the two characters 「定金」, with an amount not more than 20% of the total contract value. Take the money first and deliver afterwards. Before you agree to let the other side take the goods and pay later (granting a credit period), look into the other side first.
- In plain language: Only the two characters 「定金」 bring the double rule: if you break the contract, the money cannot be recovered; if the other side breaks it, you get double back. The part above 20% of the contract value does not count. Written as 「订金」, the law has no such rule. Granting a credit period is lending your own money to the other side for nothing; if they fail, it is a bad debt.
- Benefit: A deposit counts only once the money has actually reached the other side (it is formed on actual delivery). If the party paying the deposit breaks the contract, the money cannot be recovered. If the party receiving the deposit breaks it, the original sum must be returned and an equal amount paid on top (double return). The part above 20% of the total contract value (the subject matter value of the main contract) is not treated as a deposit. Where the agreed liquidated damages are wildly higher or wildly lower than the actual loss, the court may lower them or raise them at the request of one party. Where both the liquidated damages and the deposit are written into the contract, a real breach lets you claim one or the other, not both (nationwide, in force from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (全国人大) (2020). Civil Code, 民法典（第五百八十五、五百八十六、五百八十七、五百八十八条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: 「订金」 has none of the rules that a deposit has under the Civil Code, and it is generally treated as an advance on payment. That is the standard view in practice, and this section has not verified any case law. A credit period is lending your own money to the other side interest-free; if they fail, it is a bad debt. Before granting one, go to the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and look up the other side's record, to see whether it has been sued.

### 16. Sign a written contract within the first month of employment and complete the social insurance registration within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The employer's share of the social insurance contributions has to go on being paid, and the rate differs from place to place. Signing the contract itself costs nothing; just use the model contract from the local human resources and social security department.
- In plain language: If a person has worked a full month without a written contract, from the second month you must pay double wages every month, and that runs until the full year is up. Social insurance has to be registered within 30 days of the employment starting; failing to do it brings a fine of 1 to 3 times the contributions due. The law has no such thing as 「the employee voluntarily gives up social insurance」.
- Benefit: Where a person has worked a full month but not a full year without a written contract, you must pay double wages every month. Counting from the person's first day at work, you must complete the social insurance registration for them within 30 days. Failing to do it first brings an order to correct. Past the deadline, the fine is 1 to 3 times the social insurance contributions due, and the person who handled it is fined a further 500 to 3,000 yuan. Where social insurance is not paid on time or not paid in full, a late payment charge accrues from the day it fell due at five ten-thousandths per day. Past the deadline it is still unpaid, there is a further fine of 1 to 3 times the arrears. The employer has to file itself and pay the full amount on time. - Notes: Double wages run from the second month right up to the full year, so the longer you delay the more it costs. ### 17. Pay wages according to the contract and the law, and put raises, loans and bonuses in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Write the wage clearly into the contract. Raises, bonuses, money you advance and loans all go on a signed piece of paper. No verbal promises. Do not pay wages out of your own pocket on the company's behalf either. The hard part is following the same rules when a friend asks.
- In plain language: Wages must be paid on time and in full according to the contract and the national rules. If they go unpaid, the employee can go straight to the court for a payment order. If you lend money to an employee out of your own pocket, write a written IOU stating the amount, the interest and the date for repayment.

### 18. Sell before you build: use pre-orders and small orders to prove someone will buy, then put money into production
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: From tens to hundreds of yuan, to make a sample or a page. Then go and find some dozens of real customers and get them to pay in advance or place small orders. That is several weeks slower than starting production straight away. The hard part is holding back from making the goods first.
- In plain language: One study followed 116 new Italian companies for about a year. Entrepreneurs who set up a hypothesis first and then tested it against market feedback did better, were also more willing to change direction in time, and did not give up any earlier. So if nobody will pay in advance and nobody will place a small order, do not believe that 「it will sell once it exists」.
- Benefit: A randomised trial that followed 116 Italian start-ups for about a year. A randomised trial means splitting people at random into two groups, training only one of them, and then comparing the results of the two groups. The finding was this: entrepreneurs who had been trained to decide by 「putting forward a hypothesis first, then testing it strictly against market feedback」 performed better, were also more likely to change course in time for another plan, and were not more likely to abandon the business early. The authors explain it this way: the practice lowers the chance of going on pouring money into projects that look promising but will not work out (false positive projects) (Italy, published 2020)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Camuffo A, Cordova A, Gambardella A, Spina C (2020). A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial. Management Science 66(2):564-586. <https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249>
- Notes: There is only one such study, the sample is small, and it was done in Italy, so the grade is B. ### 19. Once the sample is made, run it through the production checklist before you talk about starting

The minimum order quantity and the payment terms set how much you have to advance first. The inventory sets when the money comes back. ### 20. Keep the receipts and the supplier's details for every batch you buy, and do not buy stock priced well below the market: when an employee buys counterfeit goods, the boss is the one convicted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Keep these for every batch: the purchase contract, the invoice, the payment record, and the supplier's business licence and authorisation documents. Then set one internal rule: whoever buys the goods signs for them, and anything priced well below the market is not bought at all. The hard part is turning down the low-priced stock that looks very profitable.
- In plain language: Selling counterfeit goods is enough for a conviction at 30,000 yuan of earnings or 50,000 yuan of sales, with 3 years or less. Stock sitting in the warehouse unsold counts as well. 「I did not know it was fake」 is not for you to decide: a buying price plainly below the market with no reason you can give is treated as knowledge. Only with the purchase contract, the invoice, the payment record and the supplier's details do you have a route to escaping liability.
- Benefit: Knowingly selling goods bearing a counterfeited registered trademark: where the earnings (illegal gains) reach 30,000 yuan, or the amount sold reaches 50,000 yuan, you have reached the threshold for criminal punishment. The sentence is 3 years or less, with a fine, or a fine alone. Reaching ten times that standard, the sentence is 3 to 10 years together with a fine. Stock still sitting in the warehouse unsold counts too: where the value of the goods reaches three times the sales amount standard, that too amounts to the crime. You do not have to admit that you 「knew」. The 2025 judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate lists five situations in which knowledge is directly established, unless you can produce evidence that you genuinely did not know. First, knowledge that the registered trademark on the goods has been altered, swapped or covered over. Second, forging or altering the trademark owner's authorisation document, or knowing that the document has been forged or altered. Third, having been punished criminally or administratively for selling counterfeit goods and then selling the same kind again. Fourth, buying or selling with no justifiable reason at a price plainly below the market price. Fifth, after being found by an administrative law enforcement agency or a judicial organ, transferring or destroying evidence such as the infringing goods and the accounting records, or providing false proof. Where a company commits this, 「对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员」 is punished under the same article. The company pays the fine, and the person who made the call and the person who handled it are punished under the same article. So if an employee bought the goods, the one who is fined and convicted is still the person who made the call. In the other direction, the Trademark Law leaves a way out: where at the time of sale you genuinely did not know the goods infringed another's registered trademark, 「能证明该商品是自己合法取得并说明提供者的，不承担赔偿责任」. Whether you can escape liability depends entirely on whether you have the receipts and the supplier's details; without them that provision is unavailable to you (nationwide, the judicial interpretation in force from 2025-4-26)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2020). - Notes: Cosmetics, alcoholic drinks, mother and baby goods, digital products and clothing are the categories where these cases cluster. The common point is that the same product has a very wide gap in price. Labels like 「channel goods」, 「end-of-line stock」 and 「overseas duty-free」 do not replace the receipts. Beyond criminal punishment there is also an administrative penalty: where infringement is established, the infringing goods and the tools are confiscated, and where the turnover is 50,000 yuan or more a fine of up to 5 times may be imposed on top. If you find something wrong after the stock has arrived, stop selling and seal it at once, then contact the brand owner or the market regulation department. Do not go on selling, and do not destroy the goods or the records. Destroying evidence lands you squarely in the fifth situation above, which 「presumes you knew the goods were counterfeit」. For using someone else's artwork see item 21 (make your own artwork or buy a licence). There is one routine aimed squarely at this: someone turns up uninvited and offers to give you a batch of pens or other small goods free, to put in the shop, sell and split the takings. A few days later someone comes to buy, takes evidence on the spot, then claims in damages for infringement. ### 21. The artwork on the goods, the packaging, the hang tags and the promotional images is either your own or licensed; changing a colour or adding an icon does not count as 「altered」
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Licences for a genuine stock library start at tens of yuan per image. Having a designer produce something original costs a few hundred to a few thousand yuan. For every image used on the goods, keep the licence certificate and the purchase record. The hard part is not using images you found at random by searching online.
- In plain language: Changing a colour on someone else's image, swapping the background colour or adding your own icon is still using their work without permission. Copying someone else's artistic work for profit, to the point of 30,000 yuan earned, 50,000 yuan of business done, or 500 copies printed, amounts to the crime of infringing the copyright, and carries 3 years or less. On the trademark side, being 「close enough to cause confusion」 is already infringement.
- Benefit: The Copyright Law puts 「未经著作权人许可，以改编、翻译、注释等方式使用作品」 on the same footing as direct copying as an infringing act. The threshold for criminal punishment is here: for profit, without the permission of the copyright owner (著作权人) of the work, printing artistic works for sale (reproduction and distribution). Or the copies printed add up to 500 (sheets). Reaching any one of these figures amounts to the crime of infringing the copyright. The sentence is 3 years or less, with a fine, or a fine alone. Reaching ten times the standard, the sentence is 3 to 10 years. Beyond criminal punishment there is civil damages and an administrative fine: where the public interest is harmed, the illegal gains may be confiscated and a fine of 1 to 5 times the amount of illegal business operations imposed. For checking a trademark before production, see item 19 (once the sample is made, run it through the production checklist). In the other direction, if someone else uses your own images, you enforce your rights under these same rules.

### 22. Do your job by the rules, and before a favour outside the rules, work out whose responsibility it is
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. At work, follow the system, the medical orders and the process. Do not make decisions for other people beyond your own authority. Before you step in to help, ask yourself one question: if something goes wrong, who is responsible. The hard part is resisting a request to 「make an exception」 on the spot.
- In plain language: Take a nurse as an example. The regulation states that on finding a patient's condition critical the doctor must be notified at once, and on finding medical orders that break the rules the point must be put to the doctor who gave them. Other jobs work the same way: first see where your own rules draw the line for 「must report」. The law does not leave you standing by while someone dies either: a voluntary emergency rescue that injures the person rescued carries no damages.
- Benefit: Taking a nurse as an example, the professional boundary the regulation sets out is this. On finding a critical condition, notify the doctor at once. In an emergency, in order to save a dying patient, the necessary emergency care may be carried out first. On finding medical orders that break the law or violate the technical standards for diagnosis and treatment, raise it with the doctor, and report it to the head of the department if necessary. The Civil Code provides separately: where a person voluntarily performs emergency rescue and as a result injures the person rescued, the rescuer does not have to pay damages (bears no civil liability) (nationwide)

### 23. If it fails, exit through the proper procedure: cancel through the simplified route if you can, go through bankruptcy if the assets do not cover the debts, and do not simply leave it alone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: You can do the simplified cancellation yourself: post it on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System for 20 days. Where debts are still outstanding, you first have to sort the accounts, collect what is owed and pay what is due; that step is called liquidation. Going through bankruptcy means applying to the court, which takes months. The hard part is not wanting to face it after a loss and putting it off indefinitely.
- In plain language: Where there are no debts and no wages, taxes or social insurance outstanding, all the investors sign a commitment, and if nobody objects during the 20 days of posting it can be cancelled through the simplified route. A sole proprietorship needs no posting: if there is no objection within 10 days it is cancelled outright. If debts cannot be paid, apply for bankruptcy. Leave it alone, fail to file an annual report for 2 years and cannot be reached, and the licence is revoked; the legal representative who bears responsibility cannot be a legal representative again for 3 years.
- Benefit: There are no debts to others and no debts owed to you, or they have all been settled. No wages, social insurance or taxes outstanding. All the investors have signed a written commitment. Meeting these conditions and posting it for 20 days without objection lets you go through the simplified cancellation. A sole proprietorship needs no posting; if the competent department raises no objection within 10 days it is cancelled outright. A company that cannot pay a debt that has fallen due and whose debts exceed its assets (being insolvent) may apply to the court for reorganisation, settlement or bankruptcy liquidation. Where a company has already been dissolved without liquidation and its debts exceed its assets, the person responsible for the liquidation should apply for bankruptcy liquidation. Leaving it alone brings these consequences. Failing to post the annual report on time gets the business placed on the list of abnormal operations. Failing to file an annual report for 2 consecutive years, being placed on that list and not correcting it, and being unreachable at the registered address gets the business licence revoked. A legal representative of a company whose licence is revoked, who bears personal responsibility for it, may not act as a company's legal representative or as a director, supervisor or senior manager for 3 years counting from the day of revocation. Once a business is on the list of abnormal operations, even the simplified cancellation is unavailable (nationwide)
