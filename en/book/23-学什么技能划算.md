# 23. 学什么技能划算

这一节算的是钱和时间，第 2 条另外算死亡率。前六条回答一个问题：还要不要接着读书。第 1 条先说法律划掉了哪一段选项，第 2 条算读书和寿命的关系，第 3 条看全国人的学历都在哪一档。第 4 条讲读不起的时候有哪些资助，第 5 条讲没考上普高还有什么路，第 6 条把「读书还是打工」算成一道题，给一个自己动手算的方法。后七条回答另一个问题：决定学点东西之后怎么挑。中国按工种分的工资统计，从公开的官方渠道很难逐项核实。所以这里只写查得到出处的几件事：多读一年书工资大概多多少（也叫教育回报率）、证书合不合法怎么验、培训补贴从哪里来、挑技能时看哪个维度更不容易被顶掉。具体某个工种一个月挣多少，本书不给数字。第 14 到 19 条回答第三个问题：定下来学什么之后，怎么学才省时间。这六条算的是时间，不和钱做换算。第 20 到 23 条回答第四个问题：已经在做专业技术工作了，职称从哪里报、怎么考、别踩什么坑、评上值多少。

# 23. Which skills are worth learning

This section measures money and time, and item 2 measures mortality as well. The first six items answer one question: whether to carry on with education. Item 1 sets out which part of the options the law removes. Item 2 works out the relationship between education and lifespan. Item 3 looks at where the national education level sits. Item 4 covers the financial help available when you cannot afford it. Item 5 covers what is left if you do not get into a general senior high school. Item 6 turns "study or work" into a question with a method you can work through yourself. The next seven items answer a different question: how to choose once you have decided to learn something. China's wage statistics by occupation are hard to verify item by item through public official channels, so only the things that can be traced to a source are written here: how much an extra year of education is worth in wages, also called the return to education, how to check whether a certificate is legal, where training subsidies come from, and which dimension to look at when choosing a skill so that it is harder to replace. This book gives no figure for what a particular trade earns in a month. Items 14 to 19 answer a third question: once you have settled what to learn, how to learn it without wasting time. Those six items measure time and are not converted into money. Items 20 to 23 answer a fourth question: if you already work in a technical or professional field, where a professional title is applied for, how the exam works, what traps to avoid, and what it is worth once you have it.

### 1. Under 16 years old there is no "go out to work" option: an employer is fined 5000 yuan a month for hiring you, and the employers who do hire are running it black
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. It takes a minute to work out how old you are.
- In plain language: Finishing junior high school usually happens at 15 or 16, which sits exactly on this line. An employer who takes you on under 16 is breaking the law, and hiring one child worker carries a fine of 5000 yuan for every month of employment. So the employers who are willing to take you are the ones who know they are breaking the law and do it anyway. If something does happen, the medical bills and the compensation can only be claimed from that employer, and if it defaults or runs off, nobody is standing behind it.
- Benefit: Article eleven of the Compulsory Education Law: 「凡年满六周岁的儿童，其父母或者其他法定监护人应当送其入学接受并完成义务教育」, that is, every child who has reached the age of six shall be sent to school by their parents or other legal guardians to receive and complete compulsory education. Article fourteen: 「禁止用人单位招用应当接受义务教育的适龄儿童、少年」, that is, employers are prohibited from hiring children and adolescents of an age that should be receiving compulsory education. Article fifty-eight: where school-age children and adolescents are not sent to school without a proper reason or in accordance with this Law, the township people's government at the local level, or the education administrative department of the county-level people's government, 「给予批评教育，责令限期改正」, that is, gives criticism and education and orders the correction to be made within a set time. Article two of the Provisions on the Prohibition of Child Labour: state organs, social organisations, enterprises and public institutions, private non-enterprise units and individual industrial and commercial households 「均不得招用不满16周岁的未成年人」, that is, may not hire anyone under 16 years old. Article six: punishment is imposed 「按照每使用一名童工每月处5000元罚款的标准给予处罚」, that is, at the standard of a fine of 5000 yuan for every month in which one child worker is employed, and where the correction is not made by the deadline it is 10,000 yuan per person per month. Article seven: anyone who introduces a child worker into employment 「按照每介绍一人处5000元罚款」, that is, is fined 5000 yuan for each person introduced. Article ten: where a child worker falls ill or is injured, the employer 「应当负责送到医疗机构治疗，并负担治疗期间的全部医疗和生活费用」, that is, must take responsibility for taking the child to a medical institution for treatment and for bearing all the medical and living costs during the treatment, and must also make a one-off payment where the child is permanently disabled or dies. Article thirteen leaves a single exception: arts and sports units, with the consent of the parents or other guardians, may hire professional artistic workers and athletes under 16 years old (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2018). Compulsory Education Law (second revision of 2018, Articles eleven, fourteen and fifty-eight). <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202110/t20211029_575949.html> (published by the Ministry of Education); State Council (国务院) (2002). Provisions on the Prohibition of Child Labour (State Council Order No. 364, Articles two, six, seven, ten and thirteen). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61798.htm>
- Notes: The person who benefits is yourself. Working after you turn 16 is lawful. But the jobs that are keen to take on a 16-year-old with no qualification are exactly the ones described in item 10 in this section (choosing skills that resist automation): fixed processes, the easiest for a machine to take over. The overtime pay, annual leave and work injury benefits described in section 19 all depend on there being an employment relationship and on evidence. A black job gives you neither, and those are the things you cannot get.

### 2. Put "is studying worth it" into the mortality account too: each extra year of education lowers the adult risk of death by about 1.9%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. What it costs is the time and energy of those extra years of study; for the tuition, see item 4 in this section (secondary vocational tuition and grants). The hard part is carrying on without giving up in the middle.
- In plain language: Each extra year of study lowers the risk of death in adulthood by about 1.9% on average. The effect is clearest between 18 and 49, where each year is worth about 2.9%, and above 70 it is only 0.8%. People who can keep studying are often better off to begin with, and how much of this is the effect of the study itself cannot be separated.
- Benefit: All the earlier studies were gathered up and pooled together, a systematic review and meta-analysis. The search ran from month 1 of 1980 to month 5 of 2023, and finally included 603 studies, 70 locations in 59 countries and 10,355 observations. Education and adult mortality, which is deaths from all causes added together, show a dose-response relationship: the more study, the larger the fall. Each extra year of education lowers the risk of death by 1.9% on average (95% uncertainty interval 1.8 to 2.0, which means the true value falls roughly within this range). For people aged 18 to 49 each extra year lowers it by 2.9% (2.8 to 3.0). For people over 70 the fall is 0.8% (0.6 to 1.0). No difference was seen between men and women. Split by national level of development, using the Social Population Index, no difference was seen either.
- Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- Sources: IHME-CHAIN Collaborators (2024). Effects of education on adult mortality: a global systematic review and meta-analysis. The Lancet Public Health, 9(3), e155-e165. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00306-7>
- Notes:Disputed. The authors report publication bias themselves (p<0.0001), which means the studies with the prettier results are likelier to get published. The results also differ a long way across the studies. Everything included here is observational follow-up, not a trial that assigned people to groups. Years of schooling are tangled up with family income, region and occupation, and this kind of study cannot separate how much of the 1.9% comes from the schooling itself and how much comes from the fact that people who can keep studying were better off to begin with. So it shows strongly that more schooling and a longer life point the same way, but it does not mean that if you go and study you will live 1.9% longer. The person who benefits is yourself.

### 3. Before deciding whether a qualification has lost its value, look at the national education structure: in every 100,000 people only 15,467 have a university-level education
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You only have to look at the figures below.
- In plain language: The 2020 census found that across the country only 15467 people in every 100,000 went on to college or above, about 15%. 34507 had finished junior high, which is the largest group of all. People over 15 had been in school for 9.91 years on average, just past junior high. So a junior high qualification in China is not "good enough". It is the most ordinary level there is.
- Benefit: The standard reference date of the seventh national census was 2020-11-1. Of the national population, 218360767 people had a university-level education, that is college and above. 213005258 had senior high, including technical secondary schools. 487163489 had junior high. 349658828 had primary school. Compared with the sixth national census of 2010, the number with a university-level education in every 100,000 rose from 8930 to 15467. Senior high rose from 14032 to 15088. Junior high fell from 38788 to 34507. Primary school fell from 26779 to 24767. The average years of schooling of the population aged 15 and above rose from 9.08 years to 9.91 years. The illiteracy rate fell from 4.08% to 2.67%
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National Bureau of Statistics (国家统计局) and the Office of the Leading Group for the Seventh National Population Census of the State Council (国务院第七次全国人口普查领导小组办公室) (2021). Communiqué of the Seventh National Population Census, 第七次全国人口普查公报（第六号）: education level of the population, 人口受教育情况. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/tjgb/rkpcgb/qgrkpcgb/202302/t20230206_1902006.html>
- Notes: These figures only say how scarce a qualification is among the population. Whether studying is worth it for a particular person has to be worked out with the method in item 6 in this section (how to calculate study or work). One more thing to note: the census counts the whole national population, old people and small children included. The share of young people who go on to university is much higher than this figure, so comparing it with your own age group overstates your advantage. Used the other way round, it does answer the claim that studying is useless, because the denominator is the whole labour force competing with you for the same jobs. The person who benefits is yourself.

### 4. When you cannot afford it, work through the policy first: most secondary vocational tuition is already free, grants are 2300 yuan, and student loans go up to 20,000 yuan a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before term starts, ask the school's student financial aid office once and hand in the papers once.
- In plain language: In public secondary vocational schools, rural students, urban students in agriculture-related majors and students from families in financial difficulty are already exempt from tuition. For secondary vocational schools and general senior high schools, the grant is 2300 yuan a year from the spring term of 2025. At university the grant is 3700 yuan a year, and a student loan can be for up to 20,000 yuan a year. If the money is not enough you can defer, or find another way round. It rarely comes to having to drop out now and go out to work.
- Benefit: Exempting secondary vocational students from tuition is policy that has applied since the autumn term of 2012. The text reads: 「对公办中等职业学校全日制正式学籍一、二、三年级在校生中所有农村（含县镇）学生、城市涉农专业学生和家庭经济困难学生免除学费（艺术类相关表演专业学生除外）」, that is, tuition is waived for all rural students including those from county and town seats, urban students in agriculture-related majors and students from families in financial difficulty, among the full-time students officially enrolled in years one, two and three of public secondary vocational schools, excluding students in performance-related arts majors. The student financial aid policy was adjusted in 2024. The national grant for undergraduate and junior college students was raised from 3300 yuan per student per year to 3700 yuan. The National Encouragement Scholarship was raised from 5000 yuan to 6000 yuan. Both apply from the autumn term of 2024, with the scholarship from 2024. The national grant for secondary vocational schools and the national grant for general senior high schools were both raised from 2000 yuan per student per year to 2300 yuan, applying from the spring term of 2025. National student loans were adjusted too, from the autumn term of 2024. For undergraduate and junior college students, 「每人每年申请贷款额度由不超过16000元提高至不超过20000元」, that is, the amount each student may apply for each year was raised from not more than 16000 yuan to not more than 20000 yuan. For postgraduate students, 「由不超过20000元提高至不超过25000元」, that is, from not more than 20000 yuan to not more than 25000 yuan. The interest rate is the Loan Prime Rate (LPR) for the same period and grade less 70 basis points, that is 0.7 percentage points lower (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Finance, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Education and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (财政部、发展改革委、教育部、人力资源社会保障部) (2012). Opinions on Expanding the Scope of the Tuition-Free Policy for Secondary Vocational Education and Further Improving the National Grant System (Cai Jiao [2012] No. 376). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2013/content_2332779.htm>; the relevant department heads of the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (财政部、教育部、人力资源社会保障部有关司局负责人) (2024). Answers to reporters on the questions of adjusting the student financial aid policy. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202410/content_6983672.htm>; Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Education, People's Bank of China and National Financial Regulatory Administration (财政部、教育部、人民银行、金融监管总局) (2024). Notice on Adjusting and Improving the Relevant Policies on National Student Loans (Cai Jiao [2024] No. 188). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202411/content_6984812.htm>
- Notes: The people who benefit are yourself and your children. Which students the tuition waiver covers and how many bands the grant has are both set by each province. Private schools are subsidised up to the standard of the equivalent public course locally, and you still pay the difference, so check the local documents before you enrol. A student loan is a loan, not a grant, and it has to be repaid after you graduate. But it separates "I cannot pay the tuition now" from "are these years worth it", so do not treat running out of money as the conclusion. The training subsidies and temporary assistance available during unemployment are covered in section 7; if the household really has lost its income, apply for those first.

### 5. Not getting into a general senior high school does not mean the road ends: secondary vocational schools have linked admissions and separate examinations, and skilled jobs may lower the education requirement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you fill in the application choices, check the vocational education admissions platform in your province once.
- In plain language: The 2022 Vocational Education Law states that vocational education and general education are of equal importance. Secondary vocational schools can take students through linked admissions into higher vocational schools, and higher vocational schools must set aside part of their admissions plan or hold a separate examination specifically for secondary vocational graduates. Employers may not put a barrier in your way because you graduated from a vocational school. Missing the points for a general senior high school is a change of route, not stopping at a junior high qualification.
- Benefit: The Vocational Education Law, in force from 2022-5-1, Article three: 「职业教育是与普通教育具有同等重要地位的教育类型」, that is, vocational education is a type of education of equal importance to general education. Article thirty-seven: secondary vocational schools 「可以按照国家有关规定，在有关专业实行与高等职业学校教育的贯通招生和培养」, that is, may, as the state provides, run linked admissions and training with higher vocational education in the relevant majors. Higher vocational schools 「可以按照国家有关规定，采取文化素质与职业技能相结合的考核方式招收学生」, that is, may, as the state provides, admit students using an assessment that combines academic qualities with vocational skills. Education administrative departments at provincial level and above have to set up a unified vocational education admissions platform, publish school, major and admissions information together, and provide a service for looking up and applying. Article fifty-three: 「职业学校学生在升学、就业、职业发展等方面与同层次普通学校学生享有平等机会」, that is, students in vocational schools enjoy equal opportunities with students in ordinary schools at the same level in terms of moving on to further study, employment and career development. Higher vocational schools and ordinary higher education institutions that provide vocational education 「应当在招生计划中确定相应比例或者采取单独考试办法，专门招收职业学校毕业生」, that is, shall set a corresponding share in the admissions plan or use a separate examination to admit vocational school graduates specifically. And 「用人单位不得设置妨碍职业学校毕业生平等就业、公平竞争的报考、录用、聘用条件」, that is, employers may not set application, hiring or appointment conditions that hinder equal employment and fair competition for vocational school graduates. Posts in public institutions advertised openly that carry a vocational skill level requirement 「可以适当降低学历要求」, that is, may lower the education requirement appropriately (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2022). Vocational Education Law (2022 revision, Articles three, thirty-seven and fifty-three). <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202204/t20220421_620064.html> (published by the Ministry of Education)
- Notes: The people who benefit are yourself and your children. These are channels created by law, not a guarantee of admission. Which majors run linked training, what share is set aside for separate admissions and which subjects the vocational education examination covers are all set by each province. Before you fill in the choices you must check your own province's vocational education admissions platform and admissions plan for that year, rather than reasoning from another province's experience. When choosing a secondary vocational major, run it through the dimensions in item 10 in this section (choosing skills that resist automation): a major that puts you on site and in a non-standard environment is harder to replace. To choose a school, look at whether it appears in your province's admissions plan for that year. Then verify the certificates it issues with the three steps in item 8 (check whether a certificate is in the catalogue). Ignore any admissions publicity promising 「包分配」, a guaranteed placement, or 「保过」, a guaranteed pass.

### 6. Turn "study or work" into a question with numbers: the wages you earn three years earlier against the income gap over the following decades
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Sit down and do the sum once. It takes about half an hour.
- In plain language: Lay the two choices out on paper and compare them. Starting work earlier earns you the wages of three years in low-entry jobs, and only that once. Carrying on with study buys back the slice you earn extra each year afterwards, multiplied by the thirty or forty years that remain. In urban China an extra year of study brings average earnings 4.0% higher (1988) to 10.2% (2001). The data is old, so it gives you an order of magnitude only.
- Benefit: This is an estimate for urban China from 1988 to 2001. How much an extra year of study raises earnings is called the return to education. The text says 「从 1988 年每受教育一年 4.0% 大幅上升到 2001 年的 10.2%」, that is, it rose sharply from 4.0% for each year of education in 1988 to 10.2% in 2001. Most of the rise came after 1992. What it reflects is the extra slice earned by people who go on to university compared with those who do not, that is, the higher education wage premium, getting larger. This is the domestic Chinese order of magnitude you can use when converting an extra year of study into an annual income gap. For the global average, see item 7 in this section (the global average return to an extra year of education)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Zhang J, Zhao Y, Park A, Song X (2005). Economic returns to schooling in urban China, 1988 to 2001. Journal of Comparative Economics, 33(4), 730-752. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jce.2005.05.008>
- Notes: Before you do the sum, take off the tuition waivers and grants in item 4 in this section (secondary vocational tuition and grants), and set against them the account in item 2 in this section (an extra year of education and mortality). The data runs up to 2001, covers urban areas only and cannot be used as today's figures. The global average of 9% in item 7 of this section is the same. If you really work it through this way, the answer differs from person to person. High tuition, a major that does not match the job and a long course can come out genuinely not worth it, but that shows only that this particular programme is not worth it, and says nothing about study being useless. Several things also sit outside the formula: the education thresholds set by law, see item 5 in this section (linked admissions and separate examinations in secondary vocational schools); resistance to being replaced, see item 10 in this section (choosing skills that resist automation); and whether you can change industry. The person who benefits is yourself.

### 7. Keep the baseline in mind: the global average private return to an extra year of education, the part that lands on your own income, is about 9% a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Keep this figure in mind and use it as a ruler for weighing up any "learn something" plan.
- In plain language: On a global average, an extra year of study puts about 9% a year on your own income, and the return for women has been higher than for men throughout. This figure is about formal schooling, so it does not apply directly to a course you sign up for. Use it as a ruler: if a course costs you 30,000 yuan and half a year, ask whether the income change it brings matches what one extra year of study would bring.
- Benefit: A review covering 1950 to 2014, 139 countries and 1120 estimates works out that 「多受一年教育的全球平均私人回报率是每年 9%」, that is, the global average private rate of return to an extra year of education is 9% a year. The private return is the part that lands on the person's own income: an extra year of study puts about 9% on average on income. The private return to higher education is rising, while the social return, the part that lands on society as a whole rather than on the person, remains high. The average return to education for women has continued to exceed that for men.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Psacharopoulos G, Patrinos HA (2018). Returns to investment in education: a decennial review of the global literature. Education Economics, 26(5), 445-458. <https://doi.org/10.1080/09645292.2018.1484426>; World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 8402. <https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-8402>
- Notes: This is the global average for an extra year of study. It is not the return on signing up for a course, and it is not a Chinese figure; it is an order of magnitude for comparison. Its use is to give every training plan one ruler: if a course costs you 30,000 yuan and half a year, you should ask whether it can bring an income change equivalent to one year of formal education. Note too that a global average hides large differences between fields and between countries.

### 8. Before paying for a certificate, check whether it is in the national catalogue of vocational qualifications or on the Ministry of Human Resources list of registered assessment bodies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. It takes a check of ten-odd minutes.
- In plain language: Some organisations run courses and charge fees and hand out certificates freely in the name of new occupations and skill levels, and some impersonate authoritative bodies. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security has run a campaign against this. Before you sign up, check three things. Is this occupation in the national catalogue of vocational qualifications? Is the issuing body an assessment body registered with the human resources department? Can the certificate number be found on an official platform? If not, the enrolment fee is money thrown away.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security has run a nationwide campaign on exactly this. The document names the abuses: 「一些机构和单位以新职业职业资格、职业技能等级等名义随意举办培训、评价、发证活动，乱收费、滥发证；一些机构和单位虚假或夸大宣传，甚至假冒权威机构名义组织培训、评价、颁发证书等」, that is, some organisations and units run training, assessment and certification activities arbitrarily in the name of new occupational qualifications and vocational skill levels, charging fees without order and issuing certificates without restraint, while others advertise falsely or exaggerate, or even organise training, assessment and certification in the name of authoritative bodies. The measures include joint enforcement with the cyberspace, public security and market regulation departments, 「严厉打击假冒鉴定机构、人社部门备案评价机构的评价发证活动」, that is, severely cracking down on assessment and certification activities that impersonate appraisal bodies or registered assessment bodies of the human resources department. Non-compliant publicity on websites and in new media is shut down under the law. The document also requires the authorities to 「引导公众了解证书查询渠道和方式，判断证书合法合规性和含金量」, that is, guide the public to the channels and methods for looking up a certificate, and to judge whether a certificate is lawful and compliant and what it is worth.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (人力资源社会保障部) (2022). Notice on the Special Governance of "Boutique Certificates" in Technical and Vocational Skills. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/18/content_5679641.htm>
- Notes: Check in this order. First, is this occupation in the National Catalogue of Vocational Qualifications (《国家职业资格目录》)? If it is not in the catalogue, it is not a national vocational qualification. Second, is the issuing body an assessment body registered with the human resources department? Third, go to the official platform for looking up skill talent assessment certificates and see whether the certificate number can be found. If it cannot, do not enrol at all. Anything carrying 「国际」, international, 「高级」, senior, or 「注册」, registered, and promising 「包过」, a guaranteed pass, or 「挂靠有收入」, income from affiliation, carries the highest risk.

### 9. For training, go through the government subsidy channel first, and do not pay for a commercial course straight away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, or a large reduction. The training itself takes time. The hard part is resisting the urge to pay for a course on impulse and looking up the subsidy catalogue first.
- In plain language: From 2025 to the end of 2027, the state plans to run subsidised training for more than 30 million places. The money comes from several channels, among them employment subsidy funds, the unemployment insurance fund and enterprises' employee education funds. The scarcer the trade and the higher the cost of the training, the larger the subsidy. If you are employed, ask your employer first. ### 10. When choosing a skill, look first at whether it needs hands and on-the-spot judgement, because those are hardest for automation to replace
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before you choose, run the jobs you are considering through this dimension.
- In plain language: On the American estimate, about 47% of jobs are at a high risk of being taken over by computers, though a later estimate broken down into specific tasks gives a much lower figure. Machines have three hurdles they cannot get over. Manual dexterity. Coming up with something new. Dealing with people. So jobs that put you on site, in a non-standard environment, where you have to judge things as they come, are harder to replace, and jobs with a fixed process and mostly information handling carry the highest risk.
- Benefit: Each of 702 American occupations was rated one by one on how likely the job is to be handed over to a computer, using a computability probability estimate. The result was that about 47% of jobs fall in the high-risk band. What decides the rating in the model are three hurdles machines cannot yet get over, which the authors call engineering bottlenecks. First, perception and manipulation, which includes finger dexterity, manual dexterity and working in tight spaces and awkward positions. Second, creative intelligence, the ability to come up with something new. Third, social intelligence, the ability to deal with people. So work in a non-standard environment that needs manual skill and judgement on the spot is consistently less likely to be replaced, and that is not an accident of a few occupations. Jobs with a fixed process and mainly information handling carry the highest risk.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Frey CB, Osborne MA (2017). The future of employment: how susceptible are jobs to computerisation? Technological Forecasting and Social Change, 114, 254-280. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.08.019>
- Notes:Disputed. The study is an unpublished working paper from 2013, and what it rates is whether a whole occupation can be handed to a machine. Later work broke the question down into individual tasks and got a much lower high-risk share. It also does not say in which year any of this happens. When you use it, do not fix on the figure of 47%; look at the three places where it says machines are stuck. On-site installation, maintenance and nursing are more resistant to replacement than pure paperwork, and that is exactly why. So "learning a skill" should first and foremost mean one that puts you on site, facing a non-standard environment.

### 11. Before you start, check whether this skill has a graded assessment, and whether the grade can be turned into money

- In plain language: The state is promoting a new eight-level worker scheme, which requires employers to link post wages to skill levels and lets outstanding employees be recognised at a level without going by length of service. - Notes: This is the direction of policy, not a wage increase already being paid out, and how far it has been implemented varies a great deal between localities and employers. ### 13. For the same money and time, choose a short course that leads straight to a job

### 14. After studying, close the book and test yourself, and do not go back and reread
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money, and no increase in the total time. Take the time you would have spent rereading and use it to write from memory with the book shut, or to do questions. The hard part is that self-testing exposes what you did not remember on the spot, which feels worse than rereading.
- In plain language: Reading something and then reading it again feels familiar, but it is not well remembered. Testing yourself once with the book shut works noticeably better, and adding short quizzes in class raises grades by a moderate amount. Ranking ten common study methods, self-testing comes in the top band. After finishing a section, write down the key points from memory first, then turn back and check.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 222 independent studies and 48478 students: classroom quizzes raise academic achievement, with an effect size in the moderate range (g = 0.499). The effect size depends on several conditions: what study method the control group used, whether the quiz format matches the final exam, whether the material is matched, whether corrective feedback was given, how many times the quiz was repeated, where and when it was taken, how long the intervention lasted, and what experimental design was used. A separate review that assessed ten common learning methods one by one rates "practice testing" and "distributed practice" in the top band (high utility), on the grounds that they work for learners of different ages and abilities, and have been tested across several assessment formats and real educational settings.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Yang C, Luo L, Vadillo MA, Yu R, Shanks DR (2021). Testing (quizzing) boosts classroom learning: A systematic and meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin, 147(4), 399–435. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000309>; Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: Self-testing is there to force you to pull things out of memory, not to score you. How to do it: after finishing a section, shut the book and write the key points from memory, then turn back and check. Corrective feedback works better, so when you check the answers look at where you went wrong. The benefit is graded "large" on the basis of the effect size and the grading. Both of these sources measure grades rather than hours, so they have not been tested against a time-saving threshold. Self-testing and item 15 in this section (spread the same time across several days) are the two highest-rated methods in the same review, and they work well together.

### 15. Spread the same time across several days rather than finishing it in one go
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money, and the total time does not change either. Only the schedule changes: the same 3 hours, split into three days of 1 hour each. The hard part is starting a few days ahead instead of putting it off to the last night.
- In plain language: The same amount of time learned across several days is remembered better than the same time learned in one go. 317 experiments have all been measuring this, and they agree. There is also a practical rule: the further ahead you have to remember it, the longer the gap between reviews should be. If the test is tomorrow, review on the day. If it is a month away, go over it every few days.
- Benefit: This is a meta-analysis bringing together 184 articles, 317 experiments and 839 assessments of the effect of distributed practice. It compared two things: learning in one go against learning split across several sessions, which is the spacing effect, and short gaps against long gaps, which is the delay effect. The conclusion is that the gap between reviews and how long you have to remember it together determine retention on the final test: the longer you need to retain it, the longer the gap that gives the best retention. A separate review that assessed ten common learning methods one by one rates distributed practice in the top band (high utility).
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Cepeda NJ, Pashler H, Vul E, Wixted JT, Rohrer D (2006). Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354–380. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354>; Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: Best used on top of item 14 in this section (test yourself with the book shut): spread the self-testing itself across several days. Most of the material in the Cepeda meta-analysis is word-list memory and the like, and no general number of days is given for the best gap for complicated content. "The further away the test, the longer the gap" is the rule the text gives, with no specific schedule. Cramming the night before does work at the time; the problem is how fast it fades.

### 16. Do not make underlining, rereading and summarising your main study method
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money, and it saves time. The hard part is that these are the easiest to do and give the strongest feeling of "I am studying".
- In plain language: What students use most is underlining and rereading, and in the ranking of ten common study methods both sit in the bottom band. Writing summaries, the keyword mnemonic and imagining a picture to go with a passage are also in the bottom band. They are not entirely useless, but the conditions under which they work are too narrow, and the same time is better spent on self-testing.
- Benefit: A review that assessed ten common learning methods rates them by whether the effect carries across learning conditions, learner characteristics, material and assessment format. The two rated in the top band are practice testing and distributed practice. Those rated in the middle band are elaborative questioning, self-explanation and interleaved practice. The five in the bottom band are: summarising, underlining or highlighting, the keyword mnemonic, imagining a picture to go with a passage, and repeated reading. The review states that most students say they use repeated reading and underlining, but that neither reliably raises grades, and that they should be replaced by other methods, such as using practice testing instead of repeated reading.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: Highlighting is fine for marking the places you are going to test yourself on. The problem is treating the highlighting itself as the learning. The review also makes clear that the bottom band does not mean zero: the conditions under which these work are narrow, and they need more research. For what to do instead, see item 14 in this section (test yourself with the book shut) and item 15 in this section (spread the same time across several days).

### 17. Mix question types when you practise, and do not do twenty of one kind in a row
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Change from working through the workbook in order by question type to doing it in a mixed order. The hard part is that accuracy is lower on the spot when you mix them, which feels like going backwards.
- In plain language: Doing a long run of the same kind of question goes smoothly at the time, and afterwards you may not be able to tell which method to use. Practising several kinds mixed together works better in the long run, by a moderate amount. This technique is picky about material: it works best for diagrams and for categorising, is a little weaker for mathematics, and for vocabulary words it is worse than blocked practice.
- Benefit: This is a multi-level meta-analysis of 59 studies, 238 effect sizes and 158 samples. The overall effect of interleaved practice over blocked practice is in the moderate range (Hedges' g = 0.42). By material: drawing is the best (g = 0.67), and other visual material is also good; the effect for mathematical tasks is smaller (g = 0.34); the overall effect is not significant for expository text and taste materials; and for vocabulary, blocked practice is better instead (g = −0.39). The regression analysis shows that the more similar the categories are to one another, the less similar things are within a category, and the more complicated the material, the greater the benefit of interleaving.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Brunmair M, Richter T (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin, 145(11), 1029–1052. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209>
- Notes: The review quoted in item 16 in this section (do not make underlining your main study method) rates interleaved practice in the middle band, on the grounds that there was not enough research at the time. Put the two together and the use is this: mix practice for content where you have to "recognise which kind this is and which method applies", such as question types in mathematics, case files and which rule of law applies; practise in blocks material you have to memorise, such as vocabulary words and definitions of terms.

### 18. Do not pick a study method on the basis of "I am visual and he is auditory"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. You simply stop picking material and courses by this classification, and stop paying for a style assessment.
- In plain language: Many people believe that everyone has a learning style of their own, such as visual or auditory, and that you learn better when you are taught to your style. Very few studies have tested this carefully, almost none support it, and a few come out exactly the other way. People do have preferences, but there is no evidence that lessons matched to a preference are learned better, and no reason to pay for a style assessment.
- Benefit: A review commissioned to assess the evidence on learning styles points out that showing that "teaching to learning style works" requires grouping students by style first, randomly assigning each group to a different teaching method, and then testing everyone with the same test. The criterion for real evidence is the appearance of one particular interaction effect: a method that works best for one style must not be the best for another style. The review concludes that almost no such interaction has been found. There is a great deal of literature on learning styles, but very little of it uses an acceptable experimental design, and several of those studies come out directly against the matching hypothesis. On that basis the authors consider the existing evidence insufficient to support using learning style assessments in education.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pashler H, McDaniel M, Rohrer D, Bjork R (2008). Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 9(3), 105–119. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x>
- Notes: The review also makes clear that this does not mean every version of learning style theory has been tested and refuted; many have never been tested at all. What you want to avoid is paying for a style assessment and picking a training course by style. For the criteria to use when choosing training, see item 9 in this section (training goes through the government subsidy channel first) and item 13 in this section (choose a short course that leads straight to a job).

### 19. Set questions for yourself in terms of how you will use the material, instead of reciting it once and counting that as done
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money, and no extra time. Only the practice changes: "reciting it once through" becomes "setting questions for yourself in terms of how you will use it". The hard part is having to work out first how these things will be used later.
- In plain language: Testing yourself with the book shut does more than check the few words you had memorised. 192 comparisons put together, involving more than ten thousand people, show that it works too for questions with a changed format, for applied problems and for reasoning problems, by a moderate to small amount. There are two situations where it is almost useless: reversing the question and the answer, and material you have only seen in the book and have never been tested on.
- Benefit: This meta-analysis brings together more than 40 years of research, covering 67 published and unpublished articles, 122 experiments and 192 transfer effect sizes, with 10382 people in total. Compared with a control that rereads without being tested, the learning produced by practice testing does transfer to other situations, with an effect size d=0.40 (95% CI 0.31 to 0.50). Transfer is strongest where the question format is changed, where applied problems and reasoning problems are tested, where the problem is a medical diagnosis, and where a clue word of similar meaning is tested. Transfer is weakest where the stimulus and the response are reversed, where the material was seen at first learning but never tested, and where the problem is a worked example. Among the moderators, whether the response format is the same in practice and in the test, whether retrieval practice with detailed feedback has been done, and whether the first quiz was answered correctly all strongly affect whether positive transfer appears. The authors also corrected for publication bias in two ways. The effect sizes of the moderators were barely affected, but the intercept estimate came down markedly: when none of the conditions above is present, positive transfer often cannot be seen.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pan SC, Rickard TC (2018). Transfer of test-enhanced learning: meta-analytic review and synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 144(7), 710-756. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151>
- Notes: The word "recite" normally runs two things together: going over the material once more, which is rereading and underlining, and actively recalling with the book shut. The first is rated lowest in the review that ranks ten study methods; to see the ratings themselves, see item 16 in this section (do not make underlining, rereading and summarising your main study method). What you want is the second: active recall with the book shut, described in item 14 in this section (test yourself with the book shut), and done across several days, see item 15 in this section (spread the same time across several days). How to do it is to work out first how you will use this later: whether you have to pick the right answer on a paper, or judge it on the spot, and then set questions for yourself in that form. It is also worth doing the reverse, because transfer in the reversed direction was the weakest in this meta-analysis, so both directions need practice.

### 20. Work out which series and which grade you are in before you apply for a professional title, then find the application channel from the nature of your employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Look up your province's application notice for that year once. It takes about half an hour.
- In plain language: Professional titles are divided nationally into 27 series, and each series into junior, intermediate and senior. In general you apply level by level. Where you work for an organisation, the organisation checks and posts the application before sending it up. In a private enterprise you can go to a professional title application service point, or apply through a talent intermediary service organisation or an industry association. Self-employed people apply to the assessments organised by the local human resources and social security department.
- Benefit: There are 27 professional title series nationwide, such as economics, engineering, health and publishing. Titles are set in three levels: senior, intermediate and junior. Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40 applies to enterprises, public institutions, social organisations, individual economic organisations and self-employed people. An applicant generally applies level by level. The organisation you work for has to check the material and post it inside the organisation, and the posting period is not less than 5 working days. For private enterprises and self-employed people, a personnel agency or a similar body may carry out the checking, posting and recommendation on your behalf. Localities have to set up professional title application service points in technology parks, high-tech development zones and similar places, and may also accept applications through talent intermediary service organisations, chambers of commerce and industry associations, and learned societies. Self-employed people who meet the conditions take part, according to where they are registered, in the assessments organised by the local human resources and social security department. Where material can be verified online, you may not be required to produce a separate certificate. Where the material is incomplete, the assessing body has to tell you in one go what is missing. People in private enterprises generally apply where the employment relationship sits. Where you have been posted elsewhere and have worked there continuously for a full year, you may apply at the place you were posted to. Retired people may not apply. Where a public institution's staff member has been given a punishment of demotion or above, that person may not apply while the punishment is in force (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

A paper is no longer required in every case: the 2016 reform opinions say not to make a paper a hard threshold for applied professionals, and no unified requirement is set for the foreign language and computer exams in professional titles. Skilled workers have a route too: highly skilled people who meet the conditions may take part in professional title assessments in the engineering series. Civil servants may not take part in professional title assessments.

### 21. Junior and intermediate titles such as accounting are won by a national unified exam, so check your education and years of work before you register
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: The registration fee is set by each province. Preparing for the exam takes several months of your spare time.
- In plain language: In some series the junior and intermediate titles are won by passing an exam, and passing is enough, with no separate assessment afterwards. Accounting is one: an accounting assistant needs senior high school or above, and an accountant needs a bachelor's degree with 4 years of accounting work, or a junior college diploma with 5 years. Holding certain vocational qualifications may also count directly as holding the corresponding professional title.
- Benefit: The 2016 reform opinions provide that where a profession uses a national unified exam for junior and intermediate titles, no corresponding assessment or recognition is carried out. In fields where professional titles and vocational qualifications are closely linked, holding a vocational qualification is recognised as holding the corresponding professional title, and may also count as a condition for applying for the level above. Take accounting as the example. Accounting assistants and accountants sit a national unified exam. The senior accountant exam is combined with an assessment, and the highest grade generally uses assessment alone. The entry conditions are as follows. Accounting assistant: senior high school graduation or above, including technical secondary schools, vocational high schools and technical schools. Accountant: a doctoral degree; or a master's degree with 1 year of accounting work; or a second bachelor's degree or a postgraduate course with 2 years of work; or a bachelor's degree with 4 years of work; or a junior college diploma with 5 years of work. Those with a professional master's or doctoral degree in accounting are exempt from the paper on Financial Management when sitting the accountant exam (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

### 22. Do not pay an agency to assess your title for you, do not buy a ghostwritten paper, and do not falsify your material: if it is checked out, the title is withdrawn and a record is kept for 3 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is resisting the advertisements promising a pass for money.
- In plain language: A title obtained with false documents, a ghostwritten paper or plagiarised work is withdrawn once it is checked out. The record of dishonesty is kept for 3 years and goes onto the national credit platform. If the personal undertaking you sign when you apply turns out to be untrue, you may not apply again for 3 years. The human resources departments also run specific campaigns against agencies over false advertising, impersonated assessments and fake certificates.
- Benefit: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40 provides that a title obtained by supplying false documents, plagiarising another person's work or academic results, or by other improper means is withdrawn. At the same time it is recorded in the integrity file for professional title assessments, brought into the national credit information sharing platform, with a retention period of 3 years. The 2024 supervision measures name several kinds of violation by applicants: false undertakings and fabricated information, faked or ghostwritten papers, faked achievement records, and lobbying. An applicant has to sign a personal undertaking at the time of application, and if it is untrue, that person may not apply for 3 years. The human resources departments work with the public security, cyberspace and market regulation authorities to investigate agencies running false websites, false advertising, contract traps, impersonated assessments and fake certificates. Serious cases that may involve a crime are transferred to the competent authorities (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

### 23. Getting a professional title does not mean a pay rise: first find out whether your employer appoints by post quota, or whether being awarded does not mean being appointed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask the human resources department of your employer once.
- In plain language: A professional title is the basis for appointment, assessment and promotion, but being awarded and being appointed are two different things. Public institutions generally assess within the post quota, and being awarded usually leads to appointment. Organisations that do not run post management may separate assessment from appointment, and being awarded does not mean the organisation gives you the matching post and pay. After you change employer or move to another place, the title may have to be assessed again or formally confirmed.
- Benefit: Article two of Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40 states that the result of a professional title assessment is an important basis for the appointment, assessment and promotion of professional and technical staff. The 2016 reform opinions provide that public institutions which fully implement post management, and where the academic level of professional and technical staff is closely tied to the duties of the post, generally carry out professional title assessments within the post structure quota. Organisations that do not run post management, and series that are widely applicable and spread across all kinds of social organisation, together with new occupations, may separate assessment from appointment. When professional and technical staff move across regions or across organisations, the title is assessed again or formally confirmed according to the level of management authority, except where the state has provided otherwise (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40 (人力资源和社会保障部令第 40 号) (2019). Interim Provisions on the Administration of Professional Title Assessments. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5437137.htm>: Articles two and twenty-nine; General Office of the CPC Central Committee and General Office of the State Council (中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅) (2016). Opinions on Deepening the Reform of the Professional Title System. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5163445.htm>: 「对于全面实行岗位管理、专业技术人才学术技术水平与岗位职责密切相关的事业单位，一般应在岗位结构比例内开展职称评审。对于不实行岗位管理的单位，以及通用性强、广泛分布在各社会组织的职称系列和新兴职业，可采用评聘分开方式。」
- Notes: The grade is B because the documents only say how assessment and appointment fit together, and give no national figure for "how much more you earn once you have the title". What you can act on is this: in a public institution or a state-owned enterprise, ask the human resources department what post vacancies your organisation has and what its appointment rules are. In a private company, ask your boss whether this certificate counts and whether it comes with money.
