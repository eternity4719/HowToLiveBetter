# 20. Caring for a newborn

This section measures infant mortality and money. It takes in only the things that decide whether the child lives, or what you have to spend, and only those where the difference is large and the evidence is solid. How to soothe a baby and the other childcare details are not in this book. For what to do before the birth and on the day you leave hospital, see section 27.

### 1. Put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with nothing soft on the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. You have to swap the mattress for a firm one, which costs a few hundred yuan. The hard part is holding your line with older relatives who say otherwise.
- In plain language: Put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface. The adult and the baby sleep in the same room, but not in the same bed. No pillows, no duvets, no cot bumpers, no soft toys and no shaping pillows on the bed. A reclined cradle or a baby seat is for sitting only, not for sleeping in. In the United States alone, about 3500 babies a year die in their sleep.
- Benefit: In the United States about 3500 babies a year die of sleep-related causes. That covers sudden infant death syndrome, deaths where no cause was found, and babies who suffocated by accident in a bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics gives a safe sleep environment as 「仰卧位；使用坚实、不倾斜的睡眠表面；同房不同床；避免柔软的寝具和过热」, that is, on the back, on a firm non-inclined sleep surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with no soft bedding and no overheating. Several other things lower the risk as well: breastfeeding, keeping the baby away from nicotine and alcohol, vaccination on schedule, and using a pacifier
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: 「床上不放任何软东西」means no pillows, no duvets, no cot bumpers, no soft toys and no shaping pillows. No breastfeeding pillow either, however safe it looks. A reclined cradle or a baby seat is not a place to sleep. Within this section, this is the item where the difference is largest and where you most need to hold your line against older relatives.

### 2. Give the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hepatitis B vaccine is on the national immunisation programme, so it is already free. The hospital gives it on the day the baby is born, and you do not have to go anywhere else for it.
- In plain language: The baby needs the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of birth. Among people who catch hepatitis B in infancy or early childhood, about 95% go on to develop it chronically. Most people who catch it as adults clear it on their own. So the earlier this shot goes in, the better it works. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the baby also needs hepatitis B immune globulin.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization says 「所有婴儿都应在出生后尽早（24 小时内）接种乙肝疫苗」, that is, all infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth, within 24 hours. The reason is that 「婴儿期和幼儿期感染约有 95% 会发展为慢性肝炎」, that is, about 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood develop into chronic hepatitis. Most infections picked up in adulthood clear without treatment
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- Notes: If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the baby also needs a shot of hepatitis B immune globulin after birth. Settle this at the antenatal checks rather than after the birth. For the screening the mother herself needs, see section 1

### 3. Complete every vaccine on the national immunisation programme, free throughout; if you miss one, only the unfinished doses are made up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The law says 「接种单位接种免疫规划疫苗不得收取任何费用」, that is, a vaccination provider may not charge any fee for vaccines on the national immunisation programme. You have to make several trips to a vaccination clinic according to the month age. Keep the vaccination certificate safe and do not lose it.
- In plain language: Vaccines on the national immunisation programme are free throughout, and a vaccination provider may not charge for them. Children are checked for their vaccination certificate when they start nursery and when they start school, and any missed doses are found and made up. If you miss a dose, only the unfinished doses are made up. You do not start again from the beginning, and you can change manufacturer and carry on. Eczema, urticaria, allergic rhinitis and asthma can all be vaccinated as long as the condition is stable. So can a newborn with jaundice.
- Benefit: When a child starts nursery and school, the nursery and the school must inspect the vaccination certificate. Where a child has not had the programme vaccines as required, it must be reported to the vaccination provider, which then helps press the guardian to make up the doses. Missed doses therefore cannot be put off, and they are certain to be found at school entry. The programme now in force is the 2026 edition. The human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine is now on the national immunisation programme. The starting age for the diphtheria, tetanus and pertussis vaccine has moved from 3 months to 2 months, 5 doses in total, 1 dose at each of 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months and 6 years old. Eligible children in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps have also had Japanese encephalitis vaccine routinely since March 2026. Several vaccines can be given on the same day: at present every vaccine on the national immunisation programme can be given together under the schedule or under the catch-up rules. Two or more injectable vaccines have to go into different sites, and 「严禁将两种或多种疫苗混合吸入同一支注射器内接种」, that is, it is strictly forbidden to draw two or more vaccines into the same syringe for one injection. Where they are not given on the same day, two or more injectable live attenuated vaccines must be at least 28 days apart. No interval is set between inactivated vaccines, oral live attenuated vaccines and other vaccines. For anyone under 18 years old, catch-up starts as early as possible: 「只需补种未完成的剂次，无需重新开始全程接种」, that is, only the unfinished doses are made up and there is no need to restart the whole schedule. The same vaccine from a different manufacturer can be carried on with (nationwide, 2026 edition of the schedule)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (全国人大常委会) (2019). Vaccine Administration Law, 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html> (reprinted by the Yunnan Provincial Health Commission, 云南省卫生健康委员会); National Disease Control and Prevention Administration and National Health Commission (国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委) (2026). Questions and Answers on the Children's Immunisation Schedule for the National Immunisation Programme and the Explanations, 2026 edition, 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: The most common mistake is 「孩子体质不好，先别打」, that is, the child has a weak constitution, so hold off for now. The official position is this. An "allergic constitution", a family history of allergy and a previous allergic reaction to food or medicine are none of them reasons to withhold a vaccine. Atopic dermatitis (eczema), urticaria, allergic rhinitis and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated as long as the condition is stable. Antihistamines and inhaled steroids do not stop vaccination either. Physiologic and breast-milk jaundice in newborns are also fine. Only two things stop a child: a previous acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, or a severe allergy to a known component of the vaccine. Children with immune deficiency, and children on chemotherapy or radiotherapy or immunosuppressants, need an individual assessment, so explain the situation at the vaccination clinic and decide there. For the first hepatitis B dose, see item 2 in this section. Non-programme vaccines you pay for yourself carry a vaccine fee and a vaccination service fee, which have nothing to do with the free schedule described here.

### 4. Breastfeed only for the first 6 months, not even water; from 6 months add solid food and keep breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money, and you save money on formula. What it costs is time, because feeding takes a good part of every day. The hard part is keeping it up, since you cannot stop in the first 6 months.
- In plain language: Start feeding within the first hour after birth. For the first 6 months give breast milk only, not even water. From 6 months add solid food, with an iron-rich animal food every day, and keep breastfeeding to 2 years old and beyond. If breast milk is not enough or you cannot breastfeed, use formula and do not blame yourself. What you feed matters far less for the child's safety than the sleeping position does.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends 「在出生后第一个小时内开始母乳喂养」, that is, starting breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. For the first 6 months, 「纯母乳喂养，即不提供其他任何食物或液体，包括水」, that is, exclusive breastfeeding with no other food or liquid at all, water included. Then 「从 6 月龄起开始添加安全充足的辅食，同时继续母乳喂养到 2 岁或以上」, that is, from 6 months old start safe and sufficient complementary food while continuing to breastfeed to 2 years old and beyond. The service guide of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) also recommends exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months. The same guide requires that from 6 months old 「每天的辅食必须包含含铁丰富的动物性食物」, that is, the day's complementary food must contain an iron-rich animal food
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>; General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2024). Service Guide for Assessing Nutrition and Feeding of Infants and Young Children (for Trial), 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: If breast milk is not enough or your body does not allow it, use formula. The gap between breast milk and formula is far smaller than the one for safe sleep. How often to add solid food: 1 to 2 times a day at 6 to 8 months, and 2 to 3 times a day at 9 to 12 months. What the child eats in a day should cover at least four of the seven categories. When a child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, whether to avoid peanuts, see item 12 in this section (do not avoid peanuts).

### 5. Make up formula with water above 70 ℃, let it cool before feeding, and throw away what is left
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Each time you wait a few extra minutes for the feed to cool. The hard part is waiting for the water to come down before mixing, when the baby is crying with hunger.
- In plain language: Formula is not sterile and may carry a bacterium called Cronobacter sakazakii. Once a newborn catches it, the reported case fatality rate runs from forty per cent to eighty per cent. When you prepare a feed, pour the hot water into the bottle first and wait until the temperature has fallen to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder. At that point the feed is still above 70 ℃, which kills the bacterium to below one hundred thousandth of its starting count. Let the feed cool before giving it, and throw away what is left.
- Benefit: Formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii. The reported case fatality rate after infection in a newborn is 40% to 80%. Laboratory work shows that pouring the hot water into the bottle first and waiting until the temperature falls to about 73.8 ℃ before adding the powder leaves the feed above 70 ℃ in most cases. That reduces the bacterium by more than 5 log units, down to below one hundred thousandth of its starting count
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>; Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>; World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Notes: The 70 ℃ threshold comes from a public health guideline. Beary and colleagues put it this way: 「针对高危婴儿照护者的公共卫生指南建议用加热到至少 70 ℃（158 ℉）的水冲调配方奶粉以灭活微生物」, that is, public health guidelines for the carers of high-risk infants recommend preparing formula with water heated to at least 70 ℃ (158 ℉) in order to inactivate microbes. Preterm babies, babies with a low birth weight and babies under 2 months carry the highest risk. Do not use water that has sat in a thermos all day. Do not leave a made-up feed standing at room temperature until the next feed.

### 6. No honey before 1 year old
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money
- In plain language: Do not give honey to a child under 1 year old. Honey water, honey mixed into solid food, and cakes that contain honey all count. Honey may carry the bacteria that cause botulism. The gut flora of an adult can hold them down, and an infant's cannot. Home-made pickles and fermented foods follow the same logic.
- Benefit: The US CDC says 「不要给 1 岁以下的儿童喂蜂蜜」, that is, do not give honey to children under 1 year old, because 「蜂蜜可能含有引起肉毒中毒的细菌」, that is, honey may contain bacteria that cause botulism. The gut flora of an adult can hold these spores down, which is the dormant form of the bacteria, and an infant's cannot.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: What counts is honey water, complementary food mixed with honey, and cakes containing honey. The same logic applies to home-made pickles and fermented foods, which babies should not eat either.

### 7. The vitamin K shot at birth is not optional
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan to a few tens of yuan. This is normally already covered by the hospital bill for the birth, and there is nothing extra to pay.
- In plain language: Newborns store very little vitamin K, and without a supplement they can bleed. The late form often shows up as bleeding inside the brain. In places that do not give the shot routinely, 478 in every 100,000 newborns get this bleeding. With the shot, the estimate is a fall of more than seventy-eight per cent. Regular hospitals in China give it as routine. If you gave birth at home or in an unlicensed place, ask whether you had it.
- Benefit: Newborns store very little vitamin K. Without a supplement, vitamin K deficiency bleeding can occur, and the late form often presents as intracranial bleeding, that is, bleeding inside the brain. The Belgian consensus recommendation is 「出生时肌肉注射 1 或 2 毫克维生素 K 一次」, that is, a single intramuscular dose of 1 or 2 milligrams of vitamin K at birth. Where no systematic prophylaxis is provided, vitamin K deficiency bleeding runs at 478 cases per 100,000 newborns, and that figure comes from 42,000 newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimate that prophylaxis lowers the incidence by 「高于 78.5%」, that is, by more than 78.5%
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>; Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: If you give birth in a regular hospital in China, the hospital gives this shot routinely and you need not think about it. If you gave birth at home or in an unlicensed place, go and confirm it yourself. Oral vitamin K works less well than the injection, and the injection here means one given into the muscle.

### 8. A baby under 3 months old at 38 ℃ goes straight to hospital, with no watching it at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan for registration and tests
- In plain language: If the baby is under 3 months old and the temperature reaches 38 ℃, go straight to hospital. Do not watch it at home, and do not give a fever medicine first and then wait. A baby at this age can have a serious bacterial infection and show nothing but a fever. So 「看着精神还行」, looking bright and alert, is not a reason to wait at home. A baby less than 8 days old is not even covered by the guideline, and needs to go at once.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics has written a dedicated guideline for full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days who look reasonably well. It defines fever as 「体温 ≥38.0 ℃」, that is, a temperature of 38.0 ℃ or above. It splits infants into three bands by age, 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days and 29 to 60 days, and gives one management pathway for each. Infants under 8 days old are not even within the scope of this guideline, which means this stage needs care immediately
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Notes: At this age a serious bacterial infection can also show up as nothing but a fever. So looking 「精神还行」, bright and alert, is not a reason to watch the baby at home. Do not give a fever medicine on your own first and then wait and see.

### 9. Never shake a baby, however tired and angry you are
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is stopping your hands when you are too tired or too angry.
- In plain language: A baby's head is large and the neck has no strength. Shaking hard causes bleeding in the brain and in the eyes, which can kill the baby or leave a lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to settle a baby does not count, and the danger is shaking you cannot control. When you are about to lose your temper, put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes. Letting the baby cry for a while is safer than holding the baby and shaking.
- Benefit: A baby's head takes up a large share of the body and the neck muscles are weak. Vigorous shaking causes intracranial and retinal bleeding, that is, bleeding in the brain and in the eyes, and can be fatal or leave a lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to settle a baby does not count, and the danger is uncontrolled violent shaking. Medicine calls this kind of injury from shaking and impact 「虐待性头部外伤」, abusive head trauma. It is the leading cause of death from head injury in children under 2. Between 1999 and 2014 in the United States it killed close to 2250 children under 5
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>; Choudhary AK et al. (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: When you really are about to lose control, put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes. Letting the baby cry for a while is far safer than holding the baby and shaking. If you are caring for the baby alone and are chronically short of sleep, remember this one above all. For what to do with your own temper, see section 3

### 10. Judge nappies on three things rather than the brand: fit, how often you change them, and whether they have been named in a spot check
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred to a few thousand yuan a month, depending on the grade you buy
- In plain language: No official body has ever ranked which brand is best. There are only two things you can check: the product quality spot-check notices of the State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局), and the investigations regulators carry out into specific incidents. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack and try it for a week, look for nappy rash and marks on the skin, and only then buy in bulk.
- Benefit: No official body has published a ranking of which brand is best, so this book recommends no brand. Only two kinds of information can be checked. One is the product quality spot-check notices of the State Administration for Market Regulation. The other is the investigations regulators carry out into specific incidents. After 「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」, the formamide problem in nappies for infants and toddlers, drew attention in month 6 of 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation led the formation of a joint investigation team to look into it
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局) (2026-06-22). The State Administration for Market Regulation led the formation of a joint investigation team to check on 「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」：「针对媒体反映的"婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题"，市场监管总局、工业和信息化部、国家卫生健康委、国家疾控局高度重视，成立联合调查组，核查婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺有关问题，并依法依规处理。有关情况将及时公布。」. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_78c0425db7604b27bd7a3f36ba319975.html>; reprinted by the Chinese Government Website (中国政府网). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm>
- Notes: When the joint investigation team was set up it only said 「有关情况将及时公布」, that the situation would be published in due course. TODO (to be verified: the conclusion of the investigation. As of 30 September 2026, no conclusion on nappies could be found on the SAMR website or in recent national spot-check notices). How to buy in practice: expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack and try it for a week, look for nappy rash and marks on the skin, and only then buy in bulk. Nappy rash comes mainly from changing too infrequently and from poor breathability, and has less to do with the brand than people imagine. For how to check the spot-check notices, see section 5

### 11. For big items, work through borrow, then second-hand, then new, and do not buy the whole set at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No extra money, and what you save usually adds up to thousands of yuan. The hard part is resisting the urge to buy everything at once.
- In plain language: Strollers, cots, swaddles and toys are all used for a short time, and there are plenty of second-hand ones. Ask to borrow first, buy second-hand if you cannot borrow, and only then buy new, which usually saves over a thousand yuan. Do not buy second-hand car seats or mattresses, because damage to a seat after a crash cannot be seen with the eye. For big sums such as a confinement centre or an early education course, wait 24 hours before deciding.
- Benefit: Strollers, cots, swaddles and toys are all used for a short time, and second-hand supply far outruns demand. Two things really must not be economised on and are not recommended second-hand: car seats and mattresses. Structural damage to a car seat after a crash cannot be seen with the eye
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: the author's own experience, no direct literature; for the evidence on car seats, see section 1, and for impulse spending, see section 5
- Notes: This book neither recommends nor rules out confinement centres, early education classes or baby swimming pools. They are all large sums you can choose to spend or not, and the 24-hour cooling-off period described in section 5 applies to them.

### 12. Severe eczema or an egg allergy is not a reason to avoid peanuts: add them early under a doctor's guidance, but never a whole nut
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter is a few tens of yuan. You feed it a few times a week, which fits in with what you already do. Before you start, take the child to see a doctor once for an assessment.
- In plain language: For infants with severe eczema or an egg allergy, giving peanuts regularly from 4 to 11 months leaves only 1.9% with a peanut allergy at 5 years old, against 13.7% among those who avoided peanuts completely. Among children whose skin test already showed some reaction, the figure is 10.6% for those who ate peanuts and 35.3% for those who avoided them. See the doctor before you start. Never give a whole peanut, because it can block the airway.
- Benefit: The British LEAP randomised trial. 640 infants were enrolled, on condition that they had severe eczema, an egg allergy, or both. They were 4 to 11 months old at enrolment. They were randomised into two groups, one eating peanut products regularly and one avoiding peanuts completely, and the peanut allergy rate was read off at 60 months. Among the 530 children whose peanut skin test was negative at enrolment, 13.7% in the avoidance group were allergic against 1.9% in the group that ate peanuts (P<0.001). Among the 98 children whose skin test was already weakly positive at enrolment, the figures were 35.3% in the avoidance group and 10.6% in the group that ate peanuts (P=0.004). Serious adverse events did not differ between the two groups.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Du Toit G, Roberts G, Sayre PH, et al. (2015). Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(9), 803-813. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850>; General Office of the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委办公厅) (2020). Core Messages on Health Education for Infant and Young Child Feeding, 婴幼儿喂养健康教育核心信息. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-08/01/content_5531915.htm>; Perkin MR et al. (2016). Randomized Trial of Introduction of Allergenic Foods in Breast-Fed Infants. New England Journal of Medicine, 374(18), 1733-1743. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1514210> (the EAT trial of ordinary infants, referred to in the Notes)
- Notes: **The form must be peanut butter thinned out or peanut powder mixed into complementary food. Never give a whole peanut.** The feeding guidance from the National Health Commission is plain about it: 「整粒花生、坚果、果冻等食物易吸入气管，引起窒息，婴幼儿应当避免食用」, that is, whole peanuts, nuts, jellies and similar foods are easily drawn into the airway and cause suffocation, and infants and young children should avoid eating them. For what to do when someone is choking and cannot speak, see item 26 in section 13. This applies only to high-risk children: those with severe eczema, or those already allergic to eggs. **See a doctor for an assessment before you start; do not try it at home.** Before enrolment the LEAP trial gave every child a skin test, and children whose skin test reaction was greater than 4 mm were excluded and never given peanuts. The evidence in ordinary children is much weaker. In a separate trial of 1162 ordinary breast-fed infants, the early-introduction group had 5.6% food allergy against 7.1% in the usual group on an intention-to-treat basis, which is not a significant difference (P=0.32). It is significant only among the children who actually followed the plan (2.4% against 7.3%). That way of counting tends to overstate the effect, and the authors themselves say the intention-to-treat analysis failed to show efficacy, while also showing that early introduction is safe. China's official feeding documents currently say nothing about whether to introduce allergenic foods early or to avoid them, so this item rests on the international trial evidence. Note the timing too: LEAP started at 4 months, while China starts complementary food at 6 months, see item 4 in this section (breastfeed only for the first 6 months). At what age a high-risk child starts and how to start, ask the doctor.
