# 29. 遭遇重大打击之后

亲人去世、被告知重病、失业、离婚。这几件事在研究里有个共同点：出事的不只是心情，还有身体。风险最高的那一段，集中在头几周到头一年。这一节不讲怎么「走出来」，只讲这段时间里哪些事必须有人盯住，哪些决定必须往后放，什么时候该去挂号。多数条目算的是死亡概率。谈花钱和待遇的四条算的是钱：别一上来就花钱做哀伤咨询（第 9 条）、打 12356 和挂心理门诊（第 11 条）、不可逆的大决定往后推（第 12 条）、别拿死当还债的办法（第 13 条）。两类数字不互相换算。没有亲人也没有朋友可找的，「找个人盯住」这句话怎么落地见第 6 条。

亲人去世当时的手续、遗体和该取的钱见第 25 节。老人生前的监护与遗嘱见第 17 节。失业能领什么见第 7 节。情绪低落时的运动与光照见第 3 节和第 22 节。有自杀念头时先打 12356 见第 1 节第 25 条、念头的时间尺度见第 1 节第 32 条、救回来之后的后遗症见第 1 节第 33 条。这些本节都不重复。

# 29. After a severe blow

A relative has died, you have been told you have a serious illness, you have lost your job, you have divorced. These events share one thing in the research: what suffers is not only the mood but the body. The risk is at its highest from the first weeks to the first year. This section does not talk about how to "come through" it. It covers what has to be watched during this period, which decisions have to be put off, and when to go and register with a hospital. Most items here measure the probability of death. The four that talk about money and benefits measure money instead: do not go straight to paid grief counselling (item 9), calling 12356 and registering for a psychological outpatient clinic (item 11), putting irreversible big decisions off (item 12), and not using death as a way to pay off debts (item 13). The two kinds of numbers are not converted into one another. If you have neither relatives nor friends to turn to, how "get somebody to keep an eye on you" actually works is in item 6.

The procedures at the time a relative dies, the body, and the money you can claim are in section 25. Guardianship and wills before an older person's death are in section 17. What you can claim after losing your job is in section 7. Exercise and light when you are low are in section 3 and section 22. If you have thoughts of suicide, call 12356 first, see item 25 in section 1, for the time scale of those thoughts see item 32 in section 1, and for the after-effects once someone is saved see item 33 in section 1. None of that is repeated here.

### 1. In the first days after a relative dies, do not stay on your own, and if you have heart disease, high blood pressure or diabetes keep taking your medication; if you have pressure across your chest, call 120
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Do not stay on your own for the next few days, and keep taking your medication.
- In plain language: In the first day after a relative dies, the probability of a heart attack is 21.1 times the usual, and it falls day by day after that. Among people aged 60 to 89, the proportion who have a heart attack or a stroke within 30 days of their spouse dying roughly doubles, and falls back after a month. If you already have cardiovascular disease, do not stop your medication in those days. Pressure across the chest, breath you cannot get, weakness down one side of the body: call 120 immediately.
- Benefit: 1985 patients with acute myocardial infarction, compared within the same person across the different windows before and after onset. 270 of them (13.6%) had lost an important person in the 6 months before onset, and 19 had lost one within 1 day before the heart attack. Within 24 hours of an important person's death, the rate of acute myocardial infarction rose 21.1-fold (95% CI 13.1–34.1, so the true multiple is somewhere between 13 and 34). It fell day by day after that. In terms of actual numbers: among people with a 5% ten-year risk of a heart attack, there is 1 extra case for every 1394 people who have just lost a relative. Among people with a 20% risk, 1 extra case for every 320. A second British study compared 30447 widowed people aged 60 to 89 with 83588 matched controls. Within 30 days of bereavement, 50 people in the widowed group (0.16%) had a heart attack or a stroke, against 67 in the control group (0.08%). IRR 2.20 (1.52–3.15), that is about 2.2 times the risk in the control group. For heart attacks, 2.14 (1.20–3.81), about 2.1 times. For strokes, 2.40 (1.22–4.71), about 2.4 times. The effect weakens after 30 days. Two other conditions were also looked at within 90 days. One was non-myocardial-infarction acute coronary syndrome, where the heart's blood supply fails suddenly but not far enough to count as a heart attack, IRR 2.20 (1.12–4.29). The other was pulmonary embolism, where a clot blocks a vessel in the lung, IRR 2.37 (1.18–4.75)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Mostofsky E, et al. (2012). Risk of acute myocardial infarction after the death of a significant person in one's life: the Determinants of Myocardial Infarction Onset Study. Circulation. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.111.061770>；Carey IM, et al. (2014). Increased risk of acute cardiovascular events after partner bereavement: a matched cohort study. JAMA Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.14558>
- Notes: Hand the pill box to somebody else to keep, and do not rely on your own memory. Do not drive long distances in those days. Do not try to carry it with alcohol; the account for alcohol is in section 2. How to recognise a heart attack or a stroke and how to call an ambulance, see section 13. Both studies followed groups of people and looked back, so they cannot separate how many of them were going to have an event anyway. But both point the same way and cover the same period. If there is nobody to hand the pill box to and you are certain to be alone in the house for those days, see item 6 in this section.

### 2. In the week you are told you have a serious illness, do not go for the results on your own, and put off every decision other than treatment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Have somebody go with you to pick up the results. Push back by a few days any big decision other than treatment.
- In plain language: In the first week after a cancer diagnosis, the probability of suicide is 12.6 times that of people without a diagnosis, and the probability of dying of cardiovascular disease is 5.6 times. By the first year the suicide risk has fallen to 3.1 times, and the harder the cancer is to treat the more marked this is. So take somebody with you the day you collect the results, and do not stay on your own at home afterwards. Things like resigning or selling the house, leave until your next appointment.
- Benefit: Swedish national registers followed more than 6,070,000 people (6,073,240 people) over 1991 to 2006. Compared with people who had not been diagnosed with cancer, the relative risk of suicide in the first week after diagnosis was 12.6 (95% CI 8.6–17.8), that is about 12.6 times. The true multiple is somewhere between 8.6 and 17.8. The incidence in that week is 2.50/1000 person-years, that is 2.5 cases per 1000 people per year. By the first year it falls to 3.1 (2.7–3.5, 0.60/1000 person-years), about 3.1 times. Cardiovascular death in the first week 5.6 (5.2–5.9, 116.80/1000 person-years), about 5.6 times. The first 4 weeks 3.3 (3.1–3.4, 65.81/1000 person-years). The rise falls off quickly within the first year after diagnosis. The harder the cancer is to treat and the lower the chance of surviving it, the more marked the rise is. Comparing the same group of patients across different windows gives the same conclusion
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Fang F, et al. (2012). Suicide and cardiovascular death after a cancer diagnosis. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1110307>
- Notes: These are data on cancer patients. There is no source of the same weight for other serious diagnoses. The number of people involved is actually small: a suicide rate of 2.50/1000 person-years in the first week, that is 2.5 cases per 1000 people per year. This is written down not to frighten you but to show that somebody has to be there in the days just after you find out. Long-term management after diagnosis, reimbursement and follow-up are in section 16 and section 24. If you have nobody to go with, see item 6 in this section (put the neighbours and the community list in place of "that person").

### 3. After losing your job, fix your daily routine, your medical insurance and the rhythm of your job hunt first; do not spend the whole day at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Go and register as unemployed and claim unemployment insurance benefits first, then put job hunting into a fixed schedule. The hard part is getting up on time when nobody is making you.
- In plain language: An unemployed person afterwards has about a sixty per cent higher probability of dying than a person in work, the rise is more marked in men and in the not very old, and it is highest in the first ten years. Take out the effect of smoking and drinking and the rise is about a quarter smaller, and that part is in your own hands. Go and register as unemployed and claim unemployment insurance benefits first; while you are receiving them, medical insurance is paid by the unemployment insurance fund.
- Benefit: 42 studies, 235 estimates of the risk of death and more than 20 million people pooled together. After taking out the effect of age and similar conditions, the average HR for all-cause death among the unemployed is 1.63, about sixty per cent higher than for people in work. Men are higher than women. The effect is marked for people in the early and middle part of their working life and smaller in the later part. The risk is highest in the first 10 years of follow-up and falls after that. In the subset of studies that also took health behaviour such as smoking and drinking into account, the average HR is 24% lower, that is the calculated rise is about a quarter smaller
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). Losing life and livelihood: a systematic review and meta-analysis of unemployment and all-cause mortality. Social Science & Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2011.01.005>
- Notes: This is a study that followed groups of people and looked back, so it cannot tell cause from effect. People who were already in poor health were also more likely to lose their job. But "after taking out the effect of lifestyle habits the effect is 24% lower" gives you somewhere to start. What you can claim after losing your job, where to claim it and how to keep social insurance going, see section 7. What compensation you are owed on dismissal, and not to sign a voluntary resignation, see section 19.

### 4. If a relative died by suicide, accident or murder, do not expect to tough it out; go and look for professional help
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Calling 12356 costs nothing. To see a doctor, registering for a psychological outpatient clinic costs what an ordinary outpatient appointment costs. The hard part is being the one who asks.
- In plain language: Among people whose spouse died by suicide, the chance of a mental disorder being diagnosed within five years is about eighty per cent higher than in the general population for men, and seventy per cent higher for women. Compared with people whose spouse died of another cause, it is seventy per cent higher up to double. People bereaved this way more often feel they have been pushed away by others, and feel ashamed. That is the reason to go and find someone yourself, instead of waiting until you feel better.
- Benefit: Danish national registers followed 6.7 million adults over 1980 to 2014, totalling more than 136 million person-years (the years all of them were followed, added together). For people widowed because their spouse died by suicide, the risk of a newly diagnosed mental disorder within 5 years, compared with the general population, was IRR 1.8 in men (95% CI 1.6–2.0), that is about eighty per cent higher. Women 1.7 (1.6–1.8), about seventy per cent higher. Compared with people widowed by other causes of death, IRR 1.7 in men (1.5–1.9) and 2.0 in women (1.9–2.2), that is seventy per cent higher up to double. In this group, suicidal behaviour, mortality and the proportion needing municipal assistance were all higher as well. Physical conditions such as cirrhosis of the liver and sleep disorders also carried a raised risk. A separate 57 studies are summarised one by one and not pooled. Bereavement by suicide is associated with four things. First, a raised suicide risk in the partner. Second, a raised risk that a parent is admitted to a psychiatric ward after a child has committed suicide. Third, a raised suicide risk in the mother after an adult child has committed suicide. Fourth, a raised risk of depression in a child after a parent has committed suicide. Compared with people bereaved by other violent deaths, people bereaved by suicide more often say that they felt pushed away by others, and felt ashamed
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Erlangsen A, et al. (2017). Association Between Spousal Suicide and Mental, Physical, and Social Health Outcomes: A Longitudinal and Nationwide Register-Based Study. JAMA Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2017.0226>；Pitman A, et al. (2014). Effects of suicide bereavement on mental health and suicide risk. The Lancet Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(14)70224-X>；Kristensen P 等 (2012). Bereavement and mental health after sudden and violent losses: a review. Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1521/psyc.2012.75.1.76>（the review referred to in the notes）
- Notes: A sudden, violent death means an accident, a murder, a disaster. After a death like this, the proportions of post-traumatic stress disorder, depression and grief that has got stuck are all higher than after an illness. Recovery is slower as well. What to do when you have thoughts of suicide yourself, see item 25 in section 1.

### 5. In the first six months after a spouse dies, hand meals, sleep, the medication for a chronic illness and the follow-up appointments to one fixed person to keep an eye on them
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Find a specific person, a child, a brother or sister, or a friend, and have them come round or telephone on a set schedule. The hard part is asking someone to take this on.
- In plain language: Within six months of being widowed, the probability of dying in the same period is about forty per cent higher than for someone who has not been bereaved. After six months it falls to about fifteen per cent higher. Men are about twenty-three per cent higher; the figure for women is so small it may be chance. This gap is not something you can carry by telling yourself to think positive. What to do is pin the medication, the meals and the follow-up appointments onto one specific person, and not rely on your own memory.
- Benefit: 15 studies that registered first and then followed people forward, 2,263,888 people pooled together. When bereavement has lasted less than 6 months, the RR of death is 1.41 (95% CI 1.26–1.57), that is about forty per cent higher than for people who have not been bereaved. After 6 months it is 1.14 (1.10–1.18), down to about fifteen per cent higher. Men RR 1.23 (1.18–1.28), about twenty-three per cent higher. Women 1.04 (1.00–1.08, not statistically significant, that is this difference may be chance). No clear difference was seen between people under 65 and people aged 65 and over. A separate analysis pooled 123 papers, 1377 risk estimates and more than 500 million people. After taking out differences in conditions, HR 1.23 (1.19–1.28), that is about twenty-three per cent higher overall. Men are still higher than women. Men 1.27 (1.19–1.35), twenty-seven per cent higher. Women 1.15 (1.08–1.22), fifteen per cent higher
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon JR, et al. (2011). Widowhood and mortality: a meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023465>；Shor E, et al. (2012). Widowhood and mortality: a meta-analysis and meta-regression. Demography. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s13524-012-0096-x>
- Notes: This is a study that followed groups of people and looked back; other factors cannot be taken out of it. A married couple's lifestyle and illnesses are similar to begin with. But the pattern that "the first six months are far higher than the ones after" is the same in two independent meta-analyses, and it is worth treating as a window of care. Men's risk is higher, and they also ask for help less often. If there is a widowed older person at home, sort out the accounts, guardianship and wills together as in section 17. If you cannot find such a person (you live alone, you have no children left, or your children are gone too), hand this to the community and to your phone, see item 6 in this section.
### 6. If you have neither relatives nor friends, replace "the person keeping an eye on you" with three things: a neighbour who can get into your home, the community's visiting list, and an emergency contact in your phone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is speaking up, telling a neighbour and the residents' committee "I live on my own, something has just happened at home".
- In plain language: With neither relatives nor friends, there are two things to look after: that somebody finds out if something goes wrong, and that you take your medication and eat on time. The medication and the meals rest on you: write a list, set an alarm, do not rely on memory. Somebody finding out rests on three things: leave a spare key with a neighbour, ask the residents' committee whether you can be put on the visiting and care list, and set up an emergency contact in your phone and not switch it off.
- Benefit: 44,573 outpatients, all of whom had or were at risk of atherothrombosis (the kind of condition where plaques grow inside the arteries and clots form easily). Doctors in several countries registered them into the international REACH project, entry from 2003-12 to 2004-12, and they were followed for 4 years. 8,594 of them (19%) lived alone. Among those living alone, all-cause death over the 4 years was 14.1%, against 11.1% for those living with others. Cardiovascular death 8.6% against 6.8%. Both log-rank P<0.01, so the difference is unlikely to be chance. The gap differs by age group (interaction P=0.03). At 45 to 65, all-cause death living alone against living with others was 7.7% against 5.7%. After taking out other conditions, HR 1.24 (95% CI 1.01–1.51), that is about twenty-four per cent higher. At 66 to 80 it was 13.2% against 12.3%, HR 1.12 (1.01–1.26), about ten per cent higher. Above 80 it was 24.6% against 28.4%, HR 0.92 (0.79–1.06), no rise seen. A 2022 opinion from the Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) and ten other departments provides for visiting and care services. The subjects are elderly people who live alone, have empty-nest households, have children left behind, are disabled or severely disabled, and families in special family-planning circumstances. The forms of service are 「定期上门入户、电话视频、远程监测」, that is regular visits to the home, telephone and video, and remote monitoring. Townships (subdistricts) do the headcount and the screening, village (residents') committees help; they record the basic information and 「接受探访关爱服务的意愿」, the willingness to receive visiting and care services, and bring it into grassroots grid management, which means each area is assigned to a named person to keep an eye on it. By the end of 2025 「确保特殊困难老年人月探访率达到 100%」, that is make sure the monthly visiting rate for elderly people in special difficulty reaches 100%, and 「对失能的特殊困难老年人，每月探访关爱不少于一次」, at least one visit a month for disabled elderly people in special difficulty. The document also advocates that households install smart call systems, smart water meters, health monitoring products and elderly monitoring devices, so that 「一旦监测异常，能够及时预警，并同步向紧急联系人发送提示信息」, that is as soon as monitoring shows an abnormality an alert can be raised in time and a message sent at the same time to the emergency contact. Where service staff find an emergency they 「应当第一时间协助拨打紧急求助电话」, that is they shall assist in dialling the emergency number at the first opportunity
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Udell JA, et al. (2012). Living alone and cardiovascular risk in outpatients at risk of or with atherothrombosis. Archives of Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archinternmed.2012.2782>；Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部) and ten other departments (2022). Guiding Opinions on Carrying Out Visiting and Care Services for Elderly People in Special Difficulty（关于开展特殊困难老年人探访关爱服务的指导意见）(民发〔2022〕73 号). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-10/13/content_5718017.htm>
- Notes: How to do the three things. First, give a spare key or the temporary code of your door lock to a neighbour or to the building management, and say "I am on my own for the next few days". You do not need to be close to them; you only need somebody to know you are in the house. Second, tell the community residents' committee that you live alone and ask whether you can be put on the visiting and care list. Among the elderly, those who live alone, have empty-nest households or have no children left are named official subjects for the service; while you are there, ask whether your area has free smart call devices or smart water meters to install. Third, set up an emergency contact and a medical emergency card in your phone, and do not silence it or switch it off for those days. There are two reasons for the B. First, the link between living alone and mortality comes from a study that followed a group and looked back, so the reverse explanation, that people who were already in poor health and worse off are more likely to live alone, cannot be taken out; the original paper itself says the result remains to be confirmed. Second, the visiting and care document covers elderly people only. There is no matching scheme for anyone under 60. What you can rely on is one line in that document, which requires grid workers and social workers to 「及时发现家庭变故、失业、失学等心理危机风险」, that is identify in good time psychological crisis risks such as family upheaval, job loss and dropping out of school (see item 11 in this section, call 12356 and have somebody to talk to). Why "somebody in the room" is worth so much, see item 1 in section 13 (pressing on the chest). Of cardiac arrests outside hospital, 79.2% happen at home. Where somebody is there doing compressions, 16.1% are discharged alive, against 3.9% where nobody is. Nobody in the room puts you in the lower band. Two more things you can do in passing. A funeral itself means going to the funeral home, the community health service, the police and the housing provident fund, several counters in all (see section 25). Put that into a fixed schedule of one trip out a day, and the arrangements for going out during those days are already there. The hotline can be called as often as you like; it is not a single permitted call (see item 11 in this section, call 12356 and have somebody to talk to). "Living alone" is itself a long-term risk factor. The mortality ratio for living alone is 1.32, about thirty per cent higher, see item 10 in section 22 (treating regular contact with other people as a health expense). But this item only covers the next few days; the long-term account is a separate one.

### 7. After a parent dies, tell the child the truth, let them attend the farewell, and do not send them somewhere else to "change the scenery"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is that you are in pain yourself and still have to say it clearly to the child.
- In plain language: Children who lose their father or mother before the age of 18 have about a fifty per cent higher probability of dying in early adulthood than children who have not been through it. Where the parent died by accident or suicide it is about eighty per cent higher, and where the parent died of illness about thirty per cent higher. What the family can do is tell him the truth, let him attend the farewell, not interrupt his schooling, and not send him to a relative's house to change the scenery.
- Benefit: The entire populations born in Denmark from 1968 to 2008 and in Sweden from 1973 to 2006 are counted. A sample of 89.3% of those born in Finland from 1987 to 2007 is added in. Altogether more than 7.3 million people. 189,094 of them lost a parent before the age of 18. All-cause mortality in this group of children was 50% higher than in those who had not been bereaved (mortality ratio 1.50, 95% CI 1.43–1.58, that is about 1.5 times). Where the parent died by accident, suicide or another unnatural cause it was 1.84 (1.71–2.00), about eighty per cent higher. Where the parent died of illness or another natural cause it was 1.33 (1.24–1.41), about thirty per cent higher. The figure is highest where the child's cause of death falls in the same category as the parent's
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Li J, et al. (2014). Mortality after parental death in childhood: a nationwide cohort study from three Nordic countries. PLoS Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001679>
- Notes: Do not make up "has gone away on a long trip" when you talk to the child; the arrangements for guardianship and support have to be put in writing. The grade is not B because the numbers are weak. This is a follow-up study of the entire populations of three countries, and the numbers themselves would support an A. It is B because between that number and the specific practice of "how to talk to the child and whether to let him attend the farewell" there is no direct trial evidence. The authors say so themselves: after bereavement there is a shortage of data on the parent-child relationship and on how children live. The increase contains both an inborn-constitution part and a long-term effect left behind by the bereavement. Naming a guardian and arranging property, see section 17. Child support and schooling, see section 18.

### 8. If grief has gone six months and you are still stuck and cannot get through your days, register with psychiatry or clinical psychology
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The registration fee plus the treatment. This kind of treatment runs in courses; in the trials it averaged 16 sessions over 19 weeks. The hard part is being the one who goes and registers.
- In plain language: About one in every ten bereaved adults gets stuck: six months or more of missing the person so much that they cannot eat, cannot work and cannot look after their children, going numb, and feeling that life has no meaning. This is not a matter of not being strong enough. It has diagnostic criteria and a treatment of its own. Targeted treatment has an effective rate of 51%, ordinary psychological therapy 28%, and it takes effect sooner as well.
- Benefit: 14 studies pooled together, in adults whose relative did not die by violence and who themselves had not been seen in psychiatry. The prevalence of prolonged grief disorder is 9.8% (95% CI 6.8–14.0), that is about 10 in every 100 bereaved adults. The older a person is, the higher the proportion. A separate study tested whether these diagnostic criteria are accurate. It interviewed 291 bereaved people three times, at 0–6, 6–12 and 12–24 months after the bereavement. The core symptom for diagnosis is intense longing for the dead person. At least five of these nine have to be added to it. The first five are emotional numbness, shock, feeling that life has no meaning, not trusting other people, and pain from this loss. The other four are difficulty accepting it, not recognising who you are now, avoiding reality, and being unable to go on with life. These symptoms have to be there every day, or be severe enough to stop you functioning. And they must still be there at least 6 months after the relative died, and still affect daily life. A separate trial with 95 people assigned at random to two groups gave 16 sessions over an average of 19 weeks. The effective rate for treatment of complicated grief was 51%. Ordinary interpersonal psychological therapy 28% (P=0.02, so the difference is unlikely to be chance). The former took effect sooner as well. Number needed to treat 4.3, that is on average every 4.3 people treated gives one extra person who improves
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Lundorff M, et al. (2017). Prevalence of prolonged grief disorder in adult bereavement: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Affective Disorders. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2017.01.030>；Prigerson HG, et al. (2009). Prolonged grief disorder: Psychometric validation of criteria proposed for DSM-V and ICD-11. PLoS Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1000121>；Shear MK, et al. (2005). Treatment of complicated grief: a randomized controlled trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.293.21.2601>
- Notes: Do not count the months yourself. The real test is whether it is affecting eating, sleep, work and looking after the children; if it is, go and register. The departments are called psychiatry, psychology or clinical psychology, and every public psychiatric specialty hospital has them. This diagnosis goes under different names in different sources: complicated grief, and prolonged grief disorder. The standards also differ on how long it has to last: some say 6 months, some say 12. The 9.8% figure comes from 14 studies that differ quite a lot in method, and the authors warn that it may not carry over to other populations.

### 9. Do not go straight to paid grief counselling; first check whether your grief really has got stuck (the symptoms in item 8)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, and you save the counselling fee instead.
- In plain language: Offering psychological counselling to everyone who has been bereaved brings very little benefit, and after a while it cannot be seen, because most people come through it on their own. But for people who are already clearly not coping, treatment works as well as it does for other psychological problems. So check against item 8 first (grief has got stuck, go to psychiatry), and only then spend the money.
- Benefit: 61 controlled studies pooled together. Offering psychological interventions to the bereaved in general brings very little benefit at the end of treatment, and after some time the benefit cannot be seen. Interventions that take in only the bereaved who are already clearly not coping work as well as psychological therapy for other psychological problems. Studies that do no screening beforehand do badly, because the group that received no intervention would have come through it on their own over time anyway. A separate 14 randomised trials pooled together: treatment given to people who already have a problem is clearly better than the control group both at the end of treatment and at later follow-up, and the effect grows over time. Preventive interventions given to people who do not yet have a problem do not work. A separate study followed 205 people from before the death of a spouse through to 6 and 18 months after it. This study identified five courses. They are ordinary grief, long-term grief, long-term depression, getting better during the bereavement, and resilience (being able to take it). Of these, the resilient course is the commonest and the ordinary-grief course relatively uncommon
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Currier JM, et al. (2008). The effectiveness of psychotherapeutic interventions for bereaved persons: a comprehensive quantitative review. Psychological Bulletin. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.134.5.648>；Wittouck C, et al. (2011). The prevention and treatment of complicated grief: a meta-analysis. Clinical Psychology Review. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2010.09.005>；Bonanno GA, et al. (2002). Resilience to loss and chronic grief: a prospective study from preloss to 18-months postloss. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.83.5.1150>；Stroebe M 等 (2007). Health outcomes of bereavement. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(07)61816-9>（the review referred to in the notes）
- Notes: The point is "not everybody has to do it", not "nobody should do it". People at high risk of the kind covered by item 4 (a relative died by suicide, accident or murder) and people who have already got stuck, of the kind covered by item 8 (grief has got stuck, go to psychiatry), are exactly the ones for whom there is evidence that it should be done. The conclusion of a Lancet review says the same: grief is not an illness, and most people do not need professional intervention. Spend the effort on the people at high risk. And on those who already have complicated grief, depression after bereavement, or a stress disorder.
### 10. After you separate or divorce, do not let medical insurance lapse and do not lean on alcohol
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Keep the medical insurance going and stop the alcohol. The hard part is that this is a period when leaning on alcohol is easy.
- In plain language: People who are separated or divorced have a clearly higher probability of dying early than people who are still married, and the rise is bigger in men and in younger people. Divorce is not necessarily the cause; people who divorce may already differ in health and income. There are two things in your own control: do not let medical insurance or the check-ups lapse, and do not lean on tobacco and alcohol.
- Benefit: 32 studies that registered first and then followed people forward, pooled together, covering more than 6.5 million people, 160,000 deaths, more than 755,000 divorces and 11 countries. Compared with people still married, separated or divorced adults had a clearly higher risk of early death. The rise is more marked in men and in younger people than in women and in older people. The authors discuss four possible pathways. First, social selection: people who were already in worse health or worse circumstances are more likely to end up divorced. Second, loss of resources, where income and protection stop. Third, changed health behaviour, with smoking, drinking and sleep getting worse. Fourth, chronic psychological stress (long-term tension)
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Sbarra DA, et al. (2011). Divorce and Death: A Meta-Analysis and Research Agenda for Clinical, Social, and Health Psychology. Perspectives on Psychological Science. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691611414724>
- Notes: The original only says "clearly higher" and compares the groups; it gives no multiple that can be cited, so no figure is given here. The benefit is graded medium on the basis of "you know the direction, and there is no reduction you can apply a threshold to". Property, debts and bride price in a divorce, see section 8 and section 10. Arrangements for the children, see section 18.

### 11. To have somebody to talk to, call 12356; minors and teenagers call 12355; to see a doctor, register for a psychological outpatient clinic
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Both hotlines are free. A psychological outpatient clinic is registered for like an ordinary outpatient appointment.
- In plain language: If you want somebody to talk to, call 12356. That is the national psychological assistance hotline, and it is free. Minors and teenagers have 12355, the teenage comprehensive service platform. If you want to see a doctor, register for the psychological outpatient clinic of psychiatry, psychology or clinical psychology. Where there is family upheaval, job loss or dropping out of school, the community grid worker and the social worker are supposed to spot it themselves.
- Benefit: A 2026 plan from the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) and 25 other departments requires the working system of the 12356 psychological assistance hotline to be improved. Stable operation has to be guaranteed and the connection rate raised. A provincial 12356 information platform is to be built. The mechanism linking 12356 with service hotlines such as 110 is to be strengthened. The 12355 teenage comprehensive service platform is to be built up and used well, working together with 12356. There are three targets for 2030. First, 80% or more of administrative villages and urban communities are to have a psychological counselling room or a social work room and provide psychological services. Second, among the public psychiatric specialty hospitals at each level and the hospitals of each county (district), at least 1 is to provide psychological outpatient services. Third, every province is to have a provincial 12356 psychological assistance platform. The workforce for psychological crisis intervention and psychological assistance is made up of psychiatrists, psychological therapy technicians, psychological counsellors, social work professionals and volunteers. The plan also requires three kinds of person to keep an eye out and find problems early. These three are grid workers (the staff responsible for an area within the community), social work professionals and volunteers. They are to identify in good time psychological crisis risks such as family upheaval, job loss and dropping out of school (nationwide, 2026-3)
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) and 25 other departments (2026). Notice on Issuing the Implementation Plan for a Sound Social Psychological Service System and Crisis Intervention Mechanism（关于印发健全社会心理服务体系和危机干预机制实施方案的通知）(国卫医政发〔2026〕8 号). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065035.htm>
- Notes: The grade is B because the counselling rooms in villages and communities and the psychological outpatient clinics at county level are targets for 2030, not things every place has now. 12356 and 12355, on the other hand, you can call today. When 12356 opened, and how long it is answered each day, see item 25 in section 1. Psychological counselling and psychological therapy are not the same thing. Only a psychiatrist can make a diagnosis or write a prescription, and the treatment has to be done at a qualified medical institution. The "healing" and "spiritual counselling" sold by the hour on the market are not part of this system.

### 12. In the first three months after a shock, put every irreversible big decision off
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You are only putting the decision off, and you may miss some opportunities because of it. The hard part is that at the time it feels as though the opportunity will not wait.
- In plain language: In this period your judgement of every "opportunity" is at its worst. People who say they can reach the person who died, who say they can get your money back for you, who tell you to resign and start again in another city, who want you to go into business with them, will all come to you. Anything over one month's income, or where the cost of changing your mind is high, put off by three months. Say it out loud first to somebody with no stake in it, and do not decide on the same day.
- Benefit: Avoid disposing of property in a way you cannot undo during the period just after a blow, when judgement is at its worst. These things include selling the house, resigning, investing a lump-sum pension or compensation payment in one go, remarrying in a hurry, and standing surety for somebody else. The amounts involved in decisions of this kind usually run from tens of thousands of yuan to the price of one flat, and the benefit is therefore graded medium. Official documents also list "family upheaval, job loss and dropping out of school" as psychological crisis risks that the community is to screen for proactively, so the state a person is in during this period is already treated as one that needs help from outside
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: The author's own experience, no direct literature; the wording "family upheaval, job loss and dropping out of school" as psychological crisis risks is from National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) and 25 other departments (2026). Implementation Plan for a Sound Social Psychological Service System and Crisis Intervention Mechanism（健全社会心理服务体系和危机干预机制实施方案）(国卫医政发〔2026〕8 号). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065035.htm>
- Notes: If there is nobody around you who has no stake in the matter, call 12356 and say it out loud there (see item 11 in this section, call 12356 and have somebody to talk to), or write it down yourself and read it back three days later. The "three months" is a line the author drew himself, with no literature behind that specific length, so it is graded C. The common scams that target the bereaved and older people are in section 17 (investing in retirement, reverse mortgages on a home) and section 6 (fortune telling). The traps in guaranties and IOUs are in section 8. There are a few payments you have to go and claim yourself, and they are not on the "put it off" list: the housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits, and the three payments on a work-related death, see section 25 and section 19.

### 13. Do not use death as a way to pay off debts: life insurance does not pay for a suicide in the first two years, a work injury is not recognised, and the debts still come out of the estate first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. You are only getting this one account straight.
- In plain language: A life insurance contract does not pay out if you commit suicide within two years; it only returns the part of the policy value that can be returned. A work injury is not recognised either, and you get none of the three payments on a work-related death. The debts are taken out of the estate you leave first, and your family gets only what is left over. They also have to bear the health cost described in item 4 in this section (bereavement after suicide or a violent death).
- Benefit: Article forty-four of the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国保险法）covers contracts in which the payment of insurance money is conditional on the death of the insured person. Counting from the date the contract was made or the date the contract's effect was restored, if the insured person commits suicide within two years, the insurer does not bear the responsibility for paying the insurance money. Where the person committing suicide is a person without capacity for civil conduct, that is, someone who in law cannot take responsibility for their own actions, this does not apply. In that case the insurance company shall return the cash value of the policy as agreed in the contract, that is, it only returns the part of the policy value that can be returned and does not pay the sum assured. The sum assured on a life insurance policy is usually in the tens of thousands of yuan, and the benefit is therefore graded large. Item three of article sixteen of the Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance（工伤保险条例）provides: 「自残或者自杀的，不得认定为工伤或者视同工伤」, that is, where a person injures themselves or commits suicide, it shall not be recognised as a work injury or treated as one. The three payments on a work-related death under article thirty-nine all fall away as a result. First the funeral subsidy, paid at 6 months of the local average monthly wage of employees in the previous year. Second the pension for dependents. Third the one-off work-related death benefit, at 20 times the national per-capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the previous year. Article one thousand one hundred and fifty-nine of the Civil Code（民法典）provides: 「分割遗产应当清偿被继承人依法应当缴纳的税款和债务」, that is, when an estate is divided it shall pay off the taxes and debts that the deceased was lawfully liable for, so the estate pays the taxes and debts first and only what is left is divided. Article one thousand one hundred and sixty-one provides: 「继承人以所得遗产实际价值为限清偿」, that is, an heir repays debts within the actual value of the estate received, so only to the extent of what was inherited and with nothing more to be found from their own pocket, and one who renounces the inheritance bears no repayment responsibility
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2015 revision). Article forty-four of the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国保险法）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7c4060811>；State Council (2010 revision). Regulations on Work-Related Injury Insurance（工伤保险条例）（国务院令第 586 号）articles sixteen and thirty-nine. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；National People's Congress (2020). Articles one thousand one hundred and fifty-nine and one thousand one hundred and sixty-one of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China（中华人民共和国民法典）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: Only the money account is counted here. The other items in this section and in section 1 are about not getting to this point. Article forty-four of the Insurance Law covers only the first two years; it is written out so that the provision is complete, not to give you a timetable. The health cost to the family, see item 4 in this section (a spouse whose partner died by suicide has a 70% to 80% higher risk of a newly diagnosed mental disorder within 5 years). What to do when the thought comes up, see items 25 and 32 in section 1, and the after-effects once someone is saved, see item 33 in section 1. The standards for the three payments on a work-related death, see item 16 in section 19. The specific procedures for the estate and the debts, see item 9 in section 25 (collect the housing provident fund and the social insurance benefits one by one).
